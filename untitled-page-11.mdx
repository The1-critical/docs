Sharks are ancient predators, perfectly evolved for the hunt. Their contract is not one of friendship or mutual respect. Those who sign it become hunters in the truest sense, their blood forever tinged with the salt of the deep. The Mist Village once held a monopoly on this contract, its shinobi using sharks to patrol the waters around their island home. But the times have changed. The Shark Contract has spread beyond Kiri's borders, finding its way to those who understand that the hunt is not a means to an end, it is the end itself.

The sharks do not care for companionship.  They respect only the hunt, and those who prove themselves worthy of joining it. They are weapons, plain and simple, living, breathing, ravenous weapons. The sharks do not care who wields them, as long as they hunt.

And they are very, very good at hunting.

## Techniques

### –( Blood Hunter Form )—

You become a kin of the contract, your body taking on shark-like features. Your skin takes on a blue or orange hue, your teeth become serrated, and your senses sharpen to an almost predatory keenness. You are no longer merely human—you are a hunter.

- **Chakra:** 25
- **Upkeep:** 5
- **Speed:** 15
- **Seal Speed:** 20

**Effects:** You gain a sharp sensitivity to blood. You receive a +1 bonus to Awareness per Wound severity (Minor=1, Major=2, Severe=3, Critical=4) on any target within your sensing range. This bonus stacks across multiple wounds. If the target has an active Bleed status, this bonus is doubled.

You gain the Shark Jaws attack:

**Shark Jaws**

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Damage:** (STR/8)d4
- **Speed:** 6

While in a Grapple (whether Attacker or Defender), this attack has +3 Accuracy. It inflicts Bleed equal to (damage dealt)/30 (rounded down).

---

### –( Dire Hunter )—

Your transformation deepens. The blood calls to you, and you answer.

**Effects:** These effects apply while Blood Hunter Form is active. You suffer no penalties for being underwater. While underwater, your movement speed increases by 20%. You may use Survival to track targets underwater. Your Shark Jaws die increases from d4 to d6. You may increase to d8 by increasing Stamina cost by 2 per attack.

---

### –( Apex Hunter Transformation )—

You shed the last vestiges of your humanity and become one of Dakkokki-ō's Apex Hunters. Your body is a weapon. Your mind is a blade. You are the apex.

- **Chakra:** 25
- **Upkeep:** 8
- **Speed:** 15
- **Seal Speed:** 25

**Effects:** Your CQC Damage Bonus is multiplied by 1.5 when using Shark Jaws. Your Shark Jaws die increases to d8, base Speed to 12. You may increase to d10 by increasing Stamina cost by 4 per attack. When you hit a target with Shark Jaws that has an existing Wound, reduce your Chakra Exhaustion penalty by the severity of their highest wound (Minor=1, Major=2, Severe=3, Critical=4).

## Summons

### Shark Pup

#### 1. Summoning

A small, hyperactive shark pup from the polar regions, a creature that has learned to slide across ice and leap from the water to snatch prey. The adults are too wild to be tamed, but the pups are... enthusiastic.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 15

#### 2. Behavior

The Shark Pup is aggressively playful. It sees the world as a game, and everyone as a potential playmate. It does not understand fear. It does not understand "no." It understands only the hunt, and it wants to be the best hunter you've ever seen. Praise is its reward. Lack of praise is punishment.

**Blood Scent:** The Shark Pup does not suffer Visibility Penalties against targets with an active Bleed status.

**Machinations of the Mighty:** Whenever you summon a Shark Pup, subtract the minimum XP required to summon it (1000 XP) from your Earned XP. The resulting X value determines bonuses:

| Bonus | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Vitality | (X/50) × 10 |
| Accuracy, Defensive, Skill, Genjutsu rolls | (X/200) |
| Damage Bonus | (X/50) × 0.1 |
| Movement Speed | (X/200) × 0.1 |

Round down in all cases. X has a maximum of 3500.

#### 4. Abilities

**Standard Actions:** The Shark Pup may use Dodge, Move, and Block without spending AP.

**Leap Attack** _(Speed 10, 5 AP)_: The Pup leaps and bites, dealing 5d4 Slashing damage. It gains a free Speed 0 Grab attempt with the same Accuracy. Inflicts Bleed equal to (damage dealt)/30.

**Chew** _(Speed 3, 1 AP)_: While the attacker in a Grab, the Pup chews on the defender, automatically dealing 2d4 damage and increasing their current Bleed by 1 (or inflicting Bleed 1 if none exists).

**Belly Slide** _(Speed = AP)_: The Pup slides across water or ice at 3 yards per IC. AP spent equals IC traveled. Each use consumes 1 gallon of water/ice from the battlefield.

#### 5. Terms of Contract

When combat ends, the excited Pup will not unsummon. It will continue attacking any nearby targets. To dismiss it, the summoner must perform the Praise action (Speed 12)—pets, belly rubs, and enthusiastic encouragement. Opponents may attempt an opposed Espionage roll against the Pup's Awareness to steal the praise and dismiss it themselves.

### Great White

#### 1. Summoning

The ocean's apex predator. Twenty feet of muscle, teeth, and hunger. The Great White is not a pet. It is not a companion. It is a weapon, one that will turn on you if you show weakness.

- **Chakra:** 25 + X × 3
- **Speed:** 20 + X × 5
- **Seal Speed:** 20

X = number of sharks summoned (max 3, unlocked at intervals)

You may summon 1 Great White at 2200 XP, 2 at 3900 XP, and 3 at 5200 XP. With Single-Handed Seals, you may summon the Great White inside someone's Water Prison. Summoning brings 2d10+10 gallons of water to the battlefield.

#### 2. Behavior

The Great White has a strict priority list. It will attack targets in the following order:

1. The target with the highest Bleed status
2. The target with the most wounds (count, not severity)
3. The target with the lowest Vitality (or the lowest HP in some situations)

If no target meets these criteria, the Great White becomes docile.

**Underwater Agile:** The Great White does not suffer AoE dodge penalties underwater, and gains +5 to dodge them.

**Bestial:** Cannot be reasoned with. +10 bonus to Genjutsu defense.

**Dire Blood Hunter:** Gains +X Awareness per wound severity on targets (stacking). Doubled if the target has Bleed.

**Large:** 5-yard melee range.

#### 4. Abilities

**Standard Actions:** May use Dodge, Move, Block, and Parry without spending AP.

**Swat** _(Speed 8, Stamina 13, 5 AP)_: Tail sweep. 8d12 Blunt damage (8d80 on land). Knockback 2d6 yards (4d6 on land). Counts as basic unarmed attack.

**Rend** _(Speed 15, Stamina 12, 10 AP)_: Massive bite. 12d10 Slashing damage. +3 Accuracy underwater. 25% bonus to wounding. Inflicts Bleed equal to (damage dealt)/30. On hit, free Speed 0 Grab attempt with same Accuracy.

**Gnash** _(Speed 10, Stamina 8, 7 AP)_: While attacker in a Grapple, deal 6d10 Slashing damage. If the target has Bleed from Rend, increase it by (damage dealt)/30. Consecutive uses increase wounding damage by 10% per use.

#### 5. Terms of Contract

The Great White will turn on its summoner if:

- You have the highest Bleed status within 20 yards
- You have the most wounds within 20 yards
- You have 0 Vitality and the lowest HP within 20 yards

It will unsummon if it reaches Suffocation 15 or fails three Stamina checks against Suffocation since being summoned.

### Sand Shark

#### 1. Summoning

A terror of the earth. The Sand Shark is a finned titan with rock-hard skin, a serpentine body, frog-like legs, and long fins for arms. It burrows through the earth with terrifying speed, a shark that does not need water.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Speed:** 25
- **Seal Speed:** 25

#### 2. Behavior

The Sand Shark always knows where its summoner is. It depends on vibrations to locate prey above ground. It will rip through the earth to grab and pull targets under. It can also serve as an effective subterranean mount, carrying its summoner through sandy dunes.

#### 3. Attributes

**Subterranean:** No Suffocation or Immobility penalties underground. -10 Visibility Penalty against targets that do not move within 10 IC.

**Rocky Hide:** 80 DR.

**Huge:** Stamina and CE penalties reduced by 1. While grabbed, functions normally and has an 8-yard reach.

#### 4. Abilities

**Burrow** _(Speed +4, +1 AP)_: Ascend or descend into the earth. Can be linked with attacks.

**Bite** _(Speed 10, Stamina 8, 4 AP)_: 12d12 Piercing damage. Inflicts Bleed equal to (damage dealt)/30. On hit, free Speed 0 Grab attempt.

**Rake** _(Speed 12, Stamina 15, 12 AP)_: While attacker in Grapple, deal 12d4 Piercing damage (doubled for wounding). Doubles all existing Bleed tiers. Target is automatically removed from the Grapple.

**Doton Jutsu** _(Varies, 5 AP per Rank)_: The Sand Shark can perform Doton jutsu up to C-rank while underground. Cannot create Mud Clones, Earth Shaking Palm, or Crushing Earth Hand. Can use Tremorsense at 0 cost.

#### 5. Terms of Contract

Cannot be summoned underwater; doing so is a Breach of Contract (30 OOC days cooldown). Automatically unsummons at Fatigue 3.

### Dakkokki-ō — The Thresher King

#### 1. Summoning

The Avatar of the Shark Contract. Dakkokki-ō is a titan of the deep, a creature of legend that commands storms and whirlpools. On land, thunderclouds gather and rain falls. At sea, a maelstrom swirls around its summoning point. To summon the Thresher King is to make an offering, a sacrifice worthy of its attention.

- **Chakra:** 60
- **Speed:** 35
- **Seal Speed:** 45

#### 2. Behavior

Dakkokki-ō expects a worthy hunt. If summoned without sufficient prey, it will consume the summoner instead. Its rival is Ka Riu, the Salamander Avatar, and it will rise to any opportunity to fight the Salamander Avatar. It does not speak. It does not negotiate. It hunts.
