## Uniques {#uniques}

<Tip>
  Unique Points come from a pool of **5** at creation (**4** for major-clan characters). Creation-only entries are marked. Upgrade paths run 1 UP → 2 UP → 3 UP; you pay the difference to trade up, and you cannot downgrade back. Every card links straight to that unique's full rules entry.
</Tip>
