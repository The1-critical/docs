title: "Southern Province"
---

The Southern Province is experiencing a profound internal crisis under the shadow of Konohagakure. Once a political center of stability and cooperation for nearly a century, the village is now fracturing internally rather than facing external collapse. Despite projecting an image of functionality through continuous diplomatic engagements and infrastructural projects, Konohagakure is split into two overlapping entities: the Public Leaf, which strives for reform and legitimacy amid growing urban discontent, and an unofficial faction known as Root, which embraces controlled violence and preemptive measures for state preservation.

The Public Leaf represents institutional efforts to mend relations with outer territories by advocating for economic redistribution and de-escalation. However, its effectiveness is hampered by Root, which utilizes psychological warfare, blackmail, and covert operations to maintain continuity, often undermining public trust in governance. This power struggle has led to widespread paralysis within the village, where bureaucratic stall tactics and mutual sabotage are common, even as external threats prompt a fleeting unification against adversaries.

The Department of Internal Integrity is now a critical arm of authority, tasked with maintaining a façade of order while many citizens perceive it more as a psychological tool than a genuine force for stability. Concurrently, the Uchiha Clan, once restored to military prominence, faces its own fissures, with younger members contemplating separation amid fears of political stagnation and Root's manipulative tactics.

Discussion of the Fire Country's diminishing dominance is becoming increasingly common, generating a sense of unease among older and younger generations, albeit in contrasting emotional contexts. While the older citizens hold onto the triumphant legacy of the Fire Country, the youth approach its potential decline with a pragmatic detachment. Remnants of the Will of Fire endure, but they now manifest more as inertia than as hopeful idealism, igniting debates about whether the civilization's flame is extinguishing or merely beckoning perilous change as fear of extinction intensifies.

## **[Major] Aburame**

The Aburame Clan operates as a covert intelligence network within Konohagakure, deeply integrated into civilian and military life. Their kikaichū insects have evolved into sophisticated surveillance tools capable of infiltrating secure environments. Officially recognized as the Silent Net, this living surveillance system collects and metabolizes sensitive information, preventing it from being simply deleted. Additionally, upon reaching Chūnin rank, Aburame undergoes an "advanced alignment evaluation," which serves as a psychological reassessment related to loyalty to the Confederacy. Some are then reassigned to the Kuromaku, a function focused on manipulating information through active removal rather than record-keeping. This two-tier system remains largely overlooked, known only to a select few within the Confederacy.

--( **Rumor Has It** )--

- Leaf Nin: The Aburame are the ultimate infiltrators and intelligence gatherers. Their silence is a force to be reckoned with, and their insects see and hear everything the village needs to know without leaving a paper trail.
- Other Nin: They are unnerving. Their bugs will crawl into anything, seals, sensors, weapons, and eat the chakra right out of it. They are best avoided unless you want your own allies to turn on you after an encounter.

-( **The Kikaichū Swarm** )-

The keystone of all Aburame techniques, the Kikaichuu swarm provides a versatile mix of offense, defense, and utility, limited only by the size of the Aburame's swarm. Although most Aburame have a swarm within them, it takes dedication and effort to raise that swarm into a fighting entity.

-=[ **Kikaichū Hive** ]=-

The bond between host and swarm is not instantaneous. It is cultivated through years of chakra attunement, until the distinction between the Aburame's body and the colony inside it becomes meaningless. Your first rank grants you a Swarm with a maximum Swarm Size of 1 and one queen Kikai. Each rank thereafter increases your maximum Swarm Size by 1, and you gain one additional queen Kikai every 3 ranks (1, 4, 7, etc). This swarm is inside your body, and must be deployed to be used.

- Type: Ability
- Base Cost: 5
- Scale: 0
- Limit: 4 + (Total XP/400)

**Effects:** You gain access to the following actions:

**Deploy** _(Speed X)_<br />You release a swarm of Size X from your body, where X is no greater than your maximum Swarm Size. You may also deploy up to X/2 queen Kikai, to your max number owned. Your swarm forms a cloud in an X yard radius around you.

While deployed, any time you are hit by an area attack, you lose 1 Swarm Size worth of insects; if the attack wounds you, you lose 1 additional Swarm Size for each level of wound's severity (1 for Minor, 2 for Major, etc.) For every two Swarm Sizes lost, you also lose 1 queen Kikai, if any are deployed.

If you are inflicted with an Ignite effect, you lose 1 Swarm Size every 5 IC until the Ignite is removed.

If you have deployed a swarm of size 10 or greater, any loss of Swarm Size is doubled until the Swarm Size is reduced below 10. Loss of queen Kikai is not doubled.

A damaged swarm recovers at a rate of 1 Swarm Size every 3 OOC hours. If all of your queen Kikai die, you instead recover 1 Swarm Size every 6 OOC hours; upon recovering your maximum Swarm Size, you regain 1 queen Kikai every 3 OOC hours.

**Recall** _(Speed X/2 or +X/2, Interrupt)_<br />You may recall X Swarm Sizes worth of insects into your body, where X is no greater than your currently deployed Swarm Size. These insects cannot be used for actions or jutsu until you take a Deploy action to redeploy them.

You may also recall your swarm as part of a dodge defense. Used in this way, you add the speed of your dodge to the speed of the Recall action; if the new speed would invalidate your dodge, you cannot use this action. On a successful dodge, your swarm avoids all damage and is recalled; on a failed dodge, your swarm takes damage as usual, but is returned thereafter.

**Defense Formation** _(Speed 0)_<br />You may prepare your swarm to intercept an attack.

When you take this action, you may dedicate any number of your deployed Swarm Size to prepare to defend you. Swarm Sizes dedicated to defense cannot be used to attack, or perform any other clan jutsu; they may only be used for the Bug Repellant action. For the purposes of the Bug Repellant action, X is equal to your dedicated defense Swarm.

**Bug Repellant** _(Speed 5 + X, Interrupt)_<br />You may deflect an attack using your dedicated defense Swarm. Roll a ninjutsu parry with a bonus of +X. If you succeed, reduce the damage of the attack by up to (CHA \* X); if this reduces the damage to 0, nullify all other effects of the attack (wounds, conditions, etc). For every (CHA) damage reduced in this way, you lose 1 Swarm Size, to a minimum loss of 1.

Whether this action succeeds or fails, you may choose to remove any number of Swarm Sizes from your defense swarm; they can then be used to attack, or to perform any other clan jutsu, but can no longer be used for the Bug Repellant action.

**Internal Defenses** _(Special)_<br />While a deployed swarm can be an overwhelming fighting force, an internal swarm still offers several powerful benefits.

You gain a +1 bonus to Genjutsu defense for every 3 Swarm Sizes left undeployed within you. Additionally, you reduce the duration of all poisons, and their conditions, by 1/2 for every 8 Swarm Sizes left undeployed within you. (To 1/4th duration at 16 Swarm Size, 1/8th at 24 Swarm Size, etc.)

An Insect Clone's attacks are always considered to be Held Back, as detailed in the Abilities chapter; as such, a clone uses only half of your relevant damage bonus, and the severity of any wounds they inflict is reduced by one category.

## **[Major] Akimichi**

Despite their reputation as a stable and trusted Noble Clan, the Akimichi agricultural foundation has been incorporated into Confederacy logistical planning, creating a structural dependency where their physiological output and settlement stability are directly tied to military readiness. This produces a systemic condition in which any disruption to Akimichi food networks is treated not as a cultural or humanitarian issue, but as a potential strategic failure cascade across multiple allied formations.

Over time, this dependency has introduced an unresolved vulnerability: Akimichi populations cannot be allowed to fail, but they also cannot be allowed to fully disengage from Confederacy structures without destabilizing regional force projection. Internal contingency documents refer to this as a "sustainment paradox," where preservation and exploitation converge into the same operational requirement.

Unverified reports within Root-aligned channels suggest early-stage planning for enforced redistribution protocols in the event of agricultural destabilization, effectively converting Akimichi communities into controlled continuity reserves rather than independent civilian populations. The existence of such protocols remains unconfirmed, but their circulation alone has begun to generate quiet resistance patterns in isolated Akimichi settlements.<br />

--( **Rumor Has It** )--

- **Leaf Nin**: Akimichi are easy to like. Loud, friendly, always eating. You hear that southern drawl before you even see them. Like they never left the farm. The worst part is the voice stays the same. That same southern swagger just keeps going through it.
- **Other Nin**: It's initially humorous until the fighting begins. The Akimichi retain their calm demeanor, which is unsettling as they transform unexpectedly. Their relaxed tone persists even when they become formidable and leaves even the mightiest of opponents unprepared.

**--( Calorie Conversion )--**

Akimichi can convert excess calories to chakra. This can be used for just about anything that requires chakra, though it's most efficient when combined with their clan's techniques. Overuse of this ability results in them literally eating away at their own body; once all the fat stores have been used, muscles and organ tissue is converted next.

- Type: Ability
- Base Cost: 1
- Scale: 1
- Limit: RES / 10

Effects: Each time you take of this ability you gain five Calorie Points (CP). CP can be spent to lower the Chakra cost of any Akimichi clan jutsu at a 1:1 ratio, to a minimum of 0. You can spend them to reduce the cost of any other jutsu at a 4:1 ratio, but not below half that technique's normal cost. Alternately, you can spend 50 CP to not advanced a Fatigue category when failing a Chakra Exhaustion or Stamina roll.

You recover RES/5 calories per day, or half that amount if you can't eat at least two full meals. You can choose to stop at your maximum (5 per rank) or go up to double that value (with 4 ranks, you'd have a normal maximum of 20, and a true maximum of 40). Every ten CP you are over your normal max (round up) incurs a -1 penalty to CQC Accuracy, Weapon Accuracy, dodge rolls, Athletics rolls, and any other rolls which depend upon mobility. Any time you move (as a move action, or part of another action) the total distance you travel is reduced by a number of yards equal to that penalty.

Do note that as you use calories in battle, that penalty will go away.

**--( Akimichi Energy Bars )--**

Tasty, individually-wrapped energy bars which come in dozens of flavors, including peanut butter, chocolate chip, blackberry, celery, and milk. They're packed full of calories and simple-carbohydrates, and Akimichi are able to metabolize them almost instantly.

- Type: Item
- Cost: 20 Ryo each

Effects: Eating a bar is a Speed 4 action that restores 5+ (RES)/20 Calories. You may only eat up to (RES)/20 energy bars in an eight-hour period; any more, and you're too full to keep eating.

You may eat more than one energy bar at once, by adding 2 to the Speed of the action per energy bar beyond the first.

**–( Nara Clarifications )–**

Listed here are some mechanical clarifications for some niche circumstances you may encounter whilst using Kagemane No Jutsu.

- The victim uses all of your accuracy stats, seal speed reduction, dodge bonus, and movement speed for their mimicked actions, but uses their own damage bonus, stamina bonus, and chakra exhaustion bonus. If you have Nimble Hands, Single Handed Seals, or Exceptionally Fast, your victim gains the benefits of those uniques also when mimicking your actions.
- When using an attack of any sort if you and your opponent have different ranges for whatever reason on mimicked moves (large unique, being forced to use different ninjutsu, different strength for throwing weapons) and one of those ranges would not put the opponent within that range, the attack auto misses.
- If your opponent knows no ninjutsu or genjutsu they simply flail about vaguely in the same manner you are. This mechanically counts as declaring “no action”action.
- In the case of Genjutsu, even if your opponent doesn’t know any genjutsu and cannot return a genjutsu technique towards you, they are still aware a genjutsu technique has been used.
- If you use a CQC whilst the victim is holding something in their hands, they drop whatever is in their hands before mimicking the CQC.
- If you use any action with a seal speed whilst the victim is holding something in their hands, they drop the item in their hands before mimicking the seals you are performing
- The victim does not need to have taken the appropriate talent to be forced to use a Hyper Element technique, but they must have the correct elemental affinity.
- If you draw a weapon, your opponent draws whichever weapon they have which shares the most tags, roll a dice if there is a draw between multiple weapons.
- When forcing someone to declare no defense against an attack, if you succeed effects which have scaling damage based on a failed defense (Such as Crosswave, or Fuuma Shuriken) do not gain any bonus.
- You cannot force someone who has the “Full Pockets” unique to use their unique to reflexively purchase an item.

Even whilst in Kagemane your victim can still take the following actions:

- Genjutsu being used by Sharingan.
- Hozuki hydrate, or reform
- The “Meditate” action.
- The “Close Gates” action.
- The “Search” action.
- The “Rest” action.
