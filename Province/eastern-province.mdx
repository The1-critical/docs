title: "Eastern Province"
---

The Eastern Province is notably the sole geopolitical success in the post-war landscape, benefiting from regional instability, primarily facilitated by Kirigakure. Kirigakure has evolved into a rich and powerful entity, adept in information and financial warfare, achieving stability through the Mastermind philosophy. The Mizukage's reforms have ensured equal standing among clans, redirecting internal disputes towards external economic competition, particularly through monopoly matches with neighboring regions, especially the Land of Lightning.

Kumogakure plays a critical role due to its Transcontinental Railway and Canal project, developed in collaboration with Sunagakure and financed by the Iron Country, aimed at enhancing southern coast shipping. Kirigakure has capitalized on this relationship by investing heavily in Kumogakure and taxing its shipping operations. However, the ongoing Sand Civil War has disrupted concrete supply lines, threatening the canal's construction and compelling Kumogakure to contemplate military intervention to secure essential materials and labor.

Internally, Kumogakure is split between the conservative Silver Branch, advocating for isolation, and the militaristic Golden Branch, which aims to rearm and challenge Kirigakure's dominance. This factionalism perpetuates relentless weapons production. Conversely, Kirigakure seizes the crisis as a chance to solidify control, utilizing its efficient agencies, including the International Engagement Agency (IEA) and intelligence sectors like D.I.R.E and O.S.S., to manage public perception and maintain secrecy.

Amidst these complexities, the Sea Emperor's Armada poses an external threat that could disrupt Kirigakure's trade monopolies. Kirigakure continues to manipulate narratives to preserve its facade of stability, as any internal strife or failure could jeopardize its financial integrity and influence over Kumogakure, especially with the Golden Branch's agenda challenging its trade hegemony.

## [Major] Hōzuki

Located on the outskirts of Kirigakure, the Hōzuki have gradually grown in power alongside the village's sophisticated militarization. Their primary role is as the village's Guardians and 'Peacekeepers', a title that grants them authority to enforce the will of the Mizukage with terrifying finality. Their famous ability to liquify themselves entirely makes them uniquely suited for ambushes and, crucially, silent retreat and intelligence gathering. Should an operation turn against them, they simply melt away, only to return later with overwhelming force. The Hōzuki are key members of the International Engagement Agency (IEA), serving as diplomats and ambassadors. Their smooth, public-facing demeanor is a necessary mask, hiding the chilling truth that no one truly understands the limits of their ability. Within Kirigakure, every resident knows that dissent is a terrifying prospect because every puddle could potentially contain a Hōzuki listening in. Their uncatchable nature makes them the ultimate asset for maintaining Kiri's illusion of stability and policing the financial flows managed by the IEA<br />

## [Major] Kaguya

The Kaguya were originally barbaric, blood-thirsty warriors who were nearly wiped out following a failed coup against Kirigakure. Their resurgence was "negotiated" back into the village, turning them into the most feared, yet least trusted, major clan. Their terrifying Shikotsumyaku is touted as the ultimate Taijutsu ability, reflecting their fierce bloodlust and famous refusal to surrender, preferring a bloody death to a peaceful surrender. The core of their philosophy is: He who kills by the sword must die by one; as such, every shinobi they encounter courts death. Mindful of their past betrayal, the Kaguya operate under strict, unspoken limitations. All Kaguya are branded at a young age with two dots above their eyebrows, a marking that commands respect and fear within the village, symbolizing their subjugated loyalty. They are monitored closely and intentionally kept out of intelligence roles. Their raw, noble ferocity is now a key part of the Mastermind machine. They form the lethal, external muscle of the Korvidae, partnered with the Chikami Clan and Sword Clans. These three clans have developed a rather uncanny kinship, as many households intermarry and form military outposts around trade posts to combat pirates.

## [Major] Swordsman Corps

The Swordsman Corps is no longer just an elite operations unit; it is an established institution and the physical expression of Kirigakure's martial discipline. Militaristic and merciless, only students who display an abnormal talent or fascination with swordsmanship are selected for training. Their defining philosophy is the rejection of reliance on inherited power: they adamantly reject the concept that natural talents or bloodlines can match their rigorous training and specific focus on kenjutsu. To graduate from their initial school, children must perform near-death battles before they even enter the standard academy. This rigorous discipline makes them the perfect tool for maintaining the facade of authority and protecting Kiri's financial interests. A disproportionate number of the Mizukage's honor guard are drawn from the Corps, and they form the structural foundation of the Office of Strategic Surveillance (O.S.S.), ensuring that intelligence gathered by the Niou (D.I.R.E.) is backed by swift, decisive force.

In response to the external crisis, the Corps has taken on a new, high-risk mission: Sword Corps children now typically go Kaiju hunting. This is a direct consequence of the Sea Emperor's Armada stirring up the deep ocean, forcing the Corps to utilize their elite skills to protect Kiri's vital maritime assets and trade posts. They also provide the third, tactical element of the lethal Korvidae strike team.

Well, at some point in the past some member of the Swordsman Corps decided to present themselves as a samurai, and adopted the term to refer to their own paired weapons. -=[ **Clan Swords** ]=-

Upon becoming a Chuunin or reaching 2500 XP, a Mist swordsman will receive their own, personalized blade. Most of them will have kept, used, and taken care of a single sword, usually a katana, from their days in the academy–in these cases, they usually opt to have that weapon reforged.

Though they don't partake in the forging process, the ninja will be there the entire time, and it's followed by a ritual consecration, and a four-day period of solitary meditation in which the shinobi is alone with only their blade, fasting and subsisting off only just enough water to stave off dehydration. The relationship between sword and swordsman is… interesting. Most swordsmen take care of their blade better than their teammates, and it's not even considered unusual for them to name and talk to it (if the sword starts talking back, though, that just means they're crazy).

There's more to this than lasting effects of the hallucinations from four days of fasting and partial dehydration. Members of the swordsman corps always know where their sword is, in terms of distance and direction. It could be on the other side of the world, and given enough time, they'd be able to track it down.

Many claim their swords are 'loyal' to them (others just say their weapons are unique and they're the only ones who know how to use them properly); and, indeed, a borrowed or stolen sword functions as an ordinary katana, nothing more, in the hands of anyone but its rightful owner. Similarly, Weapon Focus: Katana works on all Clan Swords, even if the exact nature of your weapon turn it into.. something else.

In the event one of these swords is hopelessly lost (say, tossed to the bottom of the ocean, or thrown into a volcano) a new one can be forged for the swordsman, though this is not done lightly; if given the chance, most would literally fight through hell to get their sword back. It takes four OOC days to obtain a replacement sword, after which the original will no longer recognize its former owner. A member of the swordsman corps who accepts a replacement blade when it's in any way possible to reclaim their old one is viewed with severe disdain by their fellow swordsmen. (Using a regular katana in the meanwhile, however, is perfectly acceptable).

Your sword begins with 20 points which you can spend on it at your discretion. The first thing for you to do is choose its Structure features; this is its basic shape and so on. There are 5 primary characteristics to select here, and each one has a point cost listed next to it; for example, "(2) Small" means it costs 2 of your 20 points to make your weapon have the 'Small' tag added to it.

After selecting all your Structure options, you move on to Features. Although prices are in the same format, the layout is different.

Features relate to the true nature of your weapon, as measured by its favorance and balance between Yin and Yang Chakra, according to its chosen construction of its Blade, Hilt, and Tassel, three tiers that you can choose to spend points on. Blade features can be taken at will, while Hilt features require at least 5 of your sword customization points in the associated group; to take a Tassel feature, you must have spent 10 points in that group, and may only take one Tassel feature for your sword in total, even from the same group.

For example, if you spent 7 points on Blade features and 3 points on Hilt features in Yin, you could take either of the Tassel Yin features. You would still be restricted to Blade features for Balance and Yang features, unless you also spent enough points in those trees.

You must spend at least 1 point in one of the three primary trees of Features, and any unused points can not be spent after its construction, and are simply lost.

## **[Minor] Niou**

The Niou possess one of the shinobi world's most coveted talents: true, specialized invisibility. Despite this, they have intentionally avoided fame by downplaying and obfuscating their abilities. They encourage the rumor that their technique is merely "Mist ninja trickery," relying on chakra-enhanced fog and ninjutsu. Due to their extreme rarity, they are easily passed off as just a large extended family within Kirigakure, not a clan. They often adopt orphans who keep their original surnames, making it harder for foreign powers to connect the dots. This disciplined secrecy and their unwavering, quiet loyalty make them the indispensable core of Kiri's information network. Niou led the Directorate of Intelligence, Recon, and Espionage (D.I.R.E.). Their ability to listen, observe, and transmit data without ever being seen is the foundation for Kiri's wealth and stability. They gather so many secrets that their only release is to privately brag of their exploits to one another, avoiding exposure to the outside world. They are the unseen hand that guarantees the Mastermind's complete knowledge.

--( **Rumor Has It** )--

- **Mist Nin:** If Kiri knows a secret, it's because Niou heard it whispered. They run D.I.R.E., but you'll never see their Director. They know you're coming before you even leave home
- **Other Nin:** There are no 'invisible' ninja, only tricksters and illusionists. The Niou are just highly trained in Mist's obfuscation arts. However, if you see the two small black dots on a Hunter-Nin's equipment, that's a Niou tracker.

–( **Genshin no Jutsu - Invisibility Technique** )–<br />The pride of the clan, and their most closely guarded secret. This technique wraps the user in a layer of chakra, and projects their surroundings across their skin. A slight distortion is the only indication that anything is out of the ordinary, the technique even providing the illusion of distance.

- **Type:** Ninjutsu, Rank D
- **Chakra:** 10
- **Upkeep:** 4
- **Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:**

You become invisible. While invisible, you may take the Hide action without needing to fulfill its normal requirements, with a +5 bonus; while hidden, you are always considered to be one Stealth category higher than you actually are. When making a Stealth roll, or an Espionage roll to be unnoticed (such as pilfering something, or picking a pocket) you roll 2d20.takeHighest(1) instead of 1d20. Those Espionage rolls also receive a +5 bonus.

Your Dodge rolls receive a similar benefit: you roll 2d20.takeHighest(1) rather than 1d20. This doesn't apply against area-of-effect techniques.

You lose all of the above benefits against those that can see Chakra (i.e., against activated Doujutsu, which is normally limited to Hyuuga and Uchiha), and are instead considered to be one Stealth category lower than you actually are against them while you maintain this technique.

=[ **Maboroshi no Jutsu - Phantom Technique** ]=-<br />You project your chakra over another person, granting them invisibility as well. This requires you to make physical contact with them when it's first applied, and they must remain within (CHA/5) yards for its effects to persist.

- **Type:** Ability
- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** CHA/40

**Effects:**

You can conceal one additional person, or person-sized object, with Genshin no Jutsu per Rank. This requires Genshin to be active on yourself, and each additional person or object concealed this way increases its Upkeep by 2. People and objects concealed this way may use your Stealth roll in place of their own; if you're hiding a group, you make only a single Stealth roll for the entire group.

–( **Meimei Arashi - Invisible Tempest** )–<br />Wrapping a weapon or held object in your chakra-induced veil of invisibility is easy; unfortunately, that does not extend to thrown weapons, as their invisibility comes to an end as soon as they leave your grasp. This technique exists specifically to patch that hole in the Niou's invisibility: it allows you to veil one or more thrown weapons in your chakra, keeping them unseen until they've struck their target.

- **Type:** Ninjutsu, Rank C
- **Chakra:** Special
- **Requires:** Genshin no Jutsu active

**Effects:**

This supplements a throwing or multi-throw attack by making it an automatic surprise attack. Its Chakra cost is equal to half of the technique's Stamina cost. If used while hidden, this reduces your Stealth TN by only 2 each time it's used.

## [Minor] Yotsuki

The Yotsuki Clan of Kumogakure embodies the fierce spirit of the Land of Lightning. Renowned for their Energy Flow, they harness chakra from their surroundings or their enemies' attacks, converting it into a dynamic aura that enhances their strength, speed, and durability. While the most common manifestation of this cloak is lightning-based, skilled Yotsuki can adapt it to other elements, turning the enemy's power against them. Whether their unique ability is a secret technique or a Kekkei Genkai is a closely guarded secret, but its effectiveness on the battlefield has made the Yotsuki a cornerstone of Kumogakure's military might. The clan members themselves are as bold and electrifying as their abilities, often taking on boisterous, impulsive personalities. True to their homeland, they carry catchy, rhyming names with the letter A.

--( **Rumor Has It** )--

- **Cloud Nin:** The Yotsuki are essential for dealing with opponents who rely too much on their elemental ninjutsu as they easily adapt to whatever situation they find themselves in.
- **Other Nin:** They are merely Taijutsu savages who use strange techniques to cheat and lack true Ninjutsu depth.

**-–( Kiryu - Energy Flow )–-**<br />The cornerstone of the Yotsuki Clan's power, this cloak absorbs elemental energy from attacks or the environment, granting resilience and adapting its properties to reflect the absorbed element. The user glows faintly with the hue of the absorbed chakra, striking an imposing figure in combat. This technique is the foundation for all subsequent Elemental Cloak abilities.

- **Type:** Ninjutsu, Rank D
- **Speed:** 10
- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 5
- **Special:** If you have Affinity with all active Elements, -3 Upkeep
- **Tags:** Preparation, Nintaijutsu

**Effects:**

Select one element you have Aptitude for when you activate this technique. Your body becomes attuned to that element while you maintain it, causing an aura of that element to wreathe their body, protecting and empowering them. You become immune to all non-environmental Status Effects from that element (though any existing status effects from that element before Kiryu's activation continue to apply normally), reduce damage taken from that element by 25%, and cause your CQC attacks to be considered the element chosen for the purposes of elemental interactions, as well as a bonus based on your chosen element:

- **Doton:** You gain (8 + NDB) \* (1 + X) Damage Reduction. If you hit someone who is Prone with a damaging, non-grapple CQC attack, you gain the Damage Reduction from this feature as added damage to your attack.
- **Fuuton:** Whenever you succeed on an unarmed parry, you deal your NDB \* (Parry's final Speed + X) damage back to the attacker automatically, and can elect to knock them away from, or pull them toward, you (NDB + X)/2 yards.
- **Katon:** CQC attacks apply Burns 3, while all Combo Counter and Combo Bonus lines gain "+0.2 \* (1 + X) CQC DB", with a maximum bonus of your CQC DB.
- **Raiton:** CQC Taijutsu reduce their speeds by (1 + X), which does not reduce their damage, and bypass half of an opponent's DR.
- **Suiton:** After successfully defending against an attack, you may perform a Basic Unarmed Attack as a Speed 0 interrupt at its normal cost; it is defended against at its normal speed, but does not advance your IC, though it may only be performed once every (14 - X \* 2) IC. Every 10 gallons of Water on the battlefield causes you to be able to make this Interrupt attack at +1 Range, to a maximum of (NDB + X) yards, though they are still considered melee attacks, not ranged.

X begins at 0 for this technique.

**-=[ Elemental Ascendance ]=-**<br />With sufficient training, a Yotsuki can further concentrate their element, and even combine elements as they please. While some choose to fully commit themselves to a singular element, others utilize the full abundance of nature in order to create multi-colored auras that allow for truly impossible types of taijutsu.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** CHA/25, Max 4

**Effects:**

For every Rank in this Ability, you can choose when you activate Kiryu, or as a separate Speed 6, CE 12 action, to do one of the following: Increase X by 1, or activate another Element. In the latter case of adding additional elements, you do not gain additional status effect immunities (which are based solely on your 'main' element), however you do take less damage from all elements chosen, and your attacks are considered to be simultaneously all elements utilized for the purposes of defending against them. You can utilize the separate action to likewise change which element is considered your main element for status effect immunities.

You must meet all of Kiryu's normal rules for which elements you take (meaning you must have that element's Aptitude), and activating more than one element causes Kiryu's Upkeep to increase by 1 per additional element beyond the first, alongside potentially increasing it further if you do not have Affinity for all elements activated to qualify for its Special feature (meaning normally two elements active would be Upkeep 5 + 1 = 6, even if one of them is your affinity, as you are utilizing one which is not).
