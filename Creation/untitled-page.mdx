---
title: "Setting Lore"
---

The setting takes place eighty years after the great shinobi war. Peace was established at the end of that war, and on paper peace has continued ever since. The known world is unified under a single governing body, the Shinobi Confederacy. It was originally formed by the unifying of the five great Shinobi Villages from the prewar era, and was supposed to create a unity that all people could prosper under.

However, there is still resource scarcity, border disagreements, economic inequality, centuries old grudges, and the wills of men who see a world they think they can improve, if only they were allowed more power. The five great villages are now unified, but the Shinobi world is more fragmented that it's ever been. Villagers have splintered into factions that have splintered further into subfactions. Webs of alliances, trade agreements, special permissions, and suspected less-than-legal operations have been mapped, compromised, propagandized, and mapped again in seven different ways, all of which are true, and all of which are contradictory. 

The world has industrialized in the last eighty years, but industrialized in the context of a hundred competing powers all simultaneously trying to hide any all ties to any and all assets. Institutions are announced, created, attributed, shifted, merged, split, transferred, and renamed until no true institutions exist anymore, only the vague sentiment of ever watching powers. There is no open war, but "unaffiliated agents", "local insurgents", and "law enforcement" create warzones of city streets in a way that none of the great shinobi wars ever managed to. .

Natsugakure, an old mining colony who mines have long since run dry, has been slowly dying in a forgotten corner of this new hostile world, and was on schedule to disappear completely before, all of a sudden, attention started to drift towards it. The usual slurry of "unaffiliated agents" submitting strange public information requests in recognizable patterns designed to obfuscate what is actually being asked for. Decades old businesses and logistics offices suddenly filing paperwork despite having no employees. And then, alarming of all, the presence of actual village genin, wearing actual village associated headbands, turning up in Natsugakure. No one knows why this forgotten mining village suddenly has the eyes of every major power in the Great Peace's fragmented world suddenly staring directly at Natsugakure, but everyone knows something is happening. And whatever it is, it might just give the edge needed for whichever of the powers that be that discovers it to break free from the pack and start to establish some kind of foothold in whatever new world emerges from the current age of conflict.
