title: "Character Creation"
---

**Come up with an Idea!**

The strongest characters tend to start from a concept rather than a stat spread. It doesn't have to be complex, a fighting style, a phrase that captures how they move through the world, a single relationship that defines them. Something to build around makes every other decision easier. If nothing comes to mind, that's fine. Pick a Province and let the rest follow.

#### **Choose your Province and Clan!**

Your Province is your origin, your political allegiance, and the culture that shaped your early training. Each village has its own traditions, methods, and reputation, worth reading about before committing, though plenty of ninja are outliers within their own home which we call Travelers.

Most Provinces are home to several clans. Clans are defined by blood or by covenant, and the techniques they carry are closed to outsiders, you cannot join a clan, only be born into one. Exceptions are rare and require GM approval.

Choosing a clan is not required. Clanless ninja carry their own advantages: a **10% XP Boon** and **XP/20 Skill Points** in place of the standard allocation.

#### **Unique Points**

Unique Points represent rare personal qualities, trained specializations, and things about your character that set them apart from the broader shinobi population. New characters begin with **5 Unique Points**. Major clans reduce this to 4 due to the power of their inherited techniques.

Some Uniques are marked **Creation Only** and may not be taken after character creation.

Some Uniques carry an **Upgrade From/Into** notation. This means you can pay the difference in cost between two related Uniques to trade one for the other, you spend the gap, lose the old benefits, and gain the new ones. You cannot downgrade to reclaim points.

No Unique may be taken more than once.

#### **Attributes**

The five primary attributes are Strength, Resilience, Chakra, Dexterity, and Agility. They define your character's raw physical and chakra-based capabilities and feed into nearly every derived statistic.

New characters start with **20 in all five attributes** and have **25 additional points** to distribute freely, with no more than 10 assigned to any single attribute at creation.

**The Character Sheet**

All characters use a standardized Google Sheets document. To begin filling yours out, go to File → Make a Copy, rename it to your character's name, and start working through the fields. A completed sheet must be reviewed by a GM before you can begin play — this catches errors before they become problems.

A character background is optional but appreciated. It doesn't need to be long. A paragraph giving the GMs something to work with goes a long way.

#### **Skill Point**

New characters begin with **10 Skill Points** plus **XP/25** additional points as their experience accumulates. Clanless ninja receive **XP/20** instead. Some Uniques modify these numbers further.

A single Skill Point raises one skill by one rank. Concentrating your early points into two or three skills is strongly recommended over spreading them thin.<br />

#### **Starting XP**

Once attributes, Uniques, and skills are settled, spend your starting XP on Jutsu and Abilities. Kawarimi, Henge, and Bunshin are worth considering as a baseline — most ninja learn all three before graduation, and they remain useful throughout a career. A rank in Elemental Aptitude and some investment in Money: Requisition are also common early choices, the latter being required to carry most equipment.<br />

**Submit Sheet to GM/ST**

When you feel your sheet is complete, have a Gamemaster (GM) or Storyteller (ST) look over it to make sure everything is in order. This also allows the sheet to be added to their records for bookkeeping and planning purposes.

It is optional but recommended to also include a character background for your character. It doesn’t have to be particularly long or complex, just something that gives the GMs and STs some personal character hooks they can work with regarding your character in the future. On the official sheet, this can be placed in the Character Bio tab.

#### **One Last Note**

Unique Points, Experience Points, and Skill Points cannot be spent during any active event or combat. They may only be allocated before or after.
