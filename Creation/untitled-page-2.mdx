---
title: "Attributes"
---

Attributes are the numerical foundation of your character, how strong they are, how fast, how chakra-capable, how precise. Baseline unaugmented human statistics sit around 20. Ninja exceed this quickly, the product of chakra flowing through and reinforcing the body in ways that have no mundane equivalent.

There are five primary attributes. Each feeds into derived statistics and, critically, into the accuracy calculations that determine how reliably your character lands attacks. **How you build your attributes is how you build your fighting style.**

#### **Strength (STR)**

Raw physical force, how hard you can hit, how much you can lift, how much Raw physical force, how hard you can hit, how much you can lift, how much punishment you can absorb through sheer muscle and frame.

- Your CQC Damage Bonus is STR/15
- Your Vitality is (RES × 8) + (STR × 4)
- CQC Accuracy (all Unarmed, Combo, and Grapple Taijutsu) is 10 + STR/10
- CQC Parry (deflecting CQC with your body or unarmed blocks) is 1d20 + STR/10

STR is the brawler’s stat. A character who invests here hits harder in close quarters _and_ connects more reliably _and_ deflects physical attacks more effectively, the same investment covers offense and defense simultaneously when fighting at melee range.<br />

#### **Resilience (RES)**

Your body's capacity to absorb damage, resist conditions, and keep functioning under strain. Not how hard you hit, but how much you can take.

- Your Vitality is **(RES × 8) + (STR × 4)**
- Your HP is **(RES × 3) + CHA**
- You add **RES/10** to all Stamina rolls

#### **Chakra (CHA)**

The depth and quality of your chakra reserves, and your instinctive command over them. This stat governs not just the raw power of your jutsu but how precisely your chakra-sculpted techniques find their mark.

- Your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus is **CHA/15**
- Your HP is **(RES × 3) + CHA**
- You add **CHA/10** to all Chakra Exhaustion rolls
- **Ninjutsu Accuracy** (all categories of ninjutsu) is **10 + CHA/10**
- **Chakra Parry** (deflecting ninjutsu and other chakra-based techniques) is **1d20 + CHA/10**

CHA is the ninjutsu specialist's stat. The same investment that makes your jutsu devastating also makes them accurate and makes you resilient against incoming chakra techniques. A CHA-primary character commands the field at range.

#### **Dexterity (DEX)**

Precision, fine motor control, and the capacity to respond to a rapidly changing environment. Where STR describes raw force, DEX describes the technical mastery that lets a weapon find exactly where it needs to go.

- Your Weapon Damage Bonus is DEX/15
- Your base Weapon Accuracy (all Weapon Taijutsu and Ranged Taijutsu) is 10 + DEX/10
- Weapon Parry (deflecting Weapon Attacks with your weapon) is 1d20 + DEX/10
- The Seal Speed of all jutsu you perform is reduced by DEX/10
- Action points are based off DEX, DEX/10

DEX is the weapon specialist’s stat. A character who invests here lands weapon and thrown attacks with high reliability and forms seals faster than their peers.<br />

#### **Agility (AGI)**

Reaction speed, spatial awareness, and the ability to be somewhere other than where an attack lands.

- You add **AGI/10** to all Dodge rolls
- You add **AGI/10** to all Initiative rolls

#### **Derived Statistics**<br />

**Vitality**

The buffer between a healthy ninja and a seriously hurt one. Damage depletes current Vitality first. Maximum Vitality is unaffected by damage.

Vitality = (RES × 8) + (STR × 4)

**Hit Points**

When Vitality runs out, remaining damage carries into HP. At 0 HP you are incapacitated and cannot act. Automatic death occurs at negative 100% of your maximum HP.

HP = (RES × 3) + CHA

**CQC Damage Bonus (CQC DB)**

Increases damage dealt with unarmed physical attacks. Calculated to one decimal place, rounded down — STR 25 gives CQC DB 1.6, not 1.7.

CQC DB = STR/15

**Weapon Damage Bonus (WDB)**

Increases damage dealt with weapon based attacks rather than unarmed physical attacks.

WDB = DEX/15

**Physical Damage Bonus (PDB)**

If something refers to your Physical Damage Bonus, it means that it increases both your CQC Damage Bonus and your Weapon Damage Bonus.

The Two-Handed bonus (when applicable) increases only the stat-derived component of your damage bonus by 33%, not flat additions from abilities or jutsu.

**Ninjutsu Damage Bonus (NDB)**

The ninjutsu equivalent of CQC DB. Applies to all chakra-based attacks rather than physical ones.

NDB = CHA/15

**Willpower (WP)**

The measure of your ability to push past your limits — to spend a point at a critical moment and tip the scales.<br />WP = 1 + (STR + RES + CHA + DEX + AGI) / 250
