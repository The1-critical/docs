---
title: "Abilities  "
---

Abilities represent the fruits of your character's training and the ways in which they've developed as a shinobi. What abilities you take will play a large role in shaping how you perform in combat and other situations.

Abilities are more specific than skills; they differ from jutsu in that while a jutsu is a specific technique, an ability is a way of doing something or a permanent increase in your capabilities. Most are passive improvements, granting new capabilities or making you better at something you could already do. You may choose not to use any given ability at any time, effectively holding back.

## Purchasing Abilities

Abilities are acquired by spending XP. Some list only a Base Cost — you spend that much XP and the ability is yours. Others list both a Base Cost and a Scale, meaning they can be purchased more than once. The first rank costs the Base Cost; each subsequent rank costs the previous rank's cost plus the Scale.

_Example: Enhanced Hearing has Base Cost 4, Scale 3. Rank 1 costs 4 XP. Rank 2 costs 7 XP. Rank 3 costs 10 XP. All three ranks together cost 21 XP._

Multi-rank abilities include a Limit entry controlling how many ranks you can have. A limit such as "Chakra Control /5" means you divide your Chakra Control ranks by 5 (rounded down) to find your maximum. A limit with a max suffix caps it regardless. Abilities with a Requires entry cannot be purchased at all unless you meet the listed condition.

## Chakra

### 1. Nature Control

#### -=[ Elemental Affinity ]=-

Every ninja has a natural affinity for one of the five primary elements, though some don't discover it until later. For each rank, you gain a +1 bonus to Chakra Exhaustion and Chakra Control rolls involving that element. The Chakra Control requirement to learn jutsu of that element is treated as 1 point lower per rank. Some clans begin with a specific Elemental Affinity. This grants one rank for free but locks your choice. However, you apply that Elemental Affinity to both elements that comprise your clan's advanced element.

- **Base Cost:** 0
- **Scale:** +1
- **Limit:** CHA /20, max 5
- **Special:** You may only ever take this ability for a single element.

#### -=[ Elemental Aptitude ]=-

Chakra exists in five elemental natures. Converting your own chakra into an element, the prerequisite for using that element's ninjutsu — is what this ability represents. Each rank grants access to one of the five elements. Certain clans begin with one or more ranks for free, but still pay the normal cost when buying beyond what they started with.

_Example: A Touu begins with Water and Wind at no cost. Their third element costs (55 + 35) 90 XP._

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** +25
- **Limit:** 3

### 2. Shape Control

#### -=[ Homing ]=-

With precise chakra control, you can curve and guide your ninjutsu to find their mark more reliably. You may give any damage-dealing ninjutsu attack a bonus of up to +1 Accuracy per rank by increasing its Chakra and/or Stamina cost by 3 for each +1 added. This does not affect the AP-driven cost cap, which remains based on the technique's printed base cost. Ninjutsu with a Range of Touch, those with an Area, and interrupt ninjutsu that become attacks (such as Sickle Wind or Rising Phoenix Blast) cannot benefit from Homing.

- **Base Cost:** 8
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** Chakra Control ranks /5, max 6

#### -=[ Improved Area ]=-

Your ninjutsu techniques cover more ground, the additional area controlled by the density and reach of your chakra. This increases the area of effect of any damage-dealing ninjutsu by up to your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus. You can apply some, all, or none of this bonus to any given technique. The bonus cannot exceed the technique's base area.

- **Base Cost:** 20

#### -=[ Improved Range ]=-

Your chakra holds its coherence further from your body than most — whether through speed, compression, or simple endurance of the projection. This increases the range of any ninjutsu (other than those with a Range of Touch or Melee) by up to double your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus in yards. The bonus cannot exceed the technique's base range.

- **Base Cost:** 20

#### -=[ Potent Chakra ]=-

You compress your chakra more densely before release, preserving energy that would otherwise dissipate. Each rank increases your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus by 0.2.

- **Base Cost:** 1
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** CHA /10
- **Requires:** 80 CHA

### 3. Chakra Control

#### -=[ Refined Chakra Control ]=-

Your precise handling of chakra means you waste less of it, spending it more efficiently and getting more out of each technique. Each rank gives you a +1 bonus to Chakra Exhaustion rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Chakra Control /5, max 5

#### -=[ Wall Walking ]=-

A standard exercise taught early in most villages: focusing a fixed amount of chakra at the soles of your feet allows you to adhere to any surface, vertical or inverted. With this ability you can scale walls, run across ceilings, and move along any surface at your normal movement speed.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Requires:** 8 Chakra Control

#### -=[ Water Walking ]=-

Harder than wall walking by a significant margin — the surface is constantly moving beneath you, requiring the strength and distribution of your chakra to change in real time. Once internalized, it becomes as natural as keeping balance on a rocking surface. You can stand and move on water at normal speed.

- **Base Cost:** 15
- **Requires:** 15 Chakra Control, Wall Walking

### 4. Genjutsu

#### -=[ Genjutsu Awareness ]=-

You notice when the details of your environment don't quite line up — a resonance that sounds off, a door with no handle, light falling from the wrong angle. Each rank gives you a +1 bonus to defensive Genjutsu rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** Lower of Enhanced Hearing or Enhanced Sight ranks

#### -=[ Genjutsu Competence ]=-

Genjutsu is notoriously difficult to pick up. Even basic proficiency requires significant practice — which this ability represents. Each rank gives you a +1 bonus to offensive Genjutsu rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 0
- **Limit:** (XP + 200) /400

#### -=[ Genjutsu Mastery ]=-

You have moved beyond competence into genuine command of the craft. Each rank gives a +1 bonus to all Genjutsu rolls. You can have one rank for each rank of Genjutsu you know at least one technique from — knowing an E-rank, a D-rank, and a B-rank would allow three ranks here.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** Special (see above)

#### -=[ Genjutsu Training ]=-

This ability grants you access to entry-level Genjutsu techniques. Without it, Genjutsu of any kind is entirely unavailable to you. It's a start.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** Research 4

#### -=[ Illusion Multi-Targeting ]=-

You can extend a single-target Genjutsu to cover multiple people simultaneously. Every 1 point of Willpower spent allows your Genjutsu to target two additional people using the same roll and with no extra cost.

- **Base Cost:** 25
- **Requires:** Know at least one C-rank Genjutsu

#### -=[ Mettle ]=-

Your training covers not just physical endurance but mental resistance — making you harder to mislead and more capable of deploying that same subtlety against others. Each rank gives a +1 bonus to all Genjutsu rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 8
- **Scale:** 3
- **Limit:** Resistance /5

## Equipment

### 1. Ninja Tools

#### -=[ Money: Requisition ]=-

As a shinobi, your village supplies most of your equipment. This ability represents your authorized load-out — a limit more than a resource you spend. You can replace used items, exchange ones you no longer need, and restock at will as long as you're not in the field. Each rank allows you to carry 50 Ryo worth of equipment from the Equipment chapter at any one time.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 0
- **Limit:** None

#### -=[ Money: Savings ]=-

A personal reserve of funds set aside over time. Each rank gives you 100 Ryo to spend as you see fit. This money cannot be refunded or replenished — spent or broken items are simply gone. Items with the Consumable tag cannot be purchased with Savings. XP spent on Savings does not count against the XP Cap (but is still spent normally), allowing a small buffer for those approaching their limit.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 0
- **Limit:** 5% of your total XP

#### -=[ Bingo Book ]=-

A regularly updated pocket reference published by each village, cataloguing missing-nin and notable enemy shinobi: their bounties, reputations, and any capabilities the village is willing to share. Owning this ability means you keep a current copy. With it, you can make Research rolls on anyone with enough Infamy to earn a listing.

- **Base Cost:** 5

#### -=[ Icha Icha Novels ]=-

Jiraiya's legendary series. Comedic, adult, and surprisingly instructive. Countless shinobi have honed their Stealth and Espionage abilities by sneaking these through academy inspections and reading them under desk. Each rank means you've read one novel. Every novel read gives +1 to Espionage rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** 4

#### -=[ Tag Concealment ]=-

You know how to plant an explosive tag so that a casual observer won't notice it. Requires something to hide it under — loose soil, leaf cover, or similar. Hiding a tag is a Speed 10 action; anyone watching you will see what you're doing, so this requires stealth. When placed, make an Espionage roll. Anyone entering the tag's range may make an Awareness roll against your result (Enhanced Hearing does not add to this roll). A sizzling tag gives away its presence once it begins counting down.

- **Base Cost:** 10

### 2. Weapons, General

#### -=[ Finesse ]=-

Your weapon lands with perfect precision, whether from years of training or natural grace. Each rank increases your Weapon Damage Bonus by 0.2.

- Base Cost: 1
- Scale: 1
- Limit: DEX /10
- Requires: 80 DEX 

#### -=[ Weapon Focus: (Weapon) ]=-

You've trained specifically with a single weapon type, chosen from the Equipment chapter. Each rank gives +1 Accuracy and +1 to your damage bonus with that weapon on attacks. Any attack using Weapon Focus has its Stamina cost increased by 3 per rank applied; you may choose how many ranks to apply on any given attack. You may take this ability for multiple weapons, each treated as a separate ability with its own cost and scale. Weapon Focus does not apply to defensive rolls or actions with no Stamina cost.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** (DEX − 30) /30, max 3

#### -=[ Blunt: Stun ]=-

Your blunt weapon attacks stagger, disorient, and generally leave your opponents in worse shape than they'd like. Each rank adds Stun 1 to your blunt weapon attacks, or increases existing Stun by 1. Applies only to damaging attacks that connect.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** STR /20, max 5

#### -=[ Piercing: Penetrate ]=-

Your piercing weapons bypass armor entirely.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** 40 STR

#### -=[ Slashing: Perfect Cut ]=-

Where most attacks deal 20% damage per point your Accuracy beats a defense roll, yours deal 25% per point — meaning you reach full damage at 4 points over rather than 5.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** 40 DEX

### 3. Weapons, Melee

#### -=[ Ambidextrous ]=-

You've trained your off-hand to perform as well as your dominant one. Each rank reduces the Accuracy penalty on Dual Wield attacks; every odd rank (1, 3, and 5) additionally gives +1 to Parry rolls when using weapons to defend.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** DEX /20, max 5

#### -=[ Dual Wielding ]=-

You attack with a weapon in each hand simultaneously. Halve each weapon's Speed (rounded up), then add those together for your Dual Wield Attack's Speed. The lower Accuracy of the two weapons applies. Damage is rolled separately for each weapon using its halved Speed. Stamina cost is the higher weapon's cost plus one third of the lower. This cannot be done with two-handed weapons. Dual Wield carries a −5 Accuracy penalty.

Lowering the Speed of a Dual Wield Attack also lowers the effective Speed used for damage calculation on both weapons equally. Weapons marked Dual calculate damage at their natural Speed regardless.

- **Base Cost:** 25
- **Requires:** 40 DEX

#### -=[ Monkey Grip ]=-

Through raw strength, chakra conditioning, and creative disregard for physics, you can wield two-handed weapons in one hand. Switching grip is a non-action. One-handed use loses the two-handed 25% damage bonus increase. At rank 2, you may dual-wield two-handed weapons if you also have Dual Wielding.

- **Base Cost:** 30
- **Scale:** 10
- **Limit:** STR /50, max 2

#### -=[ Quick Draw ]=-

The Speed of your Draw Weapon action is reduced by 1 per rank, to a minimum of 0.

- **Base Cost:** 7
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** DEX /30

#### -=[ Weapon Throw ]=-

As a Throw Weapon action (ranged rather than melee), you may throw any melee weapon you're holding up to STR/4 yards, dealing normal weapon damage with +5 to the weapon's normal Stamina cost.

- **Base Cost:** 15
- **Requires:** 40 STR, 40 DEX

#### -=[ Large: Momentum ]=-

You use your weapon's mass and swing arc against your opponent's attempts to intercept it. Each rank applies −1 to any enemy's Parry roll against your attacks.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** STR /30, max 3

#### -=[ Polearm: Trip ]=-

You can sweep the blunt end of a polearm at your opponent's legs, allowing you to use the E-rank Trip technique as a Weapon Taijutsu technique so long as you know it.

- **Base Cost:** 10

#### -=[ Small: Flurry ]=-

Small weapons reward volume over force. Each time you attack with a Small weapon, the total Speed of your next attack with that weapon is reduced by 1, stacking for consecutive attacks (three attacks would give the fourth a −3 reduction). As always, Speed cannot fall below half its base value. Maximum reduction equals your ranks in this ability.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** DEX /25, max 4

#### -=[ Sword: Iaijutsu ]=-

The first time you attack with your sword in a battle — provided it has not yet been drawn for parries, jutsu, or any other purpose — that attack's Speed is halved. For damage calculations, the attack still uses its original Speed. Usable once per battle even if you carry multiple swords.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** 50 DEX, 40 AGI

### 4. Weapons, Ranged

#### -=[ Assassin's Eye ]=-

You have a precise eye for targeting bodies at distance. Each rank reduces the Accuracy penalty for called shots with ranged weapons by 1.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** DEX /30, max 3
- **Requires:** Enhanced Sight 3

#### -=[ Combat Acupuncture ]=-

You can target vital pressure points with senbon. Whenever you make a called shot with senbon that deals damage, the target suffers a −2 Immobilization penalty. Each subsequent application increases the penalty by 1. The maximum penalty from this ability is your ranks + 1.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** DEX /20, max 5
- **Requires:** Medicine 10

#### -=[ Explosive Tag Connection ]=-

As a Speed (10 + 5×X) action, you attach and prime X tags to kunai hilts or arrows. These may be pre-attached before battle, though the priming action is still required. You can maintain up to (Chakra Control)/5 primed tags at once. Each item may carry only one tag; the item is destroyed by the explosion. Tags detonated by thrown kunai or arrows do so 15 IC after impact. A tagged kunai counts as two weapons toward your multi-throw limit; you may include up to one tag per rank in a single throw. If the target is struck they can pull the kunai out as a non-action and attempt to exit the area. Jutsu effects that cause tags to explode on contact override these rules.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 3
- **Limit:** Chakra Control /5

#### -=[ Improved Range ]=-

You can throw weapons farther than most. Your maximum range with thrown weapons increases by your Weapon Damage Bonus.

- **Base Cost:** 15

#### -=[ Quick Hands ]=-

You can add one extra weapon to a multi-throw without increasing its Speed. The Stamina cost still increases normally (the extra weapon counts toward the total for Stamina purposes).

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** DEX /25

#### -=[ Senbon Multi-Throw ]=-

You can multi-throw senbon in the same way as shuriken and kunai.

- **Base Cost:** 15
- **Requires:** 30 DEX

#### -=[ Senbon User ]=-

Senbon are unforgiving weapons for those without the training to use them — this ability represents having that training. Your senbon’s inherent −2 Weapon Accuracy becomes a +1 Weapon Accuracy bonus. Damage dealt by senbon is doubled for the purpose of determining wound severity.

- **Base Cost:** 15
- **Requires:** 25 DEX

#### -=[ Surgical Precision ]=-

When you wound an enemy with a senbon attack, you have a 10% chance per rank for the wound to be upgraded one severity. If the attack doesn't wound but deals any damage, you instead have a 10% chance per rank to cause a Minor wound. Each rank also raises the attack's Stamina cost by 1; you may choose whether and to what degree to apply this ability on a given attack. On a multi-throw with senbon, this is applied before the one-rank wound severity reduction from multi-throwing.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Medicine /2, max 10

#### -=[ **Custom Loads** ]=-

Whilst the sheer amount of gunpowder you can have on your person remains a hard limit, you become increasingly expert at packing that gunpowder into your firearms in such a way that you 'get more boom for your buck'.

Each rank of Custom Loads increases the damage of Pistols and Pepperboxes by 6d8, the damage of Battle Rifles by 2d10 (per round), and the damage of Sniper Rifles by 9d12.

- **Base Cos**t: 4<br />**Scale**: 2<br />**Limit**: (DEX − 35) / 25

## Physical

### 1. Vigor

#### -=[ Brute Force ]=-

Your physical strikes carry exceptional force — whether from raw muscle, technique, or both. Each rank increases your Physical Damage Bonus by 0.2.

- **Base Cost:** 1
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** STR /10
- **Requires:** 80 STR

#### -=[ Cadence ]=-

Once your rhythm is established, it sustains itself. All your Combo jutsu have −1 Stamina added to their Combo Counter line. This cannot reduce a technique's cost below half its normal value.

- **Base Cost:** 30
- **Requires:** +6 or higher bonus to Stamina rolls, not counting Fatigue bonuses

#### -=[ Endurance ]=-

You can absorb a hit in ways that would compromise anyone else. When you declare a defensive interrupt as a Block, you may declare it as an Endurance Block — reducing damage by an additional 10% at rank 1, or an additional 25% at rank 2. This can only be used once every (30 − RES/10) IC.

- **Base Cost:** 25
- **Scale:** 10
- **Limit:** 2
- **Requires:** 60 RES

#### -=[ Fortitude ]=-

You've trained to better absorb impacts. Each rank gives +1 to Resistance rolls when you Block — and if you've declared Block as a variable speed action, this bonus extends to any effects requiring a Resistance roll that you wouldn't normally get to defend against (such as the B-rank Poison Mist). The bonus applies even if you subsequently dodge a specific attack while the Block is active.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** STR /20, max 5

#### -=[ Good Lungs ]=-

You recover from sustained exertion with an almost unsettling efficiency. Each rank causes the Rest action to reduce your Stamina penalty by 1 additional point — at two ranks, a single Rest lowers your penalty by 3.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** RES /80

#### -=[ Hard to Kill ]=-

You are, as the name suggests, difficult to put down. Each rank increases your maximum Vitality and HP by 3% and grants 2 Damage Reduction. Wounds are always calculated based on your unmodified true maximums — the 3% bonus does not factor into wound thresholds.

- **Base Cost:** 1
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** RES /10
- **Requires:** 80 RES

#### -=[ Healthy ]=-

Regular exercise and decent habits have given you a meaningful edge in sustained exertion. Each rank gives +1 to Stamina rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Athletics /5, max 5

#### -=[ Power Attack ]=-

You put additional force behind your unarmed strikes. Each rank lets you increase the Stamina cost and die size of your Basic Unarmed Attack by 2. At rank 1, you could deal 2d6 damage for Stamina 7; at rank 4, 2d12 for Stamina 13.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** STR /20, max 4

#### -=[ Resilient ]=-

Grapples just don't damage you the way they damage most people. Each rank reduces damage you take from Grapple Taijutsu by 5%.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** RES /20, max 10

#### -=[ Rigorous Training ]=-

You have spent time in genuinely unpleasant conditions — under waterfalls, across deserts, through extreme cold — and your body has adapted. Each rank gives +1 to Resistance rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** RES /20

#### -=[ Shadowboxing ]=-

When your target vanishes, you don't let it break your momentum. You can perform the Search action without breaking your current Combo. If the Search doesn't locate your target, your Combo Counter decreases by 1.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** Athletics 10, Awareness 10

#### -=[ Unarmed Focus ]=-

Your basic punches and kicks are more dangerous than they look. Each rank lets you roll one additional die when making a Basic Unarmed Attack.

- **Base Cost:** 3
- **Scale:** 0
- **Limit:** (STR − 20) /10

### 2. Mobility

#### -=[ Evasion ]=-

Your conditioning means you can get clear of area attacks faster than most. The dodge penalty from area-of-effect attacks based on their size is reduced by 2 per rank, to a minimum of 0.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** AGI /20

#### -=[ Runner ]=-

You're built for both the sprint and the long haul. Each rank increases your AGI for movement purposes by 5% when taking a Move action. Movement as part of an attack and techniques such as Shunshin do not benefit.

- **Base Cost:** 3
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Athletics /4, max 5

#### -=[ Sudden Attack, CQC ]=-

You sacrifice damage output for speed and penetration, trying to overwhelm a defense before it can fully form. For each rank, you may take up to +1 CQC Accuracy on a damaging CQC attack; each +1 reduces its damage by 10%.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** STR /20, max 5

#### -=[ Sudden Attack, Weapon ]=-

 You sacrifice power for a faster, sharper strike: For each rank, you may take up to +1 Weapon Accuracy on a damaging weapon attack; each +1 reduces its damage by 10%.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** DEX /20, max 5

## Sensory

### 1. Enhanced Senses

#### -=[ Enhanced Hearing ]=-

You've learned to stop filtering out sounds that most people tune out automatically, giving you the ability to focus on quiet, distant, or obscured noises when it matters. Each rank gives +1 to Awareness rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 3
- **Limit:** CHA /10, max 4
- **Special:** Enhanced Hearing and Enhanced Sight share a Scale. Four ranks of one makes the first rank of the other cost (4 + 3×4 =) 16 XP.

#### -=[ Enhanced Sight ]=-

Your attention to visual detail is exceptional. You notice what others overlook — a shadow moving at the wrong speed, a face that doesn't quite match its surroundings. Each rank gives +1 to Awareness rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 3
- **Limit:** CHA /10, max 4
- **Special:** Shares a Scale with Enhanced Hearing (see above).

#### -=[ Blind Fighting ]=-

Smoke, darkness, hostile ninjutsu — you've trained to operate when your vision fails. The maximum Visibility Penalty you suffer is reduced by 1 per rank. This does not change the actual severity of visibility conditions as they apply to others (enemies can still Hide from you at the normal threshold), only the penalties you personally suffer.

- **Base Cost:** 7
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** CHA /20, max 8

#### -=[ Keen Senses ]=-

You're exceptionally good at zeroing in on a target once you start looking. The cumulative Search action bonus increases from the normal +2 per attempt to +3 at rank 1 and +4 at rank 2.

- **Base Cost:** 8
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** Enhanced Sight ranks /2

### 2. Perception

#### -=[ Instinct ]=-

You react before you fully process the threat. Each rank gives +1 to Dodge rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 8
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Survival /5

#### -=[ Sixth Sense ]=-

You feel what you can't fully explain — killing intent, the wrongness before an ambush, the pressure of something about to happen. Each rank gives +1 to Awareness and defensive rolls. Whenever you would be hit by a Surprise or Sneak Attack, Sixth Sense activates automatically, allowing you to defend against it normally. Each activation reduces both bonuses by 1. At 0, Sixth Sense stops functioning. This resets at the start of each day.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** CHA /40

## Subtlety

### 1. Deception

#### -=[ Cunning ]=-

You understand the tells people look for and know exactly how to withhold or falsify them. Each rank gives +1 to CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy, your misdirection creates genuine openings that straightforward aggression wouldn’t find.

- **Base Cost:** 8
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Espionage /5

#### -=[ Cunning Blow ]=-

After successfully defending against an attack, the first attack you make against that attacker gains +4 Accuracy. Successful defense means you avoided being hit entirely by actively choosing to defend (dodge, parry, or similar jutsu-based defenses). Block does not qualify. Neither does negating all damage through DR or the Berserker's no-defense option. Active defenses that stop an attack completely (such as Earth Shore Return) do qualify. Defensive interrupts that become attacks do not gain this bonus themselves, but also do not consume it.

- **Base Cost:** 50
- **Requires:** Espionage 20

#### -=[ Stalker ]=-

You can follow someone for a long time without being noticed. Each rank gives +1 to your Stealth TN while already hidden. Every odd rank (1, 3, 5) additionally gives +1 to CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy on Sneak Attacks made from stealth.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 3
- **Limit:** DEX /20, max 5

#### -=[ Subterfuge ]=-

You know how to make a lie sound like the truth and the truth sound like a lie. Each rank gives +1 to Espionage and Stealth rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 3
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Espionage /3, max 5

### 2. Ninja Tactics

#### -=[ Basic Training ]=-

The fundamentals of shinobi conduct, environmental awareness, deception, staying invisible, tend to get deprioritized by genin who would rather learn to shoot fireballs. The ones who took these seriously tend to outlive the ones who didn't. Each rank provides +1 to CQC, Ninjutsu, Weapon Accuracy, defensive rolls, and skill checks.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** Lowest of Awareness /5, Espionage /5, or Stealth /5; max 5

#### -=[ Defensive Formation ]=-

You position yourself between your charges and whoever is trying to reach them. Declare this as a variable speed action, designating up to 1 + XP/1000 targets within AGI/20 yards. If a designated target is attacked during your Defensive Formation, you may immediately attempt to Take the Hit as an interrupt, without advancing your next IC as normal for an interrupt, with a +10 bonus (replacing the normal +5). Against area-of-effect attacks, you take full damage and they take only half (reducible by their own defenses). The formation ends automatically if a protected target moves beyond your radius. This is considered an “Active Defense”, you may simultaneously maintain any two “Active Defenses” at once.

- **Base Cost:** 25

#### -=[ Guard ]=-

You hold a ready position with your weapon, prepared to redirect incoming attacks with your next response rather than force alone. Guard is a variable speed action like blocking. While guarding, you have +5 Accuracy on QCQ parries, Ninjutsu Parries, and Weapon Parries. Your CQC parry and Weapon parry action becomes Speed 0. This is considered an “Active Defense”, you may simultaneously maintain any two “Active Defenses” at once.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** 45 DEX

#### -=[ Safety Dance ]=-

You keep your guard up even while catching your breath. When you take a Rest action, you are also considered to be Blocking until your next non-interrupt action.

- **Base Cost:** 60
- **Requires:** 60 STR

#### -=[ Survival Tactics]=-

The first lesson in staying alive: don't be found. These foundational techniques extend into general field operations, moving quietly, reading terrain, staying ahead of pursuit. Each rank gives +1 to Stealth and Survival rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** AGI /10, max 5
- **Requires:** Survival 5

#### -=[ Total Defense ]=-

You abandon offense entirely and focus on staying alive. Declare this as a variable speed action in the same manner as blocking. While in Total Defense, your Dodge interrupt becomes Speed 0 and gains +3. You may simultaneously maintain any two of: Block, Guard, Total Defense, and Defensive Formation.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Requires:** 45 AGI

## Slice of Life

### 1. Willpower

#### -=[ Desperation ]=-

Genuine danger brings out something in you that ordinary circumstances don't. When defending against an attack, you may spend 1 Willpower to allow your interrupt to be valid as long as its Speed is double or less that of the incoming attack, rather than the standard half or less. This persists for the full IC — if four attacks arrive simultaneously, all four defenses benefit — but applies once per person and cannot be activated a second time that IC. A subsequent interrupt triggered by your own defensive action (such as a counter) would not receive this benefit.

- **Base Cost:** 40
- **Requires:** 3 Willpower

#### -=[ Determination ]=-

You have more will than most people know what to do with. Each rank increases your Willpower by 1.

- **Base Cost:** 50
- **Scale:** 25
- **Limit:** XP /1000

#### -=[ Focus ]=-

When something has scrambled your senses or locked up your limbs, you can push through it. By spending 1 Willpower, you end any Stuns you are currently suffering and become immune to further instances of that specific status until the end of the current Initiative Count.

- **Base Cost:** 40
- **Requires:** 3 Willpower

#### -=[ Guts ]=-

You refuse to fall. Whenever you would be reduced to 0 HP or lower, make a TN 25 Stamina check. On success, you are left at 1 HP and cannot have your HP reduced until the end of that IC. On failure, Fatigue advances normally and you take the damage as usual. Damage negated through this ability still wounds you normally. Clones of any kind cannot benefit from Guts.

- **Base Cost:** 50
- **Requires:** 3 Willpower

#### -=[ Limit Break ]=-

Through sheer will, you push yourself beyond what your body should allow. Once per day per rank, you may reroll any roll, or replace a CQC, Ninjutsu, or Weapon Accuracy value with a d20 + your normal corresponding Accuracy bonus (not including the base 10). On a d20 Limit Break, if the result is below 11, add 10 to the die roll. Using Limit Break immediately advances one Fatigue category, chosen to apply before or after the action’s associated Fatigue roll(s). This can be declared after you’ve seen whether your original roll would have succeeded.

- **Base Cost:** 20
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** Willpower /2

### 2. Initiative

#### -=[ Defensive Reflexes ]=-

You're ready before you know what you're ready for. When rolling Initiative, you may declare Defensive Reflexes. You are treated as Blocking at the start of battle but take −4 to your Initiative roll. This penalty is reduced by 1 for each rank beyond the first.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** −2
- **Limit:** RES /20, max 3

#### -=[ Reflexes ]=-

Your body moves ahead of your conscious decision. Each rank gives +1 to Initiative, and every even rank gives an additional +1 to Athletics rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** AGI /10, max 20

#### -=[ Rush ]=-

You don't warm up. You're already there. You begin battle with (AGI × ranks) /20 AP.

- **Base Cost:** 4
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** AGI /20, max 5

#### -=[ Preparation ]=-

As part of your Initiative roll, you may activate one or more techniques with Upkeeps that affect only yourself. You must pay their Chakra or Stamina costs normally. The total of all activated techniques' (Speed + modified Seal Speed) cannot exceed what you rolled for Initiative.

- **Base Cost:** 40
- **Requires:** 60 DEX

### 3. Action Points

#### -=[ Active ]=-

You generate more AP than most, keeping up a constant tempo in battle. Each rank increases the AP you gain per 20 IC by 1, at 3 ranks, you'd gain 8 AP every 20 IC instead of 5.

- **Base Cost:** 2
- **Scale:** 1
- **Limit:** AGI /20

#### -=[ Principle of Motion ]=-

You move at speeds that suggest you've found a loophole in how time works. At any point after your first non-interrupt action in combat, you may spend a minimum of 8 AP to bring your next action 1 IC earlier per 2 AP spent. This can only bring your next action forward to the current IC — not further. You cannot use it on an IC where you've already taken an action (defensive interrupts don't count). It cannot accelerate ongoing actions, such as Handseals in progress, Delayed actions, or Variable Speed Actions. You cannot use Principle of Motion while Stunned, and cannot use it again within 10 IC of the last use.

- **Base Cost:** 60
- **Requires:** 100 DEX, 100 AGI

#### -=[ Surge ]=-

You can spend AP for a tactical edge in a specific exchange. Spending 8 AP gives +1 to CQC, Ninjutsu, or Weapon Accuracy; spending 5 AP gives +1 to a defensive roll. Either way, Surge caps at a +5 bonus and must be declared at the time the roll or attack is made.

- **Base Cost:** 40
- **Requires:** 80 AGI

### 4. Skill

#### -=[ Anatomical Knowledge ]=-

Your knowledge of the human body has taken a practical turn. Each rank causes your CQC attacks and Weapon Attacks to calculate wounds as though their damage were 5% higher.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Medicine /5

#### -=[ Doctor ]=-

You can do more for injured allies than most field medics dream of. Roleplaying with you for at least an hour counts that day as 4 days of wound recovery for your patients (see Wound Recovery on the Wounds page), and lets their Critical wounds heal that day. You can treat a number of patients at the same time equal to your ranks in Doctor, doubled if you treat them in a Hospital. At rank 1, this requires one First Aid kit per patient. At rank 2, no First Aid kit is required.

In addition to this, if your Compound has a Hospital, you can admit patients into it which lets them temporarily gain the benefits of your Hospital as if they were a member of your Compound.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 5
- **Limit:** (Medicine − 5) /5, max 3
- **Requires:** Medical Training

#### -=[ General Knowledge ]=-

A broad base of knowledge with deep pockets in several areas. Where direct talent won't carry you, this does. Each rank gives +1 to Medicine, Chakra Control, and Toxicology rolls.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Research /5

#### -=[ Pain Knowledge ]=-

A thorough understanding of what disrupts a body's equilibrium, from toxins to pressure points, translates directly into greater effectiveness in dealing damage. Each rank increases all damage you deal by 2%.

- **Base Cost:** 6
- **Scale:** 4
- **Limit:** Toxicology /5

#### -=[ Skill Focus: (Skill) ]=-

An intense, narrow dedication to a single skill at the cost of breadth. Each rank increases your rank in the chosen skill by 1 as an actual rank increase — not a bonus — ignoring and not counting against the normal skill rank limit. You may take Skill Focus for multiple skills, each treated as a separate ability with its own scale.

- **Base Cost:** 2 Skill Points
- **Scale:** +1 Skill Point per odd-numbered rank (Ranks 1–2 cost 2 SP; ranks 3–4 cost 3 SP; and so on)

#### -=[ Specialization ]=-

You've practiced a skill until your floor is solid. Whenever you roll below a 5 on the d20 for that skill, treat the result as a 5 instead. Each time you take this ability it applies to a different skill. Does not apply when Willpower is spent or effects that follow Willpower's rules are used.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 0
- **Requires:** At least 5 ranks in each skill it's applied to

## Development

#### -=[ Holding Back ]=-

You know precisely how much force you're applying and choose to apply less. Declare when you attack. A held-back attack uses half your normal damage bonus, and any wounds it inflicts are reduced one severity — though this does not prevent a wound from ending any Genjutsu the target was suffering. Rank 1 allows holding back on basic attacks and E-rank jutsu; each additional rank extends this to the next rank up (D, C, B, A). S-rank techniques cannot be held back.

- **Base Cost:** 5
- **Scale:** 2
- **Limit:** Max 5

#### -=[ Progression ]=-

Normally, the amount you've raised one attribute must be within 3 points of the amount the next-highest has been raised. Each rank loosens this restriction by 1, allowing a wider gap between your highest and lowest attribute investments.

- **Base Cost:** 10
- **Scale:** 0
- **Limit:** XP /750
