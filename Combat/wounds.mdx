title: "Wounds"
---

A shinobi's guide to injury. You have trained your body to be a weapon. You are not supposed to bleed now, are you? And yet, the blade finds its mark. The fireball catches you mid-dodge. The fist collapses your ribs. You are not invincible. You never were. What you are, and I don't mind telling you, is someone who refuses to stay down. That is the difference between a dead shinobi and a living one. Not the absence of injury. The will to survive it, don't you know?

Not all injuries are created equal, my dear fellow. Experience, the kind that leaves scars, teaches you to recognize the shape of your own suffering.

Blunt wounds are the ache of impact. The force transferred through armor, through muscle, into the soft places where you keep your organs. A dull, persistent throb that deepens with every breath. A limb that moves wrong. The sickening crunch of bone against bone. Blunt wounds are accountants, if you will. They do not scream. They sit in the back of your awareness, adding up the cost of every action.

Energy wounds are the burn that lingers. Fire. Lightning. Raw chakra turned against you. These wounds cook. Red, blistered skin. Nerves screaming. Your body is fighting to regulate its own temperature. Energy wounds disrupt, you see. Burns accumulate. Your body cannot regulate itself, and every action makes it even worse.

Slashing wounds demand your attention, and I must say, they are quite persistent. The blade, the claw, the shard of broken glass. Dreadfully sharp edges that part flesh like water. A sudden, sharp shock of cold. Then heat. Then the wetness of blood running down your skin. Slashing wounds bleed. They accumulate and then reopen. You simply cannot ignore them. They will not let you.

Piercing wounds are precise, surgical, and utterly destabilizing. The spear, the arrow, the needle-sharp point that slides between your ribs. These wounds do not just damage; they disrupt, don't you know. Your nerves scream. Your muscles spasm. Before you can act, you must overcome the Flinch. If you fail, you botch. Your attack misses. Your dodge fails. Some say that although the wounds are liveable, its consequences are not. 

Minor wounds are the price of admission. Major wounds are the cost of failure. Severe wounds are the cost of survival. Critical wounds are the cost of victory, and I daresay, quite a steep one at that. You will be wounded. It is an inevitable, old sport. What matters is what you do next. You will stop the bleeding. You will stabilize the wound. You will get back up, and you will do it with style. That is what it means to be a shinobi. To get back up, again and again, until the fight is won or you cannot rise anymore. The wound is not the end, the wound is the beginning of the next fight.

## **Wound Recovery**

Wounds heal on a flat timer based on their severity. Downtime cannot be spent to recover from wounds.

| Severity | Recovery |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Minor** | Heals at the end of the battle |
| **Major** | 4 days of healing |
| **Severe** | 8 days of healing |
| **Critical** | 16 days of healing. Only heals on days you roleplay in a Hospital or with a Doctor |

Healing is counted in real-world (OOC) days. Each day, every wound you have heals by:

| That day | Days of healing |
| :-- | :-- |
| No medical roleplay | 1 |
| Roleplay for at least an hour in a Hospital | 2 |
| Roleplay for at least an hour with a character who has the Doctor ability | 4 |

A Doctor can provide this to a number of patients at the same time equal to their ranks in the Doctor ability. This limit is doubled when they treat patients in a Hospital.

# **Blunt (Minor)**

### **(1–20) Ringing Noise (Head)**

There's a drumming inside your skull that won't stop. Distracting doesn't begin to cover it. You take a **-1 penalty to all d20 rolls**, and **-2 Genjutsu** **Defense**. Your head's already unreliable, so the illusions find easier purchase.

### **(21–40) Bruised Ribs (Torso)**

The bones, not the skin over them. Ribs hurt more than they have any right to. Breathing is the worst part. Deep breaths especially. Every action you take has its **Speed increased by 1**, similar to Paralysis.

### **(41–60) Winded (Abdomen)**

A solid shot to the gut caught your solar plexus, compressed your diaphragm, or managed both at once. You'll be fine. Probably. Until then, your **Stamina penalty increases by 4** and you're hit with **Stun 4**.

### **(61–80) Bruised (Arm)**

Your arm is bruised. It hurts. If you attack without using that arm, you take a **-1 Accuracy penalty** from the distraction. Your **Seal Speeds increase by 1**.

### **(81–100) Bruised (Leg)**

Something hit your leg and now it hurts. You'll feel better after you walk it off, or sit down and let the pain fade. Until the end of battle, your **base movement speed drops by 10%** and you take a **-1 penalty to Dodge rolls**.

## **Blunt (Major)**

### **(1–20) Dazed (Head)**

That one left you reeling. Once the stars clear, you'll be mostly okay.

You **lose all AP you currently have**. Any effects with Upkeeps end, doujutsu excluded, because you've lost the concentration to hold them. If you were standing on water or a wall, you fall.

Everything seems slightly off after that. You can't quite place what. Sounds are muffled, colors muted, and things keep moving at the edge of your vision. This distraction gives you a **-3 penalty to all skill checks and defensive Genjutsu rolls**.

### **(21–40) Collapsed Lung (Torso)**

Your lung quit its day job. Unfortunately, that day job was letting you breathe. Your **Fatigue rolls take a -4 penalty**. You can survive with both lungs collapsed, though that pushes the penalty to **-12** rather than -8.

### **(41–60) Groin Shot (Abdomen)**

If this was a called shot, you should probably have a serious talk with your opponent about what counts as fair fighting. Even ninja have standards.

It's a **Stun 7**, and you're probably feeling more than a little nauseous. Don't be ashamed about doubling over and whimpering. Until it heals, your **Agility counts as 25% lower** for determining movement speed. You also take a **-3 penalty to Resistance rolls** until recovery is complete.

### **(61–80) Sprained Arm (Arm)**

Your elbow or wrist got forced to bend in a way it isn't meant to, and it sprained from the blow. You take a **-4 penalty to rolls and Accuracy** for attacks using that arm. That includes grapple rolls, grapple TNs, and all two-handed weapons. Single-handed weapons held in the other hand, ninjutsu, and taijutsu jutsu that specify kicks only suffer a **-2**. Your **Seal Speeds increase by 5**.

### **(81–100) Sprained Leg (Leg)**

The muscles and ligaments in your knee or ankle stretched past what they can take. Any time you move, including dodging, the pain leaves your **Accuracy and d20 rolls at a -4 penalty for the next 15 IC**.

## **Blunt (Severe)**

### **(1–20) Concussion (Head)**

Somebody turned out the lights. It's very dark, and you are likely to be eaten by a grue. You get over that before your enemies can capitalize, or before you're eaten. Whichever comes first.

You take a **-5 penalty on all d20 rolls**, and the **AP you gain is halved**, rounded down. Whenever you suffer a head wound, you have a **10% chance per level of that wound to be knocked unconscious**.

### **(21–40) Broken Ribs**

You are in horrible, terrible pain. You'd be screaming or mewling if you weren't a ninja. Breathing feels like being stabbed, and depending on how badly those ribs are broken, that might be exactly what's happening.

Blocking's effectiveness drops by **30%**, from 50% to 20% for most attacks, and from 25% to nothing against weapons. You take a **-4 penalty to Accuracy and d20 rolls**. Every action you take has its **Speed increased by 2**.

### **(41–60) Crushed Organ (Abdomen)**

It's not as bad as it sounds. There's severe bleeding and bruising inside the organ itself, and it hurts horrendously, but it isn't bleeding into the rest of your body. That means it can only get so bad.

Your **maximum HP and Vitality drop by 10%**. Your current values of both drop by 10% of your maximum. You take a **-5 penalty to Resistance rolls**.

### **(61–80) Broken Arm (Arm)**

You can't use the arm in grapples, and you take a **-5 penalty to grapple rolls and grapple TNs**. You can't hold two-handed weapons with it. Attempting any other taijutsu with it, unarmed or weapon, incurs a **-5 Accuracy penalty** and denies you your **damage bonus** for that action.

You can still form handseals. When you finish, make a **Resistance roll with a -10 penalty** against the base Seal Speed. Failure means you flinch. You still get the jutsu off, but you inflict **Stun 12** on yourself.

### **(81–100) Broken Leg (Leg)**

Severe damage to your leg leaves you with a reduced ability to get around the battlefield. If things get bad enough, you can still power-hobble on it. It doesn't directly penalize your movement, but moving adds **Stun 4**, even when the movement is part of an attack.

While the leg is broken, you can't use any jutsu or abilities that increase your movement speed.

With crutches, a cane, or something similar, you move at **half speed**.

## **Blunt (Critical)**

### **(1–20) Fractured Skull (Head)**

You got hit in the head hard enough that you're lucky you don't have more brain damage than you do. Usually this comes with nasty lacerations and prolific bleeding. Not always.

Aside from **Stun 15**, you take a **-5 penalty to Accuracy and all d20 rolls**, doubled for skill checks.

Until this heals, any time you suffer a Head Wound, of any type or severity, roll 1d20. On a 1–10, you suffer the wound normally. On an 11–20, you’re unconscious and comatose for one day for each rank of the wound’s Severity (Minor = 1, critical = 4).

### **(21–40) Sternal Fracture (Torso)**

Your sternum is that long, sturdy piece of bone and cartilage running down the middle of your chest that all your ribs connect to. It's hard to break. Nice job pulling that one off.

The break itself isn't the worst part. Whatever hit you hard enough to do it also broke several ribs and caused significant internal damage. It's easy to tell your chest is ruined. There's a grinding noise of bone on bone whenever you breathe or move too much. The agonizing pain is a pretty good hint too.

You take a **-15 penalty on all Stamina checks**, your **damage bonuses are halved**, and you take a **-5 penalty on all defensive rolls**.

### **(41–60) Rupture (Abdomen)**

Your abdomen matters because it's full of squishy parts that are important for your continued survival. Whatever trauma you just went through ruptured one of those squishy bits, and now it's bleeding into your insides. Blood belongs inside you. Just not inside your abdominal cavity.

Every time you act, movement included, you **lose HP equal to the Speed of the action**. You also lose **1 HP every 5 IC**. In theory, this could wound you again.

Treating this with First Aid requires a **Medicine skill of at least 10**. It generally involves cutting open the victim's abdomen and letting it drain, or in rare cases ventilating it, before figuring out what's actually bleeding and making it stop. Treatment is a **Speed 25 action**, but it stops the persistent HP loss.

Any time you take an action, you have a **5 × (action's Speed)% chance** of causing the injury to act up again. That brings back all its effects and requires treatment to bring it back under control.

### **(61–80) Shattered Arm (Arm)**

Your arm is so broken that the breaks have breaks. It's useless. You can't perform handseals, hold weapons, or use items with it.

The pain is horrible and makes concentrating almost impossible. You take a **-6 penalty to d20 rolls and Accuracy**, and a **-4 penalty to Fatigue checks**.

### **(81–100) Shattered Leg (Leg)**

There are broken legs. There are severely broken legs. Then there's your leg.

You can't stand without something to prop you up. Because you're a tough-as-nails, super-resourceful ninja, you can still move, though your **movement distance is divided by 4**. You take a **-8 penalty to Dodge** and **-4 to other d20 rolls**.

Once you’ve been treated in a Hospital or by a Doctor and put in a splint, you can walk. So long as you carry a crutch, cane, or walking stick, which occupies a hand, you move at half speed and suffer only half the above penalties. You can do this without the aid, though each time you take an action requiring movement, dodging included, or get hit, you have a 50% chance of your leg giving out and suffering the full penalties again.

# **Energy (Minor)**

### **(1–20) Sun Spots (Head)**

You blinked a fraction too late. Now vague shapes that look a lot like the blast you just took are floating around in front of your eyes, swimming through your vision every time you try to focus on something. Annoying more than anything else. You have a -4 Visibility Penalty, which fades at a rate of 1 per 10 IC.

### **(21–40) Flash Fever (Torso)**

The hit caught you square in the center of your body. Which is good, in theory, since that's what your torso is for. But whatever hit you also pumped enough heat into you that your internal thermostat has given up and gone home. You feel vaguely sick and tired. You'll shake it off eventually. You have a -2 to Fatigue rolls until the end of battle.

### **(41–60) Stomach Blister (Abdomen)**

A nasty blister bubbled up from whatever hit you. Plasma trapped in a layer of skin that swelled up like a little balloon. It's gross. It's also uncomfortable in a way that's hard to ignore. You have a -1 Immobilization penalty until the end of the fight, unless you decide to pop it as a Speed 8 action. If you do, you take a fourth of the damage that caused this injury all over again. That additional damage can't cause a wound on its own.

### **(61–80) Burned (Arm)**

You've been burned. It hurts. The skin is red and starting to swell, and touching it, or having it touched, sends a spike through your arm. You have a Burn 3, and a -2 to Accuracy until it goes away. The pain makes using that limb a genuine negotiation.

### **(81–100) Burned (Leg)**

You've been burned. Seriously, it hurts. Your leg is red and putting weight on it, running on it, doing anything on it, is an exercise in suffering. You have a Burn 3 and a -2 to Dodges until it goes away. The pain of using the affected limb is the problem.

## **Energy (Major)**

### **(1–20) Facial Burn (Head)**

There's a nasty burn across your face now. Blistered. Partially charred. Disorienting in ways you didn't expect. The smell of burnt flesh is stuck in your nostrils, and the taste of it lingers in your mouth, so every deep breath makes you nauseous. The more you exert yourself, the more you smell it, the sicker you get.

Every time you make a Stamina or Chakra roll, you increase your penalty to both by 2.

### **(21–40) Scorched Chest (Torso)**

The majority of your body has been burned. Chest, back, stomach. Any hair that used to be there is gone. It isn't deep, since it covers such a large surface area, but that's almost worse. So much exposed, aggravated tissue. It makes it easy for anyone to make your day worse.

You take an additional 10% damage when hit, after all normal reductions but before determining if you suffer wounds. One of the worst parts is how it makes getting additional wounds that much easier.

### **(41–60) Scalded Stomach (Abdomen)**

Whatever hit you burned away what covered your midriff and had enough power behind it to heat up your internal organs. Your organs are sensitive about that kind of thing. You are afflicted with a Stun 8 and have your current and maximum Hit Points and Vitality reduced by your RES until this heals.

### **(61–80) Second Degree Burn (Arm)**

The majority of your arm is burned. Not life-threatening, but the amount of skin that's been damaged is substantial. Using that arm in a fight is a great deal more painful than it should be.

When you perform handseals, attack, or parry, you take twice the base Speed of the action as damage, which bypasses reductions like DR. You also have a -2 to Accuracy.

### **(81–100) Second Degree Burn (Leg)**

One of your legs has been burned down to the dermis, the layer beneath the epidermis. Multiple small blisters have formed at the surface, and the skin has reddened and toughened considerably.

You have a -5 Immobilization penalty, along with a Burn 3. When the Burn status is removed, the Immobilization penalty drops to 3.

## **Energy (Severe)**

### **(1–20) Blinded (Head)**

You kept your eyes open a little too long when something was aimed right at your face. Staring directly into a superheated jutsu aimed at your head is about as smart as staring into the sun. Your whole face was scalded, but your eyes took the worst of it.

You have a -3 Visibility Penalty, which is reduced by 1 every 10 IC, and a Stun 3. Not so bad on its own. Until the next time you're hit by a jutsu that inflicts Energy wounds, regardless of whether it actually wounds you.

When that happens, your Visibility Penalty increases by 1 per rank of the jutsu. 1 for E, 3 for C, and so on, to a maximum of -8. The technique has to deal damage to trigger this.

### **(21–40) Dark Halo (Torso)**

The name comes from the burns of the entire dermis in a solid, unbroken ring around your body. It isn't as cool as it sounds. Burns of that magnitude can cut off circulation to the rest of the body.

You're going into shock. At the start, it isn't so bad. It reduces the bonus to CE and Stamina rolls from Fatigue by 1, so +5 becomes +4. Every time your Fatigue increases, though, the bonus lowers by one per level. If you were at Fatigue 3 when you received this wound, then went up to Fatigue 5 in the same battle, you'd have only +2 to Chakra Exhaustion and Stamina rolls per Fatigue category, for a total of +10 at Fatigue 5.

### **(41–60) Side Burns (Abdomen)**

The sides of your ribcage have been charred and are almost black. Hence the name that resembles a much less gruesome hairstyle. Aside from making the definition of your ribcage disturbingly obvious, it also makes it harder to breathe.

It isn't so bad here and there, but that quick, almost gasping inhale you take when you try to push yourself onward makes you pause and lose your breath. Slows you down when you want to be at your quickest.

Whenever you spend AP in combat, you must first pay an additional amount of AP equal to your current Fatigue level plus 1. This surcharge does not affect the action's Speed, pay any costs associated with it, or otherwise count in any beneficial manner. It does count against any penalties or limitations regarding spending AP you may have.

Every time you spend more than your current Willpower in AP on an action, including the surcharge from this wound, you are afflicted with Suffocation 1. This Suffocation penalty persists for the duration of the fight but resets to 0 after combat.

### **(61–80) Muscle Melting (Arms)**

Not as literal as the name implies, but no less serious. You've been burned so deep that the muscle itself has been affected. Aside from intense pain, it weakens the muscle as a substantial section becomes damaged and useless.

Your damage bonuses, Physical and Ninjutsu, are lowered by 25%, and you have a -1 penalty to Accuracy. As you continually use the muscles and chakra involved, the heat builds and spreads, worsening the injury. More damage to both muscles and tenketsu.

Whenever you deal damage, the Damage Bonus used is lowered by 5%, to a minimum of 50%, halving your damage bonus. When either reaches 50%, your Accuracy is lowered by an additional 1 point, to a maximum of -5.

### **(81–100) Fused Foot (Leg)**

The skin of one of your feet, odds right, evens left, has been burned so badly that the toes are more or less a single section. Melted together. The rest of it isn't much better, but the most pressing issue is how it impairs function in battle.

You have a -5 to Dodges, since shifting your weight and pivoting on that leg is now genuinely difficult. It makes turning into and away from blows harder too, imposing a -2 penalty to Parries. Running effectively is off the table. You lose the benefits of any effects which increase your AGI for the sole purpose of movement, like the Runner ability or Racing Thunder Technique, and your movement speed is halved on top of that.

How you fix it medically is unpleasant to hear about and worse to experience. You should want medical attention for it.

## **Energy (Critical)**

### **(1–20) Eye Welded Shut (Head)**

You got hit in the face by a giant fireball or a bolt of lightning. Awful for a lot of reasons. Aside from the deep-tissue burns across half your face, the heat was enough to melt the surface of one of your eyelids, fusing it shut.

Any ranks in Enhanced Sight are lost until you recover, along with any abilities dependent on it. That eye is effectively useless. You take a -3 Visibility Penalty.

It gets worse. The movements of your eyes are linked. Where one goes, the other follows. So while one eye is damaged and basically glued by molten flesh to your eyelid, it's still trying to move around, aggravating the injury further.

Every 10 IC that passes, you suffer a -1 penalty to Accuracy, Fatigue rolls, and defensive rolls, with a maximum of -10. If you close both eyes, these penalties reduce by 1 every 20 IC, to a minimum of half the highest value reached. If it got up to -7, closing your eyes couldn't reduce it below -4. Closing both eyes also incurs a -10 Visibility Penalty, so there's that.

### **(21–40) Atrial Fibrillation (Torso)**

The burns to your chest are so deep and severe that they're affecting your cardiovascular system. Specifically, the heart, and the rhythm at which it beats. This is the less lethal form of fibrillation. Your heart is still able to beat and pump blood. It just does so irregularly.

Still pretty bad. Fighting causes your pulse to shoot up, which increases the workload on your heart and makes every skipped beat more likely and more devastating. Every 15 IC, you take a -1 to Accuracy, d20 rolls, and Defensive rolls. This penalty can be reduced by 1 by taking a Rest action, but if you do, you don't lower your Stamina penalty as normal.

The more you fight, the worse it gets. There's only so much even a ninja can do to support themselves while their heart isn't working right. This penalty can only be reduced to half its highest amount reached. If it becomes a -5 to Accuracy, d20 rolls, and Defensive rolls, you can only reduce it by Resting to -3.

This penalty doesn't increase outside of combat but will continue to affect you every time you fight. One of the worst problems with this injury is that it's a chronic condition the body can't fix on its own once it's happened. You must receive medical attention.

### **(41–60) Slow Roasted (Abdomen)**

When something is cooked poorly, the outside burns first. The inside is typically not that bad. But when something is really charred, the outside blackens and dies, and it encapsulates the interior, preventing heat from escaping.

That's basically what happened to you. Your skin is flaking from your stomach and looks horrifying every time you breathe in and out. The real problem is how your innards have been superheated and can't effectively cool off. The result is a constant feeling of sickness that seems impossible to treat.

You automatically advance one Fatigue level. Penalties to your Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls are not reset to 0 when you advance a Fatigue level, and you advance an additional Fatigue category for every 10 points you fail a roll by, rather than every 15.

You can still Rest, but it becomes a Speed 15 action, to which AP still cannot be applied. It is very, very difficult for your body to come back from this.

### **(61–80) Red Right Hand (Arms)**

The name is something of a misnomer. Your hand is black. You can't feel it. You can't use it. It's still there, though it's hard to tell how much of it is salvageable at a glance, because it's just that badly charred.

You lose the usage of a hand. You cannot make seals, hold two-handed weapons, and so on. You take a -8 to Accuracy rolls and a -4 to Defensive rolls. Parrying suffers the penalty to Defensive rolls, not Accuracy rolls, as normal.

Recovering from this is about as painful and drawn-out as you'd expect from losing most of your hand.

### **(81–100) Melted Leg (Legs)**

Your clothing and leg were both burned so horribly that it's hard to tell where one ends and the other begins. Much to your displeasure, you can still feel the limb. Or rather, the mass of agonizing pain where your functional leg used to be. When you move, bits of black flake off. Probably best not to think about whether that's clothing or flesh.

This reduces Agility by 75% for the purposes of movement. It also prevents you from using movement-enhancing jutsu like Shunshin or Racing Thunder Technique. The Doton Mud Wave Technique can still be used normally, as it doesn't rely on actual use of your legs.

# **Slashing (Minor)**

### **(1–20) Blood in Eyes (Head)**

Oh no. You're bleeding from the face. Because fights must be dramatic, the attack cut you in just the right way, maybe right across the eyelid, maybe over the forehead, and now blood is dripping into your eyes and distracting you every few seconds.

You take a **-2 Visibility Penalty**. You can wipe the blood away as a **Speed 3 action**, which removes the penalty for the next **10 IC**.

### **(21–40) Armor Tear (Torso)**

Something sliced across your chest and hacked your armor clean off your body. On top of being a nasty gash that'll scar if you don't get stitches, you're hit with **Bleed 4**, and any armor you're wearing is removed. Clothing stays, obviously. Armor covering a different location, like your head, is untouched.

### **(41–60) Sliced Stomach (Abdomen)**

This is what happens when you can't make your body concave enough. You take a cut right across the belly. Fortunately it isn't deep, just in a bad spot. Your **Stamina and Chakra costs increase by 2** until the end of battle.

### **(61–80) Lacerated (Arm)**

You got a nasty gash running along your arm. It didn't cut anything vital, but blood is seeping down and off your fingertips. All **Seal Speeds are made at +2**, unless you can do them one-handed, in which case you can avoid the penalty by using the unwounded arm. You also take a **-2 to all grapple rolls and TNs**.

### **(81–100) Lacerated (Leg)**

You got a nasty gash running along your leg. Not life-threatening, but it's staining your clothes and oozing down your leg. You take a **-1 Immobilization Penalty** that fades after **20 IC**.

Any time you take a **Move action**, moving as part of an attack is fine but Shunshin is not, the wound reopens. That applies a new -1 Immobilization Penalty with a **cumulative +5 IC duration increase** each time. So 25 IC the first time, 30 IC the second, 35 IC the third, and so on. This can keep happening as long as the wound remains, even after the initial penalty has faded.

## **Slashing (Major)**

### **(1–20) Profuse Bleeding (Head)**

You got a gash on your face. Maybe a slice into your cheek. You're bleeding a lot, which head injuries tend to do. The blood loss isn't the issue. The fact that blood is running over your face and into your mouth is.

As your mouth fills with blood, breathing gets progressively harder and not vomiting gets progressively harder too. You gain a **Suffocation 1** effect every **20 IC**. Whenever you **Rest**, the severity of the Suffocation drops by **1**.

It’s also amazingly easy to get this infected.

### **(21–40) Pulmonary Gash (Chest)**

Something cut through your pectoral muscle and between your ribs to nick your lung. Impressive feat. If they were a real badass they'd have gone for the heart. Be sure to taunt them about that.

You gain a **Bleed effect of 3 × Fatigue Level, minimum 3**. Every time your Fatigue level increases, you gain another **Bleed 3**. If you already have Bleed from this wound, the existing Bleed increases by 3 instead.

It’s a deep but narrow injury.

### **(41–60) Open Cavity (Abdomen)**

Your abdominal cavity, the part of your body that holds all your organs together in a nice safe environment, has been opened. It really wasn't meant to be.

You can **no longer Rest**. Your bonus to Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls from Fatigue drops to **+3 per Fatigue level** instead of +5. The penalty you incur from failing a Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion roll becomes **(action's cost)/4** instead of /5.

### **(61–80) Muscle Tear (Arm)**

Your bicep, tricep, or both have been partially torn, which makes functioning in combat a lot harder. Your arm goes from a dry noodle to a limp one.

Your **STR is reduced by 25%**. That affects everything derived from STR you're hoping it doesn't. Vitality, Damage Bonus, damage dice with taijutsu, all of it.

### **(81–100) Muscle Tear (Leg)**

Your quadriceps have been torn. Not to fear. The tendon is fine and you haven't been hamstrung. You're not going to be put down like an animal, though this should teach you to treat pets better.

Your **AGI is reduced by 20%**. That affects everything derived from AGI you're hoping it doesn't. Dodge, Initiative, max AP, movement speed.

## **Slashing (Severe)**

### **(1–20) Avulsed Ear (Head)**

You have a nasty cut right across your ear. Not only does it look brutal, but there's blood seeping into your inner ear, which does nasty things to your sense of coordination.

If you have the **Enhanced Hearing** ability, you lose all benefits from it, along with anything that depends on it, such as Blind Fighting. With blood pooling in the ear making balance difficult, the **highest level of Taijutsu you can perform is reduced by one**. From A to B, B to C, C to D, and so on.

You're also treated as being **one Fatigue category higher than you actually are** for the purpose of penalties, but not benefits. If you're at Fatigue 1, you take a -2 to Accuracy and -4 to defensive rolls instead of the normal -1/-2. Under normal circumstances, that means you fall unconscious at one Fatigue less than usual.

The blood can be drained easily with medical attention, but not in battle. It'll probably scar either way, less likely with treatment.

### **(21–40) Clavicle Cut (Torso)**

The horizontal bone connecting your sternum to your scapula has been fractured. You can probably feel part of the bone jutting out from the skin if you look down at it.

The worst part is the pain, which can't really be avoided because the clavicle helps the scapula stay in position so the arm can hang properly. You take **-2 to Accuracy and d20 rolls**, plus **Paralysis 4**. Any time you perform an action with a Stamina cost, you take **twice that amount in damage**, which bypasses reductions like DR.

Crash course in anatomy: you have two clavicles. Odd result means right, even means left. With proper medical attention, a trip to a doctor or a Medicine roll of 20+ using a First Aid kit, you can get the arm completely immobilized against your body. That reduces the Paralysis to **2** and halves the damage from Stamina rolls, but means you can't use that arm or hand at all.

### **(41–60) Chopped Liver (Abdomen)**

Your liver has been cut. There's no good side to this one. The liver handles toxins and protein digestion. It's kind of vital for staying alive, and it happens to be bleeding out at a very significant rate.

You gain a **Bleed 10** effect. Whenever you take an action with a Speed greater than the current severity of this Bleed, even if it's dropped to Bleed 0, the Bleed increases by **2**.

This also messes with your body's ability to process chemicals properly. If you're under the effects of a **Soldier Pill or Blood Pill**, they end immediately, including the Fatigue reduction, which can knock you unconscious. Taking either pill with this wound does nothing beneficial. The Akimichi clan's pills still work normally.

### **(61–80) Hand of Benediction (Arm)**

No, the name's not a joke. It's a real term.

Somehow the median nerve around the elbow has been severed, resulting in loss of proper hand function. It doesn't hurt much since a nerve was struck, but your pinky and ring fingers can no longer be felt or manipulated, and your thumb is more or less unopposable.

If you had anything in your hand at the time, odds for right, evens for left, you're automatically disarmed. Same for any two-handed weapon you were holding. You can't wield a weapon in that hand, and any unarmed attacks made with that limb don't receive your damage bonus.

Your **DEX bonus to Accuracy becomes DEX/20**, and **Seal Speeds increase by 50%** before any reductions are applied, such as uniques or DEX/10. You can avoid the Seal Speed increase by performing seals one-handed with the opposite hand, if you're able to do so.

### **(81–100) Hamstrung (Leg)**

One of the major tendons of the leg, located behind the femur, has been cut. You can no longer walk or stand properly. Running is completely out of the question.

You're **automatically Prone**, as if tripped. Your **AGI bonus to Dodge becomes AGI/20**. Even when you get up, your **movement speed is cut in half**, and you can **no longer move as part of an attack**. You can still use jutsu to increase movement, but the total distance is halved.

You no longer benefit from the **Active** ability, and your **AP cap becomes AGI/4**. Any Athletics rolls use only half your total ranks in the skill.

## **Slashing (Critical)**

### **(1–20) Cut Throat (Head)**

The good news: you were not beheaded. The bad news: they didn't fail by much. Your carotid artery was hit, along with your trachea. Blood is flying everywhere, and you're going to suffocate from it if you don't do something smart. Like surrender. Or run.

You start with **Suffocation 2**. Every time you take an action with a Speed greater than 0, its severity increases by 1. The Move action notably does not trigger this. Instead of making a Stamina 15 roll every time your Suffocation increases as you would normally, you make such a roll **every 10 IC**. All other normal Suffocation rules apply.

You also gain a **Bleed status with severity equal to that of your Suffocation**. If special abilities would reduce the effect of Suffocation on you, the Bleed's severity is determined before they're applied.

**First Aid** sets the Suffocation penalty to 0. That doesn't stop it from increasing again. Nothing does, until you recover from the wound. Ouch.

On the bright side, you're a super implausible magic ninja. If you carefully apply pressure to your neck with both hands, you can temporarily suspend both the Suffocation rolls and the Bleed damage. You may take no other actions while doing this, not even Speed 0 ones. This suspension applies after you've been doing it for at least **10 consecutive IC**.

### **(21–40) Injured Spine (Torso)**

In simple terms, your spinal cord relays signals from the body to the brain and back. It also has several neural circuits that control reflexes and instinctual actions ninja train themselves for. Like moving out of the way of the technique that caused this.

Fortunately your reflexes didn't seem that crucial anyway, since you let this happen, so you probably aren't that bad off. That's a lie. Whenever you take an action with a Speed greater than **RES/10**, two things happen.

First, you suffer a **Stun of half its Speed**. Second, you acquire **Paralysis 1**, or increase your existing Paralysis from this wound by **1**. The Paralysis lasts until the end of battle.

### **(41–60) Disemboweled (Abdomen)**

Congratulations. You've got what is potentially the most terrible injury one can receive. Your abdomen is full of squishy organs that, as it turns out, are pretty important for keeping you alive. Now there's a giant cut across your midriff and they're all trying to sag out of your body.

Your **Fatigue penalties are doubled**, -2 to Accuracy and -4 to defensive rolls per level, but the bonuses are not. Any jutsu with an **Upkeep cannot be used**, as it's impossible to maintain the necessary focus.

Until you receive treatment, any **called shots to your abdomen automatically deal their damage to your Hit Points**.

### **(61–80) Avulsed Arm (Arm)**

Something just cut through a good portion of your arm. It's still connected, but it's flopping, you can't really control it, and it's bleeding everywhere. So. That's happening.

The arm is useless. You can't hold weapons, any you're holding drop, make handseals, or use items. You take **Bleed 6**, **-8 to Accuracy**, and **-4 to defensive rolls**. Every time you perform an attack that deals damage, your Bleed from this wound increases by **DB/5, minimum 1**. If it's down to 0, you acquire a brand new one.

### **(81–100) Achilles Tendon Cut (Leg)**

Named after a mythic, nigh-invincible man who supposedly had a single tendon as his weak spot. Turns out that's actually a weak spot for everybody. Yours just got cut. The pain isn't quite as bad as the sight of your foot dragging along unresponsively.

You can't really stand properly. You take **-8 to Dodge rolls**, and **-4 to Accuracy and Athletics**.

The worst part is you just can't move. You can't move as part of an attack or use jutsu that affect movement, like Shunshin. You can still take a Move action, but at that point you're really just rolling and crawling, so the total distance moved is **divided by 4** after all other factors are applied. You can still "move" to get out of the way of Area of Effect attacks, but it suffers the same /4 as regular movement.

# **Piercing (Minor)**

# -=[ Flinch ]=-

Piercing wounds are about deep, focused trauma. Spears, bullets, broken glass bottles and stilettos sting deeper than any gash. It causes involuntary spasms and severely disrupting motor control. This is captured mechanically by the status bFlinch.

When you have Flinch X, before attempting any defensive interrupt action (parry, dodge, etc.), you must succeed on a Stamina roll against a Target Number of 10 + Flinch Severity. On success, the action proceeds normally. On failure, the action is Botched, it automatically fails and any AP spent is lost, so yes you could get punched after failing to activate a defense. You may spend 1 Willpower to remove all Flinch.  

Flinch severity decreases by 1 every 10 IC outside of combat, or every 20 IC in combat. Botching a defense  reduces Flinch by 3 immediately. The roll does not increase your stamina penalty.  

### **(1–30) Needling (Torso)**

You've got a small but fairly deep incision from what struck you. The initial shock of pain was worse than the wound really is, though.

Flinch 1 (TN 11). You take a Stun 3, which increases by 1 for every time you take this wound in the same battle.

### **(31–60) Flesh Wound (Abdomen)**

You've been shanked in the gut. It managed to miss your vitals, but it's still going to cause blood to trickle out of you.

Flinch 2 (TN 12). You take a -1 to Chakra Exhaustion and Stamina rolls.

### **(61–80) Reflexive Flinch (Arm)**

The back of your hand was hit, causing your fingers to spasm momentarily. Any weapons you're holding are dropped, and any seals you're forming are automatically aborted.

Flinch 1 (TN 11). You take a -1 to Accuracy for the next 50 IC.

Repeatedly applications of this wound do not stack the penalty to Accuracy, but instead add 50 IC to its duration (or, if it has expired, re-apply it).

### **(81–100) Muscle Stab (Leg)**

They struck your quadricep, which is a fairly easy target, all things considered.

Flinch 1 (TN 11). If you spent the IC before you received this wound moving, you are inflicted with a Stun 8; whether you were moving or not, though, you have a -1 to Defensive rolls for the next 50 IC.

Repeated applications of this wound do not stack the penalty to defensive rolls, but instead add 50 IC to its duration (or, if it has expired, re-apply it).

## **Piercing (Major)**

Every Major Piercing wound you're suffering from reduces your current and maximum AP by 4. This stacks additively with all other AP reductions, including multiples of the same wounds.

### **(1–30) Punctured Cavity (Torso)**

The body has several cavities, areas where spcific internal organs are house and kept together. One of them has been pierced and now has a hole in it, which is filling up with blood.

It's painful to move, or do basic things like breathe. You have a penalty to Fatigue and d20 rolls equal to the number of Torso wounds you have, including this one, up to a maximum of -4.

Multiple instances of this wound count towards raising the penalty, and all lower your AP normally, but their penalties don't stack--that is, if you had five Punctured Cavities, your total penalty would be -4, not -20.

Flinch 4 (TN 14).

### **(31–60) Organ Hemorrhage (Abdomen)**

Your abdomen happens to have several internal organs all closely packed together. While space-saving, it also happens to mean that if you're hit there, one of them is bound to be injured in some form or fashion, and that's exactly what happened.

What that boils down to is that you're bleeding, a lot. You take a -1 penalty to Accuracy and defensive rolls, and any time you take an action (including defensive ones) with a Speed higher than your current AP, this wound's penalty increases by 1, to a maximum of -4.

Repeated applications of this wound raise the penalties by 1 each, though still not above -4. These penalties last until the wound has healed.

Flinch 4 (TN 14).

### **(61–80) Joint Pierced (Arm)**

One of your arm's joints has been struck and is now filling with blood and becoming inflamed. It makes it much harder to properly manipulate the appendage, on top of the very strange and unnerving sensation you get when you move the arm and can feel the collected blood sloshing around.

The base speed of all your attacks is increased by 2, and the effective speed for determining their damage is decreased by 2. Thus, a basic unarmed attack would be changed from Speed 8 to Speed 10, but deal damage as if it was Speed 6.

The increase and decrease cannot exceed 50% of an action's base speed. For example, after 20 separate applications of this wound your basic unarmed attack would be Speed 12, and deal damage as if it was Speed 4. You may still use AP to reduce these actions to half their new base speed.

Flinch 4 (TN 14).

### **(81–100) Joint Pierced (Leg)**

Though they missed fracturing your knee cap, they still managed to pierce the joint itself, and it's not collecting (and leaking) blood which isn't supposed to be there. Like many wounds, on its own it isn't so bad, but when aggravated, it becomes very problematic.

For every yard you move, including as part of an attack or with the aid of a jutsu, your movement speed afterwards is decreases by 1%, to a maximum of a 50% reduction. Multiple applications of this wound do not increase its maximum reduction; however, if you had two instances of it your speed would be reduced by 2% for every yard you moved thereafter, and so forth.

Finally, this applies a -1 penalty to defensive rolls for every 15% it reduces your movement speed.

Flinch 4 (TN 14).

### **(1–30) Rib Spearing (Torso)**

The object that struck you did so in such a way as to cause your ribs not just to fracture, but to split entirely. Both ends are curved inward, making movement, breathing, and life in general very painful.

You take a -2 to Accuracy, Fatigue, and d20 rolls just for having your ribs broken. ### **(31–60) Cavitation (Abdomen)**

Cavitation is an interesting phenomen typically only created by high velocity projectiles. Ninja being ninja, of course, means that this could happen with even the most inconspicuous of objects.

What Cavitation is, is basically a wave of pressure that forces things--in your case, your internal organs--to shift in location, causing a cavity where there was none. It results in your organs being crushed, missshapen, tearing, and dislocated. This is especially problematic for the abdomen, wherein everything is already densely packed. There is, shall we say, a space issue.

The end result is that you automatically advance a fatigue category, and the category at which you fall unconscious is reduced by one (from six to five, normally, meaning if you fail a fatigue roll at fatigue 4, you fall unconscious).

The only good part about this injury is that once your organs are displaced--well, they're displaced. If you are already suffering from this injury and are unlucky enough to have it happen again, you advance a fatigue category, but the category for unconsciousness is not lowered more than once, regardless of how many times you suffer this wound.

Flinch 6 (TN 16).

### **(61–80) Tricep Tear (Arm)**

They missed your humerus bone, but managed to pierce right through the tendon that connected a portion of your tricep to it. As any medically-savvy ninja knows, your tricep is pretty vital for doing things like punching, and holding objects.

If you're using a weapon, then your damage bonus is halved; unarmed only has its damage bonus reduced by a fourth. You take a -3 to Accuracy, but against disarm attempts, this increases to -5 (whether you try to parry or dodge it).

Flinch 6 (TN 16).

### **(81–100) Hamstrung (Leg)**

Your hamstring, or some similarly important muscle in your leg, got cut badly enough that it's not doing its job any more. You can stand, barely, and you can balance, sort of.

Flinch 6 (TN 16). Track how many yards you move. Every time you travel more than AGI/10 yards cumulatively, you take another Flinch 1.

You can only accomplish 15 IC of movement before having to take a Rest action. Movement as part of an attack counts towards this limit. Any effects which require you to channel chakra through your legs (such as standing on walls or water, or jutsu such as Mizutamari) end, and cannot be used until you've healed.

### **(1–30) Punctured Lung (Torso)**

Okay, so, first off, calm down. It's not as bad as it... okay, yeah, it's as bad as it sounds. The good news is, it's not actually lethal. ### **(31–60) Pierced Liver (Abdomen)**

You're bleeding from the midsection. And, the blood is black. Every ninja knows at least a little about anatomy, first aid, and injuries, and everyone ninja knows what black blood means: you're bleeding from the liver and about to die.

Flinch 8 (TN 18).

### **(61–80) Shoulder Orifice (Arm)**

Someone has created another orifice in your body. As in--if there wasn't blood pouring out from both ends--someone could look right through your shoulder and see what's out the other side. In Pain is an understatement.

On top of that, your arm is pretty much worthless. You're not really able to feel anything that the arm or hand happens to touch, and the only way you get it to do so is by flopping it against something as dead weight.

Flinch 8 (TN 18). You lose the usage of your arm. If you don't have single handed seals, you can not make seals anymore. You have a -6 Accuracy penalty, can't use two-handed weapons, and when you multi-throw the total number of weapons you can throw is halved.

When you're first hit, this inflicts a Bleed 15 which will go away normally.

### **(81–100) Penetrated Artery (Leg)**

Your femoral artery has been hit, and blood is literally spraying out of you into the air. Aside from the general pain involved and the fact that it's completely disgusting, you're losing a potentially fatal amount of blood and if it isn't stopped--no, a hand and some pressure won't stop this--you're going to die.

Flinch 12 (TN 18). You have the effects of Hamstrung.

Your maximum Vitality is reduced by 10%. Every time you take an action, your current and maximum Vitality are reduced by (that action's base Speed)\*2; every time you spend AP, your current and maximum Vitality are reduced by (AP spent).

If you have 0 Vitality remaining, your current HP, maximum HP, and maximum Vitality are all reduced by that amount. This includes move actions, unless you move at only one tenth your normal speed. If your maximum Hit Points are reduced to 0, you die.

When this wound heals, your lost maximum HP and Vitality are restored.
