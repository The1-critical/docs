The culture of **"The Honourable"** was founded by the riff-raff students who were denied entry into more sophisticated Taijutsu dojos. They decided to fight amongst themselves in high-stakes **1v1** or **2v2 (Tag Team)** duels, where the infliction of wounds counts toward victory.

This is an unsanctioned, high-risk duel conducted outside of standard sparring rules, designed to settle disputes, establish social rank and with extreme prejudice subdue rivals when words fail. Unlike normal spars, the Wounds and Fallout inflicted in The Honourable are far more deadly. Challenging someone incurs significant narrative implications and is often used to prove yourself. The consequences are dire, luring only the bravest or the most foolish to step forward when high-stakes conversations turn sour.

## I. Initiation & Structure

This duel ends when one side reaches a pre-agreed condition, such as:

## II. The Point System

Points are accumulated by inflicting lasting damage and maintaining better resource control. The side with the most points when the duel ends is the winner.

[Visit page](https://nw-shotai.mintlify.site/Honourable)

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="A. Wound Points">
    Points are gained based on the cumulative severity of Wounds inflicted on the opponent(s).

    | Wound Severity | Points Awarded |
    | --- | :-: |
    | <Icon icon="bandage" iconType="solid" /> **Minor Wound** | **1 Points** |
    | <Icon icon="droplet" iconType="solid" /> **Major Wound** | **2 Points** |
    | <Icon icon="hand-fist" iconType="solid" /> **Severe Wound** | **3 Points** |
    | <Icon icon="skull" iconType="solid" /> **Critical Wound** | **4 Points** |

    <Tip>
      **2v2 Rule** — Wound Points are the **accumulative total** inflicted on _both_ members of the opposing duo.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="B. Fatigue Differential">
    Points are awarded for maintaining a significantly lower Fatigue level than the opposing side.

    <Note>
      **Formula:** Points = **1 + (Opponent's Fatigue Level − Your Fatigue Level)**
    </Note>

    In a 2v2 duel, the opponent's Fatigue Level used is always the **highest individual Fatigue Level** reached by any member of the opposing duo.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="C. Prone Penalty">
    <Warning>
      If an opponent becomes **Prone** during the duel, their side's current point total is immediately reduced by **−1 Point** as they suffer aura loss.
    </Warning>

    Staying on your feet is also important, trust me on this, the kunoichi don't want to see you on your ass. 
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## III. Rewards & Fallout

The Honourable can only end once there is a winner. The standard time limit is **120 IC**, however forfeiting or being knocked out before then decides the winner immediately.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Winner" icon="crown">
    Receives a **×1.5 XP multiplier** for claiming victory and honour.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Loser" icon="face-thermometer">
    Receives a **×1.25 XP multiplier** for surviving the painful lesson.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  **Killing your opponent has consequences.** The Honourable is a duel of dominance, not execution. Fatal outcomes carry serious narrative fallout. :3
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Quick Reference — Point Cheat Sheet" icon="book-open">
  - **Minor / Major / Severe / Critical Wound** → 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 points
  - **Fatigue Differential** → 2 × (Their Fatigue − Yours)
  - **Prone Opponent** → −1 to their side's total
  - **2v2** → Wounds stack across both opposing members; use their highest Fatigue
</Accordion>
