## Introduction

Chakra is everywhere, an intangible force permeating the world. For the most part, shinobi ignore it. It's like knowing there's oxygen all around: necessary for life, but not particularly worthy of attention.

It does play an important role: shinobi naturally respire chakra from the environment, just as they inhale and exhale air. But because chakra is omnipresent, it never matters.

It's called _natural chakra_. Very rarely, some ninja learn how to access it. The scarcity of this ability has a lot to do with the fact that in most situations, it's impractical: accessing nature chakra requires one to be able to sit calmly and undisturbed for a significant amount of time. Ninja rarely have that opportunity.

In the age of the Confederacy, the old ways of natural chakra are fading. The forests that once hummed with energy have been logged. The mountains have been hollowed. The rivers have been dammed. Those who still seek the path of the Sage are rare, and often viewed with suspicion. The world has moved on. But the chakra of the world has not. It waits, patient as stone, for those willing to be still enough to hear it.

---

## Learning

Learning to access nature chakra is not something one simply does. It takes months of slowly learning to harmonize oneself with the world, gradually learning to deliberately calm the flow of chakra within their own body. Similarly, someone who can use nature chakra does not progress in stages; it's a natural growth, a process of inner learning.

However, that would be horribly difficult to represent with game mechanics, so natural chakra is conveniently broken down into three tiers. They are, in order: **Enlightened**, **Adept**, and **Sage**.

Within each tier, there are several abilities. By each ability there is a listed XP cost, which you must pay to learn it. You can purchase any ability within a tier you have access to, so long as you meet its listed requirements.

## Tiers

### Enlightened

The foundation of the path. You learn to sit still, to quiet your mind, and to simply _be_. These abilities require no combat activation; they are the building blocks upon which everything else rests.

#### Meditation _(5 XP)_

The most basic ability someone seeking to harness natural chakra must master. You sit down, close your eyes, and become perfectly still, clearing your mind. Obviously, this is easier said than done. A novice will find themselves thinking about not thinking, then their nose will start to itch, they'll get fidgety… and that is where most give up in frustration and go back to studying high-power ninjutsu.

When meditating you cannot talk, or even open your eyes to look around, though you do remain aware of your surroundings to the extent of what you can feel and hear. You cannot maintain any Upkeeps greater than 0 while meditating.

In battle, you may declare Meditate as an action. When meditating, you do nothing. Meditate as an action lasts until you declare that you Abort it; you do not need to declare how long you'll be meditating when you begin.

There are two ways to end meditation:

- **End Meditation:** A Speed 5 action which cannot have its Speed reduced.
- **Disruption:** If you take any action other than End Meditation, if you are hit by any damaging attack, or if you are moved in any way. When disrupted, you have a -3 penalty to Accuracy, Fatigue, and all opposed d20 rolls for the next 5 IC. You lose 50% of your accumulated NC (rounded down), retaining the remainder.

#### A Tranquil Body _(10 XP)_

As you meditate your body relaxes and is refreshed, weariness ebbing away. Every 10 IC you spend meditating, your Stamina penalty is reduced by 3.

#### A Tranquil Mind _(10 XP)_

By emptying your mind, you reach a state of total serenity. Your emotions fade away, letting you assess things calmly and rationally. Every 10 IC you spend meditating, your Chakra Exhaustion penalty is reduced by 3.

#### A Tranquil Soul _(10 XP)_

_Requires: A Tranquil Body, A Tranquil Mind_

When your mind and body are in harmony, each completely silent and at ease, your spirit becomes the same. Once you've been meditating for 20 IC, you may sacrifice a point of Willpower to lower your Fatigue level by 1. After doing so, you must wait at least 20 IC before using it again.

#### Path to Tranquility _(5 XP)_

_Requires: A Tranquil Soul_

If you spend at least 100 IC meditating, the first time you fail a Chakra Exhaustion or Stamina roll in the next ten minutes, you advance one less Fatigue category. Your Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion penalties still reset to 0.

#### A Dream of the World _(10 XP)_

_Requires: A Tranquil Soul_

As one sinks into meditation, they become detached from their worldly senses and the accompanying distractions. Only by doing so can they notice the minute variations in the world's own chakra.

While meditating, you can tell how many people are within X yards, where X is the number of IC you've spent meditating, up to a maximum of your CHA. This does not, however, allow you to tell anything else about them—even their direction or distance.

In combat, this allows you to find hiding enemies. Every 5 IC you spend meditating while a hidden enemy is within your range, you may make a Chakra Control roll against their Stealth TN, with a +2 bonus for every 5 IC (total) you've been meditating.

### Adept

The intermediate stage. You have learned to feel the world's energy. Now you learn to carry it within yourself.

#### One With Nature _(10 XP)_

_Requires: Path to Tranquility_

Up until now, you've managed to 'sort of' meditate properly: you're almost sitting still, you're mostly not distracted. Becoming one with nature requires a higher level of discipline. The phenomenon commonly described as being 'one with nature' is where a person is so still, so unresponsive, that the world's ambient chakra can flow unhindered through their chakra coils, as if they were just another tree or current of wind.

As this takes place, nature chakra builds up within the shinobi's chakra system. While there are dangers associated with it, they don't manifest at this level.

Every 2 IC you spend meditating, you get one nature chakra counter (NC). You may have a maximum of up to `(RES + CHA) / 4` nature chakra counters.

If your meditation is broken through any means other than an End Meditation action (or, once you have the ability, activating Sage Mode), you lose 50% of your accumulated NC (rounded down), retaining the remainder.

Once you stop meditating, this chakra is quickly cycled through your system and disposed of. You lose one NC every 5 IC when not meditating.

#### Body of Metal _(15 XP)_

Nature is unyielding; it is powerful, and will crush any who think themselves its better. For every NC you currently have:

- Your damage bonuses are increased by 0.1 with everything but weapons.
- With weapons, your damage bonus is increased by 0.1 for every 2 NC you currently have.

#### Body of Water _(15 XP)_

Nature is adaptable; it changes to meet the needs of its current situation, not because it yields, but because it will always be victorious under any circumstances. After seeing the result of a Chakra Exhaustion or Stamina roll, you may spend 3 NC to increase the result by 1. There is no limit on how many NC you may spend this way on a single roll.

#### Body of Fire _(15 XP)_

Nature is furious; it destroys, it obliterates. It is the raging wildfire, the roar of thunder. You may spend NC to increase your damage bonus for a single attack:

- 0.3 for every NC spent (unarmed)
- 0.1 for each NC spent (weapon attacks)

You may increase your base damage bonus by up to 25% of its base value using Body of Fire.

#### Body of Wood _(15 XP)_

Nature is patient; it grows slowly, knowing that there is no need to rush. When taking any action, you may spend up to 5 NC to improve the result of any Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion rolls it causes you to make. Every NC spent gives you +1 to that roll's result.

#### Body of Earth _(15 XP)_

Nature is gentle; it nourishes, strengthens, and protects. While flowing through your chakra coils it infuses every bit of your body, reinforcing your skin, bones, muscles, and nerves.

Every NC you currently have gives a Damage Reduction (DR) of 2, to a maximum of RES × 2.

#### A Dream of a Dream _(15 XP)_

_Requires: A Dream of the World_

Every person alters the flow of chakra around them in a unique, distinctive way. When you sense someone using A Dream of the World, you can identify their chakra in the future. Having met someone in the past doesn't mean you can immediately identify their chakra.

### Sage

The pinnacle. You have moved beyond understanding. You have become part of the world.

#### Path to Inner Peace _(30 XP)_

_Requires: One With Nature, Hold Back 5_

Before, your meditation was almost childish. What you thought was perfection was in truth a tribute to its own flawed nature. What you thought was a deep understanding of the world was an immature attempt to define something which no words could hope to describe.

You're not sure when you realized how wrong you've been—it was simply apparent, one day, an epiphany. Understanding your flaws, you have not overcome them, but rather accepted them, absorbed them into yourself, and in doing so let them dissolve like shadows in the light.

All meditation effects take place twice as quickly:

- You gain 1 NC every 1 IC spent meditating.
- You may use Tranquil Soul every 10 IC.
- A Dream of the World's radius increases by 2 yards every IC.

Additionally, you can at this point talk to others without breaking your meditation, and the End Meditation action is now Speed 0 for you. Once per battle, you can spend a point of Willpower to add the Interrupt quality to your End Meditation action, allowing you to do it as part of a defensive response, preventing you from taking the normal penalties to having your meditation disrupted as you act (and allowing its benefits to apply to that defense).

#### Sage Mode _(30 XP)_

_Requires: Path to Inner Peace, Basic Training 5_

The pinnacle of senjutsu is, appropriately enough, "Sage Mode." Activating it requires flawless self-control.

Sage Mode requires perfectly balancing sage chakra with one's own "yin" and "yang" chakra, two things that most ninja never even need to concern themselves with. By drawing in enough nature chakra and maintaining it in perfect harmony, a heightened state of superhuman performance can be reached.

You may activate Sage Mode as a Speed 5 action (which may not have its Speed reduced) once you have at least 25 NC stored, and only if you are Meditating. Doing so immediately ends your meditation, and you may not meditate again until Sage Mode has ended.

Sage Mode lasts until you run out of NC. While it is active, you lose 2 NC every 5 IC (instead of 1), beginning the first time you take a non-interrupt action.

While in Sage Mode, your current NC for the purpose of determining benefits from nature chakra's passive effects (such as Body of Earth and Body of Metal) is always considered to be the amount you had when you entered Sage Mode.

**For example:** If you entered Sage Mode with 60 NC, and had spent 40 of them, you would still receive `(60 × 2) = 120` Damage Reduction from Body of Earth until your Sage Mode expired.

#### Awakening: Water _(15 XP)_

While in Sage Mode:

- You have a +1 bonus to Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls for every 10 NC you had when you activated Sage Mode.
- After seeing the result of an attack (whether it hits or not) or a defensive roll (successful or not), you may improve the result by up to 3 by spending NC, at the rate of 5 NC per +1.

#### Awakening: Fire _(15 XP)_

When using Body of Fire in Sage Mode, every 1 NC spent increases your damage bonus by 0.5 (or 0.2 for weapon attacks), to a maximum of 50% of your base damage bonus.

#### Awakening: Earth _(15 XP)_

While in Sage Mode:

- Your damage reduction from Body of Earth cannot be bypassed or reduced by effects which would normally do so.
- When an attack wounds you, you may spend nature chakra counters to lower its severity at the rate of 5 NC per level.

**Example:** For 15 NC, you could negate a Severe wound entirely.

#### Awakening: Air _(15 XP)_

While in Sage Mode:

- You have a +1 bonus to Accuracy and Dodge for every 20 NC you entered Sage Mode with.
- For every 10 NC, you have a +1 bonus to Athletics, Awareness, Chakra Control, and Resistance rolls.

#### Awakening: Void _(30 XP)_

_Requires: A Dream of a Dream_

When in Sage Mode, A Dream of the World and A Dream of a Dream are both automatically active at all times, with a radius of your Sage Mode NC. If you entered with 54 NC, the radius is 54 yards.

As a Speed 5 action you may make a Chakra Control roll to locate all hidden enemies in range, with a +1 bonus for every 10 NC you have. If you've previously located someone this way during the current instance of Sage Mode, this bonus is raised to +1 for every 5 NC you have—it's easier to find them after the first time, until your Sage Mode ends.

## Environmental Resonance

The world is not uniform. Natural chakra flows differently in different places. A Sage who learns to read the land can use this to their advantage—or find themselves dangerously out of balance.

| Environment | NC Gain Modifier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High Resonance** | +1 NC per 1 IC | Ancient forests, sacred mountains, massive waterfalls, deep oceans, chakra-rich battlefields. |
| **Normal Resonance** | Standard | Most natural locations: forests, plains, ordinary mountains, coasts. |
| **Low Resonance** | +1 NC per 3 IC | Dense cities, industrial environments, burned wastelands, sealed underground facilities. |
| **Extreme Resonance** | +2 NC per 1 IC | Sacred locations specifically connected to Natural Chakra. However, excess accumulation becomes dangerous. Maximum safe NC is halved in these locations. |

The GM determines the resonance of a location based on its nature and history. A forest that has been logged is lower resonance than one that has stood for centuries. A battlefield soaked in blood may be high resonance—but unstable.

## Residual Nature Chakra

When your Meditation is disrupted, you lose only 75% of your accumulated NC (rounded up), retaining the remainder.

**Important exceptions:**

- If your Meditation was voluntarily ended with End Meditation, you retain NC normally.
- If Sage Mode ends, normal NC rules apply.
- Certain extreme effects may still force total NC loss.

This represents the fact that Natural Chakra has already entered your system. Disruption stops the harmonious flow, but it doesn't magically erase every molecule of energy from existence.

## Sage Techniques

Sage Techniques are abilities available only while in Sage Mode. They consume NC and represent the active expression of natural chakra mastery.

**General Rules:**

- Each Sage Technique requires Sage Mode and a specific NC cost.
- You may use any Sage Technique you have learned.
- Sage Techniques consume NC, reducing your Sage Mode duration.

---

### Sage Art: Sprit Kata _(20 NC)_

_Requires: Sage Mode_

Nature Chakra extends the user's perception and physical presence beyond the immediate boundaries of their body.

When making an unarmed attack while in Sage Mode, you may spend 20 NC to create a delayed or displaced impact effect. The target cannot benefit from bonuses derived specifically from predicting the precise location of the user's limbs when defending against this attack.

This does not ignore Dodge entirely. It simply removes bonuses from abilities that rely on prediction or reading the user's movements such as Willpower and defensive interrupts that work with dodging. 

---

### Sage Art: Natural Barrier _(15 NC per 10 IC)_

_Requires: Sage Mode_

Create a zone of harmonized natural chakra around you. The zone extends to a radius of CHA/10 yards and lasts for 10 IC.

Allies within the zone gain:

- +2 to defensive rolls.
- Damage Reduction equal to your current NC/10 (minimum 1).
- +2 to Resistance rolls.

While maintaining the barrier, you may not move more than 5 yards per IC and may not make offensive actions. If you move more than 5 yards in an IC, the barrier collapses.

---

---

### Sage Art: Nature Sense _(10 NC)_

_Requires: Sage Mode, A Dream of the World_

As a Speed 5 action, you may spend 10 NC to expand your sensory awareness dramatically. For the next 20 IC, you can sense the precise location of any living creature within CHA × 2 yards, regardless of Stealth or concealment. This does not identify them—only their location.

---

### Sage Art: Sage Recovery _(15 NC)_

_Requires: Sage Mode_

As a Speed 8 action, you may spend 15 NC to immediately reduce the severity of one wound you are suffering from by one category (Critical → Severe → Major → Minor → None). This can only be used once per battle.

---

### Sage Art: Natural Binding _(15 NC)_

_Requires: Sage Mode_

As a Speed 10 action, spend 15 NC to cause natural growth to erupt around a target within CHA yards. Vines, roots, or stone (depending on terrain) restrain the target, inflicting a -6 Immobilization penalty. This lasts until the target succeeds on a Resistance check or deals 200 damage to the restraints.

In High Resonance environments, their roll would be reduced by -5 and the restraints have 250 HP.
