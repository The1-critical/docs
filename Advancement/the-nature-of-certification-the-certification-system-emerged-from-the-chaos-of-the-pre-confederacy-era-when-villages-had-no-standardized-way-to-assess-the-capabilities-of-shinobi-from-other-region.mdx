title: "The Nature of Certification"
---

The certification system emerged from the chaos of the pre-Confederacy era, when villages had no standardized way to assess the capabilities of shinobi from other regions. A warrior from one village might claim mastery of a technique, but without formal documentation, their claims were impossible to verify. The Confederacy's Certification Bureau was established to address this problem, creating a standardized system that allowed villages to assess the capabilities of shinobi from across the Confederacy.

Today, the certification system serves three purposes. First, it provides a formal record of a shinobi's training and capabilities, allowing villages to make informed decisions about assignments and promotions. Second, it creates a standardized curriculum that ensures all shinobi receive a baseline level of training, regardless of their village of origin. Third, it provides a path for advancement for shinobi who may not have the opportunity to earn Field Honours or Norms, allowing them to demonstrate their capabilities through formal examination.

---

## The Certification Hierarchy

Certifications are organized into a hierarchy of five tiers, each representing a progressively higher level of expertise. This tiered structure ensures that shinobi can demonstrate their growing mastery in a consistent and measurable way, while also allowing villages to assess the capabilities of shinobi from other regions.

| Tier | Designation | Description | Typical Recipients |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier I | Foundation | Basic competency in a skill or field | Academy students, Genin |
| Tier II | Proficient | Demonstrated practical application | Genin, Chuunin |
| Tier III | Advanced | Mastery of complex techniques and concepts | Chuunin, Jounin |
| Tier IV | Expert | Recognized authority in a specialized field | Jounin, Special Jounin |
| Tier V | Master | Legendary-level expertise recognized across the Confederacy | Sannin, Kage |

The tiered structure is designed to be progressive. A shinobi cannot earn a Tier II certification without first earning the Tier I certification in the same field. This creates a clear path of advancement and ensures that shinobi have a solid foundation before moving on to more complex material.

## Categories of Certification

Certifications are organized into several broad categories, each representing a different area of shinobi competence. This categorization allows shinobi to specialize in areas that suit their talents and interests, while also ensuring that they receive training in the fundamentals of shinobi practice.

### I. Academy Certification

Academy certifications are the foundation of the shinobi education system. They represent the formal acknowledgment that a shinobi has completed the required training and possesses the fundamental skills necessary to serve their village.

#### Academy Graduation Certification (Tier I)

The most common certification in the shinobi world. This certification is awarded to students who successfully complete the academy's graduation examination, demonstrating proficiency in basic combat, stealth, chakra control, and tactical thinking.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Academy Board
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of academy training curriculum
- **Examination Format:** Written test (theoretical knowledge) and practical demonstration (combat, stealth, chakra control)
- **Notes:** This certification is required for promotion to Genin. Students who fail the examination may retake it after additional training.

#### Academy Honors Certification (Tier II)

Awarded to students who demonstrate exceptional performance during their academy training. This certification is often the first step toward early promotion or special assignments.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Academy Board
- **Prerequisites:** Academy Graduation Certification, top 5% of class ranking
- **Examination Format:** Comprehensive practical examination covering advanced academy curriculum
- **Notes:** Students with this certification are often assigned to elite genin teams or given special training opportunities and are called Prodigies.

---

### II. Combat Proficiency Certifications

Combat proficiency certifications represent a shinobi's demonstrated competence in specific combat skills. These certifications are among the most common and most valued, as they directly reflect a shinobi's ability to survive and succeed in combat situations.

#### Basic Combat Proficiency (Tier I)

The foundational combat certification, demonstrating that a shinobi can effectively use basic combat techniques, including unarmed combat and the use of standard weapons.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Combat Training Division
- **Prerequisites:** Academy Graduation Certification
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of combat techniques against standardized targets
- **Notes:** This certification is required for promotion to Chuunin. Shinobi who fail the examination may retake it after additional training.

#### Weapon Specialization (Tier II)

Certification in a specific weapon type, demonstrating proficiency beyond the basic level.

- **Specializations:** Kenjutsu (sword), Bojutsu (staff), Shurikenjutsu (throwing weapons), Kyujutsu (bow), Kusarigamajutsu (chain weapons), and others
- **Issuing Authority:** Village Combat Training Division
- **Prerequisites:** Basic Combat Proficiency, documented training hours
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of advanced techniques and combat scenarios
- **Notes:** Multiple specializations may be earned. Each requires separate examination and documentation.

#### Master Weapon Specialist (Tier III)

Advanced certification in a specific weapon type, demonstrating mastery of complex techniques and the ability to teach others.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Combat Training Division
- **Prerequisites:** Weapon Specialization, demonstrated teaching experience
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of mastery techniques, teaching demonstration
- **Notes:** Shinobi with this certification are often assigned to train other shinobi in their weapon specialty.

---

### III. Jutsu Certifications

Jutsu certifications represent a shinobi's demonstrated competence in specific categories of techniques. These certifications are essential for shinobi who rely heavily on ninjutsu, genjutsu, or taijutsu.

#### Basic Ninjutsu Proficiency (Tier I)

The foundational ninjutsu certification, demonstrating that a shinobi can effectively perform basic ninjutsu techniques.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Ninjutsu Research Division
- **Prerequisites:** Academy Graduation Certification, Chakra Control 10
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of E-rank and D-rank ninjutsu
- **Notes:** This certification is required for promotion to Chuunin. Shinobi who fail the examination may retake it after additional training.

#### Elemental Certification (Tier II)

Certification in a specific elemental nature, demonstrating proficiency in techniques of that element.

- **Specializations:** Katon (Fire), Suiton (Water), Doton (Earth), Fuuton (Wind), Raiton (Lightning)
- **Issuing Authority:** Village Ninjutsu Research Division
- **Prerequisites:** Basic Ninjutsu Proficiency, Elemental Aptitude
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of C-rank elemental techniques
- **Notes:** Multiple elemental certifications may be earned. Each requires separate examination and documentation.

#### Advanced Elemental Certification (Tier III)

Advanced certification in an elemental nature, demonstrating mastery of complex techniques and the ability to combine elements.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Ninjutsu Research Division
- **Prerequisites:** Elemental Certification, Chakra Control 25
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of B-rank elemental techniques, theoretical examination on elemental interactions
- **Notes:** Shinobi with this certification are often assigned to research or teaching positions.

#### Genjutsu Proficiency (Tier II)

Certification in genjutsu, demonstrating the ability to effectively use illusion techniques.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Genjutsu Research Division
- **Prerequisites:** Genjutsu Training ability
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of D-rank and C-rank genjutsu
- **Notes:** Genjutsu certifications are relatively rare, as genjutsu is notoriously difficult to master.

---

### IV. Specialized Training Certifications

Specialized training certifications represent expertise in specific non-combat fields. These certifications are often required for shinobi seeking to fill specialized roles within their villages.

#### Medical Certification (Tier I)

The foundational medical certification, demonstrating basic knowledge of first aid and field medicine.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Medical Corps
- **Prerequisites:** Medicine 10, Chakra Control 10, Medical Ninjutsu Rank C
- **Examination Format:** Written test (medical knowledge) and practical demonstration (first aid techniques)
- **Notes:** This certification is required for shinobi assigned to medical support roles.

#### Advanced Medical Certification (Tier III)

Advanced medical certification, demonstrating the ability to perform complex medical procedures and use medical ninjutsu.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Medical Corps
- **Prerequisites:** Medical Certification, Medicine 20, Chakra Control 25, Medical Ninjutsu Rank B
- **Examination Format:** Written test (advanced medical knowledge) and practical demonstration (medical ninjutsu)
- **Notes:** Shinobi with this certification are often assigned to hospital or research positions.

#### Sealing Certification (Tier II)

Certification in fuuinjutsu, demonstrating the ability to create and apply basic seals.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Sealing Division
- **Prerequisites:** Seal Training ability, Research 25
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of D-rank and C-rank sealing techniques
- **Notes:** This certification is relatively rare, as fuuinjutsu is a specialized field.

#### Advanced Sealing Certification (Tier IV)

Advanced sealing certification, demonstrating the ability to create complex seals and apply them in combat situations.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Sealing Division
- **Prerequisites:** Sealing Certification, Research 20, Chakra Control 26
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of B-rank sealing techniques, theoretical examination on seal theory
- **Notes:** Shinobi with this certification are often assigned to high-security operations.

#### Interrogation Certification (Tier II)

Certification in interrogation techniques, demonstrating the ability to extract information from unwilling subjects.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Intelligence Division
- **Prerequisites:** Espionage 25
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration (simulated interrogation) and theoretical examination
- **Notes:** This certification is often held by ANBU operatives and intelligence specialists.

---

### V. Leadership Certifications

Leadership certifications represent a shinobi's demonstrated ability to lead and coordinate others. These certifications are essential for shinobi seeking promotion to leadership positions.

#### Squad Command Certification (Tier II)

Certification in basic squad leadership, demonstrating the ability to lead a small team in combat situations.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Command Training Division
- **Prerequisites:** Chuunin rank, documented leadership experience
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration (simulated mission command) and theoretical examination (tactical planning)
- **Notes:** This certification is required for promotion to Jounin.

#### Tactical Planning Certification (Tier III)

Advanced leadership certification, demonstrating the ability to plan and execute complex missions.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Command Training Division
- **Prerequisites:** Chuunin, Squad Command Certification, documented mission planning experience
- **Examination Format:** Submission of a detailed mission plan, practical demonstration of execution and two worst case scenario stress test.
- **Notes:** Shinobi with this certification are often assigned to command positions.

#### Strategic Command Certification (Tier IV)

Expert-level leadership certification, demonstrating the ability to command large forces and plan campaigns.

- **Issuing Authority:** Village Command Training Division
- **Prerequisites:** Tactical Planning Certification, documented command experience
- **Examination Format:** Submission of a detailed campaign plan, oral examination on strategic theory
- **Notes:** This certification is typically held by Jounin, Special Jounin, and above.

---

### VI. Confederacy Certifications

Confederacy certifications are awarded by the Shinobi Confederacy's central authority. These certifications carry significant weight, as they represent recognition by the Confederacy itself.

#### Confederacy Combat Certification (Tier III)

Certification by the Confederacy, demonstrating combat proficiency at a level recognized across the Confederacy.

- **Issuing Authority:** Confederacy Assessment Bureau
- **Prerequisites:** Combat Proficiency Certification from a recognized village
- **Examination Format:** Practical demonstration of combat techniques, sparring with Confederacy assessors
- **Notes:** This certification allows shinobi to serve in Confederacy operations.

#### Confederacy Leadership Certification (Tier IV)

Certification by the Confederacy, demonstrating leadership ability at a level recognized across the Confederacy.

- **Issuing Authority:** Confederacy Assessment Bureau
- **Prerequisites:** Leadership Certification from a recognized village
- **Examination Format:** Submission of a detailed mission plan, oral examination on leadership theory in front of the five kages during the annual summit
- **Notes:** This certification is required for shinobi seeking command positions in Confederacy operations.

#### Legendary Certification (Tier V)

The highest level of certification, awarded to shinobi who have demonstrated extraordinary achievement recognized across the Confederacy.

- **Issuing Authority:** Confederacy Assessment Bureau, with Kage approval
- **Prerequisites:** Recognition by at least two Kage or equivalent authorities
- **Examination Format:** Submission of a detailed portfolio of achievements, review by a panel of Kage or their representatives
- **Notes:** This certification is typically held by Sannin and above. It is not earned through examination but through recognition of exceptional achievement.

## The Lore of Certifications

### The Pre-Confederacy Era

Before the establishment of the Shinobi Confederacy, there was no standardized certification system. Each village maintained its own records, and there was no way to verify a shinobi's claims about their training or capabilities. A shinobi from one village might claim mastery of a technique, but without formal documentation, their claims were impossible to verify.

The problem became acute during the early years of the Confederacy, as villages began to cooperate more closely. Shinobi from different villages were expected to work together, but there was no way to assess their relative capabilities. A jounin from Konoha might have vastly different training and experience than a jounin from Kirigakure, and there was no way to compare their qualifications.

The Confederacy's Certification Bureau was established to address this problem. The Bureau created a standardized system of certifications, with clear requirements and examinations that could be administered by any recognized certifying authority. This system allowed villages to assess the capabilities of shinobi from across the Confederacy, facilitating cooperation and reducing the risk of mission failure.

---

### The Certification Bureau

The Certification Bureau is a department of the Shinobi Confederacy responsible for maintaining the certification system. The Bureau's primary responsibilities include:

- The Bureau maintains a comprehensive database of recognized certifying authorities, certifications, and certified shinobi. This database allows villages to verify the certifications of shinobi from other villages.
- Due to its existence, the Bureau establishes standards for certifications, ensuring that they represent a consistent level of proficiency.
- The Bureau reviews certification requirements regularly and updates them as needed.

The Bureau is headquartered in the Confederacy's central administrative center in the Land of Iron's capital Gingakure, with regional offices in each of the major villages. The Bureau's staff includes representatives from all the major villages, ensuring that the system reflects the needs and values of the entire Confederacy.

---

### The Politics of Certification

Despite the Confederacy's best efforts, the certification system is not immune to politics. Certifications from major villages carry more weight than those from smaller villages, simply because the major villages have more resources and more established training programs. A certification from Konohagakure's Academy is worth more than one from a small frontier dojo, regardless of the actual quality of the training.

The Certification Bureau has attempted to address this issue by creating a process for recognizing independent certifying authorities. However, the process is slow and bureaucratic, and many independent certifying authorities are never recognized.

---

### The Future of Certification

The certification system continues to evolve as the Shinobi Confederacy grows and changes. New certifications are being developed to address emerging needs, and existing certifications are being updated to reflect changes in training and practice.

Some of the emerging trends in certification include:

- **Cross-Training Certifications:** As villages become more integrated, there is growing demand for certifications that demonstrate proficiency in multiple fields. These certifications allow shinobi to serve in multiple roles and to adapt to changing circumstances.
- **Technology Certifications:** The Confederacy's growing reliance on technology has created demand for certifications in areas such as communications, surveillance, and information analysis. These certifications are expected to become more common in the coming years.
- **Counterterrorism Certifications:** The Confederacy's growing intelligence apparatus has created demand for certifications in areas such as interrogation, counter-intelligence, and information analysis. These certifications are expected to become more common in the coming years.
- **Civilian Bodyguard Certifications:** The Confederacy's growing emphasis on civilian protection has created demand for certifications in areas such as disaster response, humanitarian aid, and civilian evacuation. These certifications are expected to become more common in the coming years.
