---
title: "Shadow Clone  "
---

 Perhaps the pinnacle of development for the ninja replication technique stands this most audacious of jutsu. Whereas the basic bunshin creates what are merely illusions, projections of chakra intended to deceive the eye, the Shadow Clone Technique employs chakra to construct a perfect, tangible duplicate of the original practitioner. These copies are, however, notoriously fragile and unstable in nature; a single blow delivered with force and purpose will shatter them like real flesh and blood, causing them to dissipate in a puff of smoke mere moments later.

---

#### –( Kage Bunshin no Jutsu — Shadow Clone Technique )–

- **Cost:** 1 Fatigue level or 1 Willpower per clone created
- **Speed:** 5, plus 2 additional Speed per additional clone
- **Seal Speed:** 5, plus 2 additional Speed per additional clone

**Effects:** Shadow Clones function much like their illusory counterparts, yet with far more substantial consequences for their deployment. So long as they persist in the physical world, you cannot regain any Fatigue or Willpower expended in their creation. The Fatigue level changes incurred by creating or dismissing Kage Bunshin do not reset your Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion penalties, nor do they provide relief from any status effects currently afflicting you, such as Sealed Tenketsu or similar debilitations.

Upon their manifestation, each Shadow Clone inherits all Wounds currently borne by your physical form, though they do not inherit any status effects affecting you. Critically, any Wounds sustained after their creation, and any Upkeeps activated subsequent to their appearance, do not transfer to the clones themselves. Conversely, the clones will copy any Upkeeps you are actively maintaining at the precise moment of their creation—specifically those marked with the 'Preparation' tag—which then operate solely upon the clone in question for so long as it maintains them.

Shadow Clones possess remarkable autonomy and capability. They may perform virtually any action available to you save creating additional clones; they can form handseals, execute jutsu, engage in combat, and pursue independent strategy. However, they are always created at Fatigue 1 (inheriting all associated penalties and bonuses as if newly exhausted), and should they fail a Fatigue roll or sustain any damage whatsoever, they are immediately destroyed and dissipated.

The cost of their destruction is absolute and irrecoverable. When a clone is destroyed, you do not regain the Fatigue level or Willpower spent to create it. Only through deliberate voluntary dismissal can you reclaim expenditures. As a Speed 5 + X × 2 action, you may end 1 + X clones. Clones end this way and vanish in a puff of smoke as soon as they next act (or at the end of the Initiative Count on which they were unsummoned, should they choose not to act). Upon their dispersal through voluntary dismissal, you regain any Fatigue levels spent to create them. Willpower spent to create clones, however, can never be regained under any circumstance.

Although you maintain no mental link with your clones, which act with complete independence from your direct consciousness, when one ceases to exist—whether through destruction or deliberate dismissal—you gain complete memories of all experiences it accumulated during its separation from you. These memories, while entirely legible and comprehensible, retain an ethereal quality akin to viewing a photograph's negatives rather than experiencing genuine recollection. This distinction prevents any confusion between what you have truly lived and what your clones have witnessed.

Yet the Shadow Clone technique harbors a critical vulnerability that has determined the fate of countless battles throughout history. Any Wounds inflicted by a Shadow Clone upon an opponent are automatically reduced by one category in severity. A technique that would ordinarily inflict a Major Wound instead manifests only as a Minor Wound; what would be a Severe Wound becomes merely Major. Over the course of prolonged combat, this reduction may prove decisive, the difference between a victory secured and an opportunity squandered.

In the crucible of combat, Shadow Clones function as extensions of your will, though at considerable cost in Action Points. For each action they undertake, they expend 1 AP per 4 Speed of the technique or maneuver desired. Basic Dodges and Parries stand as the sole exceptions to this expenditure; these defensive measures require no Action Points, though the clone's Initiative Counter still advances normally.

Consider an engagement wherein you have created a Shadow Clone. On Initiative Count 80, you command it to execute a Punch, a Speed 8 action costing 2 AP, preventing it from acting again until IC 88. Should an opponent attack your clone on IC 85, you could order a Dodge action for no AP cost, after which you cannot issue another non-Interrupt order until IC 90. Were you instead to command a more sophisticated technique such as Mizuhane Dodge, a Speed 5 action, this would cost 2 AP and delay the clone's next action until IC 93.

The clones do not possess their own Willpower reserves, but you may expend your own Willpower on their behalf, allowing them to accomplish feats otherwise impossible through determination alone.

When Shadow Clones are brought into being, they manifest with precise duplicates of all your equipment, with carefully defined exceptions:

- **Consumable Items:** Flash bombs, smoke bombs, poisons, explosive tags, and all similar expendable materials do not transfer to clones. Anything which, once used, cannot be reused remains in your sole possession.
- **Chakra-Infused Items:** Storage scrolls and similarly specialized chakra-imbued implements are physically replicated in the clone's possession; however, these replications lack all supernatural properties and function as mundane facsimiles only.
- **Electronic and Complex Mechanisms:** Cameras, communication devices, and any items possessing intricate moving parts do not function within the clone's grasp. They exist as inert duplicates only.

## **–( Multiple Shadow Clone Assault )–**

A technique developed by shinobi who refused to accept the limit of three clones as a ceiling. By pouring chakra into the shadow clone technique beyond its normal parameters and using the momentum of a committed attack to anchor the multiplication, the user creates a brief window where the usual restrictions on clone quantity simply... stop mattering. For a single, decisive moment, the battlefield fills with copies, each one real, each one dangerous, each one a drain on the user's very life force.

Type: General Ninjutsu, B-Rank<br />Chakra: 20 + (X × 5)<br />Speed: +10<br />Seal Speed: +14<br />Tags: Link, Clone

Requires: Kage Bunshin no Jutsu, at least 1 active Shadow Clone

**Effects**

This technique is used as a Link added to any damaging CQC Taijutsu or Weapon attack you perform. When activated, your active Shadow Clones (up to a maximum of 3 that you already have active) are joined by an additional X Shadow Clones that erupt into existence mid-motion, appearing as your attack begins to land. The total number of clones participating in the assault is (Active Clones + X).

X has a maximum value of your Current Fatigue Level (not your maximum, your _current_ Fatigue). Using this technique immediately advances your Fatigue by one category (after the attack resolves), regardless of success or failure.

For every clone participating (active + X), the attack gains:

- +1 Accuracy (against the primary target only)
- +1 damage dice (of the same type as the base attack)

Additionally, each participating clone forces the target to defend against an additional strike, these are not independent attacks requiring separate defensive rolls, but rather a single overwhelming assault that taxes the target's ability to respond. The target's defensive roll takes a penalty equal to the number of participating clones (max penalty -6).

If the attack misses:

- You are left with no active clones, all participating clones (including your original active ones) are destroyed in the failed assault
- You suffer Stun 15
- Your Chakra Exhaustion penalty increases by X (as the wasted chakra destabilizes your flow)

If the attack is defended against successfully (the target beats your Accuracy):

- Same consequences as a miss (no clones, Stun 15, CE penalty)

If the attack hits but is partially defended (Partial Success applies):

- You lose half your participating clones (rounded down, minimum 1), but your original active clones are _not_ necessarily destroyed, only those that were "expended" in the failed part of the assault
- Your Fatigue still advances

If the attack hits fully:

- All participating clones remain active and may continue to act normally in subsequent ICs
