    - **2d20 effects** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> on a TN you set, anything that would let you roll 2d20 and keep the higher, or reroll (such as Lucky), adds 1d6. Anything that would make you keep the lower, or force a reroll, subtracts 1d10.

## REV-024 — Pip's Lament {#rev-024}

_October 28, 2026_

<Note>
  This pass splits physical damage into a CQC Damage Bonus and a Weapon Damage Bonus, and reworks CQC Taijutsu from E- to B-Rank. Opposed rolls now use a fixed TN for the attacker. Flinch is gone in favour of Wayward's Needling, Rank Benefits is one page, and a run of abilities, uniques and jutsu are tidied to match. The character sheet also gains a Sharingan Eye Unlock page. See the [exact changes](/rev-024-changes) for every page, word by word.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Combat">
    - **Opposed Rolls** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> When one character acts against another, the attacker doesn't roll. Their TN is 10 + modifiers, or 11 + with Specialization, or 13 + with Skill Mastery. The defender rolls 1d20 and wins ties. d20 bonuses and penalties apply to TNs, and opposed Initiative rolls still roll on both sides.
      - Status rolls, Genjutsu, Poison, Grapple checks, Touch seals and Tag Concealment all use the new TNs, along with every technique, Unique, summon and piece of gear that called for an opposed roll.
      - **Killing Intent** (the Unique), **Solar Flare** and **Flash Blade** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> still roll, as exceptions.
      - **Counter Combo** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> you roll against the comboing target's CQC Accuracy TN, with +1 per CC they have above you.
    - **Attacking** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> you now declare every TN an attack sets, including ones that only apply on hit.
    - **Needling** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge> replaces Flinch on Piercing wounds, as in Wayward. It lowers your current and maximum AP: Needling 2 for Minor, 4 for Major, 8 for Severe and 16 for Critical. The extra flat AP loss for each severity is gone, and the Minor Torso wound is now called **Silhouette**.
    - **Buried** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge> the old rated Buried status is removed, and **Grounded** is renamed Buried.
    - **Prone** and **Airborne** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> you can be both at once.
    - **Draw Weapon** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge> the note that held weapons stop you using CQC Taijutsu.
    - **AP** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> your maximum is AGI/2, not DEX/2.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Damage Bonuses">
    - **CQC Damage Bonus** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> replaces Physical Damage Bonus everywhere: CQC DB = STR/15, for unarmed attacks.
    - **Weapon Damage Bonus** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> WDB = DEX/15, for weapon attacks. The Two-Handed bonus (33%) now sits under WDB.
    - **Physical Damage Bonus** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> is now only shorthand for "both CQC DB and WDB". It isn't a stat of its own.
    - Techniques and effects that touch weapons now use WDB, and anything that raises or lowers both uses PDB: Centipede poison, Chakra Bulk, Domu, Soushuujin, Paper Shuriken, mounted melee, Chakra Injection, Chodan Bakugeki and the Monkey Prince.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CQC Taijutsu">
    - **E- to B-Rank** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge> with a new introduction and Core Rules: Grapple-Finisher, Weapon Grapples, knockback while grappling, and Ignite spreading between grapplers. CQC Taijutsu can't be used while wielding a weapon unless a technique says so.
    - **Gouken — Iron Fists** and **Shishi Rendan — Lion Combo** <Badge color="green">New</Badge>
    - **Dai Dageki**, **Gawa-Ryuu**, **Mouretsu Tora no Jutsu** and **Shishi Danmaku** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
    - **Hangeki Rendan — Counter Combo** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> moves from C- to B-Rank.
    - **Feint** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> gives the target -2 to their defensive roll, instead of giving you +2 Accuracy.
    - **Jutsu Rules** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge> the "two free hands" and "Unarmed Attacks" rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Abilities">
    - **Weapon Focus** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge> into **Peak Effort** and moved to Vigor. It gives +1 Accuracy and +1 CQC DB per rank on CQC attacks, limit (STR − 30)/30, max 3.
    - **Sudden Attack** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> the CQC version is removed, and the Weapon version is now just called Sudden Attack.
    - **Homing** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> costs 2 per +1 Accuracy and is capped by the jutsu's rank.
    - **Monkey Grip** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> one-handed use loses the two-handed damage bonus.
    - **Principle of Motion** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> requires only 100 AGI.
    - **Specialization** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> raises that skill's TNs to 11 +.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uniques">
    - **Weapon Master** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
    - **Large** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> adds 2 + RES/25 extra dice to Weapon attacks only. CQC and Weapon attacks both keep +1 Range.
    - **Seal Master** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> reduces Seal Speeds by 2 + CHA/8, and every 3 points of excess reduction lowers Speed by 1.
    - **Martial Prowess** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> +3 on all Counter Combo rolls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rank Benefits">
    - **Rank Benefits** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> it's now one page, at a new address.
    - **Traveler rank benefits** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
    - **Mentor** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
    - **Skill Mastery** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> raises that skill's TNs to 13 +.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clans">
    - **Swordsman Corps** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge>
      - **Precision** lowers your WDB divisor by 1 per rank when using a sword, to DEX/12. This is your Sword Damage Modifier (SDM). Limit (DEX − 20)/20, max 3.
      - **Blademaster** loses its Sword Damage Bonus clause.
      - **Clan Swords** features use SDM.
    - **Uchiha** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> the Sharingan's -6 Visibility limit is checked after Blind Fighting.
    - **Hyuuga** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge>
      - **Sealed Tenketsu** no longer costs HP when you fail a Chakra Exhaustion roll.
      - **Byakugan** lets you use CHA/10 in place of STR/10 for CQC Accuracy while active.
      - **Jyuuken**, its conversions, **Hakke Rokujuyon Shou**, **Hakke Hyaku Nijuuhachi Shou** and **Juukenpo Ichigekishin** use CQC DB calculated with CHA in place of STR, instead of NDB.
    - **Juugo Suto** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Tenacity and Desert Ghosts use RES instead of DEX.
    - **Weapon Focus references** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge> from Teshi Sendan, the Kaguya bone weapon, Clan Swords and Paper Shuriken.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Jutsu">
    - **Chakra Scalpel** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> unarmed attacks use AGI/12 for their damage bonus.
    - **Touch range** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> jutsu with Range: Touch use CQC Accuracy.
    - **Jouro Senbon** and **Shinkuu Enzan** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> Weapon Focus lines removed or replaced with Sudden Attack.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Character Sheet">
    - **Sharingan Eye Unlock** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> page added to the character sheet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## REV-023 — Agile Development {#rev-023}

_October 22, 2026_

<Note>
  This pass reworks the Inuzuka clan from a new draft and moves four abilities back to AGI. It also tightens the Fatigue rules so Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion work the same way, and clarifies a handful of uniques, abilities and jutsu. See the [exact changes](/rev-023-changes) for every page, word by word.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Clans">
    - **Inuzuka** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge> with new flavour text, ninken rules and techniques.
      - **Ninken** has its own Initiative, uses half your CQC Damage Bonus and 125% of your movement, and dodges AoEs aimed at you. Hits stun it instead of wounding it. Its basic attack is (AGI/6)d6, can turn into a Grab, and shreds DR by AGI/5.
      - **Ninken bonuses** every 400 XP: Good Nose, Sneaky, Hardy, Energetic, Tracker Hound (each up to 5 times) and Vicious.
      - **Shikyaku no Jutsu** no longer blocks Ninjutsu, but adds 3 Seal Speed. It shreds DR by AGI/5.
      - **Tsuga**: your ninken can use it for 5 AP during Jujin Bunshin. Combos after it gain +1% damage per 2 yards travelled.
      - **Jujin Bunshin** is now Ninjutsu. Your ninken copies your CQC techniques for (Speed/3) AP; the target defends once but pays costs again.
      - **Gatsuga** and **Garouga** no longer need Combo Counter 3 or 5. X equals your Combo Counter, and one defense roll covers every attack.
      - **Sotoro** is now Ninjutsu and bans weapons. Riders double their Seal Speed.
      - **Garouga** leaves the target Prone, Airborne or Grappled.
    - **Fūma** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> moved up to second on the Western Province page, with the other Major clans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Abilities">
    - **Reflexes**, **Rush**, **Active** and **Surge** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> use AGI again instead of DEX, as in Wayward.
      - **Reflexes** limit is AGI /10, max 20.
      - **Rush** gives (AGI × ranks) /20 AP, limit AGI /20, max 5.
      - **Active** limit is AGI /20.
      - **Surge** requires 80 AGI.
    - **Doctor** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> only needs Medical Training for rank 3, which also lets you Tend to yourself with an hour of RP with another person. A requisitioned First Aid kit can't be replaced and counts as used that day. Admitting patients to your Compound's Hospital is now covered only under Hospital on the Slice of Life page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uniques">
    - **Chakra Sensor** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Scan now covers everyone within your CHA-yard sensing area, rather than everyone present. Beating a Scan no longer makes a target immune to your later Scans.
    - **Boundless Chakra** and **Endless Energy** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> can't lower a penalty below 1.
    - **Juggernaut** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> now says it changes your CQC DB, instead of your Physical Damage Bonus.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Jutsu">
    - **Weapon Taijutsu**
      - **Kousanami — Cross Wave** <Badge color="blue">Clarified</Badge> any non-rolled defense, not just Block, stops its damage dice tripling.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Combat">
    - **Stamina** and **Chakra Exhaustion** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> now read the same. Each roll adds a penalty of at least 1, and a cost of 5 or more can't be reduced below 5. Chakra costs didn't have this floor before.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## REV-022 — Wayward Returns {#rev-022}

_October 15, 2026_   <Badge color="orange">Major</Badge>

<Note>
  This pass returns most of the clans and jutsu chapters to their Wayward versions, keeping Shotai's flavour text. Ninjutsu uses CHA and NDB, weapons use DEX and WDB, and CQC uses STR. Recovery is reworked: Hitpoints, Vitality, Fatigue and Willpower reset at the start of every scene or event, wounds heal on a flat timer counted in OOC days, and timers that used to be measured in weeks now use OOC days. Broken links are fixed and many pages have readable addresses, with redirects from the old ones. See the [exact changes](/rev-022-changes) for every page, word by word.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Links & Pages (Fixed)">
    - Fixed some broken links.
    - Gave readable page addresses to Combat, Abilities, 1 UP, 2 UP, Eastern Province, Fuuton, CQC Taijutsu, Field Equipment, Ranged Weapons, Vehicles, Summoning Contracts, Wounds and Slice of Life. Old links redirect automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clans (Reverted)">
    - Yurigami, Senju, Juugo Suto (Sand Fox) and Swordsman Corps reverted to wayward version.
    - **Swordsman Corps**
      - **Kensei no Kizuna — Sword Saint's Bond** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
    - **Tsuchiya Kuchi** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge> for now.
    - **Inuzuka** <Badge color="yellow">Reworked</Badge> now a Major clan, using the Asunder version converted to CQC terms. Descriptions are unchanged.
      - **Ninken** moves at 125% of your movement, attacks with (STR/6)d6 or (AGI/6)d6, can use your grapple techniques, and can Dodge, Parry and Block.
      - **Tag team** perks every 400 XP, at +3 (Bloodhound +4), capped at XP/1000.
      - **Shikyaku no Jutsu** gives +25% AGI for movement, Slashing CQC attacks with +25% wounding, your ninken's Tag team bonuses and stealth detection. No Ninjutsu or weapons while active.
      - **Tsuga** is now (STR/10)d10, with up to AGI/5 extra yards and +1% damage per 2 yards travelled.
      - **Jujin Bunshin** takes your ninken out of initiative to attack alongside you. Its hits keep your Combo going.
      - **Gatsuga** needs Combo Counter 3: you and your ninken alternate X attacks, X up to your Combo Counter.
      - **Sotoro** gives +25% STR and AGI and lets an ally ride you.
      - **Garouga** moved to A-Rank. It needs Combo Counter 5 and makes X attacks, X up to your Combo Counter.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Jutsu (Reverted)">
    - Katon, Suiton, Fuuton, Doton, Raiton, Medical Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, Sealing Ninjutsu and Weapon Taijutsu reverted to wayward version. Technique descriptions are unchanged.
    - **Katon**
      - **Enmaku no Jutsu** moved back to E-Rank.
      - **Yoen no Jutsu** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> no longer reduces wound recovery.
    - **Suiton**
      - **Gloves of Cohesion** now has its own description.
      - **Water Release: Rainwater Slick** and **Water Release: Water Dragon Whip** are kept.
      - **Suisaishuu no Jutsu — Water-Gathering Technique** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
      - **Mizu Souzou no Jutsu** moved back to E-Rank, **Takitsubo no Jutsu** to C-Rank, **Uramiame** to B-Rank and **Hijutsu: Kirisame** to A-Rank.
    - **Fuuton**
      - **Fuuton: Fuuma Shuriken no Jutsu — Wind Shuriken** is kept. <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Re-using it while the upkeep is kept no longer breaks stealth.
      - **Senkousai: Kaze no Ryū — Gale Dragon** reworked into **Senkousai: Kaze no Ryū — Gust Dragon** and moved to C-Rank. It is now a Link and Interrupt: linked to a Katon, it leaves a dust cloud that blinds those inside and boosts Katon damage and Ignite. As an Interrupt against a melee CQC attack, the attacker must resist or be blinded, then either call off the attack or press on and hit you automatically.
    - **Doton**
      - **Jishin Shushou — Earth Shaking Palm** and **Kaigeki Chite — Crushing Earth Hand** are kept.
      - **Chigeshi — Earth Poppy** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> D-Rank technique restored.
      - **Doryu Ken — Earth Style Fist** moved back to D-Rank.
    - **Raiton**
      - **Kuwabara — Mulberry Field**, **Raisen — Lightning Flash** and **Jinraisen — Thunderclap Arrow** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> restored.
      - **Kaminari no Yaiba — Lightning Blade** is a melee weapon Link again.
      - **Raikyuu — Lightning Ball** moved back to E-Rank.
    - **Medical Ninjutsu**
      - **Mystical Palm Technique** is a healing-over-time VSA again.
    - **Genjutsu**
      - The per-technique "Partial success (fail by ≤5)" effects have been removed.
    - **Weapon Taijutsu**
      - Weapon techniques now use WDB instead of PDB, and DEX instead of STR for range and number of damage dice.
      - **Tetsu no Henji — Iron Reply** and **Namari Toresa — Lead Tracer** are kept.
      - **Kurenai no Akatsuki — Crimson Dawn** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
      - **Kousanami — Cross Wave** moved back to D-Rank, **Tentai-ite no Jutsu — Celestial Archer Technique** to C-Rank.
    - **CQC Taijutsu** <Badge color="orange">Reworked</Badge> The whole chapter is replaced with the new CQC rework.
      - New core rules: Combo Counter capped at 5, Finishers usable at any Combo Counter, and new grapple rules (halved movement, a Speed 5 interrupt to stop the defender moving, Shift Grapple and Break Grapple).
      - **Escape Grapple** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> E-Rank technique, replacing the basic action.
      - **Compression Hold** and **Suplex** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> C-Rank techniques.
      - **Rendan — Combo** moved to E-Rank.
      - **Headbutt** moved to D-Rank.
      - **Flash Step** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> D-Rank link that lets you move further as part of a melee attack.
      - **Tokken — Thrusting Shoulder**, **Daisenpuu — Great Whirlwind**, **Shishi Rendan — Lion Combo**, **Bunka-ken — Splintering Fist** and **Hasu Rendan — Lotus Combo** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
      - Most other techniques have new tags, Combo Bonuses or effects. See the exact changes for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uniques (Reverted)">
    - Unique mechanics reverted to wayward version. Descriptions are unchanged, and Specialist is kept as is.
    - **Berserker:** counter-attack Accuracy back to +3.
    - **Juggernaut:** movement penalty back to 10%, and it applies to travel jutsu and knockback. It no longer lowers Runner.
    - **Strong-Willed:** breaking a Genjutsu with Willpower is no longer limited to once per battle.
    - **Defender:** the damage attackers take is no longer unpreventable.
    - **Small:** your Dodge can always be used, even if its Speed is raised.
    - **Genius:** the Scrap crafting line is removed.
    - **Close Ties:** you can spend your Willpower for an ally even when they spend it in a way you couldn't.
    - **Martial Prowess** and **Bruiser:** CQC Grapple techniques get the extra Stun again.
    - **Seal Master:** reduces Seal Speed by 2 + DEX/8 instead of DEX/10, and doesn't apply to Links.
    - **Boundless Chakra:** each Chakra Exhaustion penalty from a jutsu is reduced by 1.
    - **Endless Energy:** each Stamina penalty increase is 1 smaller.
    - **Elementalist:** the +1 status severity is doubled when applied with a status roll.
    - **Fan of Knives:** the special Parry needs Small Thrown weapons again.
    - **Regenerative Chakra:** once per battle, a failed Chakra Exhaustion roll advances one less Fatigue level.
    - **Surging Power:** the Fatigue reduction covers offensive and skill rolls again.
    - **Elemental Mastery:** Shaping options, costs and limits reverted, including +1 Ninjutsu Accuracy, +1 Damage and rank-scaled status effects. The damage-type option is removed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Abilities (Removed)">
    - **Leadfoot** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge> — vehicles no longer require it to purchase or operate in combat or hazardous conditions.
    - **Ace** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
    - **Rapid Reload** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Combat & Status Effects (Changed)">
    - **Paralysis** reverted to wayward version: it lowers the Speed your attacks use for damage, to a minimum of half their final Speed. It no longer increases the Speed of your actions.
    - **Shock** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> restored as its own status: it increases the Speed of your actions (half severity for Interrupts) while damage uses the original Speed.
    - **Airborne** and **Grounded** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> statuses added.
    - **Escape Grapple** <Badge color="red">Removed</Badge> as a basic action.
    - The old combo rules on the Jutsu Rules page are removed; it now points to the CQC Core Rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Equipment (Changed)">
    - All weapons now use DEX instead of STR for their range and number of damage dice. This covers thrown weapons, Fuuma and Large Star Shuriken, the Daikyu and the Yumi, and the Entrenching Tool when wielded as a weapon.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recovery (Reworked)">
    - Hitpoints, Vitality, Fatigue and Willpower now reset at the start of every scene or event. Downtime can no longer be spent on recovery.
    - Mechanical timers measured in weeks (summon cooldowns, technique durations, Infamy and quarry timers, poison recovery and similar) now use OOC days, 1 week to 1 OOC day.
    - **Wounds**
      - **Wound Recovery** <Badge color="green">New</Badge> Wounds heal on a flat timer: Minor at the end of the battle, Major in 4 days, Severe in 8 and Critical in 16. Each real-world day counts as 1 day of healing, 2 if you roleplay for an hour in a Hospital, or 4 with a Doctor. Critical wounds only heal in a Hospital or with a Doctor. Downtime can no longer be spent on wounds.
      - Individual wounds no longer have their own recovery times or Downtime costs. Their penalties last until the wound heals.
      - **Kizu Saishuu no Jutsu** and **Rinji Hougou no Jutsu** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> updated to the new healing rules.
    - **Doctor and Dwellings**
      - **Doctor** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Roleplaying with a Doctor counts as 4 days of wound recovery. They can treat patients equal to their ranks at once, doubled in a Hospital.
      - **Hospital** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Roleplaying in a Hospital counts as 2 days of wound recovery.
      - **Bathhouse** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Occupants now start each scene or event with 1 Well Rested point per rank. Spend any number after a Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion roll to add +1 each. No longer boosts Vitality, Hitpoint or Fatigue recovery.
      - **Shrine** <Badge color="yellow">Changed</Badge> Roll 2d20 and take the highest on 1/1/2/2/3 out of combat rolls per event at Ranks 1–5, decided before rolling. Lucky rolls an extra 1d20 instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

_October 9, 2026_   <Badge color="orange">Major</Badge>

<Note>
  This pass walks several elemental chapters back toward their older versions.   You will notice some techniques have Radius. Numbers are written "from → to" against the most recently logged version. "Old effect" means the effect text went back to the earlier version of that technique. Ambiguous "Accuracy" and "Taijutsu" references were clarified as Ninjutsu, Weapon or CQC per the REV-019 terminology pass, and existing modern tags are kept unless stated.
</Note>

  <Accordion title="Maestro (Reworked)">
    <Warning>
      The original version was inconsistent, so this is a rework rather than a patch. It has not been playtested. Treat the numbers as provisional.
    </Warning>

    **Puppet Fundamentals** <Badge color="purple">Reworked</Badge>

    - Puppets now use **your attributes and uniques for all rolls**. Uniques that modify your Accuracy, defensive rolls, damage bonuses or skill rolls apply to puppet actions. The puppet is described as a conduit, not an independent combatant.
    - A puppet's own RES and AGI now matter for two things only: Durability (RES × 6) and movement speed (AGI/40). The caps (RES cannot exceed your DEX, AGI cannot exceed your CHA) stay. The "all puppets begin with RES 30 and AGI 30" line is no longer in the text.
    - The old "NDB instead of PDB, DEX for skill rolls, CHA in place of STR" rules are replaced. Damage bonus is now always your **NDB**, whatever the attack type.
    - **Weapon** attacks use your Weapon Accuracy and defend with your Weapon Parry −2. **CQC** attacks use your CQC Accuracy and defend with your CQC Parry −2. **Ninjutsu** actions use your Ninjutsu Accuracy and cannot use Ninjutsu Parry.
    - Puppets cannot form handseals. Any jutsu they perform must come from a source that removes Seal Speed entirely.
    - **Resistance** rolls are now added to the list of rolls a puppet cannot make (Medicine, Research and Chakra Control were already there).
    - **Immunities changed.** New: Medical Jutsu, Poison and being Disarmed. Corrosion and Frostbite are no longer immune (the exceptions are now Immobilization, Ignite, Corrosion and Frostbite, was Immobilization and Ignite).
    - A puppet at Immobilization −8 or greater cannot act (was −6).
    - **Slam:** Speed 8 unchanged. Damage is now a flat 3d6 Blunt using your PDB or WDB (was (RES/10)d6, listed with Ap 8).

    - Chakra went from 16 to 15 and Upkeep from 3 to 4.

    - It is now Finisher-only. You pick X up to CC/2, and you and your ninken alternate strikes, you first, for X × 2 attacks.
    - The dual-Accuracy system stays (your rolled Accuracy against your static Taijutsu TN, with the higher one as the effective Accuracy). It is now all-or-nothing: the target rolls defense once, and every attack either hits or misses.

    - You can't spend AP to reduce its Speed.
    - I removed the distance-based Accuracy and damage rules, the consecutive-Tsuga bonuses, the extra damage rolls per two points of margin (up to 5), and the "no Sudden Attack" line.

    **Ninken (core)**

    - The "some have wolves" aside was cut.
    - The reason for the shared advancement changed. It used to be "only as good as its master," so bonuses came from your XP. Now the two of you do all your training together.
    - The XP-scaled skills were replaced (Awareness 5 + XP/300, Stealth 5 + XP/400, Survival 5 + XP/200). The ninken now has your stats in everything except AP, WP, PDB, Vitality and HP.
    - You share one AP/WP pool, and the ninken has 50% of your Vitality, PDB and HP, with its own wound thresholds.
    - The Pack Tactics section and its every-20th-IC action rule were dropped.
    - The Basic Attack went from Speed 10 to Speed 8 and from (STR/6)d6 to (STR/6+2)d6. The half-PDB rule, the 10 DR Shred and the full-PDB bonus for attacking from Stealth were removed.

    - The rules that it can't be targeted or caught in an AoE, and that a lone ninken acts on its own Initiative with −6 Dodge and dies to one hit, were deleted.
    - Ninken automatically pass stamina checks but gain fatigue when you do.
    - They no longer track a vitality pool. Wounds are calculated from Max Vitality (50% of yours) and converted to stuns: Minor 10 IC, Major 30, Severe 50, Critical knocked out for the rest of combat.
    - The old "AoE counts as one tier higher" rule is gone.

    **Tag Team (out of combat)**

    - The ninken mirrors your ranks in Athletics, Awareness, Resistance, Stealth and Survival, up from three formula-based skills. It rolls alongside you whenever you roll one of them.
    - I added a lore paragraph on selective breeding and how the Inuzuka's chakra raises the ninken's gifts over its lifetime.

    **Ninken Abilities**

    - Swift (+3 Dodge alone), Vicious (d8/d10 Basic Attack) and Well-Trained (+1 to three skills) were removed.
    - Good Nose became Bloodhound and went from +3 Survival and +1 Awareness to +2 Survival.
    - Sneaky went from +3 Stealth to +2 Stealth, and it still gives +1 Survival.
    - Vigilant (+2 Awareness, +1 Survival), Tough (+2 Resistance, +1 Survival) and Playful (+2 Athletics, +1 Survival) are new.
    - Each ability can be taken multiple times, up to a shared cap of 1+(XP/1000). Before, each had its own limit (5, 3, and so on). The 1+ also means your first pick works at 500 XP.
