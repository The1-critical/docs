**Architect**

Not so much the ability to perform construction labor exceptionally well (Though, you’re probably great at that too), your skillset lies in the breadth of your knowledge in the many fields of understanding that need to come together in order for a complicated system like a livable infrastructure to be constructed. Structural design, material sciences, heating, ventilation, insulation, electronics, procurement logistics, waste disposal, fire prevention. A less than competent understanding in any of these fields (and in honesty, significantly more) is not something you consider even slightly acceptable.<br /><br />And, more importantly, you actually have the money to put that knowledge to use.

When constructing Compound Buildings, you may treat all skill requirements as if they’re research, instead of whatever they might have otherwise been. In addition to this, you may construct Compound Buildings to 1 rank higher than you actually have the skills to qualify for, and your compound starts with 3 building slots, instead of 2. Your Compound also gains 15 development per day, instead of the usual 5.

You retain all benefits from Full Pockets

_Upgrades from: Full Pockets_

**Avenger**

Someone did something that you cannot let go of. Whether it was done to you or someone close to you, the shape it left behind has organized your fighting life around a single long-term objective.

Choose a group, up to the size of a hidden village. You gain +2 to CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy and d20 rolls against them. If the group is clan-sized: +3. Against ten or fewer specific individuals grouped under one category: +5. Against a single person: +5 CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy and d20 rolls, plus an additional +1 per 1,000 XP they have above you. You may enter UP debt to take this unique with GM approval.

You also receive the full benefits of Killing Intent, the rage has taken its toll, and it shows.

_Upgrades From: Any 1-point unique_

**Boundless Chakra**

What would exhaust most shinobi leaves you merely inconvenienced. Your chakra replenishes with a consistency that has, on occasion, made people reconsider their tactical assumptions mid-fight.

You gain +3 to Chakra Exhaustion rolls. When you calculate the Chakra Exhaustion penalty from a jutsu, treat the jutsu's Chakra cost as 4 lower (minimum 5). When you Rest, your Chakra Exhaustion penalty drops by one more than your Stamina penalty does. The Upkeep of all your active jutsu is reduced by 1.

_Upgrades From: Expansive Chakra_ <br />_Upgrades Into: Regenerative Chakra or Surging Power_

**Bruiser**

The phrase "hits harder than they look" was coined for someone like you. It isn't about size. It's about knowing exactly how to put everything you have into a single point of contact.

Your Physical Damage Bonus increases by RES/30. You gain DR equal to STR/5. All damage-dealing CQC attacks gain Stun 2, or have their existing Stun increased by 2. CQC Grapple techniques do not benefit this Stun

_Upgrades From: Martial Prowess_ <br />_Upgrades Into: Relentless_<br />

**Cunning**

Other people's certainty is an open door to you. You understand the architecture of belief, the particular way people expect things to go, and how to build an illusion inside that expectation. There is something fundamentally unsettling about how easily you do this.

The combined Chakra Control and Research requirement for learning Genjutsu does not apply to you. You gain +2 to all Genjutsu rolls. A target doesn't gain their repeat-use defensive bonus until the third time you use the same Genjutsu against them in a battle rather than the second. You may reduce the Upkeep of one Genjutsu at a time by CHA/25, to a floor of 0, and may switch which technique receives this benefit any time you activate a Genjutsu with an Upkeep.

**Elementalist**

What most shinobi manage in one element, you manage in two. and with a refinement that draws sidelong glances from people who've been working at it for years longer than you have.

You may use two elements of your choice as though you had Elemental Aptitude for them, without consuming Aptitude ranks. Status effects from the five base elements in your Elemental Ninjutsu have their severity increased by 1

_Upgrades From: Elementally Gifted_ <br />_Upgrades Into: Elemental Mastery_

**Endless Energy**

You do not get tired. This is genuinely baffling to watch. Your body processes exertion differently, and the margin before it starts affecting your performance is several multiples of what it is for ordinary people. You've never quite understood the feel.

When you calculate the Stamina penalty from a technique, treat the technique's Stamina cost as 2 lower (minimum 5). The first time you fail a Stamina roll in any battle, you advance one fewer Fatigue category. You gain a bonus to Stamina rolls equal to RES/40. Every 15 IC, your Stamina penalty decreases by 1 on its own.

_Upgrades From: Indefatigable_<br />_Upgrades Into: Surging Power_<br /><br />

**Exceptionally Fast**

You do not simply move quickly. You have internalized speed to the degree that your actions look wrong to people watching, like frames are missing between where you were and where you are.

The base Speed of all your actions is reduced by 10%, rounded up to the nearest whole number. A Speed 11 action is reduced by 2, becoming Speed 9. This cannot reduce any action to Speed 0. Whenever you partially succeed on a Dodge, you take none of the attack's secondary effects, no status effects, penalties, or wounds from the partial. Your movement speed is increased by 20%.

_Upgrades From: Blur_

**Fan of Knives**

Most people use thrown weapons as an afterthought. You have built an entire combat philosophy around them, and it shows in the distance you can reliably cover, the way you read incoming attacks, and the things you do with your hands while other people are still drawing their weapons.

Your Ranged Weapon attacks gain DEX/20 additional Range and +1% damage per yard between you and your target, to a maximum of 15 + DEX/10%. When you hit with a Ranged Weapon attack, you are treated as having hit by 1 more than you did for purposes of Partial Success and hit-margin modifiers. During a Multi-Throw, you may throw DEX/40 additional weapons without contributing to the action’s Speed or cost.

You may perform a special Parry by combining it with a Basic Weapon Attack or Multi-Throw as an Interrupt. Add Weapon Parry’s Speed to the attack as a link. The combined action’s final Speed must be equal to or less than the incoming attack’s final Speed (not half, as usual). If the Weapon Parry succeeds, the attacker must defend against your attack using your Weapon Parry roll as its Weapon Accuracy. You must use at least as many weapons as the attacker is, all of the same type. Accuracy bonuses that reduce costs do not apply to the Parry roll.

_Upgrades From: Quick Draw_ <br />_Upgrades Into: Relentless_<br />

**Insidious**

Your plans have plans, and your contingencies have contingencies, and at no point in the process have you told anyone what any of them are. The smallest incidental damage is part of a sequence that only becomes legible at the very end.

Doses of poison cost 75 Ryo for you regardless of type. You do not need to commit to a Vector or Special Effect when purchasing poison. as a Speed 10 action, you may decide those elements for any single dose on your person. As a separate Speed 10 action, you may expose anyone struck directly by one of your slashing or piercing weapons within the past 10 IC to a dose of Injury Poison from your person, as though the poison only now took effect. Your current and maximum Toxicology ranks are each increased by 3.

_Upgrades From: Full Pockets_ <br />_Upgrades Into: Tactician_<br />

**Jutsu Master**

You have so many techniques catalogued and so clear a sense of how each one works that retrieval is essentially instant. Cross-referencing them in the middle of a fight is not a problem. Confusing them with each other is not a problem.

Forgetting the precise angle of a hand position under pressure is not a problem. The XP cost to learn jutsu is reduced by 25%, rounded up. Multiple XP reductions apply multiplicatively, with the final cost rounded up. This reduction is retroactive. Your maximum Research rank is increased by 3. All jutsu gain +1 CQC, Ninjutsu, or Weapon Accuracy. or +1 to the offense roll for Genjutsu. This bonus is doubled for any jutsu with an inherent Accuracy penalty. All jutsu deal +5% damage.

_Upgrades From: Seal Master_<br /><br />

**Protector**

Your allies are your first priority and you would like this to be clearly understood by anyone considering the tactical calculus of attacking them in your presence.

You add your RES an additional time to both Vitality and HP. You have a secondary Vitality pool equal to half your normal maximum. this pool cannot be wounded and is consumed only when you Take the Hit for someone else.

Your Take the Hit action gains +5 and its base Speed is reduced by 3. When you successfully Take the Hit, you may automatically Block without additional cost. The action automatically succeeds if any of the following are true: you and your allies are outnumbered; your side's combined XP is below 75% of the enemy's; you are protecting someone with no remaining Vitality; you are protecting someone of lower rank; you are protecting someone with 75% or less of your total XP.

_Upgrades From: Close Ties, Restrained_<br />

**Single-Handed Seals**

You did not learn this because it was easy. You learned it because you needed it, and you practiced it until the discomfort of forming seals with one hand became indistinguishable from forming them with two.

Your Seal Speed reduction is increased by an additional Espionage ranks/3. Your current and maximum Espionage ranks are each increased by 3. You may still choose to form seals with both hands, in which case your Perform Handseals action becomes immune to interruption and forced abortion.

_Upgrades From: Seal Master, Quick Draw_

**Skirmisher**

You do not fight in straight lines. You hit from somewhere unexpected, create distance before they've processed what happened, and repeat until there's nothing left to repeat on. It is methodical and, to the people on the receiving end, extremely unpleasant.

While attacking from Stealth, your damage bonus is increased by DEX/30. This increases to DEX/20 on a Sneak Attack. Your maximum Stealth rank is increased by 3. You may always take the Hide action regardless of normal requirements, and the penalty for repeated hiding is delayed by one (the first penalty applies on the third Hide, not the second).

_Upgrades From: Lightfoot_

**Tenacious**

You are not the most talented person in the room. You have been aware of this for some time. What you have instead is a specific refusal to let that be the end of the conversation.

Whenever your Fatigue advances from a failed Chakra Exhaustion or Stamina roll, you gain 1 temporary Willpower, which is lost the next time your Fatigue advances if unspent. If you spend this temporary Willpower to reroll a roll, you may choose to reroll the reroll, but if you do, you must keep the third result regardless of whether it's better or worse. Your maximum Resistance rank is increased by 3.

_Upgrades From: Hidden Potential_
