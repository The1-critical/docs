**Elemental Mastery**

You have peerless control over elemental chakra, far beyond the realm of what's considered normal, and to a level that some would even think is impossible. Choose two elements. You can use them as if you had taken the Elemental Aptitude ability for them. Your ranks in Elemental Affinity apply to all elements you can use. This doesn't mean your personal chakra nature corresponds to all five elements, only that you're equally skilled with them all. Each rank of Elemental Affinity gives you a +0.5 bonus to your Ninjutsu

Additionally, you have an Elemental Shaping pool which dictates how much you can modify any given basic Elemental Ninjutsu (advanced elements can not be shaped), with one point in it for each element you have Aptitude for, and one point for each rank of each basic element you know a jutsu of (for a potential maximum of 30). You can use your Elemental Shaping pool to modify any Elemental Ninjutsu you perform in the following ways (which doesn’t ‘use up’ your pool; its size simply determines how much you can modify any given technique):

- 15 points: +1 Target (for non-AoE jutsu)
- 10 points: Change the damage type (Blunt, Energy, Piercing, or Slashing) to another damage type.
- 8 points: +1 Damage Bonus
- 6 points: Add an effect from another element not its own: Stun 1 (Doton), Burns 1 (Katon), Paralysis 1 for 10 IC (Raiton), -1 Chakra Cost (Suiton), Knockback 1 yard (Fuuton)
- 3 points: +1 Area
- 2 points: +1 Range

There are some exceptions to the above: For jutsu which launch projectiles at targets (but not are not AoE, such as Houenka), additional targets gives a single extra projectile. Area and Range increases cannot add more than half the technique’s original Area or Range, and Range cannot modify a technique whose range is Touch. Similarly, any technique which does not list Area or Range cannot have them added to it to gain those qualities. The status effects added from Shaping (aside from Suiton’s, which is automatic) do not require a status roll, but do require you to hit with a damage-dealing ninjutsu attack, and accordingly do not apply to environmental effects (such as Hidden Mist, or Yomi Numa) though they can be added to techniques which already have them, so long as they’re a valid choice. In the case of Raiton’s Shock status, every 5 points past the first five can increase the Shock by one, or increase its duration by 10 IC.

You can pick any of the options multiple times, limited only by the size of your Shaping pool. Shaping does not ‘combine elements’ in any sort of way (overt or otherwise), but rather modifies one element based off of properties of another: A Suiton that burns might be described as pressurized to tear away at the skin on contact, while a Raiton that knocks back a target might actually seem to crackle through the air and strike from below to lift someone off their feet as it hits. Just take care not to describe a knockback Suiton as Hyouton, for example, or a Fuuton that burns as Scorch release: advanced elements are a true combination of two different chakra nature types, while Shaping is modifying the base element in the style of another. 

_Upgrades From: Elementalist_

**Regenerative Chakra**

There is a point where chakra recovery stops being impressive and starts being unsettling. You passed that point. Other shinobi watch your penalty readout recover mid-fight and quietly revise their threat assessment.

You gain +5 to Chakra Exhaustion rolls. Every 10 IC, your CE penalty decreases by 1 automatically. The total Upkeep of all active jutsu is reduced by 1 + CHA/30. Once per battle, you may fail a Chakra Exhaustion roll without advancing a Fatigue category, though your penalty does not reset. As your Fatigue climbs, your body compensates: your NDB increases by your current Fatigue level.

You have Chakra Points each battle equal to your maximum Willpower. You may spend these after seeing a Chakra Exhaustion roll result to add your current Fatigue level + 1 to the roll (Fatigue 0 adds +1, Fatigue 1 adds +2, etc.) per point spent. Alternatively, Chakra Points may be spent in place of the standard cost for creating a Kage Bunshin, voluntarily unsummoning those clones does not return the spent points.

_Upgrades From: Boundless Chakra_

**Relentless**

You do not stop hitting someone until the fight is over. This is not philosophy. It is physiology. You have trained yourself to reward forward pressure and punish hesitation, in yourself and in your opponent.

Every time you land a damaging attack, not including auto-hit Grapple jutsu, your CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon damage bonuses increase by +2 for your next attack. This bonus accumulates up to half their normal value, not including situational modifiers. Missing resets it.

Every time you miss with a damaging attack, your next action resolves as though the Speed of your missed action was half its actual value, effectively accelerating your IC return. Each consecutive miss also grants +1 cumulative CQC, Ninjutsu, or Weapon Accuracy on your next attack, up to +5. Landing any damaging hit resets this bonus.

**Super Novice**

You have yet to become particularly good at anything specific. This used to bother you more than it does now. You've developed a theory that your ceiling might actually be higher than anyone else's, you're just not there yet. The evidence is, admittedly, thin.

You gain a 10% XP bonus. Each time you are wounded during a battle, you gain 1 temporary Willpower. This lasts until you are next wounded, if unspent by then, it is replaced, not stacked.

_Upgrades From: Any 1- or 2-point unique except Elementally Gifted or Elementalist_

**Surging Power**

You have a reserve that other people do not. When everything says you should stop, something else says you don’t have to. This has saved your life more than once and cost you recovery time you’ll never fully account for.

When you spend a Willpower point, you halve your current Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion penalties. Alternatively, you may spend Willpower to completely clear your penalties to either Stamina or Chakra Exhaustion (your choice). You may also spend Willpower to ignore the full effects of one wound for the rest of the battle.

Your penalties to CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy, and defensive rolls from Fatigue are reduced by your maximum Willpower. At Fatigue 3 with 3 maximum Willpower, your CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy penalty drops to zero and your defensive penalty drops by 3.

_Upgrades From: Boundless Chakra, Endless Energy_<br />

**Tactician**

You do not fight by feel. You fight by construction, novelty as a weapon, predictability as a trap, the battlefield as a problem to be solved while everyone else is still reacting. Your raw output may not be the highest in the room, but your effective output usually is.

The first time you use any specific action in a given battle, that action gains +2 to its relevant Accuracy or +2 to its relevant d20 roll. Landing a hit that receives this bonus immediately grants you that action’s Speed/2 in AP, capped at your maximum.

By spending 10 AP, you may replace any action’s Accuracy roll (or convert it) to a 2d20kh1 roll, taking the higher of two dice, in place of a standard 1d20. As a Speed 0 action, you may exchange your initiative position with a willing ally, allowing them to act immediately while your next action takes their former slot. This costs 2 AP per IC of difference between positions, and cannot be used on a Stunned ally. Spending 1 Willpower reduces this cost to 1 AP per IC for one use.
