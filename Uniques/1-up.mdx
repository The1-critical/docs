### **1-Point Uniques**

<span id="alert"></span>**Alert**

Most people who aren't looking at you don't know you're there. You know they're there. The gap between your awareness of the world and most people's awareness of you is something you've found professionally useful.

Your maximum Awareness rank is increased by 2. You gain +3 to Espionage rolls. Your ranks in Blind Fighting and Keen Senses are treated as 1 higher than they actually are, even above normal maximums. The Scale of Enhanced Hearing and Enhanced Sight is reduced by 1. The Search action costs Speed 3 for you.<br />

<span id="blur"></span>**Blur**

You move in a way that makes it genuinely difficult to track where you'll be next. Not just fast. discontinuous. Like there's a slight lag between deciding where you're going and being there.

Your movement speed is increased by 10%. Shunshin has no Chakra Exhaustion cost, though its Speed remains. Any time you successfully Dodge, you may immediately move as though you'd taken a Speed 4 Move action.

_Upgrades Into: Exceptionally Fast_

<span id="close-ties"></span>**Close Ties**

Your allies are not colleagues or assets. They are people you care about, and that caring translates into something tangible. Your presence steadies people.

Your allies gain +3 to Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls. A single ally can only benefit from one instance of this at a time. For every ally present in battle alongside you, you gain +1 to your own Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls, up to +3. Additionally, anytime an ally of yours spends a WP, you can choose to spend one of your WP in their place (which can be done even when an Ally spends a point of Willpower in a way that you yourself could not).

_Upgrades Into: Avenger, Protector_<br /><br />

<span id="elementally-gifted"></span>**Elementally Gifted**

Elemental chakra manipulation that takes most students years of grinding work came to you early and without the usual struggle. Whether that means you've coasted or pushed further because of it is a matter of character.

You may use one element of your choice as though you had the Elemental Aptitude ability for it — without consuming an Aptitude rank. You may apply the Elemental Affinity ability's benefits to one additional element of your choice.

_Upgrades Into: Elementalist_

<span id="expansive-chakra"></span>**Expansive Chakra**

Your chakra flows with an ease that makes other ninja mildly resentful. Techniques that drain most people don't register as effort to you until they're stacked on top of several others.

You gain a bonus to Chakra Exhaustion rolls when using any jutsu equal to 1 per rank, +1 for E, +2 for D, up to +5 for A-rank. Your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus is increased by half that value (0.5 for E, 1 for D, and so on). For Action Chains, the bonus applies once using the base action's rank.

_Upgrades Into: Boundless Chakra_

<br /><span id="full-pockets"></span>**Full Pockets**

Through inheritance, enterprise, or methods you’d rather not detail, you have access to significantly more material resources than the average shinobi. Tools, traps, the right bribe at the right moment,you are prepared for contingencies others can’t afford to anticipate.

When you spend XP on Savings or Requisition, you receive double the standard amount per rank. Espionage rolls to bribe someone gain +5. You may spend Requisition spontaneously, even mid-mission, as a Speed 5 action in combat, representing that you happened to have exactly what you needed.

_Upgrades Into: Insidious_

<span id="hidden-potential"></span>**Hidden Potential**

Nobody, including you, has fully accounted for what you're capable of. This either bothers you or it drives you, both are valid responses.

You gain +1 Willpower. Your Willpower is calculated as (STR + RES + CHA + DEX + AGI)/200 rather than the standard formula.

_Upgrades Into: Any 2- or 3-point unique_<br />

<span id="indefatigable"></span>**Indefatigable**

_(adj.) Persisting tirelessly._

You are the definition. Stamina rolls that would flag other people simply don't register the same way for you. Your baseline of endurance sits meaningfully above the norm.

You gain a bonus to Stamina rolls of 1, plus 1 per rank of the technique being used  +2 for E-rank, +3 for D-rank, and so on. For basic attacks, this is a flat +2. When you Rest, your Stamina penalty decreases by one additional point.

_Upgrades Into: Endless Energy_

<span id="killing-intent"></span>**Killing Intent**

You have what it takes to end someone. Not as a matter of willingness. as a matter of capacity. Something in your chakra communicates that, and people who get hit by you feel it.

Each time you wound someone, both you and they roll 1d20 + XP/100. Your roll gains +1 for each previous wounding attack you've landed in this battle. If your result exceeds theirs, they are Stunned for a number of IC equal to the difference, to a maximum of Stun 15.<br />

<span id="lightfoot"></span>**Lightfoot**

You understand disappearance as a practical skill rather than a dramatic gesture. Finding a shadow and becoming part of it, turning your silhouette into someone else's problem.  It's reflex for you.

You gain +3 to Stealth rolls. The Speed of your Hide action is reduced by 3. While in Stealth 2, you may move without reducing your Stealth level.

_Upgrades Into: Skirmisher_

<span id="martial-prowess"></span>**Martial Prowess**

You are a living weapon, the sheer state of your being revolves around quickly overwhelming your opponent(s) with different combinations.  Now you can serve a three piece, with the soda. 

Your Combos are not broken when you switch to a new target. You gain +3 on all Counter Combo opposed rolls. All your Combo jutsu have "Stun 1" added to their Combo Counter line. Stuns with a severity below (your current Combo Counter + 2) do not break your Combo.

_Upgrades Into: Bruiser_<br />

<span id="quick-draw"></span>**Quick Draw**

You have good hands. Fast hands, precise hands. the kind that treat reaching for a weapon as a seamless part of an attack rather than preparation for one.

You may throw up to DEX/8 weapons in a single multi-throw. The Speed of Ranged Taijutsu attacks is reduced by 2. Drawing weapons requires no action. Sheathing weapons is a Speed 0 action.

_Upgrades Into: Fan of Knives, Single-Handed Seals_

<br /><br /><br /><br /><br />

<span id="restrained"></span>**Restrained**

You know exactly how much force you're applying. This is rarer than it sounds. Most people discover how hard they can hit; you know precisely how hard you're choosing to.

You gain all five ranks of Holding Back for free. While using the ability, your damage bonus is not reduced (this does not extend to your clones). You may take up to −2 Accuracy or Damage Bonus on a Held Back attack to gain up to +2 Accuracy and/or Damage Bonus on your next non-Held-Back use of the same attack. These bonuses do not stack. Any time you inflict a wound, you may reduce its severity by one category. but doing so does not prevent it from breaking any active Genjutsu.

When your attack would be lethal, you may pull back at the final moment. This leaves your target at 0 HP (or stops them from falling further below), raises all wounds inflicted by one category, and prevents the kill. This cannot be combined with the normal wound-reduction activation.

_Upgrades Into: Avenger, Protector_

<br />

<span id="seal-master"></span>**Seal Master**

You spent a frankly embarrassing portion of your childhood making handseals at nothing in particular. The payoff is that you now form them faster than almost everyone you fight alongside or against.

You reduce all Seal Speeds by (2+DEX/8) instead of (DEX/10), and for every 2 Seal Speed reduction you have over what is needed to reduce a jutsu's Seal Speed to 0, you can choose to lower that technique's Speed by 1 (as if you'd spent AP to do so). This benefit does not apply to Links (or to base actions without a Seal Speed that then gain one from a Link).

_Upgrades Into: Jutsu Master, Single-Handed Seals_

<br />

<span id="weight-trainer"></span>**Weight Trainer**

You have been fighting every day in weighted clothes and, occasionally, asking yourself whether this is worth it. The answer arrives once per significant fight.

Once per two in-character months. eight days out-of-character. you may remove the weights as a Speed 10 action and add 18 to every attribute for the rest of the battle. Your current and maximum Vitality and HP do not change, but all other derived statistics update immediately. Your hard conditioning increases your maximum Athletics ranks by 2.
