### **Creation Uniques**

_Cost: 1 UP each. Maximum of two. Creation only._

**Analyst**

You catalog the world with uncomfortable precision, not just enemies, but their habits, their tells, the exact angle they favor on the third attack of a sequence. Combat is, to you, all a dataset.

You gain +2 to Awareness rolls and earn 2 AP whenever you Rest. When any attacker uses the same category of technique against you consecutively, CQC into CQC, Katon into Katon, Weapon Taijutsu into Weapon Taijutsu, you gain 2 AP and +2 to your defensive roll against their next attack. This bonus does not stack but persists until they switch categories. It is tracked independently per attacker.

You begin with 5 ranks in Research and gain one additional rank per 200 XP.

The cost of this attentiveness is that it can paralyze you at the wrong moment. You suffer a −4 penalty to Athletics rolls.<br />

**Berserker**

You are difficult to be polite about. Reckless, furious, and constitutionally resistant to the kind of careful tactical thinking that keeps ninja alive, you compensate by being significantly harder to put down than most people expect.

You add RES × 2 to your Vitality. When you choose to take no defensive action against an attack that does not incapacitate you and is not itself an Interrupt, you may immediately counter with any CQC or Weapon technique you know as an Interrupt. This counter gains +2 to it’s CQC or Weapon Accuracy, is resolved at half its normal Speed, and cannot be reduced further. It must target whoever attacked you, and its base Speed cannot exceed the base Speed of the incoming attack.

For every 25% of your maximum Vitality you are missing, your CQC and Weapon damage increases by 5%, to a ceiling of +20% at zero Vitality. When you hit zero Vitality, you must immediately decide whether to yield or to commit to the fight until everyone else is incapacitated, even if they later try to surrender.

Your situational awareness suffers for it. You take a −5 penalty to all Stealth rolls.

**Chakra Sensor**

You experience the world as a field of subtle presences. Other people read expressions and footprints. You feel the shape and weight of what's alive around you.

Your sensing radius is equal to your CHA in yards. Within this range, you may substitute Chakra Control for any Awareness or Espionage roll, reading the fluctuations of nearby chakra instead of using conventional perception. Any successful detection tells you: the general character of someone's chakra, their Elemental Affinity, whether they possess a Kekkei Genkai (though not which one), and their current Fatigue level. A detected signature can be recognized again in future encounters.

Against targets in Stealth, your Chakra Control roll is made against their Stealth TN without the bonus granted by their Stealth level. Successfully detecting a hidden target reveals their position and distinguishes them from their clones, excluding Kage Bunshin.

Your maximum Chakra Control ranks are increased by 3. You may use the following action:

**Scan (Speed 8):** Make an opposed Chakra Control roll against all present targets. Each target's roll is penalized by their total current Upkeep. Success detects them as described above. Out of combat, a target who beats your Scan automatically passes all subsequent Scans against you within the same scene or hour.

The constant sensitivity to ambient chakra makes it harder to block out the unpleasant kinds. You take a −4 penalty to Resistance rolls.<br />

**Competitive**

There is no version of any contest where second place satisfies you. This applies to sparring matches, footraces, and arguments about whose bento is better. In a fight it translates into something genuinely dangerous.

You earn 3 bonus XP for every spar or genuine fight you participate in. Events do not count. All your attacks deal 5% additional damage, and you gain +1 to all d20 rolls.

Each miss builds frustration. After missing an attack, you gain a cumulative −1 penalty to all d20 rolls, but also +1 CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy and +3% damage, capped at −5, +5, and +15% after five consecutive misses. Landing a hit resets everything. <br /><br />

**Defender**

You have conditioned yourself to stand in the way. Not dodge, not evade, stand there and make the attack stop. Your forearm tendons are dense from deliberate abuse, your reaction time is built around interception, and your bones have the impact record to prove it.

You gain DR equal to RES/4. You may use Block as a Speed 4 Interrupt. Whenever you are struck by a damage-dealing unarmed attack, your attacker takes RES/2 damage, unprevented by any means, and incapable of wounding. Hitting you costs something.

All damage you take is treated as 10% lower for determining wounds. When you Block, this increases to 25%.

The same caution that protects you bleeds into your offense. All damage you deal is treated as 10% lower for determining what wounds it causes.

**Energetic**

You do not appear to get tired. Eventually you do, but by that point most people are already on the ground. Whether this is physiology or psychology you couldn't say and frankly haven't thought about.

You have 6 Fatigue levels rather than the standard 5. Fatigue penalties are calculated as though you were one category lower than you actually are, while retaining all other effects of your current Fatigue.

The same restless energy that keeps you moving makes it difficult to stay still long enough to really learn anything. Your maximum rank in all Skills is reduced by 2.

**Genius**

Not intelligence, rather pure aptitude. You grasp the underlying structure of techniques that take others years to internalize, and have an instinct for applying understanding before it's fully formed. Depending on your temperament this has made you either ambitious or insufferable.

The XP cost to learn jutsu is reduced by 25%, rounded up. You may learn jutsu of one rank above what your current qualifications would normally allow, though this does not bypass ability or talent prerequisites such as Medical Training or Genjutsu Awareness.

You also ignore the Chuunin and Jounin rank requirements for crafting items from Scrap.

Techniques you could not legally learn without this unique have their Chakra and Stamina costs increased by 3.

**Juggernaut**

You are a solid, hulking brute. You aren't necessarily tall, but you condition yourself hard and have always had a natural gift at receiving pain, thick, solid forearm muscles and tendons, and strong shins. You are a rough opponent to face and an even harder one to stop.

You use STR/12 rather than STR/15 to calculate your Physical Damage Bonus. You gain DR equal to STR/4, and reduce the severity of any Stun you receive by STR/20.

All your movement is reduced by 20%, and your Runner ability is considered 1 rank lower.

<br />

**Large**

You take up more space than most people. Whether that’s height, breadth, or just a general density of mass is between you and your tailor. The result is the same: you hit harder, take more hits, and have reach that makes opponents uncomfortable.

All CQC and Weapon attacks, including basic attacks, gain STR/25 additional dice, to a ceiling of double their normal dice, and gain +1 Range. You gain a +3 bonus to Grapple rolls when using your PDB. Your maximum Vitality and HP each increase by your RES. You gain +2 to Stamina rolls.

All attacks benefiting from this unique have their base Speed increased by 1.

_Cannot be taken alongside Small._

**Lucky**

Things break your way with a frequency that’s either a sign of extraordinary fortune or some very efficient passive scheming. Either way, the results speak for themselves.

On any non-combat roll, you roll twice and keep the higher result. Additionally, 2 + EXP/750 times per day, you may reroll any roll, allow a willing ally to reroll any roll, or, at the cost of two of your daily uses, force an enemy to reroll.

When you have no uses remaining, you take a −2 penalty to CQC, Ninjutsu, and Weapon Accuracy and all d20 rolls until they replenish.<br />

**Naturalist**

Nature has always made more sense to you than people. Animals treat you differently. You read terrain the way other ninja read faces, and your chakra carries something of that primal current in it.

Your maximum Survival ranks are increased by 3. You gain +3 to detect, disarm, or avoid any trap. Your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus uses CHA/12 rather than CHA/15. You gain a bonus to Chakra Control rolls equal to the rank of the jutsu you're using (+1 for E, +2 for D, and so on). You may use Survival ranks in place of Research to satisfy prerequisites for Elemental Ninjutsu.

Your maximum Research rank is reduced by 3.

**Nimble**

Speed is useful. Knowing what to do with it is better. You have both, which makes you very difficult to land anything meaningful on.

You gain +1 to Accuracy and all defensive rolls. The base Speed of all non-Variable, non-Interrupt actions you take is reduced by 1, to a minimum of 3.

Being caught off-guard has consequences. All damage you take is considered 10% higher for determining wounds.

<br />

**Small**

You are compact, quick to vanish into a crowd or a shadow, and flexible enough to fit through gaps that stop most people. What you lack in presence you compensate for in the difficulty of targeting you.

You gain +2 to Dodge rolls. Your Dodge interrupt's Speed is changed to 1 and cannot be raised above that by any effect. Area-of-effect dodge penalties for being unable to reach the edge of the area are halved and capped at −10 for you.

Your frame isn't built for punishment. You apply your RES one fewer time when calculating Vitality.

_Cannot be taken alongside Large._<br /><br />

**Skilled Shinobi**

You were never the strongest in your year, or the most naturally gifted. What you have is range, an ease with the broad skillset of what it means to be a shinobi that eludes people who are busy being exceptional at one specific thing.

You gain XP/75 additional Skill Points, and your maximum rank in all skills is increased by 1.

The same breadth that serves you in skills is a liability in combat. You suffer −1 to all Accuracy rolls.<br />

**Specialist**

You found your lane early and drove everything into it. Where others spread themselves across combat styles, you went deep on one and built everything around it.

Choose one of the following categories: **CQC Taijutsu**, **Weapon Taijutsu**, **Ninjutsu**, or **Genjutsu**. Your chosen category gains +1 Accuracy (or +3 to offense rolls, if Genjutsu), −2 to relevant Stamina and/or Chakra Exhaustion costs (or −3 if Genjutsu), and +10% damage.

Then choose one of the remaining categories that you did not select. It suffers −2 Accuracy (or −3 to all rolls, if Genjutsu), +2 to relevant costs (or +3 if Genjutsu), and −20% damage, you also receive 10% extra damage from attacks of this source.

<br />

**Strong-Willed**

You have a considerable amount of internal machinery dedicated to refusing to stop. Status effects that would limit other people are, for you, inconveniences to be pushed through rather than states to be managed.

All penalties to Accuracy, Fatigue, and d20 rolls from status effects are reduced by 1 per roll , so two separate penalties combine into one that's 1 lower, not two separate reductions. You may ignore the mechanical effects of one wound at a time; whenever you receive a new wound, you may switch which one you're ignoring. This lasts until combat ends, at which point all suppressed penalties reassert.

You gain +2 to all Genjutsu rolls. Once per battle, you may spend a point of Willpower as a Speed 0 Interrupt after failing a Genjutsu Defense to immediately break the technique, provided you recognized that a Genjutsu was used.

<br />

**Surgeon**

You understand the body as architecture. Where other medics see injury, you see structure , pressure points, vasculature, the precise angles at which force redistributes into catastrophic failure. Your hands have healed things they probably shouldn't have, and hurt things with the same precision.

All your damage-dealing attacks are treated as dealing 20% more damage for wound calculations. You may make Called Shots without accuracy penalty on any non-area attack. When making a Called Shot, you may declare a maximum wound severity, including none and any wound above that threshold is reduced by one category. Wounds reduced this way still break Genjutsu using their original severity.

You begin with 5 ranks in Medicine and gain one additional rank per 200 XP.

The clinical detachment costs you something in reactive defense. You suffer −1 to all defensive rolls.

<br />

**Warder** 

You’ve trained hard to become adept at brushing aside incoming objects that are liable to hurt you. This is useful for shinobi-related activities such as not dying.

Your CQC Parry and Weapon Parry actions become Speed 2 Interrupts and have a +2 bonus. Jutsu-based defenses reliant on a Ninjutsu or CQC Accuracy roll gain +2 to that roll and −2 to their base Speed, to a minimum of 1. You can use your CQC parry on Weapon Attacks, and your Weapon Parry on CQC attacks.

Your reliance on standing your ground while responding to attacks, however, results in a -2 dodge penalty. <br /><br /><br /><br />
