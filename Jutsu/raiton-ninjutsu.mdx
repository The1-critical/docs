Lightning Release is the element of speed and penetration. It doesn't give you much time to react. The attack is already there by the time most people see it coming. A Raiton user relies on speed and accuracy, hitting a weak point before the enemy can adjust or bring their defenses into place. That speed comes at a cost. Lightning Release is difficult to control and hard on the shinobi using it. 

Kumogakure has always had a strong tradition of Lightning Release. Its shinobi train to channel electrical chakra from a young age, and many of the village's techniques are built around sudden bursts of speed and overwhelming attacks. Raiton works best when the user can create an opening and exploit it immediately. A lightning technique doesn't need to cover an entire battlefield. It only needs one target and one clean path.

##  E-RANK

**–( Rashinban no Jutsu — Compass Technique )–**

It's about what it sounds like. You channel lightning chakra into a needle or kunai, lay it flat, and it points north. The Confederacy issues compasses. The technique was allegedly perfected by an Iwagakure jounin who kept getting lost during night patrols. His squad mocked him for three weeks until he navigated them through a blizzard to capture a Konoha supply depot. The compass was recovered from his body after the Third War. It still points north.

- **Chakra:** 5
- **Speed:** 4
- **Seal Speed:** 12

**Effects:** Allows a single kunai or senbon to be used as a compass for 6 hours or until used in an attack. It's not quite perfect, but it's good enough for most uses.

---

**–( Shougeki Heki — Shocking Burst )–**

Holding a hand out, you produce a pulse of electricity. It won't kill anyone, but it'll definitely hurt. The technique was originally developed as a non-lethal crowd control measure for riots in the Land of Lightning's mining towns. It worked too well – strikers learned to wear rubber boots. The mines are still running. The boots are still compulsory.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Damage:** 2d4 \* 1d3
- **Range:** 10
- **Speed:** 4
- **Seal Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** This inflicts Paralyze 1 for 20 IC. It can target either a single person, or everyone within the range. If the latter, it does not penalize dodges as a regular area of effect technique would.

Shocking Burst's Speed may not be reduced below 4.

## D-RANK

**–( Shuurai — Lightning Strike )–**

You focus energy to your fingertips, point at an opponent, and release a bolt of electricity. It's nothing like real lightning, but it looks similar. That's usually enough to make people flinch. Flinching is all you need. A Kumogakure chuunin once used this technique to short-circuit a human cyborg prototype during a live-fire exercise.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Damage:** 2d10 \* 2d4
- **Range:** 20
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** This inflicts Paralysis 1 for 20 IC if its target fails a status roll.

**–( Juude — Electric Charge )–**

You charge yourself with Raiton chakra, exploiting any residual charge on an opponent to make your next attack harder to evade. The technique is a favorite of Kumo's close-quarters specialists, who learned that a slightly slower block is still a block – but a block that slips is a hit. The difference is academic. The result is permanent.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Range:** Melee
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Nintaijutsu, Preparation

**Effects:** This 'charges' an opponent, and gives the next ranged Raiton technique used against them an Accuracy bonus of +1, plus the severity of any Paralysis they have. Thus, when using Raiton against someone with Juude and Paralysis 3 you'd have a +4 Accuracy bonus.

**–( Jinrai no Jutsu — Thunderclap Technique )–**

You put both hands together, forming a miniature thunderbolt. The crack is loud enough to stun anyone nearby, and the deafening effect lingers. The technique was developed by a Kiri hunter-nin who got tired of hearing his targets scream. Now they just don't hear anything at all.

- **Chakra:** 14
- **Range:** 10 radius around user
- **Special:** Stun 3
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Area of Effect

**Effects:** The Stun's severity is doubled if its victims fail a status roll.

**–( Shinkei Attou no Jutsu — Nerve Overloading Technique )–**

You send a spark of chakra into an enemy, stimulating any electrical charge already in their system. The convulsions are agonizing but brief. The technique only works if they already have Raiton-induced Paralysis or Shock – meaning you're essentially throwing a lit match into a gas leak. The resulting explosion is entirely their fault.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Range:** 15
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Projectile

**Effects:** This has +3 Accuracy per level of Paralysis on the target. It ends all Raiton-induced Paralysis effects on them, and inflicts them with a Stun equal to 5 per severity of the highest Paralysis removed. Thus, someone suffering from Paralysis 4 would be Stunned for 20 IC.

**–( Kyousou Ikazuchi no Jutsu — Racing Thunder Technique )–**

You pump your legs full of raiton chakra, increasing your running speed. The technique is all legs, no arms, which means you can't punch while sprinting – but you can run away. Sometimes running away is the victory. The Confederacy's evasion manual says so. The manual was written by someone who never had to run away.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 4
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** Your AGI is treated as 25% higher for the purposes of movement.

Additionally, you may choose to declare a Speed X move action, where X may be up to your bonus to Stamina rolls. Unlike normal movement (which is handled on an IC-by-IC basis), the movement for this action is resolved immediately. However, moving this way has a Stamina cost equal to the number of yards you travel. You may not reduce this action's Speed by spending AP.

**–( Kaminari no Yaiba — Lightning Blade )–**

With a quick burst of lightning, you force it through a metallic weapon and into your enemy. It's a burst, not a sustained flow, like a taser, not a power line. The weapon doesn't get hot. The enemy does. The difference is academic.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Damage:** As Weapon
- **Range:** As Weapon
- **Speed:** 5
- **Tags:** Nintaijutsu

**Effects:** You can create any type of weapon from the Sword category using this technique. Any bonuses to Weapon Accuracy the weapon normally has, reduces the Upkeep of this technique instead (an Upkeep of 0 is still considered an Upkeep). Any penalties to Accuracy remain and also add the same amount to the Upkeep of this technique.<br />The weapon otherwise functions in the same way as a normal weapon of its type, but it reduces any DR by 50% when dealing damage (or 100% against DR sources that are weak to Raiton).<br />You cannot use any hands occupied by the weapon you created for anything else, and you must choose how many hands you are using on this weapon when you create it (if it can be used two-handed, and this technique does not allow you to wield one-handed a weapon you couldn’t normally), this choice cannot be changed without recasting this technique. Dispelling the Jutsu does not take an action.

##  C-RANK

**–( Raikyuu — Lightning Ball )–**

Charging electricity between two weapons, you harness it into a ball and fire. The ball explodes on contact. The technique requires a weapon in each hand, which means you can't use it while holding your sword and a shield. This is fine. Real shinobi don't use shields. They use other people.

- **Chakra:** 22
- **Damage:** 100
- **Accuracy:** +2
- **Range:** 15
- **Speed:** 22
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** This inflicts Paralysis 2 for 20 IC if you to have a metal weapon in each hand. This also hits anyone within 3 yards of the target.

**–( Kanden Satsujin — Electric Murder )–**

You hold out your palms and discharge raw lightning. The damage is low per hit, but each consecutive strike compounds, building on the previous surge. The technique is named for its stacking effect, not a single murder, but a chain of them. The Kumo medical corps calls this "the trinity effect." They have not published their findings on why this attack becomes more intense.

- **Chakra:** 17
- **Damage:** 30d2
- **Range:** 6
- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** If this technique was your last attack, and you have not performed any hand seals since using it, you may use it again without performing hand seals.

Each consecutive use increases the technique's Chakra cost by 1 and adds half the damage dealt by all previous uses in the sequence to its damage for the purpose of determining Wounds. This bonus does not increase the actual damage dealt.

For example, if you use this technique three times consecutively, dealing 60, 40, and 50 damage:

First use: 60 damage, counts as 60 for Wounds.

Second use: 40 damage, counts as 70 for Wounds (40 + 60/2).

Third use: 50 damage, counts as 100 for Wounds (50 + 60/2 + 40/2).

**–( Kangekiha — Wave of Inspiration )–**

You focus lightning chakra into your hands, then place them on something conductive – metal, water, a wet opponent. The charge travels through the medium and hits everything connected. The technique is a favorite of Suiton users who want to turn their own water against them. The name comes from the "inspiration" of realizing that water conducts electricity. The second inspiration was realizing that your enemy's blood is mostly water.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Damage:** 4d12 \* 2d6
- **Accuracy:** -8
- **Range:** 40
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 16
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** If this is used on a conductive surface (such as a metal pipe, or the surface of a lake) that your opponent is in contact with, its Accuracy penalty is removed and your damage bonus is doubled.

If someone was hit by a Suiton ninjutsu in the past 15 IC, this technique's Accuracy penalty is reduced to -2, but your damage bonus is not increased.

For every point of Paralysis a target is suffering from, the Accuracy penalty is reduced by 2; however, if this hits their Paralysis status is ended.

**–( Taiyoken — Solar Flare )–**

You create a blinding flash of light. Doujutsu users think they're immune. They're not. The light is chakra‑based, not visual – it overloads the optic nerve directly. Uchiha and Hyuuga who get hit lose their eye techniques until the penalty fades. Some lose them permanently if the flare is strong enough. The technique was banned by the Confederacy after the Battle of the Daimyo's Palace, where a single genin blinded twelve Hyuuga, causing a diplomatic incident that cost Konoha three trade routes. The ban is not enforced however.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Range:** 20
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Area of Effect

**Effects:** This inflicts a -8 visibility penalty on anyone in range. Used as an Interrupt, there is a chance the sudden flash of light will cause an opponent to miss entirely; treat your defense as if you'd rolled a 5 on a dodge attempt, and if this would allow you to dodge with their visibility penalty, then they simply miss.

This negates the effects of any active doujutsu on its victims until its penalty has faded to -4.

When you use this technique, you and anyone in range make an opposed Initiative roll, with the victims getting a +5 bonus for each previous time this technique has been used in that combat. If you beat their roll, they're blinded; if not, they managed to close their eyes in time.

The penalty fades at the rate of 1 per 5 IC.

**–( Chuubu Gaki no Jutsu — Ghoul Paralysis Technique )–**

You touch an enemy and send a pulse of electricity through their nervous system. Their body locks up, muscles twitching, but they remain conscious. The technique is not harmful, but it is excruciating – like every nerve firing at once. The name comes from the ghouls of old folklore, who could paralyze victims with a touch. The folklore didn't mention that the ghouls were actually Kumo operatives wearing masks.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Melee

**Effects:** This inflicts Paralysis 2 for 20 IC, raised to Paralysis 4 for 40 IC if the victim fails a status roll.

**–( Fukushuu Kaminarikami — Avenging Lightning God )–**

You cover your skin in a static charge that lashes out at anyone who hits you. The opponent feels the intensity increase with each subsequent strike they inflict on the user. The user's skin glows faintly blue while active and it resembles the Yotsuki's lightning kiryu however isn't a strong counterpart of it.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Upkeep:** 6
- **Damage:** 4d6\*2d4
- **Special:** This technique receives only half your damage bonus.
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Energy, Contact, Preparation

**Effects:** Any time you are struck by an unarmed melee attack or metallic melee weapon, this technique 'discharges'. Your attack deals its damage as a result of their attack, and you must make a CE roll with a cost of 10 (This auto-hits and is not allowed a defense).

Avenging Lightning God's damage is increased by 10% for every 1 point of Paralyze the attacker is afflicted by.

Additionally, anyone damaged by this move is afflicted by a Paralyze 1 status for 40 IC. If they hit you again while still suffering from it, its severity is raised by 1.

## B-RANK

**–( Raijuu Hashiri no Jutsu — Lightning Beast Running Technique )–**

You slam your hands together and expel a creature made of lightning, a wolf, a tiger, a serpent, whatever form you visualize. The beast runs at your enemy, connected to you by a leash of electricity. If they dodge, you can yank the leash and redirect the beast for a second strike. The beast's form reflects the user's personality. A wolf indicates pack loyalty. A tiger indicates solitary pride. A serpent indicates... something else.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Damage:** 1d20 \* 2d10
- **Range:** 40
- **Special:** Paralysis 2 for 30 IC
- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 20
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** After seeing the result of your opponent's defense, you may choose to make them reroll it. If you do this, they keep the second result (even if it's better). If you use this option, Raijuu Hashiri's damage is halved for the purpose of determining wounds, and its Paralysis status is removed.

You may also use the redirect to send the beast after a different target; it still suffers the usual reductions this way.

**–( Raiton Bunshin no Jutsu — Lightning Release Clone Technique )–**

You create clones made of lightning. They can fight, throw weapons, and use Raiton jutsu. But if someone strikes them with a melee attack, the clone explodes – not with fire, but with electricity that arcs into the attacker. The technique is banned in most spars because it tends to kill training partners. The inventor died during testing. His clone didn't.

- **Chakra:** 15, +5 per clone
- **Damage:** 100\*1d3
- **Range:** Melee
- **Special:** Paralysis 4 for 30 IC
- **Speed:** 8, +4 per clone
- **Seal Speed:** 12, +4 per clone
- **Requires:** Bunshin no Jutsu
- **Tags:** Clone, Energy

**Effects:** You can create up to two clones. These function like regular bunshin, but may take the actions listed below. Additionally, anyone who destroys a Lightning Clone with a melee attacks takes this technique's damage automatically.

Lightning Clones are all at Fatigue 0; if they ever reach Fatigue 1, they are destroyed.

[X AP] Move: Your clones can move, where X is the Speed of the Move action.

[4 AP] Basic Unarmed: A basic unarmed attack, using your attributes.

[X AP] Basic Weapon: You may transfer any number of weapons to your Raiton Bunshin when you create them. They may attack with those weapons, using your abilities. X is half the Speed of the attack.

[X AP] Multi-Throw: Should be self-explanatory. X is half the Speed of the multi-throw.

[X AP] Raiton Ninjutsu: The clone must first perform the handseals, at the regular speed, costing AP equal to half the technique's modified Seal Speed. It then costs X AP, where X is half the technique's Speed. They cannot, however, use Raiton Bunshin.

When Lightning Clones attack, their actions are always considered to be Held Back, as per the ability in Chapter 5, even if you do not have the Hold Back ability. This means they use only half your damage bonus for dealing damage, and the severity of wounds they inflict is reduced by one category.

Lightning Clones use your base Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion bonuses, as if they were a Fatigue 0 version of you without any temporary benefits (such as having eaten a Soldier Pill before creating them). If a clone fails a Fatigue roll, it vanishes just as if it had been struck.

**–( Gian — False Darkness )–**

You form a spear‑shaped bolt of lightning and throw it. The technique's true power is its rapid fire: once you complete the handseals, you can keep throwing spears without resealing, as long as you don't use any other ninjutsu. The spear's shape is aerodynamically unstable, which means it wobbles in flight. This makes it harder to dodge, because you can't predict which way it'll curve. The instability was a design flaw.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Damage:** 75\*1d6
- **Range:** 60
- **Speed:** 15
- **Seal Speed:** 35
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** Once the technique's seals have been completed, you may repeatedly use Gian at Seal Speed 0 as long as you take no other ninjutsu or non-Interrupt actions. Thus, you could dodge, but not punch, and not used a ninjutsu-based defense.

**–( Shichuu Shibari — Four Pillar Bind )–**

You summon four stone pillars around a target, then arc lightning between them. The result is a cage of electricity that shocks anyone inside. The pillars are made of rock – don't ask how lightning does that. The inventor was a Doton specialist who wanted to prove that earth and lightning could work together. He succeeded. His colleagues still make fun of him for "cheating."

- **Chakra:** 45
- **Upkeep:** 5
- **Damage:** 2d8\*3d4
- **Range:** CHA /2
- **Area:** 25 x 25 square
- **Special:** Paralysis 5, Immobilize 8
- **Speed:** 25
- **Seal Speed:** 25
- **Tags:** Energy, Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** This technique inflicts its statuses on anyone other than the user who is within its area. They do not end until the victim has managed to leave. This technique lasts for 60 IC, or until you end the Upkeep, whichever comes sooner.

**–( Tesura Karui — Tesla Minor )–**

You throw a ball of semi‑stable lightning. It sticks to any surface, walls, floors, people. Once placed, it emits bolts at anyone nearby, up to ten times. If you place two Tesla Minors within 5 yards of each other, they will arc between themselves, creating a continuous chain that deals damage to anyone between them.

- **Chakra:** 35
- **Upkeep:** 3
- **Damage:** 100
- **Range:** 20
- **Special:** Paralysis 2, for 30 IC
- **Speed:** 25
- **Seal Speed:** 25
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** This creates a ball of energy within range on some surface. When it's created, and every 25 IC afterwards, the ball emits bolts of lightning at everyone within CHA / 5 yards. Each bolt of a lightning is a separate attack.

The ball has 10 'charges'; it can attack 10 times, and then it expires. It will not attack you, and you can specify up to (Chakra Control)/10 other people who it will ignore.

This technique uses your base Ninjutsu Accuracy for an B-rank Raiton, unmodified by status, wounds, temporary bonuses, and so on, though penalties from Fatigue do apply. The ninjutsu damage bonus is equal to yours at the time you use the technique. Because the attacks are made by the technique, and not you, you cannot modify them further (such as with Willpower or AP).

**–( Hiyaku Raikoui — Chain Lightning )–**

You fire a bolt of lightning that jumps from target to target, gaining power with each jump. The technique was originally used by Kumo execution squads to kill multiple prisoners with a single shot. The first target dies. The second suffers worse. The third... well, there's a reason the technique is classified as a war crime in three nations.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Damage:** 7d6\*1d10
- **Range:** 50
- **Speed:** 16
- **Seal Speed:** 18
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** This has an Accuracy bonus equal to the severity of Paralysis its target is suffering from, +1 for every previous target it's hit. After hitting one person it can then 'jump' to any other target within half the original range from its most recent victim, doing 10% more damage for each target previously hit.

Anyone struck by this technique has their Paralysis status raised by 1, and its duration increased by 5 IC. A person can only be hit by a single use of this technique once.

The maximum number of targets a single activation of Chain Lightning can hit is equal to your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus.

## A-RANK

**–( Kouken Rairyuu — Guardian Lightning Dragon )–**

The dragon remembers when the sky had teeth. You harness a massive amount of chakra, and a field of lightning swirls around you, coalescing into the crackling outline of an electric dragon. Legend says the First Raikage learned it from a storm dragon that lived in the clouds above Kumogakure, a creature that never touched the ground and looked down on mortals with amused contempt. The Raikage asked why it would share such power. The dragon said, "Because you're the first human who didn't flinch when I roared."

- **Chakra:** 54
- **Damage:** 5d8\*6d6
- **Range:** 80
- **Special:** Paralysis 3 for 30 IC
- **Speed:** 26
- **Seal Speed:** 30
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** When you use this technique, you may choose to lower its Chakra cost by 10. If you do so, rather than launching at your opponents the lightning dragon takes shape coiling around you.

It will automatically attack the first person to move within 10 yards of you (it will not, however, trigger this effect if you move closer to them), with +4 Accuracy. If they fail a status roll, its Paralysis effect will also be double.

You may also launch the dragon at any time while it's still around, as a Chakra 35, Speed 14 action, using 14 rather than 26 as its base speed.

Where Four Pillar Bind creates a cage, this creates a fortress. Sixteen pillars rise in four concentric squares, each layer crackling with electricity. The inner layers are deadlier than the outer ones. If you destroy one pillar, the resonance breaks and the bind collapses.

Where Tesla Minor creates a ball, this creates a spire – a towering column of electricity that pulses every so often. It was originally designed as a defensive emplacement for Kumo's border forts. The forts are gone. The spires remain, still crackling, still waiting for someone to wander close.

- **Range:** 50

**–( Raitama — Ball Lightning )–**

Upon completion of the handseals, the user forms a ball of raw, condensed lightning chakra between their palms, then encases it in a stable shell made from hundreds of interwoven chakra strands, a mesh-like barrier preventing the inner chakra from dissipating. It can be left hovering in the open or hidden inside any appropriately sized physical object, typically the ground, where it lies in wait until someone comes close enough to trigger it. When they do, it seeks them out and detonates on contact.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Upkeep:** 8
- **Damage:** 300
- **Range:** 60
- **Speed:** 50
- **Seal Speed:** 25
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** The mine must be placed within melee range. The technique's actual range can, if you wish, be set lower than 60. Whenever someone other than you enters this range, they immediately become the target of a Sneak Attack from this jutsu; the ball of lightning soars towards them without warning.

This can be left sitting in the opponent (as an effective deterrent), or submerged into any available surface (the floor, the walls). If you are within the maximum range (even if you reduced the attack range), you can prevent the ball from activating by concentrating on it (though you'd typically need line of sight to know if this is necessary).

If this is hidden inside some object, it cannot be detected without the use of a chakra-detecting doujutsu (such as Byakugan).

**–( Shimeshi Ryouiki — Dampening Field )–**

Concentrating your chakra, you project an invisible wide-area dome outward. Maintaining its stability requires you to remain practically immobile at its center, but the effects are nearly impossible to circumvent without finding and eliminating the originator. Anyone who enters the field will instantly notice its presence, their hair will stand on end and most objects will begin building a static charge.

- **Chakra:** 50
- **Upkeep:** 10
- **Range:** Self
- **Area:** CHA \*10
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 50
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** You can move up to four yards from your original location while maintaining this technique; moving (or being forcibly moved) any further causes it to end.

Within the field, all chakra-based sensing (such as Byakugan's vision, chakra-sensory ninja, and the Yurokensei technique) has a -20 penalty. Electronic and electrical devices in this area are useless. The dampening also interferes with other lightning-based chakra, reducing the damage of all Raiton in its range to 80%.

Finally, the large amounts of ambient lightning chakra interferes with earth-based chakra. Doton techniques in this area have their damage reduced by 25%, and their Chakra costs and Upkeeps (if relevant) increased by 4.
