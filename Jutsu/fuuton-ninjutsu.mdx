---

Wind Release is the element of freedom and precision. It doesn't sit still, and it doesn't need much to become dangerous. A gust can carry a leaf across a valley. The same wind, pushed hard enough and shaped with enough control, can cut through flesh before the victim even understands what happened. That is what makes Fuuton so strange. It can be gentle one moment and vicious the next. A skilled user can push an enemy back, throw dust into their eyes, sharpen the air into a cutting edge, or simply disappear into the noise of the wind. There is very little warning. By the time you notice a Wind Release user, they may already have attacked.

Sunagakure has always had a strong tradition of Wind Release. Its shinobi grow up surrounded by open desert, where the wind can change from a light breeze to a violent gale in seconds. Learning to read those changes becomes part of learning the element itself. But Wind Release doesn't belong to the Sand alone. Wherever there is air, there is something for a Fuuton user to work with. In the desert, the wind carries sand. In the mountains, it tears through narrow passes. In a forest, it slips between the trees.

**–( Fuusajin no Jutsu — Dust Wind Technique )–**

Wind is the first element a child touches and the last one a veteran respects. In the academies that still bother to teach, this is the technique they use to illustrate direction. You are not the wind. You are the reason the wind goes somewhere. Most students forget that part by graduation. Most students die before chuunin.

- **Chakra:** 6
- **Area:** 4 wide, 10 long, directly in front of you
- **Ninjutsu Accuracy:** +1
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Status:** Visibility 3
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Projectile

**Effects:** The visibility penalty lasts for 10+(Chakra Control ranks)/2 IC.

- **Speed:** 10 + X \* 2, Delay X \* 2
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Special:** This technique may not have its seal speed removed with a fan

If you are suffering from an Immobilization penalty, the distance you're able to travel with Vaulting Leap is reduced by 5% per point of Immobilization. - **Chakra:** 34

- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 24

**Effects:** When first activated, the tornado pushes everyone away from you, causing any grapples you were a part of to automatically end and everyone within (NDB) yards of you must make a status roll to resist being knocked back (CHA/10) yards.

The tornado provides a Damage Reduction of (your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus) \* 10. Any time you use a damage-dealing Fuuton jutsu, this DR is increased by (your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus) \* (Rank - 1), with E being 0, D being 1, etc. This DR doesn’t apply to Katon, and is considered 50% higher against Raiton. Non-projectile ranged attacks (such as Retsudotensho) only have their damage reduced by half the normal amount. X is the current amount of DR this technique is granting you–its Upkeep steadily increases as its power does.

Additionally, the surge of wind each time this tornado is empowered pushes enemies (along with anyone and everything else) away from you. Anyone within (your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus) yards is knocked back (1 per rank of the jutsu; 1 for E, 5 for A, etc.) \* (your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus)/3 yards, if they fail a status roll.

**–( Fuuton: Kitakaze — The North Wind )–**

The North Wind is the climax of Wind Release, a technique focused on unmaking rather than attacking. It instantly nullifies all environmental effects caused by ninjutsu, prevents new techniques from being activated, halves ignite statuses, and removes immobilities while purging lightning-induced paralysis and shock. Named after the adage "The North Wind strips the land bare," it symbolizes endings, contrasting with the East Wind's discovery, South Wind's growth, and West Wind's change. Kitakaze users embody this reset, often weary of conflicts and battles.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Speed:** 22
- **Seal Speed:** 20
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** All environmental effects caused by Ninjutsu (such as Yomi Numa, or Four Pillar Bind) end immediately, and no such techniques may be activated for (NDB \* 2) IC; however, this does not end or prevent B- or A-Rank Katon.

Any Ignite status effects have their severity halved (round down), and any Immobility penalties with set durations (meaning, ones that fade by a certain amount every so-many IC) are removed, as is any Paralysis or Shock that was originally caused by a Raiton technique (including Shock from Elemental Mastery).
