---
title: "Genjutsu"
---

Genjutsu is the art of illusion, the manipulation of the senses and the perception of reality itself. Unlike Taijutsu and Ninjutsu, which shape the physical world, Genjutsu reshapes how the world is perceived. A shinobi caught in a genjutsu is not fighting an enemy; they are fighting their own senses and their own fears.

It is the most difficult of the three shinobi arts to master. There is no affinity for it, no natural talent that makes it come easily. It requires a deep understanding of how the mind works. A single flaw in the illusion can break it. A single moment of doubt can shatter the spell. But when it works, it is devastating even an enemy who cannot trust their own eyes is an enemy already defeated.

---

## E-Rank

**–( Senpuku no Jutsu — Concealment Technique )–**

A flicker of chakra, a slight distortion of light, and the object simply ceases to exist to casual observation. The technique doesn't make it gone—it makes it forgotten.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8

**Effects:** Completely hides an object from casual observation. Against a detailed search, gives a +5 bonus to hiding rolls. You must keep contact with the object. It won't hide items during a pat-down. If the object interacts with the world (swinging a weapon, using a key), concealment ends.

---

**–( Genkoe — Hallucinatory Voices )–**

Your senses become a liability. At first, nothing—until you start paying attention. Then the technique begins its work: flickers in peripheral vision, footsteps that never arrive, whispers just beyond comprehension. The hardest part of using this technique isn't the chakra cost. It's resisting the urge to look over your shoulder when you start hearing things that aren't there.

- **Chakra:** 12
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Speed:** 7
- **Seal Speed:** 8

**Effects:** Inflicts -2 to Awareness rolls. Halves the bonus for consecutive Search actions (three searches give +3 instead of +6).

---

**–( Mousou Enmu no Jutsu — Illusory Mist Technique )–**

A light bank of fog rolls in. You can see through it, feel it—or so you think. The mist is real, but the thickness, the disorientation, the creeping dread? That's all in your head. The difference between real mist and this technique is that real mist doesn't make you doubt your own eyes.

- **Chakra:** 12
- **Upkeep:** 3
- **Area:** 25
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 7
- **Tags:** Targeted Area of Effect

**Effects:** Anyone in the area when first used defends against this technique. On failure, they take -4 Visibility Penalty. While in the mist, they suffer -2 to defensive genjutsu rolls. Those who pass see the mist for what it truly is—too thin to be a hindrance. The mist moves with its user.

## D-Rank

**–( Kasumi Juusha no Jutsu — Mist Servant Technique )–**

Phantoms rise from the ground like black oil given form. They move, they feint, they attack, and when you strike one down, another rises to take its place. The real question is: are you fighting the puppets, or are the puppets fighting you?

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 4
- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Clone, Area of Effect

**Effects:** Creates dozens of illusory clones. Anyone in combat must defend with a genjutsu roll; against those who fail, you are considered in Stealth 1. Partial success (fail by ≤5): still hidden but Stealth roll at -5. You may attack from stealth normally, each attack lowers Stealth TN by 3 instead of 5. Once found, you cannot hide among these clones again until you reapply the technique.

---

**–( Oboro Bunshin no Jutsu — Haze Clone Technique )–**

Ordinary Bunshin that answer when struck, that vanish when defeated. Except each one you destroy simply reappears, just a little further away, just a little harder to reach. You can kill a hundred of them. There will always be one more. And while you're searching, the real one is already gone.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Upkeep:** 0
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Clone, Area of Effect

**Effects:** Creates three clones. You are always 'hidden' among them, even after being hit. Observe allows a genjutsu roll to realize this is an illusion, but cannot single you out. Each time a clone is destroyed, another appears and Upkeep increases by 1 (max 6). If reapplied in the same battle, retains the previous Upkeep.

---

**–( Magen: Narakumi no Jutsu — Demonic Illusion: Hell Viewing Technique )–**

The leaves spin. They always spin. You've seen them before, from training, from battle, from the thousand stories told by veterans who survived the technique. When they clear, you'll see something you can't unsee. Something you never wanted to see again. And your mind will break, just for a moment, from the weight of it. The first time is always the worst. The second time, you know it's coming. But you still flinch. You'll always flinch.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Range:** 20
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Special:** Stun

**Effects:** Target loses 1 Willpower and suffers Stun 20. Partial success (fail by ≤5): Stun 10, no Willpower loss. After the first successful use per day, effects reduce to Stun 10 and no Willpower loss. On a target with 0 Willpower, Stun increases by 10. Breaking the genjutsu ends the Stun early, but lost Willpower is not regained.

---

**–( Magen: Jigoku Kouka no Jutsu — Demonic Illusion: Descending Hell Technique )–**

A sphere of fire forms above you. It descends slowly—too slow to be a real attack, too fast to ignore. Do you run? Do you brace? Do you try to find the caster before the fire consumes you? The technique doesn't care. The fire was never real. But your decision to run—that was very real.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Range:** 25
- **Area:** 25
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 10

**Effects:** Target has 10 IC to move outside the area. On failure, Stun 15. Partial success (fail by ≤5): Stun 8. Ending the genjutsu before the timer expires avoids the Stun. Once Stunned, the Stun is real and cannot be cleared by removing the genjutsu.

---

**–( Shinkirou no Jutsu — Mirage Technique )–**

A curtain flutters in a windowless room. A tiger snarls in a village square. A fire crackles in a rainstorm. The illusions are flawless—until you reach out to touch them. Then they dissolve into nothing, and you realize: everything you just saw was a lie. And the question is: what did you miss while you were distracted?

- **Chakra:** 10 (base) + extra
- **Upkeep:** 3
- **Range:** 20
- **Area:** 2 (max)
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** Creates an illusion affecting one sense (sight, hearing, or smell). +5 Chakra for two senses, +10 for all three. Scale limited to roughly a large piece of furniture or one person. Interaction allows a Genjutsu roll to see through it. Can be controlled or set to loop.

## C-Rank

**–( Kyousanken no Jutsu — Iron Curtain Technique )–**

You weave a harmless genjutsu over yourself. It does nothing, except make every other genjutsu work a little harder to find you. It's a wall of thorns wrapped around your mind. Not impenetrable. But enough to make the incursion cost more than it's worth.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** While maintained, +4 to defensive genjutsu rolls. Does not end when wounded. May be declared as an Interrupt when targeted with genjutsu, giving its bonus for that roll. Remains active after being used this way.

---

**–( Kori Shinchuu no Jutsu — Sly Mind Affect Technique )–**

The road is straight. The sun is in the right place. You've walked this path a hundred times. But somehow, the trees look familiar. Somehow, you've passed that same rock twice. You're walking in circles, and you don't even know it. The Sly Mind is the most patient of all genjutsu. It doesn't rush. It doesn't force. It simply lets you walk yourself into a trap.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Range:** 50
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 14

**Effects:** No combat use. Affected target walks in circles if traveling any appreciable distance (half a mile or more). Lasts 30 minutes per point you beat their Genjutsu roll by. Partial success: duration halved. Every hour, they may make a new Genjutsu roll against your original result. Following roads, maps, or compasses will not help.

---

**–( Magen: Kokoni Arazu no Jutsu — Demonic Illusion: False Surroundings Technique )–**

A door becomes a wall. A bridge becomes rubble. A sign reads the wrong number. The changes are subtle, almost imperceptible, but they are enough to send the most skilled trackers into the wilderness. And once they realize they've been tricked, they'll only be angrier. But at least they won't find you.

- **Chakra:** 17
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Area:** 30
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 15
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** Out of combat illusion over an area. Make a genjutsu roll with +5 bonus. Anyone who encounters the illusion makes a genjutsu roll; failure means they believe it's real. Partial success (fail by ≤5): they sense something is off but cannot pinpoint what; suffer -2 to further interactions. Requires Genjutsu Kai to dispel.

---

**–( Nemuri — Sleep )–**

A single touch. A moment of eye contact. Then the world fades to black. You don't remember falling asleep. You don't remember anything at all. When you wake, you'll be bound, or alone, or dead. The technique itself doesn't care which. It just puts you to sleep.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 15
- **Special:** No handseals required

**Effects:** No combat use. On successful genjutsu roll, target falls asleep for at least four hours. Partial success (fail by ≤5): falls asleep but wakes after 1 hour or if any loud noise occurs.

---

**–( Sen'un no Jutsu — Fog of War Technique )–**

You're in a fight. Then you're not. You're giving chase. Then you're not. The technique tricks your understanding and makes the world a lucid playground. It convinces you that the battle is already over, that you've already won. And while you're basking in your victory, the real shinobi is already gone.

- **Chakra:** 25 + X × 3
- **Range:** 10
- **Upkeep:** 0
- **Speed:** 25
- **Seal Speed:** 20

**Effects:** Costs 2 Willpower on success. +X to Genjutsu roll (max +5). On success, target is removed from combat for 1 minute per point you beat their roll by. The illusion breaks if the target senses imminent danger. No partial success.

---

**–( Shunou Kaimetsu — Brain Destruction )–**

The body is whole. The mind is not. The technique convinces your brain that you've been stabbed, crushed, burned—that you're dying. And even though there's no blood, no wound, no pain, you believe it. And believing it is enough to make you suffer as if it were real.

- **Chakra:** 26
- **Range:** 30
- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 12

**Effects:** Inflicts a phantom wound of Minor severity (upgrades to Major at +5, Severe at +10). The victim suffers all penalties of the wound but no HP loss. Phantom wounds break other genjutsu but don't count for wound-triggered abilities. Real wounds end the phantom wound immediately. Partial success (fail by ≤5): one severity lower.

---

## B-Rank

**–( Gosaru Tatari — Five Monkey Curse )–**

See no evil. Hear no evil. Speak no evil. The saying gets it wrong. There are five monkeys. And the fifth one doesn't speak, it makes you feel no pain. Which sounds like a mercy, until you realize that feeling no pain means you won't notice the wound that's killing you.

- **Chakra:** 32
- **Range:** 25
- **Speed:** 18
- **Seal Speed:** 16

**Effects:** Negates all ranks in Enhanced Hearing, Enhanced Sight, and abilities dependent on them. -2 Visibility penalty. Victim ignores penalties of Minor wounds—which also means Minor wounds won't break other genjutsu. Partial success (fail by ≤5): lose only one rank in each, visibility penalty -1.

---

**–( Nemurihane — Sleep Feather )–**

Feathers drift down from nowhere. They're beautiful, almost peaceful. You watch them fall, and you feel your eyelids growing heavy. You want to stay awake. You know you shouldn't sleep. But the feathers are so gentle, so soft... and you've been so tired...

- **Chakra:** 24
- **Range:** 40
- **Speed:** 15
- **Seal Speed:** 20

**Effects:** As C-rank Nemuri, but can be used at range. No combat use. Partial success: as C-rank Nemuri partial.

---

**–( Magen: Nijuu Kokoni Arazu no Jutsu — Double False Surroundings Technique )–**

You break the illusion. You're safe. Then the real world twists, and you realize the illusion you broke was just the first one. The second one is already in place, and you didn't even notice when it took hold. Twice the deception. Twice the arrogance of thinking you've won.

- **Chakra:** Special
- **Upkeep:** +5 (to first genjutsu's upkeep, or flat 5)
- **Speed:** +10
- **Seal Speed:** +10
- **Requires:** False Surroundings Technique
- **Tags:** Area of Effect

**Effects:** Layers a second Area of Effect genjutsu on top of the first. When the first is dispelled, the second immediately takes effect. Each genjutsu paid for separately with +5 Chakra. May apply a penalty to the first genjutsu (max -5) to gain an equivalent bonus to the second. Both techniques count as 'used' for future defenses. No partial success.

---

**–( Magen: Jubaku Satsu — Tree Binding Death )–**

The tree wasn't there a moment ago. You're certain of that. But it's behind you now, and branches are wrapping around your arms, your legs, your throat. You try to move, but you can't. The tree holds you still, and while you're trapped, the shinobi who trapped you is already preparing your end.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Range:** 20
- **Speed:** 18
- **Seal Speed:** 20
- **Special:** Immobilize 8

**Effects:** Inflicts Immobilize 8. Every 10 IC, target may make a new defensive genjutsu roll at cumulative +1. Target cannot voluntarily move while technique is maintained. Partial success (fail by ≤5): Immobilize 3, repeated saves every 15 IC.

---

**–( Jigyaku no Jutsu — Time Reversal Technique )–**

The mind remembers everything. The problem is accessing it. This technique pulls open doors you didn't know were locked, reveals memories you didn't know you'd buried. It's not pleasant. But for the shinobi who need to remember, it's invaluable.

- **Chakra:** 26
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 24

**Effects:** Out of combat utility. Used on willing or helpless recipients. Places subject in a trance to recall suppressed memories. Grants +5 to Research rolls about past experiences. Unravels Sennou Sousa no Jutsu effects. Once per day per person.

---

**–( Magen: Tetsuotome no Jutsu — Iron Maiden Technique )–**

Chains burst from the ground, wrapping around your legs. Then the iron maiden appears—two halves, decorated with spikes, ready to close around you. You try to move, you try to fight, but the chains hold you fast. Then the maiden slams shut, and you feel the spikes pierce your body in a dozen places at once. The pain is exquisite. The pain is a lie. But it feels real enough to break you.

- **Chakra:** 32
- **Range:** 25
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 18
- **Special:** Stun 10

**Effects:** Inflicts Minor Piercing wound on every body location. Upgrades to Major if target fails by 10+. Phantom wounds last the duration; Stun remains even if broken. Partial success (fail by ≤5): one location only, no Stun.

---

**–( Sakki — Killing Intent )–**

You've felt killing intent before. Every shinobi has. The cold, creeping certainty that the person looking at you wants you dead. This technique amplifies that feeling beyond reason, beyond control, beyond sanity. For a moment, you're certain you're about to die, and that certainty is enough to freeze you in place.

- **Chakra:** 28
- **Range:** 15
- **Special:** Stun 10 + X
- **Speed:** 16

**Effects:** Requires line of sight. Neither can suffer -6 or greater Visibility. X = amount victim failed by. Target suffers Stun 10 + X. While Stunned, defensive interrupts have doubled base speed. Stun ends if jutsu ends. No handseals. Partial success (fail by ≤5): Stun = 5 + (X/2, rounded down); defensive interrupts not doubled.

## A-Rank

**–( Nehan Shouja no Jutsu — Temple of Nirvana Technique )–**

The most powerful sleep technique. Over a hundred yards of silence, peace, and oblivion. When you wake, you'll be bound, or moved, or completely forgotten, or perhaps you won't wake at all. The technique doesn't care. It just offers sleep. And you're so very tired.

- **Chakra:** 36
- **Range:** 150
- **Area:** 100
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 30

**Effects:** No combat use. Anyone who fails falls asleep. Those with less than half the user's XP auto-fail unless they spend Willpower. Victims sleep for several hours unless awoken. Partial success (fail by ≤5): light slumber, wakes after 30 minutes or if disturbed.

---

**–( Kokuangyo no Jutsu — Bringer-of-Darkness Technique )–**

The world goes black. Not the absence of light, the absence of information. You try to see, but there's nothing. You try to hear, but your senses are muddled. You reach for your doujutsu, but you can't even tell if it's working. You're blind, helpless, and the shinobi who did this to you could be anywhere.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Range:** 50
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 26

**Effects:** -10 Visibility penalty. Negates Blind Fighting benefits. Anyone can hide from the victim. Doujutsus are completely useless. Partial success (fail by ≤5): -8 Visibility; Blind Fighting at half effectiveness; Doujutsu users suffer -5.

---

**–( Kiokure Keiren — Nervous Convulsions )–**

You reach for a kunai. Your hand spasms. You try to run. Your legs twitch and lock. The technique doesn't control you, it makes you control yourself wrong. Every action becomes a struggle. Every movement is a gamble. You try to attack, and you end up punching yourself in the gut. You try to run, and you trip over your own feet. And while you're flailing, the shinobi who did this to you is already walking away.

- **Chakra:** 35
- **Range:** 40
- **Speed:** 18
- **Seal Speed:** 27

**Effects:** Whenever the target takes a non-interrupt action, they suffer Paralysis and Shock equal to X/5 (rounded down, X = action's Speed), lasting X IC. Each application allows a new defensive roll at cumulative +1. Partial success (fail by ≤5): effects halved, duration X/2.

---

**–( Sennou Sousa no Jutsu — Memory-Concealing Manipulative Sand Technique )–**

You remember walking through the village. You remember buying a drink. Then nothing. An hour passes. Two. You don't remember any of it. You don't remember the hand that touched your shoulder. You don't remember the questions. You don't remember what you told them. And you never will.

- **Chakra:** 44
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 30
- **Seal Speed:** 30

**Effects:** Out of combat. Must be helpless or willing. Willing: can suppress any amount of memories. Helpless: opposed genjutsu roll; erase up to X hours of memories (X = amount succeeded by). Cannot be removed by Kai or wounds, requires Jigyaku no Jutsu with equal or higher roll. Byakugan cannot detect it.

---

**–( Dokushin no Jutsu — Poison Truth Technique )–**

You're floating in a dream. Soft voices ask questions. You answer them. You always answer them. You can't lie in a dream. And when you wake, you won't remember a single word you said. You'll just remember that you had a nightmare. You'll never know how much you gave away.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 50

**Effects:** Out of combat. Must be helpless. The victim is put to sleep. The interrogator asks one question (≤12 words). Opposed genjutsu rolls determine if the victim answers truthfully. The victim may spend 1 Willpower to auto-succeed and avoid answering. Cumulative +2 bonus for repeated use per session. Cannot ask the same question twice. Partial success does not apply.
