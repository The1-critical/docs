---
title: "Sealing Ninjutsu"
---

Sealing Ninjutsu is the art of permanence. Where other techniques flare and fade, seals endure, sometimes for decades, sometimes for centuries, sometimes until the end of time itself. A seal is not a jutsu in the traditional sense; it is a written contract between the user and reality, a set of symbols that commands the world to remember what was placed within it. Scrolls that hold armies. Barriers that hold gods. Marks that track a target across continents or silence a tongue before treason can be spoken. This is the power of Fuuinjutsu.

## D-Rank

**–( Kankin Kanshihon Fuuin — Confinement Scroll Seal )–**

A blank scroll, a writing implement, and a steady hand are all this technique requires. The user inscribes a series of symbols across the scroll's surface, places items upon it, and performs the necessary handseals. The items vanish, stored within the seal until they are released. Each item corresponds to a separate symbol on the scroll, and items can be sealed and released all at once or individually, as the situation demands.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 10

**Effects:** This stores up to 50 pounds of objects inside a scroll. Releasing any number of items is a Chakra 5, Speed 5 action, which can be performed by anyone who knows this technique. If used to release a weapon, it requires a separate action to draw and properly grasp that weapon.

**–( Soshoryu — Twin Rising Dragons )–**

This technique requires two storage scrolls with projectile weapons sealed inside them. When activated, both scrolls are launched into the air, giving the technique its name. The user can then leap between them, grabbing and throwing various projectiles in a seamless, fluid motion.

- **Chakra:** +10
- **Speed:** +0
- **Seal Speed:** +8
- **Requires:** Two scrolls with thrown weapons sealed inside them

**Effects:** This is a link added to a thrown weapon attack or multi-throw using weapons previously stored in scrolls with Confinement Scroll Seal. You unseal the weapons you need and immediately attack with them, bypassing the need to unseal them as a separate action. When used with a multi-throw, your DEX is increased by CHA/2 for determining how many weapons you can throw. All weapons thrown must be sealed in the scrolls.

You may also use this with the Throw Weapon ability (if you have it), allowing you to unseal and immediately attack with a melee weapon without needing to unseal it as a separate action.

**–( Sougu: Baku Ryuusei — Manipulated Tools: Attached Meteor )–**

Long, weighted chains shoot forth from a scroll and spiral around the target, binding them before the slack is removed. The technique does minimal damage on its own, but it is an excellent setup for a more potent follow-up attack, restricting the target's movement and leaving them vulnerable.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Range:** 12
- **Requires:** One scroll

**Effects:** You make a Ranged Taijutsu Attack. If you hit, this deals no damage but restrains the opponent, inflicting a -2 Immobilization penalty. This penalty increases by 1 for each point your Accuracy exceeds their defensive roll, to a maximum of -7.

The chains remain attached to the scroll, and sufficient movement on the victim's part will reduce this penalty by 1 per four yards moved. Alternatively, they may attempt to wrestle free as a Speed 6 action, rolling as the defender in a grapple with a penalty equal to their Immobility penalty from this technique, while you use your NDB in place of PDB.

**–( Shikoku Fuuin — Finger Engraving Seal )–**

Concentrating chakra into your fingertip, you can write on any surface you please. On solid objects, this creates a slight depression stained black. On flesh, it causes mild discomfort and forms an inky-black mark that fades over the course of a month, leaving no scar.

- **Cost:** None
- **Speed:** 1 per (Chakra Control Ranks/5) words

**Effects:** You can write on any surface with chakra.

## C-Rank

**–( Sougu: Tensasai — Manipulated Tools: Heavenly Chain of Destruction )–**

An advanced form of Twin Rising Dragons, this technique involves throwing a scroll into the air and leaping after it. In midair, the weapons inside the scroll are released and guided by the user's chakra, allowing them to direct far more weapons than they could by hand. The technique's name comes from the speed of its most proficient practitioners, whose weapons appear as rays of light.

- **Chakra:** Variable, Special
- **Speed:** As multi-throw
- **Seal Speed:** 15
- **Requires:** Soshoryu

**Effects:** You perform a multi-throw technique using weapons stored inside one or more scrolls. The Chakra cost is equal to whatever the Stamina cost would normally be. Your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus is used in place of your Physical Damage Bonus.

You may target any number of people within range, each with separate multi-throws. This has the same cost regardless of how many people you target, but you must use the full number of weapons. For example, if you throw 10 shuriken at 4 people, you would need 40 shuriken total. This may not be used with senbon, nor may it be used to perform called shots.

Alternatively, by doubling the number of weapons, you can make this an Area 10 attack that hits everything indiscriminately. By quadrupling the number of weapons required, you can make it Area 20 centered on yourself. In either case, the severity of wounds inflicted is reduced by one additional rank.

_Example: If you have 80 DEX, you could throw 16 shuriken to hit everyone in Area 10, with the equivalent of an 8-shuriken multi-throw. The increase in damage bonus based on the number of weapons thrown would be (8 × 0.5) = 4, not (16 × 0.5) = 8._

This technique cannot have Sleeping Moon applied to it.

**–( Netetsuki no Jutsu — Sleeping Moon Technique )–**

An exceptionally useful technique, Sleeping Moon approaches the much-desired concept of a smart seal: one that can be easily deactivated without requiring extensive effort to replace. It is not a seal itself, but rather a set of modifications that can be applied to almost any advanced sealing technique. Simpler seals are too straightforward to incorporate these alterations.

- **Chakra:** +10 + (X × 3)
- **Seal Speed:** +10

**Effects:** This supplements another sealing technique of C-rank or higher. When applied, you specify an activation condition and a deactivation condition. Possible triggers include:

- Someone passes within up to 10 yards of it.
- There is nobody within up to 10 yards of it.
- A certain word is spoken or noise is made.
- Someone touches the seal.
- A specific person touches the seal.

If keyed to touch activation or deactivation by specific people, you can specify up to X people, where X is Research ranks/5, who the seal will recognize by chakra signature. All of those people must be present when you apply the seal.

When the activation condition occurs, the seal activates. When the deactivation condition occurs, the seal becomes dormant; none of its effects apply until it is reactivated, though it remains visible. Removing the seal augmented by Sleeping Moon also removes this portion of the seal. Alternatively, the Sleeping Moon seal could be removed, leaving the original seal in place but uncontrollable.

**–( Raikou Kenka — Lightning Blade Creation )–**

A special ninja tool summoning technique, this allows weapons or other tools to be sealed with a single symbol in a scroll or, more commonly, on clothing. Touching the symbol and willing it active is all it takes to summon the sealed items.

- **Chakra:** 28
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 45
- **Special:** Exclusive; Self only

**Effects:** This seals up to CHA/5 items in a seal applied to the target item.

You may summon weapons sealed with this technique as a Speed 0 action. For melee weapons, this replaces the regular action to draw the weapon. For thrown weapons, the first time you use this technique to unseal and immediately attack with a thrown weapon, it counts as a surprise attack. Any weapon or item in Equipment may be stored in these seals, though each seal may store only a single type of item, such as kunai, wakizashi, or smoke bombs.

**–( Torii Fuuin — Sacred Gate Seal )–**

A seal placed on the user's body, typically on the palm or back of the hand, generates a temporary but powerful defensive lattice of chakra. The stronger the technique it is used against, the more taxing it becomes on the user.

- **Chakra:** 25
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 30
- **Special:** Exclusive; Self only

**Effects:** You seal your own chakra somewhere on your body, primed to form a defensive barrier when called upon.

Using this seal, you can Block as a Chakra 10 + (X × 4), Speed 6, Seal Speed 8 Interrupt. This can be used against any attack you could ordinarily Block. If used against a technique of D-rank or below, or basic attacks, X is zero. Otherwise, X equals the number of ranks the attack is above D. This technique negates 25% of the block reduction normally applied by weapons or Raiton, but you must increase X by 1 to utilize the Endurance ability with it.

Sleeping Moon cannot be placed on this seal.

**–( Tsuiseki Fuuin — Tracking Seal )–**

A very useful seal, though one with significant limitations. When applied to a person, the seal vanishes almost immediately, becoming invisible and virtually undetectable, even to those capable of seeing chakra. The one who applied the seal will then be able to sense the approximate location of the sealed individual, allowing them to be tracked across any distance.

- **Chakra:** 10, raised to 25 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 14
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** If you hit, you roll Espionage + 5 opposed by your target's Espionage. If you succeed, they believe you simply struck them with a punch or palm strike. If you fail, they realize you used some sort of jutsu on them, though they cannot identify its effect.

Once applied, the seal is invisible and undetectable. It will only be discovered by a sealing expert who knows to look for it. These seals have been known to go undetected for years.

By forming a handseal and concentrating for several seconds, you know the direction and approximate distance of the person you are tracking from your current location. This does not provide any bonuses in combat; while it tells you that your target is nearby, it lacks the precision at short distances to help you discover an opponent hidden in stealth. There is no limit on how often you may activate a Tracking Seal, though you must stop and concentrate each time, making it impossible to do while moving.

**–( Tsuyome Fuuin — Strengthening Seal )–**

A simple and reliable seal, useful when conventional locks and walls are not sturdy enough, or when one wishes to obscure certain areas from those who can see or sense chakra from a distance. The user writes the appropriate seals on paper tags in their own blood, applies them to the edges of the surface being sealed, and then performs the handseals. The tags reinforce the structure, ensuring simple ninjutsu cannot overcome them.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Speed:** 16
- **Seal Speed:** 25
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** This may be applied to any surface up to CHA/10 square yards, and no thicker than CHA/10 inches. You may divide the area by any whole number to multiply the thickness limitation by that much. The seal is visible, though only on the side it is applied to, and it can only be removed from that side. The surface gains the user's CHA × 10 HP and has Damage Reduction equal to the user's CHA, which cannot be bypassed or reduced.

This seal can only be applied to flat, stationary surfaces such as walls, floors, and ceilings. A stationary container such as a safe, lockbox, or chest is also a valid target. When you apply this seal, you may specify a time for it to end, such as two days from now. The seal will last until that time arrives, or until it is removed by some method such as Five Elements Unseal. The person who placed the seal can simply peel the tags away to destroy it.

If the surface includes a closable door, hatch, window, or similar, this strengthening effect applies to those surfaces as well. Additionally, any entryway or container sealed in this way cannot be opened; no amount of physical force or lockpicking can overcome the locks or hinges, and the hinges cannot be dismantled.

Chakra Sensors cannot detect anyone within an area enclosed by a Strengthening Seal unless they are inside that same area, which likewise prevents them from sensing outside. Byakugan cannot see through a Strengthening Seal applied to an area, as the chakra dispersed throughout blocks their vision in both directions. The Sensor or Byakugan can sense the location of the tags if they are on the same side as them, which makes the technique obvious for what it is.

## B-Rank

**–( Fuuyoso Houin — Element Sealing Method )–**

Named for its primary use of putting out fires, this sealing technique allows for the containment of elemental matter of all kinds. Proper seals must be written on a scroll, followed by the correct handseals. Once the technique is complete, the chosen element vanishes and is sealed within the scroll, with the kanji for seal appearing on it.

- **Chakra:** 20 + (X × 2)
- **Range:** 30
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 20

**Effects:** You seal any of the five basic elements inside scrolls. Up to CHA gallons or CHA/5 square yards of elemental matter can be sealed this way. This works with both chakra-based and natural elemental matter, but it lacks offensive capacity. If you try to remove wind from an enclosed area, you do not create a vacuum; you simply still the air and thin it out. Similarly, you cannot remove earth from under someone to trip them, though they may find themselves standing in a hole.

You can also use this technique to immediately end any status effect based on an element being physically upon someone, such as Immobilize or Ignite, with X being the number of people you can do this to, to a maximum of 1 + Chakra Control Ranks/5.

Alternatively, this jutsu may end an Upkeep or other persistent effect created by any element you have aptitude for which affects people other than the user. X is equal to the higher of the effect's Rank (E = 1, D = 2, etc.) or Upkeep cost. Each use of this jutsu may only end one effect, and nothing prevents someone from activating that jutsu again.

Each use requires a blank scroll for the element to be sealed in. Once sealed, the elemental matter is effectively destroyed; it cannot be released, and the scroll cannot be reused. Sleeping Moon cannot be used with this technique.

**–( Keiyaku Fuuin — Contract Seal )–**

Implemented on a summoner, this removes any control they have over their summoned creature and, in most cases, temporarily nullifies the contract altogether.

- **Chakra:** 15, raised to 30 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 16
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** Any summoning contracts the victim possesses are considered null and void until this seal is removed. In most cases, this will forcibly de-summon anything they have already summoned. Defensive summoning techniques used against this automatically fail.

**–( Ketsumei no Tsurugi — Sword of Blood Oath )–**

This seal creates a modified version of the blood pacts shinobi form with summoned creatures. Rather than a powerful and intelligent animal, however, this contract is made with a favored weapon.

- **Chakra:** 40
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 40
- **Special:** Exclusive; Self only

**Effects:** You can seal a single weapon with this technique. The weapon vanishes, and the seal manifests somewhere on your body, usually on the forearms. You can summon the sealed weapon as a Speed 0 Interrupt, already drawn. As a Speed 0 action, you may return it to the seal, causing it to vanish in a puff of smoke; you must actually have the weapon in hand to do so.

If the weapon is broken or taken more than a mile away from you, it vanishes in a puff of smoke. In this case, it will not be available for summoning for one hour. However, it is for most intents and purposes indestructible and impossible to lose permanently. The seal is invisible while the weapon is stored within it, but becomes and remains visible on your body so long as the weapon is withdrawn.

**–( Shinsei Chinmoku Fuuin — Sacred Silence Seal )–**

Often used as a so-called loyalty seal, this has seen moderate popularity among clandestine and extremist organizations within the ninja villages, as well as a means of ensuring that double agents do not become triple agents.

- **Chakra:** 15, raised to 30 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 18
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** When you apply this seal to a person, specify a topic. This may be as broad as anything involving Sand village or as narrow as the new jutsu you saw me use.

The victim may not attempt to communicate about that topic in any way, be it through speech, writing, or other methods. Intent is what matters here; if they think they are giving information about the subject, they are unable to. The same restrictions apply regarding this seal itself. While the seal prevents the victim from showing it off, it takes the form of a hexagram somewhere on their torso, back, or tongue, where it cannot be seen without someone checking for it, and is easily identifiable by anyone with Seal Training.

You may, when applying this seal, specify one exception to its restrictions. For example, you could say other Leaf nin, in which case the person with this seal could talk to other Leaf nin about the sealed topic, but no one else.

This seal trumps any genjutsu short of the Poison Truth Technique.

**–( Moji Hyouki no Jutsu — Rune Inscribing Technique )–**

A tricky seal, this allows the user to mold the chakra necessary for any ninjutsu they know and freeze it, holding it primed but inactive inside a seal. Once a trigger condition occurs, the jutsu is released. Use of this technique to prepare explosive Katon techniques and leave them around as a practical joke is strongly discouraged.

- **Chakra:** 15 + X/2
- **Speed:** 10, Delay 5
- **Seal Speed:** Special
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** When you use Moji Hyouki, select another ninjutsu you are able to perform, specifying all variable parameters at that time. The X for Moji Hyouki refers to the Chakra cost of the ninjutsu you choose, which cannot have a cost other than Chakra Exhaustion, such as Willpower or Stamina. The seal can draw on ambient gallons of water to fuel sealed Suiton techniques and stores the necessary weapons for and foregoes the need for scrolls when used with other Fuinjutsu techniques, namely Twin Rising Dragons, Heavenly Chain of Destruction, and Attached Meteor.

Moji Hyouki's Seal Speed is equal to the chosen jutsu's modified Seal Speed plus half the technique's base speed. The seal begins to prime upon its completion but cannot be activated until the end of Moji Hyouki's delay.

Moji Hyouki can be placed on any reasonably flat, solid surface at least one foot across in each direction, such as a floor, box, or reasonably flat ground. Notably, it explicitly cannot be placed on any object listed in the Equipment section. Attempts to store an object inscribed with Moji Hyouki inside a confinement scroll or other sealing technique will immediately activate it. Likewise, any object with Moji Hyouki inscribed on it cannot be safely carried into or wielded in combat; jostling the seal in this way will immediately activate it.

If you are hidden when you use Moji Hyouki, the seal itself is hidden at the same Stealth TN as you. You yourself are revealed from its use, but you are not revealed until the end of its delay, unlike normal. If you are not hidden when you use Moji Hyouki, anyone who sees you use it will know you have placed some sort of seal; anyone who knows this technique will recognize it and be capable of identifying the activation condition, but not the technique placed in it. You may choose whether the seal vanishes or remains visible after it is placed.

When it is placed, an activation condition is specified. The four possible activation conditions are:

- Someone comes within five yards of the seal.
- Someone touches the seal. The A-rank Five Elements Unseal will not trigger a touch-activated Moji Hyouki.
- If the seal is on a door or container, it may be set to activate when that door or container is opened.
- Upon command: As a Speed 2, Chakra 5 action, or as a Speed +2, Chakra +5 link to any other action, you may spend AP equal to half the sealed jutsu's base speed to activate it. Activated this way, you may set a delay between 0 and Chakra Control Ranks/5 before the seal takes effect.

When the seal activates, the stored jutsu immediately takes effect unless it has a delay, in which case its normal Delay begins. If this is an offensive technique, it will be aimed at the person who set it off, or at a specified target if triggered by the command action. Offensive techniques used this way use your Accuracy at the time of creation, unmodified by status effects, wounds, or temporary bonuses or penalties, though Fatigue penalties still apply.

Jutsu with an Upkeep cannot be placed within Moji Hyouki. Jutsu with a range or effect that only applies to oneself will only apply to and work on the object it was placed on.

## A-Rank

**–( Tensha Fuuin — Transcription Seal )–**

A rarely seen technique, this seals the necessary chakra patterns for a specific jutsu inside a person, keeping it perpetually ready within the seal. When a certain condition is met, the seal activates and the technique triggers.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 35
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** When used, declare another technique you know, pay its cost, and specify a condition. The technique immediately and involuntarily activates when that condition is met. Possible conditions include when you see the daimyo or when you hear the word orange. A target can be specified within reason, such as the daimyo or whoever said that word, but must be keyed to a sight or sound.

The technique stored inside a Transcription Seal may not be a Taijutsu or have an Upkeep. Techniques which use your Taijutsu Accuracy may only target the person the seal is applied to, not anyone else. It may not have any cost other than Chakra Exhaustion, and if it has a variable Chakra cost, you can set the variable to a maximum of your bonus to Chakra Exhaustion rolls unmodified by Fatigue or temporary or conditional modifiers. If you had a base bonus of +13, you could set X = 13, or to X's maximum, whichever is lower.

If the technique has material components such as a scroll or weapon, you must have them when placing the seal, and they are stored until the seal is released. Requisition spent on them is considered locked and cannot be reallocated while the seal remains. Elemental Ninjutsu with material components such as Ice, Sand, or Water require enough material at the time of the seal's placement, but the seal can only use an amount equal to the limitations of the person it is placed on; excess stored into the seal is added to the battlefield harmlessly.

Clan techniques require the person they are placed on to be of that clan, or else they fail to activate, with the exception of Elemental Ninjutsu.

If it is a Genjutsu or Sealing technique, it can apply to the person the seal was placed on or the person who triggered it, as you prefer. Sealing Techniques which are Self Only cannot be placed on others with Transcription. A person may only have one Transcription Seal on them at a time. This seal is invisible and undetectable except by a Fuuinjutsu expert who knows how to look for it.

**–( Gogyou Fuuin — Five Elements Seal )–**

This jutsu produces a powerful seal that disrupts the flow of chakra in the target. In most cases, this is crippling. It was a perfect punishment to warlords who pillaged a large swath of land, the commonfolk then complete the circle of life with this defenseless tyrant.

- **Chakra:** 20, raised to 40 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 18
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** If you hit, the victim suffers a -20 penalty to all Chakra Exhaustion rolls and has their Chakra Control skill ranks reduced by 15, which can also temporarily lower their ranks in abilities such as Water Walking. Any jutsu with Upkeeps they have activated end immediately due to the disruption of their chakra.

If they fail a Chakra Exhaustion roll, they fail to use the jutsu they were trying to, but still advance a Fatigue category as normal. Furthermore, even if they succeed, they must pass a Chakra Control check against the technique's Chakra cost or fail anyway.

**–( Gogyou Kaiin — Five Elements Unseal )–**

The opposite of the Five Elements Seal, but of much broader utility, this technique can remove any seal of equal or lesser strength.

- **Chakra:** 20, raised to 40 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 15

**Effects:** This can target a single seal on an object or person. On an object, it typically does not require an Accuracy roll. On a person, it requires a Called Shot to the part of the body where the seal is located. On a hit, that seal is removed. This affects any seals described in this section. This technique may be able to remove seals not described in the PHB, or it may not.

**–( Fuuja Houin — Evil Sealing Method )–**

Not all seals can be removed easily. This circumvents that by suppressing Fuuinjutsu, Juuinjutsu, or any sort of chakra-based marking on a person. The technique's greatest failing is its dependence on the recipient to provide some of the strength behind it, meaning they can overcome its effects at will, making it useless for offensive applications.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 40

**Effects:** This suppresses any seal in this section of the PHB, and many which are not. While the seal will not be removed, it will not activate or have any of its normal effects as long as the Evil Sealing Method remains in place. This technique is dependent on the recipient's willpower; all a person has to do is focus and the Evil Sealing Method will be disabled, until they spend 1 Willpower to reactivate it. Virtually any long-term chakra-based effect, even those that are not actually Fuuinjutsu, can be suppressed by it. Even the effects and abilities of bloodlines can be sealed.

**–( Tsute Fuuin — Influence Seal )–**

This seal was originally created for servants of the Daimyo, ensuring those closest to them could not spy or sabotage the feudal lords. Over time, shinobi adapted it to suit their own needs, which naturally meant it found use in combat.

- **Chakra:** 20, raised to 36 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 20
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** If you hit, make an opposed Espionage roll. If you succeed, the seal is placed without the target knowing.

The seal is inactive by default, but as a Speed 4, Chakra 10 + X interrupt in response to any non-interrupt action they take, you can create a temporary paralysis which turns their own chakra against them, preventing the action they were going to perform and forcing them to make a Chakra (X × 2) roll. If they fail, they are not fatigued, and they do not roll the Chakra or Stamina rolls associated with their action, as they are prevented from performing it. Their IC is not advanced by the action's Speed, and instead they suffer a Stun 5. X has a maximum value of your Chakra Control Ranks.

Every time a victim has previously had to roll against this seal's effects, they gain a +3 bonus to their Chakra Exhaustion roll to overcome its effects. After they have succeeded on one such roll, the seal is forced into remission and will not affect them temporarily; it cannot be reapplied or reactivated for the rest of that fight. Once the seal is subdued, it is, as far as the victim can tell, gone entirely, its effects having been broken. However, after 24 hours have passed, the seal can be reactivated by its user per the normal rules, with the cumulative bonus to resist it reset. A Fuuinjutsu expert will be able to, if they know someone has had this seal applied to them, find and remove it using Five Elements Unseal.

**–( Nenshou Fuuin — Burning Seal )–**

A highly specialized and effective sealing technique used to prevent an individual from handling weapons. Though it is applied as a single seal, it appears on both of the victim's hands, clearly recognizable as some form of ninja sealing technique. Whenever the recipient holds a weapon, the seal activates. The lines of the seal glow a dark, heated red, and the victim's skin begins to burn and smolder. Within seconds, their flesh smokes as they are baked alive. If one is willful enough not to drop the weapon, their arms will soon be reduced to charred husks.

- **Chakra:** 20, raised to 40 if it hits
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 16
- **Special:** Exclusive

**Effects:** If you hit, you apply the seal, which activates whenever the person it is applied to holds a weapon. After 10 IC, a Minor Energy Wound is inflicted to whichever arm they were holding the weapon with. In the case of a two-handed weapon, this affects both arms. Dropping the weapon ends this technique's effects immediately. If they are currently suffering from a wound inflicted by the Burning Seal, it instead inflicts a wound of one higher category on that arm: Major, then Severe, then Critical.

For example, if a shinobi drew a nodachi and held it in their right hand starting on IC 12, on IC 22 they would suffer a Minor Energy wound to their right arm. If they then began two-handing it, on IC 32 they would have a Minor on their left arm and a Major on their right. On IC 42, those would become Major and Severe. On IC 52, Severe and Critical. By IC 62, they would have a Critical Energy wound on both arms.
