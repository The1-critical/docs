---
title: "General Ninjutsu"
---

General Ninjutsu is the foundation upon which all shinobi training is built. These are the techniques taught in every academy, the tricks that separate a ninja from a civilian. From the humble Clone Technique to the life-saving Substitution, these are the tools that keep a shinobi alive long enough to learn the rest. Civilians and common folk have pioneered many of these techniques over the years, what starts as a farmer's trick or a merchant's sleight of hand often finds its way into the shinobi arsenal. The line between civilian ingenuity and ninja technique is thinner than most would like to admit.

## E-Rank

**–( Bunshin no Jutsu — Clone Technique )–**

The first lesson every academy student learns, and the last thing some enemies ever see. There is a reason this technique has survived unchanged for generations: it works. A Bunshin is not solid, not dangerous, not capable of independent thought, it is a flicker of chakra shaped into a human silhouette, a decoy, a distraction, a momentary hesitation in your opponent's reflexes that might be the only opening you need. The Confederacy's training manuals describe it as "the foundation upon which all deception is built." The manuals do not mention that most shinobi who die in their first real battle die because they forgot a foundation is only as strong as the person standing on it.

- **Chakra:** 5 + 3 per clone
- **Speed:** 6 + 1 per clone
- **Seal Speed:** 4 + 1 per clone

**Effects:**

You create up to three intangible copies of yourself. These clones are visual illusions only, they cast no shadow, leave no footprints, cannot interact with the physical world. They can, however, speak, moving their mouths and producing sound, which makes them significantly more convincing than simple afterimages.

When you first create your clones, you may choose to be considered Hidden Among Clones (see below). Your opponents must determine which figure is the real you. Any targeting that relies on visual identification requires a roll of `1d(1 + number of clones)`. On a result of 1, the attack targets you; on any other result, it targets a clone. Clones cannot dodge, parry, or use jutsu of any kind. A clone struck by any attack or effect that deals damage or an effect is immediately destroyed. If you are hit, your clones remain but are rendered largely useless, your opponents have identified you and will not be fooled again by this particular set of clones unless you take action to re-conceal yourself.

Creating new clones (whether through this technique or a different clone type) immediately destroys all your pre-existing clones.

**Actions:**

**Hide Among Clones** _(Speed 4, or Speed +6)_

You perform a quick shell-game maneuver, scrambling your position among your clones. If your opponents have singled you out, and you have room to maneuver, you may attempt to re-conceal yourself. Roll Stealth against each observing opponent's Awareness. Success means you have once again hidden yourself among the clones. Performing this as a standalone action is Speed 4; you may also link it to another action (such as an attack) by increasing that action's Speed by 6.

**Observe** _(Speed 4)_

Any combatant may take a moment to study you and your clones, looking for discrepancies — clones not casting shadows, not disturbing dust or grass, moving slightly out of sync. This action allows an Awareness roll against your Chakra Control. On a success, they can distinguish you from all your clones and recognize those clones as illusions. This effect persists until you successfully Hide Among Clones again.

**–( Henge no Jutsu — Transformation Technique )–**

To the ordinary civilian, this is the most mysterious and feared capability ninja possess: the power to wear another face, to become someone else entirely. The academy teaches it as a basic technique, but the modern intelligence apparatus knows better. A Henge in the hands of a skilled infiltrator can compromise a compound, extract a target, or start a war. In the hands of a careless genin, it gets them killed. The technique is not perfect, it never has been, never will be, but it does not need to be. It only needs to be convincing long enough for you to do what you came to do.

- **Chakra:** 5
- **Upkeep:** 0
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 4
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:**

You reshape your appearance to match that of another person or object. When imitating a person, your voice, facial features, and general build conform to the target's. However, the technique has inherent limitations: your height, weight, and physical capabilities do not change. A tall ninja cannot become a small child; a heavy one cannot become a slender courtier. The illusion is visual and auditory, not physical.

Transforming into an object grants you its shape and basic visual characteristics, but no functional properties. A rock would be visible as a rock, but it would not be hard enough to withstand a sword swing. A bird would look like a bird but could not fly. A shuriken would be aerodynamic but not sharp enough to cut. You are, fundamentally, still you — just disguised.

**Detection:**

When someone interacts with you while you are transformed, they may attempt to see through the Henge. This is an opposed Espionage roll:

| Situation | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Casual acquaintance | +5 to the observer's roll |
| Close friend, family member, or intimate partner | +10 to the observer's roll |
| Observer already suspicious | Additional +5 |
| Observer has reason to doubt your identity (e.g., you don't know a password) | Automatic detection |

If you are disguised as an object and stationary, any ninja who comes within 10 yards may notice the deception. You make an Espionage roll with a +10 bonus, opposed by their Awareness. Success means they pass without noticing. Failure means they realize something is wrong.

**Breaking the Technique:**

Henge ends immediately if you take damage of any kind. This is an inherent flaw in the technique, the chakra maintaining the illusion cannot withstand the disruption of physical harm.

**–( Kawarimi no Jutsu — Substitution Technique )–**

The third of the three foundational techniques, and arguably the one that keeps more genin alive than the other two combined. Kawarimi is not teleportation. It is not a jutsu in the conventional sense. It is a timing trick, a misdirection, a refusal to be where the attack lands. The academy teaches it with wooden logs and a straight face, and generations of genin have accepted this without questioning where the logs come from. The logs are not the point. The point is that you are no longer where you were a moment ago, and your opponent's blade or fireball or curse is striking something that is not you.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** +4
- **Seal Speed:** 4

**Effects:**

This technique is activated after you have attempted to Dodge an attack but have not succeeded fully. Specifically, Kawarimi may only be used when partial success would apply — that is, when your Dodge failed by 1 or more points, but the attack would not deal full damage. If your Dodge failed entirely (no partial success applies), Kawarimi cannot be used.

When activated, Kawarimi adds +4 Speed and its Seal Speed (modified by your DEX and abilities) to your Dodge action. The total Speed of this combined Dodge + Kawarimi action must still be valid as an Interrupt against the incoming attack (half or less of the attack's Speed). You may spend AP to reduce Kawarimi's Speed, but you cannot retroactively reduce the original Dodge's Speed.

If the action is valid, you may spend 5 AP to treat the Dodge as a success — you fully avoid the attack and its effects.

Each time you use Kawarimi in the same battle, its AP cost increases by 5. The second use costs 10 AP, the third costs 15 AP, and so on. This represents your opponents becoming wise to your trick and the technique becoming harder to execute under pressure.

You may immediately attempt to Hide (see Stealth). This is a Speed 0 action, but your opponent is allowed an immediate Awareness roll against your Stealth TN. This roll does not benefit from the cumulative bonus they would receive for consecutive Search attempts.

If Kawarimi's Seal Speed is reduced to 0, you may perform it without handseals.

**–( Ninshiki Kaado — Ninja Info Cards )–**

Information is the currency of the shinobi world, and the Confederacy's intelligence apparatus spends it freely. Ninja Info Cards are a relic of an older age, a simpler time when a scroll and a stamp were enough to move secrets between villages. They still have their uses. A blank card, encoded with chakra, can carry maps, faces, codes, or the contents of a dead drop. It can be carried through a checkpoint without raising suspicion, handed off in a crowded market, or hidden in a crack in the wall. The technique itself is simple; the applications are limited only by the user's imagination.

- **Chakra:** 5
- **Range:** Touch
- **Speed:** 4
- **Seal Speed:** 7

**Effects:**

This technique allows you to encode information onto specially prepared cards using chakra. Each card must be encoded individually, resting on a flat surface as you channel your chakra through it. Any information can be stored, text, images, maps, diagrams, encoded messages, though the limited surface area of a card restricts the quantity.

To retrieve the information, you must use this technique again on the same card. The information can only be accessed by the person who originally encoded it, unless you deliberately encode it with a shared chakra signature. You may designate up to CHA/10 other individuals whose chakra signatures can read the card.

Cards encoded with this technique appear blank to anyone who does not know what to look for. They are a staple of Konoha intelligence operations and can be procured from any major village's quartermaster.

## D-Rank

**–( Genjutsu Kai — Genjutsu Release )–**

The most common and reliable method of breaking an illusion, Genjutsu Kai is taught to every academy student and forgotten by most of them until the moment they need it most. The technique is simple in concept, brutal in execution: you halt the flow of chakra through your body, and then surge it outward, forcing the foreign chakra of the illusion out of your system. It is uncomfortable, exhausting, and absolutely essential. The Confederacy's military doctrine states that "no shinobi should enter combat without Genjutsu Kai." The same doctrine states that "no shinobi should enter combat without allies they trust to use it on them." There is a reason for both.

- **Chakra:** 10 + X
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 5

**Effects:**

Genjutsu Kai may be used on yourself or on another willing person. To use it on someone else, you must be touching them. The target immediately receives another defensive Genjutsu roll against any illusion affecting them, with a bonus of X.

The maximum value of X is `5 + (XP / 300)`, rounded down.

When used against an Area of Effect Genjutsu, the user makes a Genjutsu Offense roll against the original roll made by the creator of the technique. On a success, the entire Area of Effect Genjutsu is dispelled.

**–( Shunshin no Jutsu — Body Flicker Technique )–**

A ninja who cannot move is a ninja who cannot survive. Shunshin is the answer to that problem, a burst of chakra that propels the user across the battlefield in a blur, faster than the eye can follow and faster than most opponents can track. It is not teleportation, despite what civilians believe. It is speed, pure and simple, honed to the point where it resembles something else entirely. The Ninja field manuals recommend Shunshin for three purposes: closing distance, escaping encirclement, and, when all else fails, running away. The first two are taught. The third is implied but never written down.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Speed:** 2 or +2
- **Tags:** Link

**Effects:**

You move a distance determined by the number of Action Points you spend on this technique. For every 1 AP spent, you may move as far as you would in a Speed 1 Move action. The formula for movement distance is:

`Movement in yards = (AGI × AP) / 50`

There is no limit on the number of AP you may spend.

**As a Link:**

- **Dodge Link:** Use Shunshin in conjunction with a Dodge against an Area of Effect attack. Your movement is determined by AP spent rather than by the attack's Speed. This allows you to determine how far you travel toward the edge of the effect.
- **Take the Hit Link:** Use Shunshin with Take the Hit. The distance you can close to protect an ally is determined by AP spent rather than the Speed of your Take the Hit action.

**–( Kanashibari no Jutsu — Temporary Paralysis Technique )–**

There is a moment of perfect vulnerability before every attack: the instant when a shinobi commits to a movement and cannot change course. Kanashibari exploits that moment. The user sends a surge of chakra into the target's nervous system, disrupting the flow of signals from the brain to the body. For a few heartbeats, the victim is locked in place, not physically restrained, not held by chains or ropes, but simply unable to command their limbs to obey. It is not a killing technique. It is a setup technique, an opening, a moment of hesitation that the user and their allies can exploit. Interrogators are particularly fond of it. They have perfected the timing.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Range:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Status:** Immobilize 2 + Special
- **Tags:** Internal

**Effects:**

Your target makes a Resistance roll against your Chakra Control. On a failure, they are inflicted with an Immobilization penalty of 2, plus an additional 1 for every 4 points you beat their roll by, to a maximum of Immobilize 6.

Each subsequent use of Kanashibari against the same target in the same battle grants them a cumulative +5 bonus to their Resistance roll. This represents their body learning to recognize and resist the disruption, making the technique progressively less effective.

**–( Utsusemi no Jutsu — Cicada Shell Technique )–**

A ninja who can project their voice from nowhere is a ninja who has already won half the battle. Utsusemi is the foundation of that principle, a simple technique that allows the user to throw their voice across an area while concealing their presence entirely. To the target, the voice seems to come from all directions at once, impossible to locate, impossible to ignore. The technique does not amplify the voice; it merely makes it impossible to tell where it is coming from. This is unsettling enough for most purposes. Combined with Genjutsu and Stealth, it can create a psychological pressure that breaks even hardened veterans.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Upkeep:** 0 or 5
- **Area:** 10
- **Speed:** 2
- **Seal Speed:** 4
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:**

While hidden, you may speak as though your voice were emanating from all directions around you. This does not increase your range, your voice carries no farther than it would normally, but it makes it impossible for listeners to pinpoint your location. Activating Utsusemi while in Stealth does not break your concealment.

By increasing the Upkeep to 5, you may instead speak in a continuous stream, a monologue that never stops, never pauses, never allows a moment of silence. The words themselves do not matter; they can be threats, nonsense, recitations of old mission briefings, or simply the same phrase repeated until it loses all meaning. What matters is the absence of quiet. The target cannot use the Rest action while this effect is active. Additionally, their Stamina penalty increases by 1 for every 10 IC (capped at 5) that passes while they remain within radius of your voice, as the constant noise wears down their focus and drains their composure. This effect and the penalties end if you stop speaking or if the target moves beyond the range of your voice. A target who successfully locates you and breaks your Stealth ends this effect immediately.

**–( Haruka Bakuhatsu no Jutsu — Remote Detonation Technique )–**

Explosives are useful. They are also, by their nature, dangerous to the person using them. Remote Detonation solves that problem, or at least, it makes it less immediate. The technique allows a shinobi to set off multiple explosives from a distance, turning a set of inert tags into a coordinated demolition. It is not subtle. It is not quiet. It is, however, effective, and there are few problems that cannot be solved by the judicious application of enough explosions.

- **Chakra:** 10 + (X × 3)
- **Range:** 15 + (CHA / 10) yards
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 4 + (X × 2)

**Effects:**

You detonate up to X Exploding Tags within this technique's range. X has a maximum of Chakra Control Ranks / 5.

This technique works only on tags that you have primed — meaning you must have prepared them specifically for this purpose. The tags explode with their normal delay, which you declare when you use this technique (as described in the Equipment chapter's Exploding Tags entry).

## C-Rank

**–( Chakra Bulk )–**

The body is a vessel. Chakra is the current that fills it. Most shinobi learn to direct that current outward, into fire, into water, into the shape of a blade. Chakra Bulking turns the current inward, flooding the muscles, the tendons, the bones themselves with raw, unrefined energy. It does not make you faster in the sense of moving more quickly; it makes you faster in the sense of reacting before your opponent has finished deciding what to do. The technique originated in Iwagakure's tunnel-fighting corps, where shinobi needed to move through narrow passages and react to threats in near-total darkness. The current version is refined, standardized, and issued to every ninja who requests it. The original version was written on a scrap of canvas by a dying man who had just killed a thousand enemies with his bare hands. The canvas is still in Iwa's archives. The bloodstains are still visible.

- **Chakra:** 12 + (3 × X)
- **Upkeep:** 2 × X
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:**

You gain a +X bonus to your Physical Damage Bonus and a +X bonus to Dodge rolls while you maintain Chakra Bulking. X has a maximum of 4.

**–( Soushuujin — Manipulating Attack Blades )–**

The kunai rises. It hovers, trembling, as though unsure whether it wants to obey. Then another joins it. Then another. A dozen blades float in a loose circle around the user, each one held in place by a thread of chakra thinner than a hair. A preferred tool for bounty hunters as it removes the stress of picking up weapons during the chase.

- **Chakra:** 10 + X
- **Upkeep:** X / 3 (rounded up)
- **Range:** 5 + CHA/4
- **Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:**

You control up to X kunai simultaneously (maximum CHA/10) and project them using your chakra. This functions as a multithrow, but with the following substitutions:

- The normal Stamina cost is replaced by an equivalent Chakra cost
- You use your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus instead of your Physical Damage Bonus
- Damage bonus per weapon is +0.25 instead of +0.5
- Anyone attempting to parry your Soushuujin multithrow takes a -X/3 penalty (rounded down) to their Parry roll

The kunai remain hovering at their positions as long as you maintain the Upkeep. They cannot be retrieved by anyone else while you maintain control. Effects that destroy the weapons (such as Housenka no Jutsu) will end this technique automatically.

Uniques and abilities that affect the number of weapons you can throw or the distance they can travel apply normally to Soushuujin. However, Improved Range and Quick Hands do not apply, the technique uses chakra manipulation rather than physical throwing strength.

**–( Kuchiyose no Jutsu — Summoning Technique )–**

The blood is drawn. The hand is pressed. The world shifts. In one place, a shinobi kneels, palm pressed to earth. In another, a creature stirs, ancient and vast, its scales scraping against the stone of its den. The contract binds them, the shinobi's blood to the creature's will, the creature's power to the shinobi's purpose. The technique itself is simple. The contracts are not. They are written in blood and sealed with intent, and breaking them carries consequences that no shinobi has survived to describe. The Confederacy's archives contain a single record of a breach of contract. The page is blank. The archivist who filed it is dead.

- **Chakra:** Varies
- **Speed:** Varies
- **Seal Speed:** Varies
- **Requires:** Summoning Contract

**Effects:**

You may summon a creature bound to you by a blood contract. The specifics of each summon, its Chakra cost, Speed, and Seal Speed, are determined by the contract itself (see the Summoning Contracts section of the PHB). You can supplement this by also cutting a larger cut on your palm to generate more chakra for your summon.

To perform this technique, you must offer a few drops of fresh blood. Some shinobi make a small cut on their palm or arm; others bite the pad of their thumb. Dedicated summoners often carry scars in those locations, small and neat, almost ceremonial.

If you have a Summoning Contract talent, you may learn Kuchiyose no Jutsu without meeting the normal requirements for learning C-rank ninjutsu. The contract itself acts as the prerequisite.

**–( Kikkake Bakuhatsu no Jutsu — Triggered Detonation Technique )–**

Explosives are useful. Traps are better. A trap that explodes when you want it to is good. A trap that explodes when your enemy wants it to is not. The Triggered Detonation Technique solves this problem by allowing the user to set tags to detonate in advance, based on proximity and timing. It was developed by a Konoha quartermaster who was tired of replacing equipment that had been stolen from supply depots. His first prototype detonated three hours early and destroyed a storage shed. His second prototype worked perfectly, and the thieves were never found. The quartermaster was promoted. The shed was rebuilt. The technique spread.

- **Chakra:** 15 + (X × 5)
- **Speed:** 20 + (X × 10)

**Effects:**

You place X tags, up to a maximum of Chakra Control ranks / 5. One or more tags may be designated as "triggers." When you perform this technique, specify:

- A range between 0 and (Chakra Control ranks / 5) yards
- A time between 10 IC and (10 + Chakra Control ranks) IC

Whenever someone enters within that range of a trigger tag, all tags linked to that trigger will detonate after the specified time has elapsed.

- Tags placed with Triggered Detonation cannot be detonated by Remote Detonation
- Tags cannot be part of more than one "set"
- All tags linked by a single use must be within Chakra Control ranks yards of each other

**–( Kawarimi: Kage no Gyou — Substitution: Shadow Swap )—**

The log trick works. It has worked for generations. But logs are predictable. They are the expected response, the standard answer, the technique every genin learns and every chuunin has seen a hundred times. The Shadow Swap is something else. It is an evolution of the basic substitution, a refinement for shinobi who need to be somewhere their opponent does not expect. The technique was developed by the infamous copy ninja.

- **Chakra:** 12 + (2 × X)
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** +4
- **Seal Speed:** 4
- **Requires:** Kawarimi no Jutsu, at least one active clone (Bunshin, Mud, Rock, Lightning, Water, or Shadow)

**Effects:**

This technique is activated in the same circumstances as Kawarimi, after you have failed to Dodge an attack, but only when partial success would apply. Instead of substituting with a log and then attempting to Hide, you swap places with one of your active clones.

When activated, choose one of your clones that is within CHA/10 yards (minimum 5). You immediately exchange positions with that clone, assuming you are now 10 yards from your previous location after the swap. The clone is destroyed in the process, struck by the attack you just avoided, and the attack resolves against the clone as though it had been you.

This technique requires an Espionage roll against your target:

- You roll Espionage vs their Awareness

If you succeed, the swap is seamless. The target is completely fooled, they struck a clone and do not realize the real you has moved. You are considered to have successfully moved to the clone's position without breaking Stealth (if you were hidden before the attack). If you were not hidden, you are now in the clone's position, and your opponent does not automatically know you have moved. The clone is destroyed.

If you fail, the target recognizes the swap. They know you moved, and they know your new position. The clone is still destroyed, but you gain no concealment benefit. The attack's partial success damage is still applied to the clone, not to you.

Like Kawarimi, the AP cost of Shadow Swap increases with each use in the same battle. The base cost is 10 AP, and each subsequent use adds 10 AP (20 AP for the second, 30 AP for the third, etc.). This cost is paid regardless of whether the opposed Espionage roll succeeds or fails.

The clone used for this technique is destroyed, following the normal rules for that clone type:

- **Bunshin:** Destroyed (illusionary, no special effects)
- **Mud Clone:** Destroyed; attacker suffers -4 Immobilization if unarmed
- **Rock Clone:** Destroyed; attacker takes no special damage, but the clone is knocked prone if it hasn't been destroyed yet, they have no reason to think its not the real you
- **Lightning Clone:** Destroyed; attacker takes 100×1d2 damage automatically
- **Water Clone:** Destroyed; water used to create it is lost
- **Shadow Clone:** You can choose for the clone to put the attacker in a grapple if the attack was a melee ranged one, it is assumed their CC is 1 and the attacker is grabbed
- **Other clone:** Wood clones, Clay clones follow similar rules to Shadow clones unless destroyed

If you have more than one clone, you choose which clone to swap with when you activate the technique.

## B-Rank

**–( Shoushagan no Jutsu — Vanishing Facial Copy Technique )—**

The face is an illusion. The skin is a costume. The body beneath is just meat. These are the uncomfortable truths that underpin the Vanishing Facial Copy Technique. Developed by a rogue medic-nin who had grown tired of the limitations of Henge, this technique allows the user to steal a dead person's face and wear it as their own. The face does not decay. The chakra does not fade. It simply sits there, and you speak, and the words emerge from a mouth that no longer has a heartbeat. The medic-nin who developed the technique is still at large. The Confederacy has been hunting him for eleven years. They have never gotten close. He wears a different face every time.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Speed:** 10

**Effects:**

You physically remove the face from a corpse and place it onto your own. The corpse must:

- Have died within the past 24 hours
- Not have suffered a Severe or higher Head wound
- Be relatively intact

Once applied, your voice and skin tone change to match the deceased. Your height, build, and gender do not change. The face merges seamlessly and is completely undetectable by doujutsu or chakra-sensing effects.

The effect can be maintained indefinitely, but a Major or worse Head wound will tear the stolen face, revealing your own skin beneath. If this happens, your face and skin tone return to normal. You may also discard the face at will, ending the technique — but a discarded face cannot be reused.

Learning this technique imposes a one-time +10 Infamy increase. The Confederacy does not approve of this technique, and its use is considered a sign that the user is willing to cross lines that most shinobi would not. Most serial killers are well known for using this technique, like the Butcher of the Grasslands and the mist's infamous Yoshinori, the Surturer who is still at large.

**–( Senri Bakuhatsu no Jutsu — Long-Range Detonation Technique )—**

Explosions are better at a distance. This is not a matter of preference; it is a matter of survival. The Long-Range Detonation Technique is a refinement of the D-rank version, extending both range and capacity. The Confederacy's ordinance division has tested the technique under controlled conditions. They have determined that the maximum effective range is sufficient bombardment.

- **Chakra:** 10 + (X × 5)
- **Range:** 15 + CHA/2
- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 6 + (X × 3)
- **Requires:** Remote Detonation Technique

**Effects:**

You detonate up to (Chakra Control ranks / 5) exploding tags within range. X is the number of tags detonated. In all other respects, this technique functions identically to the D-rank Remote Detonation Technique.

**–( Kekkai Houjin — Barrier Encampment Method )—**

Four tags. Four corners. One line that cannot be crossed without consequence. The Barrier Encampment Method is a trap that does not wait. It does not hesitate. It simply waits for someone to step over the boundary, and then it reminds them why boundaries exist. The technique has been used to protect supply depots, contain prisoners, and, on one infamous occasion, to capture a rogue jounin who had been evading capture for six months. The jounin walked into the barrier. The tags detonated. The jounin did not walk out.

- **Chakra:** 25
- **Range:** Special
- **Requires:** Triggered Detonation Technique
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:**

This technique requires several minutes of setup and cannot be used in combat. You place four exploding tags at the corners of a square or rectangle, facing inward. Each side of the square can be up to double the Area of the tags you are using in length.

If someone attempts to leave the area, moving away from the center, or entering and then turning around, the tags detonate 10 IC later. Anyone still inside the area at detonation takes the combined damage of three exploding tags, regardless of their position. The explosion is directed inward, so anyone outside the barrier is unharmed. Directional defenses (such as Earth Shore Return) are insufficient, as the explosion originates from multiple directions.

**–( Dokugiri — Poison Mist )—**

The lungs are not meant to be weapons. They are meant to breathe, to draw air into the body and sustain it. The user of Dokugiri has trained their lungs to do something else entirely, to draw in chakra, to mix it with oxygen, to transform it into a chemical weapon that emerges from the mouth as a thick, purplish fog. The technique requires knowledge of chemistry, poisons, and medicine. It requires precision. It requires a willingness to accept that the poison you create will not distinguish between friend and foe. The technique was developed during the Second War, when Kirigakure's forces were outnumbered and desperate. The poison mist was the only thing that held the line. The lines held. The soldiers who created the mist did not all survive.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Range:** 25
- **Area:** 20
- **Speed:** 24
- **Seal Speed:** 16
- **Requires:** 25 Chakra Control, 25 Medicine, 25 Research, 25 Toxicology
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:**

When you create the mist, roll the second highest of your Chakra Control, Medicine, Research, or Toxicology skill rolls. This is the TN to avoid being poisoned while in the cloud.

Anyone who is not poisoned by the cloud but makes a Stamina roll while inside it, or who remains in the cloud for 10 total IC (not consecutive), is exposed to the poison. Those already poisoned do not count time spent in the cloud toward its duration.

**Poison Effects:**

| Effect | Severity |
| --- | --- |
| Poison | Toxicology / 5 |
| Paralysis | Medicine / 6 |
| Shock | Medicine / 6 |
| Suffocation | Research / 10 (applied every 20 IC) |

The cloud imposes a (Chakra Control / 5) Visibility Penalty throughout its area.

**Duration:**

- Poison duration (once target exits the cloud): Sum of all four skill ranks
- Cloud duration: Twice the poison duration
- You are not immune to your own poison mist

**–( Hari Jizou — Needle Jizo )—**

Every shinobi knows the feeling of a hair standing on end. The sensation is involuntary, a reaction to threat, to danger, to something that tells the body: move, or die. The user of Hari Jizou has learned to control that response, to direct it, to weaponize the hair itself. The technique causes hair to grow rapidly, thickening and hardening into a forest of iron-sharp needles. The hair wraps around the user, protecting them like a living shield. The hair extends outward, cutting into anyone who draws near.

- **Chakra:** 15 + (X × 3)

**–( Toumegane no Jutsu — Telescope Technique )—**

**–( Nunoshibari no Jutsu — Cloth Binding Technique )—**

Cloth is not a weapon. It is not a restraint, not a barrier, not a thing of battle. It is soft, pliable, easily torn. That is the assumption, and it is wrong. The technique requires a long roll of cloth, usually fifty yards or more, which the user throws at their target. The technique was developed by a Konoha interrogator who needed a way to hold prisoners that did not require physical contact. The interrogator retired years ago. The technique remains in use. The prisoners do not leave.

- **Chakra:** 30
- **Upkeep:** 12
- **Range:** 20
- **Accuracy:** -8
- **Speed:** 20, Delay 10
- **Requires:** 50 yards of cloth

**Effects:**

While the cloth is thrown from a distance (and guided by chakra), it's relatively slow-moving and very obvious. If this hits, the target is immediately placed at a -10 immobilization penalty, which cannot be reduced or negated without breaking free of this technique. The person trapped inside is unable to take any non-Interrupt action other than struggling to escape. This is a Speed 4 action that allows for a grapple check to escape, with them rolling as the defender against you, using your NDB in place of PDB. Note that these seals are applied to the cloth bind itself, not the individual (and as such, escaping would end all the effects)–indeed, it is impossible to apply seals to someone trapped inside Cloth Binding, as the cloth disrupts the delicate chakra frameworks needed.

**Five Elements Seal**

The victim cannot spend Chakra in any way, and all Upkeeps they have (including Upkeep 0) end immediately and cannot be reestablished.

**Sacred Silence Seal**

The target becomes unable to produce noise; any sounds they would make are muted (even without their mouth being covered). Additionally, their chakra, and that of the cloth bind, cannot be seen or sensed (by doujutsu, chakra sensors, or natural chakra). If they have any of those senses, they are similarly disabled.

**Influence Seal**

It is impossible for the victim to perform any sort of taijutsu.

**Evil Sealing Method**

If the Upkeep used to pay for the technique is ended, the person who originally paid it has 20 IC to resume the Upkeep (a Speed 10 action) before the Cloth Bind's effects ends; if they do so, the cloth bind remains intact and functioning normally.

If all four of those seals are applied, the cloth bind no longer has an upkeep and becomes effectively permanent, counting as a single Exclusive Seal (despite multiple being present on the cloth). At this point it is also possible for someone who knows Nemurihane (the B-rank Genjutsu) to apply a Rune-Inscribing Technique to the Cloth Bind (which does not count against your limit for Exclusive Seals). This will allow the victim to be placed in, and woken from, a state of suspended animation at will. A total of `100 + NDB * 20` or more damage will destroy the cloth bind, freeing the trapped individual; the cloth takes damage from anything targeting them before they do. It is also impossible to target the victim with medical ninjutsu or Genjutsu while they are still trapped inside the cloth. The victim can be freed by anyone able to remain in melee, as a Speed 25, Delay 25 action which can be Aborted and restarted until the IC spent freeing them totals to 25. After the cloth bind has been escaped (by any means), all seals on it dissipate and the cloth itself becomes useless.
