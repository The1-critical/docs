---
title: "CQC Taijutsu"
---

Close Quarter Combat is the art of fighting at zero distance. All you need is just your opponent; it is about getting inside their guard and refusing to leave. Where weapons and ninjutsu create space, CQC collapses it. It is fast, and relies on proper timing, pressure, and the ability to read your opponent's next move before they make it.

---

## Core Rules

### Technique Tags

Every CQC technique has one or more tags drawn from the following set:

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Solo** | A standalone technique that can be used as its own complete action. |
| **Opener** | May be used without an active Combo. On hit, starts a Combo at CC 1. |
| **Extension** | Requires an active Combo. On hit, increases CC by 1. |
| **Finisher** | May be used at any time only during an active combo. Using a Finisher ends the Combo. |
| **Grapple-Opener** | Functions as an Opener, but also initiates a grapple. Only valid outside of an active grapple. |
| **Grapple-Extension** | Functions as an Extension, but requires an active grapple. Does not break the combo or the hold. |

A technique tagged as both Opener and Extension may be used as either depending on context. When used as an Extension, it does not re-apply the Opener's persistent effect.

### Combo Counter (CC)

When you hit with an Opener, your Combo Counter starts at 1. Each Extension that hits increases CC by 1. CC bonuses listed on Extension techniques multiply by your current CC _before_ that hit — so your first Extension hit applies CC 1 bonuses, your second applies CC 2, and so on.

Finisher Combo Bonuses multiply by the CC at time of use. Combo Counter has a limit of (Athletics) rank.

### Combo Breaking

A Combo is broken when:

- You take a non-CQC, non-interrupt action (including Move actions, Rest, and Block unless specified)
- You use any non-CQC jutsu, even as an interrupt
- You execute a Finisher
- You attack a new target
- You are Stunned

**Defensive interrupts (Dodge and Parry) do not break a Combo.** Maintaining defensive pressure while pressing an offense is the central skill expression of CQC.

Hitting a clone of your intended target does not count as attacking a new target and does not break your Combo, but does not increase CC.

### Grapple Mixup

When you are in a grapple, you may freely use Grapple-Extensions and Grapple-Finishers from your active Combo without breaking it. If your Combo was active before the grapple was established (via a Grab used as an Extension), your CC carries over.

Breaking a Combo does not end a grapple. A new Grapple-Opener must be used before Grapple-Extensions can be used again.

The grapple rules from the PHB apply normally with the following clarifications:

- The attacker maintains one hand on the defender unless a specific technique states otherwise
- Dodge and Parry both have Speed +2 while grappling
- Neither participant can Hide from the other
- No miss chance (clones) applies between participants
- Grapple checks: Attacker rolls `1d20 + PDB + STR/15`. Defender rolls `1d20 + PDB + AGI/15`. Both round down.
- The defender may still use **Shift Grapple** (Speed 5, Stamina 5) which makes the defender now the attacker or **Break Grapple** (Speed 4) which ends the grapple any time regardless of Combo state.

### Power Attack and Unarmed Focus

When any CQC technique references your Basic Unarmed Attack, it includes Unarmed Focus ranks and may benefit from Power Attack. The **d\*** notation always refers to your basic unarmed attack die size. Power Attack does not apply to dice with fixed sizes (d6, d8, etc.).

## E-Rank

**–( Trip )–**

In the chaos of close-quarters combat, even the simplest motion can decide life or death. The user sweeps low, targeting the legs with practiced precision. Should the opponent fail to maintain their balance... they fall. A prone shinobi is vulnerable and is open to far more devastating attacks and mixups. Many a prodigy has underestimated such basics, only to have their "genius" shattered against the dirt.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Accuracy:** +3
- **Speed:** 4
- **Tags:** Solo, Opener

**Effects:** On hit, the target makes an Athletics roll against your Accuracy. They gain +3 to this roll for each previous trip attempt in this battle. On failure, they are knocked Prone. A prone target has -3 Accuracy, -2 to defensive rolls, and moves at half speed. Standing is a Speed 5 action.

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While this Combo is active, any Extension that hits a Prone target forces a new Athletics check (TN 10 + CC). On failure, the target's Combo Counter increases by +1.

---

**–( Feint )–**

Deception is the shinobi's oldest weapon. With a subtle shift of the body and intent, the user misleads their foe. The opponent's defensive reaction slows, their timing thrown into disarray. A feint when perfectly timed can shatter even the most perfect of defenses, yet patience and guts will be the ultimate decider of one's fate. The technique was first documented in the chronicles of the Warring States period, where a Konoha scout reportedly defeated three samurai in single combat using nothing but feints and footwork.

- **Stamina:** +4
- **Accuracy:** +2
- **Speed:** +5
- **Tags:** Link

**Effects:** Used alongside any other melee-range CQC attack. Make an opposed Espionage roll. On success, the target's defensive action has its Speed doubled for determining their next IC. Does not prevent the defense from being a valid Interrupt. This cannot be used in an active combo—a feint is a setup, not a continuation.

---

**–( Grab )–**

The most fundamental of grapples. You seize your opponent, locking them in place. It's not elegant, and it's not subtle, but it opens up a world of possibilities—for them, a world of pain; for you, a world of control.

- **Stamina:** 8 (miss) / 12 (hit)
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Grapple-Opener, Extension

**Effects:** You initiate a grapple. If used as a Grapple-Opener, you start a Combo at CC 1. If used as an Extension, you maintain your current CC and transition the Combo into a grapple. You must not already be in a grapple to initiate one.

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While in a grapple combo started with Grab, your Grapple-Extensions (Limb Lock, Choke Hold, etc.) apply their penalties for 15 IC instead of 10, refreshed on each successful Grapple-Extension—the hold grows tighter with each passing moment.

---

**–( Headbutt )–**

There's a reason this is the first thing every drunk in every tavern tries. It's simple, direct, and hurts. The refined shinobi version uses chakra reinforcement, turning what would be a concussion into someone else's problem. The technique's effectiveness is matched only by its absolute refusal to be subtle.

- **Stamina:** 12
- **Damage:** (STR/10)d8
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Grapple-Opener, Grapple-Extension
- **CC Bonus:** −1 Speed, +1d8 damage

**Effects:** May be used by either attacker or defender. Does not require a free hand. Automatically hits if used in a grapple. If it causes a wound, it is automatically a Head wound and its severity cannot be reduced (though it can be negated or ignored). Damage applies to both participants; the attacker also takes damage if the final damage dealt would be enough to wound themselves, using the attacker's own Vitality thresholds but not their reductions. "You hit me, I hit you. Fair's fair."

## D-Rank

**–( Teiryuu Dageki — Halting Blow )–**

In one smooth motion, the user extends their arm and pushes aside an oncoming blow, steps inward, and pulls back their forearm to slam their elbow into the opponent. It's the kind of defense that offends people, because it tells them their attack was so predictable you could counter it before they finished throwing it.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Damage:** (2 + STR/10)d4
- **Speed:** 5
- **Tags:** Opener, Interrupt

**Effects:** Used as an unarmed parry with +3 Accuracy. On successful defense, this immediately becomes an attack using the parry roll as Accuracy. Links may be added at the time of use as a defense.

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While this Combo is active, your CQC Parry interrupts (not jutsu-based parries) are Speed 1 and have a +2 bonus. This applies to bare parries only, not to Halting Blow itself or any other parry-technique.

**–( Senpuu — Whirlwind )–**

The user rotates while extending one leg, starting with a high roundhouse that's used partially as a feint. Whether it hits or misses, they continue to rotate into a second spin aimed at the lower part of the body. By the third rotation, the opponent's confusion has become a liability, and your momentum has become a weapon.

- **Stamina:** 13
- **Damage:** (2 + STR/10)d10
- **Area:** 2
- **Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Solo, Opener, Point Blank AoE

**Effects:** Hits all enemies in range. Partial defense applies at 25% damage per point hit by (rather than 20%).

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While this Combo is active, all your Extensions resolve partial defense at 25% per point rather than 20%.

---

**–( Houshou — Crushing Fist )–**

Usually considered a rude thing to do to someone, the user slams their fist into their opponent's stomach at an upward angle. If done properly, it knocks the air from their lungs and leaves them gasping for minutes. It's the kind of technique that makes people reconsider their life choices—usually while curled on the ground.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Damage:** (2 + STR/10)d8
- **Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Solo, Finisher

**Effects:** On hit, the target's Stamina penalty increases by 2, +1 per wound severity inflicted (3 at Minor, 4 at Major). If blocked, the Stamina penalty from Finisher use is halved. This is an automatic called shot to the abdomen.

---

**–( Reppuu — Gale )–**

With alarming quickness you drop low to the ground and perform a full 360-degree sweeping kick that knocks opponents off their feet when they attempt to close in for an attack of their own. The wind from the kick is almost as intimidating as the kick itself—and that's exactly how you want it.

The technique is said to have been developed by a Suna scout who was constantly ambushed in the narrow canyons of the desert. He needed a defense that could turn a surprise attack into a counterattack in a single motion. The result was Reppuu—a technique that treats aggression as an invitation.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Damage:** (2 + STR/10)d6
- **Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Opener, Interrupt

**Effects:** May be used as an interrupt immediately after successfully dodging a melee-range attack, using the dodge roll as Accuracy. May also be used as a normal attack. When used as an interrupt following a dodge, no Links may be added.

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While this Combo is active, your Dodge interrupt speed (not any interrupt jutsu that uses the dodge interrupt) is reduced by -1.

---

**–( Dainamikku Entorii — Dynamic Entry )–**

This technique is nothing more than a jumpkick at an opponent from hiding. The hiding part became required because of genin's tendency to shout out things like "HIYAH!" as they did it. Once the element of surprise is gone, so is the technique's effectiveness, which is why veterans use it once and then never again.

- **Stamina:** 12
- **Damage:** (4 + STR/6)d12
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Solo
- **Requires:** Hidden from target.

**Effects:** This technique is not affected by the damage reduction from the ability Sudden attack. After the first use of this technique in a battle (by anyone), it has −6 Accuracy for the rest of that battle.

---

**–( Shoushitsu — Rising Knee )–**

The user gets in close and slams their knee into the midsection of their opponent. While not complex, the pain experienced is enough to make them double over, leaving them vulnerable for a few seconds. It's the kind of technique that makes people respect your personal space.

- **Stamina:** 13
- **Damage:** (2 + STR/8)d10
- **Speed:** 12
- **Special:** Stun 5
- **Tags:** Solo, Finisher
- **Combo Bonus:** +1 Stun per 2 CC

---

**–( Tokken — Thrusting Shoulder )–**

By charging forward and turning at the last moment to bring a shoulder out front, the user slams into the enemy, knocking them back. It relies mostly on the weight of the user, with the expectation they'll be heavier than the person they're running into. If they're not, the technique becomes a comedy of errors rather than a combat maneuver.

- **Stamina:** 12
- **Damage:** (2 + RES/8)d8
- **Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Solo, Finisher
- **CC Bonus:** +1 knockback per 2 CC

**Effects:** Knocks target back (RES/10 + 2) yards.

**–( Throw )–**

You use leverage, brute force, or a combination of the two to physically throw an enemy, either by flipping them over you, hurling them into a nearby object, or slamming them to the ground. The ground is usually the preferred destination—it's always there, it never complains, and it hits harder than you ever could.

- **Stamina:** 8 (Solo) / +8 (linked to Grab)
- **Damage:** (2+STR/10)d10
- **Speed:** 8 (Solo) / +4 (linked)
- **Tags:** Grapple-Extension
- **CC Bonus:** +2 Stun, +2 yards knockback per 5 CC

**Effects:** Ends the grapple. Target is Stunned (severity 4 + damage after reductions / 50) and knocked back up to (STR/20) yards. Throw cannot be Blocked. While this ends the grapple, it does not end the Combo, the attacker may continue with a new Opener or a non-grapple Extension on the following action.

**–( Limb Lock )–**

While there are almost countless ways to perform it, locks are an essential part of grappling that one uses to disable some part of their opponent's body to inhibit them further than a simple grapple imparts. Though easy to perform, grapples as a whole are harder to maintain with most ninja, who are often flexible and squirrelly.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Grapple-Extension
- **CC Bonus:** Immobilization increases by 1 per CC beyond the base values listed below (max total 5 across all Immobility from this source).

**Effects:** Choose one body part to lock: Arm, Leg, or Head. This can only be applied to a given limb type once per grapple. You may have one Arm Lock, one Leg Lock, and one Head Lock active simultaneously. The next locks after the first can be initiated by spending speed 6.

- **Arm:** Immobility 2. −1 Grapple roll. Seal Speeds doubled. Both arms locked: no handseals (Single-Handed Seals reduces penalty by one stage).
- **Leg:** Immobility 3. −2 Grapple roll. Both legs locked: movement speed reduced to 0.
- **Head:** Immobility 4. −2 Grapple roll, −4 Stamina penalty. Mouth-aimed techniques (fire breath, needle spit) take −5 Accuracy.

All lock penalties last 10 IC from the most recent Grapple-Extension, refreshing each time any Grapple-Extension is used.

---

**–( Choke Hold )–**

You grab your opponent's neck and cut off either the flow of blood to their brain, or of oxygen to their lungs. Either way, it's a nasty thing to be affected by. Though as far as combat between ninjas goes, it's one of the more effective and less violent ones—which is saying something.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Grapple-Extension

**Effects:** Each use applies Suffocation 1, stacking. Requires one arm dedicated to maintaining hold. The Suffocation persists as long as you maintain control of the grapple and do not use the holding arm for anything else. Any non-grapple action (other than holds or Full Nelson) ends the Suffocation.

---

**–( Rendan — Combo )–**

Not so much a proper technique as a catch-all term for any series of attacks made in rapid succession, without breaking one's flow or allowing an opponent time to retreat. The name is simple because the concept is simple: hit them, and keep hitting them until they stop moving.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Damage:** Basic Unarmed Attack + 1d\*
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Extension
- **CC Bonus:** +1d\* damage, −1 Speed

## C-Rank

A more complex version of Senpuu which involves three kicks to the target's body going in the reverse order as before, starting low and going high to end with an axekick to the opponent's head. The speed and constant rotation is strong enough to actually lift the practitioner into the air during execution. It's the kind of technique that makes people wonder if you're actually fighting or just showing off.

- **Stamina:** 18
- **Damage:** (3 + STR/8)d12
- **Area:** 3
- **Speed:** 14
- **Requires:** Senpuu
- **Tags:** Solo, Opener, Point Blank AoE

**Effects:** Hits all enemies in range. Partial defense at ⅓ damage per point hit by.

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While this Combo is active, all your Extensions resolve partial defense at ⅓ damage per point. This replaces Senpuu's effect, it does not stack.

**–( Tsutenkyaku — Painful Sky Leg )–**

After concentrating chakra in the user's legs, they leap high into the air and come down in a destructive axe kick, using gravity to enhance the momentum behind the blow. The name is not an exaggeration, the sky itself seems to punish the target. The resulting crater is as much a part of the attack as the kick itself.

- **Stamina:** 25
- **Damage:** (3 + STR/4)d12
- **Accuracy:** −6
- **Area:** 3 + PDB
- **Speed:** 20
- **Tags:** Solo, Finisher, Targeted AoE, Ground
- **CC Bonus:** −1 Accuracy penalty (min 0) per 5 CC

**Effects:** Partial success does not apply. The primary target takes full damage on a failed defense. Even on a miss, the shockwave from impact deals (PDB × Speed)/2 damage to all in the area (including the intended target). A successful defense reduces this shockwave damage by 10% per point exceeded. Airborne targets avoid the shockwave automatically. The intended primary target, if they fail to defend, takes both the direct damage and the shockwave as one combined damage source.

**–( Kage Buyou — Shadow of the Dancing Leaf )–**

The user lowers themselves to get underneath their opponent, and then knocks them into the air with a violent rising kick, leaping after them immediately after it to position themselves in the target's shadow. The technique is named for the way the shadow follows the body, and the way death follows the shadow.

- **Stamina:** 15
- **Damage:** (3 + STR/8)d6
- **Accuracy:** +2

**Effects:** On hit, the target is treated as Airborne until their next non-interrupt action. They ignore ground effects while airborne but are exposed. The next Solo CQC or Basic Unarmed attack you make against them while airborne gains +1 Accuracy and −1 Speed per point you beat their defense, up to +5/-5

**Opener Effect (Persistent):** While this Combo is active, all your Extensions and Finishers gain +2 Accuracy and −2 Speed. Your next opener can also benefit from this accuracy and speed reduction.

**–( Uchikudakute no Jutsu — Shattering Hand )–**

Ninja can perform incredible feats of strength; after years of grueling training, some can literally reduce boulders to gravel with their bare fists. That's a handy trick to have, but most ninja aren't willing to go through the whole 'years of pain and hard work' part, so instead they invented a jutsu which accomplishes a similar effect: by channeling vibrating chakra through their hand at the moment of impact, most solid objects can be shattered with a single blow.

- **Stamina:** 20 + X
- **Damage:** (3 + X + STR/5)d12
- **Speed:** 13
- **Delay:** X
- **Tags:** Solo, Finisher
- **CC Bonus:** X maximum increases by +1 per 5 CC and reduces delay by -1

**Effects:** X has minimum 0, maximum PDB. Against a person: rolls wound location (or called shot). Before wounds and damage, it completely destroys any armor at the location hit. Against objects or out of battle: destroys up to STR/10 cubic yards of hard contiguous material (not chakra-reinforced). Cannot be used against flexible materials.

**–( Asshou — Pressure Palm )–**

You deliver a powerful, downwards palm blow to an opponent's stomach or the small of their back, brutally knocking the wind from them and dazing them. Repeated use of this can bruise a person's solar plexus, which pretty much hurts more than anything has any right to, for weeks.

**–( Hangeki Rendan — Counter Combo )–**
