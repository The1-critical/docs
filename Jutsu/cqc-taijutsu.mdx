title: "CQC Taijutsu"
---

As the name implies, CQC - or "Close Quarters Combat" - is the art of fighting in close quarters; more specifically, the art of fighting using only one's body, without tools or ninjutsu. All combat demands a mastery of distance, pressure, and timing, but CQC pushes these demands to the extreme, calling on its practitioners to not only maintain their own tempo with absolute, unrelenting confidence, but to read and adapt to their opponent's rhythm with a prescience bordering on precognition.

The necessity of such single-minded focus is why some consider CQC the purest martial art - a demonstration of raw physical discipline and personal mastery, and an expression of the true self through the most direct method and medium: the fist.

Or the elbow, or the shoulder, or any extension of the body. A master of CQC is never truly disarmed until they are dead.

---

## Core Rules

CQC Taijutsu (which does not include Basic CQC Attacks) cannot be used while wielding a weapon unless specified (such as through Weapon Grapples, detailed below).

### Technique Tags

Every CQC technique has one or more tags drawn from the following set:

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Solo** | A standalone technique that can be used as its own complete action. |
| **Opener** | May be used without an active Combo. On hit, starts a Combo at CC 1. Only one opener effect can be active for a single combo - using a new Opener replaces the previous opener effect, and does not increase the combo counter. |
| **Extension** | When used during an active combo, Extensions gain their listed "CC Bonus" and add 1 Combo Counter on hit. |
| **Finisher** | When used during a combo, gains its "Finisher Effect". May be used at any Combo Counter. Using a Finisher ends the Combo. |
| **Grapple-Opener** | Functions as an Opener, but also initiates a grapple. |
| **Grapple-Extension** | Functions as an Extension, but requires an active grapple. Hits automatically, but can be blocked unless otherwise specified. |
| **Grapple-Finisher** | Functions as a Finisher, but requires an active grapple. Grapple finishers end both the combo and the grapple. Hits automatically, but can be blocked unless otherwise specified. |

### Combo Counter (CC)

CC bonuses listed on Extension techniques multiply by your current CC before that hit, so your first Extension hit applies CC 1 bonuses, your second applies CC 2, and so on. Finisher Effects multiply by the CC at time of use. Combo Counter has a limit of 5 ranks.

### Combo Breaking

A Combo is broken when:

- You take a non-CQC, non-interrupt action (including Move actions, Rest, and Block unless specified)
- You use any non-CQC jutsu, even as an interrupt
- You execute a Finisher
- You attack a new target\*
- You are Stunned

**Defensive interrupts (Dodge and Parry) do not break a Combo.** Maintaining defensive pressure while pressing an offense is the central skill expression of CQC.

\*Hitting a clone of your intended target, or hitting a secondary target with an AoE, does not count as attacking a new target and does not break your Combo, but does not increase CC.

### Grappling and Mixups

Certain techniques initiate a grapple, or require an active grapple to use.

Grapple rules are as follows:

- The person in control of the Grapple (usually the one initiating it) is the 'Attacker' and the other person is considered the 'Defender'.
- The Attacker maintains one hand on the defender to keep the grapple ongoing; which may be released freely.
- When in a grapple, both attacker and defender suffer halved movement, and when one moves, the other moves with them.
  - When the Attacker inflicts a knockback effect on the Defender, they may choose to move with the Defender to maintain the grapple, or release the grapple.
  - When the Defender inflicts a knockback effect on the Attacker, they may immediately attempt to break the grapple by rolling a grapple check against the attacker, with a bonus of (Knockback Distance/5) to the roll - on a success, the grapple ends, and they push the attacker half of the original Knockback distance. This breaks the grapple, but does not end an active combo.
- If any grappler is suffering from the Ignite status, all grapplers take damage from it. Only the most severe Ignite deals damage, if multiple grapplers are on fire.
- Dodge, CQC, and Weapon Parry increase their base speeds by +2 for all involved in the grapple
- Neither participant can Hide from the other
- No miss chance (clones) applies between participants

Grapple checks: Attacker sets the Grapple TN at `10 + CQC DB + STR/15`. Defender rolls `1d20 + CQC DB + AGI/15`. Both round down.

Unless specified otherwise, all Grapple Jutsu may only be used by the Attacker. However, the Defender has two basic grapple actions available to them:

**Shift Grapple (Speed 5, Stamina 5):** Roll a grapple check against a single attacker grappling you. If you succeed, you become the attacker in the grapple.

**Break Grapple (Speed 6, Stamina 5):** Roll a grapple check against a single attacker grappling you. If you succeed, the grapple ends immediately.

When you are the attacker in a grapple, you are limited to using Grapple-Extenders and Grapple-Finishers. If your Combo was active before the grapple was established, your CC carries over. Breaking a Combo does not end a grapple, but a new Grapple-Opener must be used before Grapple-Extensions and Grapple-Finishers can be used again. Likewise, ending or breaking a grapple does not break a Combo.

Most grappling techniques initiate some kind of hold or lock, a position that must be held to keep an opponent restrained. The average ninja is considered to have four limbs - two arms, and two legs, allowing up to four locks or holds to be maintained.

When initiating a Limb Lock or Choke Hold, select a limb to hold the lock with - this occupies the limb in question, disallowing it from use in any other action. If both of your legs are occupied, your move speed is reduced to 0.

### Weapon Grapples

Certain weapons, such as the Kusari-gama or the Suiton's Water Whip, may be used to initiate grapples. A weapon that can be used to grapple will note as much within its stat block. When grappling with a weapon, grappling is adjusted in the following ways:

- Grappling someone with a weapon does not impose a penalty on the wielder's Dodge or Parry interrupts.
- Ignite statuses do not transfer between grapplers.
- You must keep hold of the weapon to maintain the grapple.
- You can always elect to end the grapple by simply discarding the weapon (see the Discard weapon action).
- The person you're grappling cannot move further away from you than the weapon's range: any action or effect that would move them out of range moves you as well, and vice-versa.

While grappling with a weapon, you may perform a selection of grapple moves: Limb Lock, Joint Lock, Chokehold, or Throw. Joint Lock uses the weapon's damage in place of unarmed attack damage. Most grappling weapons are considered to have unlimited limbs for this purpose.

### Power Attack and Unarmed Focus

When any CQC technique references your Basic Unarmed Attack, it includes Unarmed Focus ranks and may benefit from Power Attack. The **d\*** notation always refers to your basic unarmed attack die size. Power Attack does not apply to dice with fixed sizes (d6, d8, etc.).

## E-Rank

**–( Escape Grapple )–**

Being held in place by one person and then punched into submission by all their friends is a pretty exceptionally undignified way for a ninja to go down. Accordingly, ninja are usually taught the basics of how to writhe their way out of all manner of holds.

- **Stamina:** 5 + X
- **Speed:** 5 + X, Delay X
- **Special:** Grapple-only; Can only be used by the defender in a grapple.

**Effects:** Roll a grapple check against everyone you are in a grapple with. The "X" in this jutsu's cost and speed is the amount you fail your grapple check by (or the highest amount failed by, if in multiple grapples). Thus, if you rolled a 12 against your opponent's 16, Escape Grapple would be a Stamina 9 check and have a Speed of 9. On the other hand, if you passed your check against the Attacker's roll and you met or exceeded their roll, it would be only Stamina 5 and Speed 5. This ends all grapples you're currently in. This technique cannot be Aborted, despite its Delay.

**–( Feint )–**

Deception is the shinobi's oldest weapon. With a subtle shift of the body and intent, the user misleads their foe. The opponent's defensive reaction slows, their timing thrown into disarray. A feint when perfectly timed can shatter even the most perfect of defenses, yet patience and guts will be the ultimate decider of one's fate. The technique was first documented in the chronicles of the Warring States period, where a Konoha scout reportedly defeated three samurai in single combat using nothing but feints and footwork.

- **Stamina:** +6
- **Special:** -2 to defensive rolls
- **Speed:** +5
- **Tags:** Link

**Effects:** Used alongside any other melee-range CQC or a touch-based Ninjutsu. Your target makes an Espionage roll against your Espionage TN. If they fail, they take a -2 penalty to their defensive roll against the attack, and they double the speed of their defense for IC advancement purposes - this does not invalidate their defense. Every use of Feint against someone grants them a stacking +1 bonus to future Espionage rolls to resist this technique.

**–( Grab )–**

The most fundamental of grapples. You seize your opponent, locking them in place. It's not elegant, and it's not subtle, but it opens up a world of possibilities, for them, a world of pain; for you, a world of control.

- **Stamina:** 8 (miss) / 12 (hit)
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Grapple-Opener

**Effects:** You initiate a grapple. If used as a Grapple-Opener, you start a Combo at CC 1. You must not already be in a grapple to initiate one, meaning at CC 2 you cannot grab again as long as this opener is active.

**–( Trip )–**

In the chaos of close-quarters combat, even the simplest motion can decide life or death. The user sweeps low, targeting the legs with practiced precision. Should the opponent fail to maintain their balance... they fall. A prone shinobi is vulnerable and is open to far more devastating attacks and mixups. Many a prodigy has underestimated such basics, only to have their "genius" shattered against the dirt.

- **Stamina:** 8
- **Accuracy:** +2
- **Speed:** 4
- **Tags:** Solo, Opener

**Effects:** On hit, the target makes an Athletics roll against your Accuracy. They gain +3 to this roll for each previous trip attempt in this battle. On failure, they are knocked Prone. A prone target has -3 Accuracy, -2 to defensive rolls, and moves at half speed; standing from Prone is a Speed 5 action.

**Opener Effect:** This technique begins a combo on hit, even if the target is not knocked prone.

- **Stamina:** 10
- **Damage:** Basic Unarmed Attack + 1d\*
- **Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Extension
- **CC Bonus:** +1d\* damage, −1 Speed
