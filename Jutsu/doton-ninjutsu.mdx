Earth Release is the most defensive of the five basic elements. Its techniques favor protection and control over speed and aggression. A skilled Doton user can raise walls that stop armies, sink enemies into mud that swallows them whole, or harden their own body to become nearly impervious to harm. But Earth is not purely defensive. It can hit back when needed, and pulverize with the same relentless force that shapes mountains. 

Iwagakure has long been the home of Earth Release masters. The village's shinobi grow up surrounded by rock and soil, learning to read the land and bend it to their will. The Stone Village's techniques are among the most feared in the shinobi world, capable of reshaping entire battlefields in moments. But Doton is not confined to Iwa. Any shinobi who learns to feel the weight of the earth beneath them can call upon its power.

The techniques of Earth Release require patience, control, and a willingness to stand firm. A Doton user does not run from their enemies.

##  E-RANK

**–( Ganseki Tsuppari — Rock Thrust )–**

A brutal, unrefined eruption of the crust. The practitioner slams their heel into the earth to fracture the bedrock, then forces that shattered energy outward. It doesn't matter if it's a jagged spike or a blunt boulder; the result is the same. The target is met with the raw, uncompromising momentum of the mountain itself. It is the most basic Doton, yet it carries the terrifying honesty of a landslide.

- **Chakra:** 8
- **Damage:** 4d8
- **Range:** 6
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Blunt, Ground, Projectile

---

**–( Mimizu no Jutsu — Earthworm Technique )–**

A subterranean trap that turns the soil into a liquid-like slurry before snapping it back into solid, razor-edged needles. These spikes target the Achilles and shins, calcifying in a heartbeat. If the victim tries to run, the jagged earth shreds their legs like a woodchipper. The ground stops being a foundation and starts being a predator.

- **Chakra:** 8
- **Damage:** 4d4
- **Range:** 10
- **Radius:** 8
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Piercing, AoE, Environmental

**Effects:** Dodging this technique allows people to avoid its initial damage, but they are still inside the field of rock spikes. Anyone inside the affected area moving by any means other than the Move action takes 1d4+NDB\*(Yards move inside the Area) Piercing Damage.

A single use of Earthworm Technique can do a maximum damage of NDB\*Area damage. Once this pool of available damage has been exhausted, it disappears.

**–( Monosugai no Jutsu — Earth Shaping Technique )–**

Fingers sink into granite as if it were warm wax. The shinobi bypasses the rigid molecular structure of the stone, forcing the minerals to obey the heat of their chakra. It is the birth of a weapon directly from the environment, a crude hammer, a lockpick, or a set of handholds forged into a cliffside. The user rewrites the world's density to suit their whim. History notes this as the "Architect's War-Tool," used by the Stone Village to build fortifications in minutes that should have taken months.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 8

**Effects:** You can shape the surface of a rock, or mold it in your hands like clay. If you use this before climbing an earthen surface (be it a cliff face or a castle wall) you gain a +5 bonus to Athletics checks associated with climbing it, by making hand- and footholds (for yourself only). This doesn't work if you're, saying, climbing up the side of a cliff face using a rope. I really shouldn't have needed to say that, but, yanno. When being used to dig a hole, you can use this to dig away when you know an enemy is beneath you. This takes away CHA/20 of their Buried status, up to five taken per application of this technique. It can of course be used to take Buried off yourself as well to dig out of a hole. This is applied after the end of the speed it takes for this technique.

**–( Yurokensei — Tremorsense )–**

The shinobi syncs their heartbeat to the planet's own tectonic rhythm. By pressing a hand to the dirt, the world becomes a map of vibrations. Every footfall, every shifting grain of sand, and every labored breath of a hidden enemy pulses in the user's mind. Silence is useless; as long as the target has weight, the earth will betray their location.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Speed:** 10
- **Range:** 10 + Chakra Control skill ranks and bonuses
- **Seal Speed:** 6

**Effects:** You can perform a Chakra Control +5 check instead of an Awareness check against anyone touching the ground or any rock surface (including worked stone, such as brick walls). Anyone not hiding is located automatically. If you find someone, you know exactly where they are at the moment you use this technique (if they are close enough to be in battle, this will typically break their stealth), but do not retain "sight" of them. This cannot be used for the Observe action against clones; if Tremorsense is your only way of perceiving an opponent, you cannot use Observe to distinguish them from their clones.

**–( Shindou — Tremor )–**

A violent resonance cascade. The user overloads the local crust with earth-natured chakra, forcing the plates to grind together in a localized, artificial quake. It is designed to strip a target of their center of gravity. For those caught in the radius, the world becomes a shifting, unstable fluid that refuses to let them stand.

- **Chakra:** +5, +1 per rank above E
- **Speed:** +2
- **Seal Speed:** +3
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental, Ground

**Effects:** This technique is used in combination with a damage-dealing Doton jutsu, increasing its Seal Speed and Chakra cost accordingly. The technique has a radius of 3 yards, +3 yards per rank above E. Notably, anyone who is actually airborne ("jumping as they dodge" doesn't count) is unaffected. Within this area, the shaking ground imposes a Stun 3, +1 per rank the jutsu Shindou was used with is above E; if the technique Shindou was used with already inflicts a Stun, that Stun is increased by the jutsu's rank (instead of Shindou's normal effect). Anyone not targeted by the original attack must dodge Shindou, in which case the original technique's Accuracy bonuses or penalties are ignored. This can be used on its own as Chakra 8, Speed 8, and Seal Speed 6. In this case it's Radius 10, Stun 5, centered on (but not affecting) yourself. It does also require a strike roll in this case.

## D-RANK

**–( Doki Kantsuu no Jutsu — Earthen Skewer Technique )–**

The "Rock Thrust" was a parlor trick for warriors; this is the weapon of a killer. The user forces a needle of sediment to erupt with such velocity that it achieves a state of hyper-compression, behaving more like tempered steel than common stone. As the spike pierces the air, it siphons every drop of moisture from the surrounding soil to maintain its structural hardness, leaving a parched, cracked crater at the point of origin.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Damage:** 12d10\*1
- **Range:** 12
- **Status:** Stun 4
- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Piercing, Ground

**Effects:** Earthen Skewer inflicts Piercing wounds, and has its damage increased by 10% when checking to see if it caused a wound.

**–( Tama no Nendo — Clay Bullet )–**

A glob of chakra-saturated mud is launched with the force of a ballista. This isn't a simple projectile meant to bruise; it is a delivery system for "binding" chakra. Upon impact, the mud behaves like a non-Newtonian fluid, the harder the target struggles, the more the substance hardens and grips. Within seconds, the target's limbs are fused to their torso by a substance that has the weight of lead and the tenacity of industrial adhesive. It turns the victim's own momentum against them, leaving them a statue of filth on a shifting battlefield.

- **Chakra:** 12
- **Damage:** 5d10
- **Accuracy:** +2 
- **Range:** 6
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Blunt, Projectile

**Effects:** If the victim fails a status roll of your Chakra Control vs their Resistance, this inflicts a -3 Immobilization penalty that fades by 1 every 15 initiative counts. Someone affected by this mud may make a 1d20+STR/5 (theirs) vs. 1d20+CHA/5 (yours) check to break free. This is a Speed 5 action; for every point they succeed by, the penalty is reduced by 1. For every 5 points they succeed by, the action's Speed is reduced by 5 (potentially all the way down to 0). If you hit someone with this technique while they are still suffering from the Immobilization of a previous application, you do not reapply the penalty but extend its current penalty another 10 IC.

This jutsu uses half your NDB.

**–( Iwa Funsai Buin — Rock Smashing Staff )–**

The user reaches into the crust and drags forth a mass of stone, fusing it into a singular, unbreakable weapon. This isn't just a club; it's a localized anomaly of density. By maintaining a constant flow of Doton chakra through the grip, the user ensures the weapon doesn't shatter upon contact with steel. Every swing carries the crushing weight of the strata it was pulled from. Legendary vanguard units in the Stone Village favored the "Tetsubo" style, claiming that a single strike from a chakra-congealed staff could shatter the internal organs of a target even through the thickest samurai plating.

- **Chakra:** 12
- **Upkeep:** 1
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Nintaijutsu, Preparation

**Effects:** You create a bo staff or naginata out of rocks or dense, chakra-congealed earth. When using it, you add half your ninjutsu damage bonus to your weapon damage bonus, and add a Stun 4 (or increase its existing Stun by 3) effect to it. This weapon persists until you let go of it.

**–( Shinjuu Zanshu no Jutsu — Double Suicide Decapitation Technique )–**

The absolute terror of the unseen floor. The user turns the earth around their body into a frictionless void, allowing them to "swim" through solid rock with the grace of a shark. They emerge directly beneath the target, snatching them by the ankles and dragging them down into the crushing pressure of the deep soil. The target is left buried to the chin, their lungs compressed by the earth, unable to draw enough air to even scream.

- **Chakra:** 12
- **Range:** 2
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Melee, Ground

**Effects:** If used above ground, this jutsu causes you to submerge beneath the ground and immediately enter Stealth 2, with a +5 bonus. Your awareness of the outside world is limited to knowing whether or not somebody is in range of this technique. Submerging has the listed Chakra cost, Speed, and Seal Speed.

This instantly allows you to give yourself Buried 1–5, depending on your choice. It also leaves the soil loose enough for you to move out without using another technique. Unlike Earth Shaping, you are instantly underground rather than only at the completion of its Speed.

You may burst out and attack someone in range (making this a surprise attack, but not a sneak attack unless you had the full time to prepare with them in range), grabbing them and pulling them underground as you escape. This is Speed 10, with no Chakra cost or Seal Speed and uses your CQC Accuracy. This gives them Buried status 4. It can also be used while underground, in which case the Chakra cost is paid at the time of use, but there is no Seal Speed. In this case, it could potentially be used as a sneak attack.

**–( Doro Bunshin no Jutsu — Mud Clone Technique )–**

A reactive trap disguised as a man. These clones are built from the viscous, oily sediment found in the deepest marshes of the Land of Fire and Earth borders. When struck, the chakra architecture holding the mud in human form intentionally collapses. The mud surges forward, coating the attacker's weapon and hands in a heavy, adhesive sludge that hardens instantly upon contact with foreign chakra.

- **Chakra:** 12, +4 per clone
- **Speed:** 6, +1 per clone
- **Seal Speed:** 4, +2 per clone
- **Requires:** Bunshin no Jutsu
- **Tags:** Clone

**Effects:** These follow all the rules for regular Bunshin, with the following exceptions (including the limitation of 3 clones, etc.).

If someone strikes a Mud Clone with an unarmed attack, they suffer a -4 Immobilization penalty, reduced by 1 every 10 IC. If they attack with a melee weapon, they roll 1d20+STR/10 against your 1d20+CHA/8; if they fail, the weapon is stuck in the clone (which is exposed as a clone), and retrieving it requires a Speed 5 action and an Accuracy roll using your opponent's Dodge Bonus + 10 as the TN. This can use either CQC Accuracy or a Weapon Accuracy check.

Against ranged attacks, Mud Clones can take 50 points of damage before being destroyed (and revealed as clones).

**–( Jishin Shushou — Earth Shaking Palm )–**

Harnessing a large amount of chakra into the palm of your hand, you then slam it down into the ground in front of you to blast rocks and debris up to distract your opponents, allowing you to quickly escape their field of view or capitalize on the momentary distraction.

- **Chakra:** 13
- **Radius:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 8

**Effects:** This inflicts a Stun 5 against everyone in the area if they fail to dodge it. You may immediately Hide as part of this technique. This also removes 2 gallons of water from the field, if any is present.

## C-RANK

**–( Retsudotensho — Split Earth Turn Around Palm )–**

A catastrophic siege technique that forces the ground to spiral inward on itself. The earth behaves like a giant stone millstone, grinding everything caught in the radius into fine dust. It is particularly devastating in mountainous terrain or caves, where the ceiling becomes the floor and the walls become the teeth of the world. During the fall of the Kusagakure border forts, a single master of this technique was said to have crushed an entire gatehouse into a sphere of rubble the size of a carriage, entombing everyone inside in a matter of heartbeats. The Confederacy's post-war noted the technique left no bodies to repatriate, which was considered either a mercy or a convenience, depending on which side of the negotiation table you sat.

- **Chakra:** 26
- **Damage:** 28d12\*1
- **Range:** 25
- **Area:** 10
- **Status:** Stun 6
- **Speed:** 22
- **Seal Speed:** 20
- **Tags:** Blunt, Area of Effect, Ground

**Effects:** If this hits and does not have its effects reduced by partial defense, it also inflicts a -6 Immobilization penalty until the victim leaves the entire affected area, as they fall into the destroyed section of ground. This occurs automatically if it is used underground, such as in a cave. This also removes 4 gallons of water from the field, if any is present.

**–( Doryuu Taiga — Earth Flow River )–**

You cause a long stretch of nearby ground to become mud and slide your opponents down a torrenting river of rocks and dirt, spewing them out at the end. This often leaves people hurt, and disoriented. The technique doesn't discriminate – friend, foe, farmer's fence – everything goes in the same direction. The lucky ones only lose their footing. The unlucky ones lose their allies. The river doesn't care either way. It just flows.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Damage:** 12d4\*1
- **Range:** 20, 10 Line
- **Speed:** 18
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Blunt, Area of Effect, Ground

**Effects:** This moves anyone hit by it CHA/8+5 Yards all in the direction the river 'flows' (chosen by the line). Everyone receives a Stun equal to how far they move -5; the stun is divided by 3 if they block. If this is blocked by an effect which halts its progress (such as Earth Shore Return), nobody behind that effect is affected.

**–( Doroku Gaeshi — Earth Shore Return )–**

The user slams both palms into the dirt, triggering a localized tectonic hinge that flips a massive, rectangular slab of bedrock upward. This is a reinforced monument to survival. It serves as the ultimate "No" in the language of Doton, intercepting projectiles and ninjutsu with a sheer mass of chakra-saturated stone. Even as it crumbles into gravel, it ensures the user remains untouched by the storm.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Range:** 16
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Ground, Wall

**Effects:** This creates a wall 4 yards wide and 3 yards high that takes the hit from an attack. This wall absorbs 200 + 10\* (Ninjutsu damage bonus) damage. If destroyed, any remaining damage carries on to you (in which case you cannot defend against it further) or whoever else you used this to protect (in which case, they can). As implied up there, this can be used to protect anyone in range. However, Earth Shore Return can't be used against melee attacks, or ranged attacks originating 5 yards or less away from you. After having all its HP depleted, or at the end of the IC it was used on, the wall crumbles into dirt and gravel. This wall takes double damage from Raiton, and half damage from Suiton.

**–( Kaigeki Chite — Crushing Earth Hand )–**

A gargantuan limb of reinforced rock manifests from the terrain, mirrored perfectly to the user's own hand movements. It is an extension of the shinobi's will through the medium of the planet. This massive construct can snatch a leaping opponent from the air or pulverize stone defenses with a single fist. Though it lacks a physical connection to the user, the chakra thread connecting them is so dense that the hand moves with a terrifying, human-like fluidness, making it look as though the earth itself is reaching out to claim a soul.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Range:** 10 +CHA/2
- **Speed:** 15
- **Seal Speed:** 15
- **Tags:** Utility, Ground

**Effects:** You can use the giant rock hand to perform any non-grapple E or D rank CQC Taijutsu technique, or simply a basic attack, as long as the ability is one that would use your hands. No kicks or clan jutsu are allowed. It may use C rank techniques, but doing so counts as 2 uses. The rock hand is good for only 2 uses, at which point it breaks apart into ordinary rocks unless you pay its Chakra cost again; doing so has no Speed or Seal Speed, but still has its Chakra cost and preserves it for 3 more uses. This hand uses your CHA +50 as its STR for the purpose of determining its damage bonus, and attacks with it use your Ninjutsu Accuracy.

**–( Doro Nami no Jutsu — Mud Wave Technique )–**

Your chakra applied to your legs turns the ground below your feet into mud, which you then stand upon and ride at increased speeds across the battlefield. Good for transportation and enhancing earth attacks. The technique was refined in the marshlands of the former Rain Country, where dry land was but a myth and shinobi who couldn't move on mud simply drowned. The Confederacy's logistics corps still issues it as standard for operations in the Western floodplains, though the budget for the required chakra pills was cut three years ago. You make do.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 3
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 15
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** You increase your AGI stat for the purposes of movement by CHA/2. You may choose to end this technique at any time, or as part of another Doton jutsu. If you end it as part of a jutsu, that technique gains a Stun equal to 2+(your ninjutsu damage bonus)/3, or increases its existing Stun by that much, from absorbing the mud used in this technique. This is incompatible with effects which increase your normal movement speed--such as the Runner ability, and Racing Thunder Technique. While Mud Wave is active, you do not receive their benefits.

**–( Moguragakure no Jutsu — Hiding Like a Mole Technique )–**

Channeling your chakra into the ground, you turn the dirt or earth or rock into fine sand. It affects your entire body so you slide down quickly, making only a tunnel big enough for one person. After digging your way down you can sense everything going on above and can even cover the hole to make it seem like you never left.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Special:** Interrupt

**Effects:** You dive down up to CHA/10 yards underground granting you the buried status equal to the yards you went down and are now Stealth level 2, and are inflicted with Stun 20. If used as an Interrupt, this requires a dodge roll with a +6 bonus; success, even partial, will allow you to trigger the second half of this jutsu. Next, roll Espionage +5 vs. Awareness. If you are successful, the opponents can't tell how you left, or that there's a hole in the ground. They think you simply vanished, or died (if used as an interrupt against a sufficiently impressive attack). You are hidden at Stealth 2 at this point, but cannot move, aside from resurfacing (a simple, Speed 0 action once you can move again). This can only be used when standing on earth or unworked stone. A dirt road would be fine, as would a clay road, but you couldn't use it on a wooden or brick surface. While underground, you have an additional +5 Stealth.

**–( Dochuu Eigyo no Jutsu — Moving Underground as if Swimming )–**

This technique reduces the consistency of earth into less than fine sand by channeling chakra into it, allowing the user to dig through it with ease. The first recorded use was by a Stone Village tunneler who spent three weeks beneath a besieged fortress, surfacing inside the granary. The fortress fell not to an army but to starvation. The tunneler was never given a medal. His commanding officer said anyone who lives like a mole doesn't need recognition. He needs sunlight and to frolic in grass.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 5
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** If the user knows Yurokensei, they may activate it alongside this jutsu, allowing them to find people on the surface as easily as if they were above ground watching them, but increasing the Upkeep by 5.

This may only be used while underground, such as from the effects of Moguragakure no Jutsu, or to burrow through solid rock. It works only on unworked stone (you could burst out from under stone tiles, but not tunnel through a wall). You can move underground using CHA/2 in place of AGI for determining your movement speed. In combat, this requires you to stay close enough to the surface (to draw breathable air in) that you can be found, should somebody overcome your hiding. An attack dealing at least 100 damage will break through the surface of the earth above you, ending this jutsu and penalizing any defense you take by 6, but reducing the actual damage dealt to you by half. This in practice allows you to adjust your buried status by movement. Each degree of buried is one yard, so you move one yard up, lowers your buried by one. If you move one yard down, it increases your buried by 1.

**–( Doryuukatsu — Earth Flow Divide )–**

The user hammers chakra into the subterranean "Dragon Veins" – and no, Takuto, that doesn't mean a literal dragon's body – to trigger a tectonic rupture. The ground liquefies into a high-velocity torrent of mud and boulders, erupting from a newly birthed chasm. It is a landslide on command that sweeps platoons, walls, and camps into a killing zone. You aren't just washing the ground out from under the enemy; you are rewriting the geography of their grave. Iwagakure's expansionists favor this technique for "border adjustments." The mud covers everything. No one has to file paperwork about what was there before.

- **Chakra:** 35
- **Damage:** 25d20\*1
- **Range:** Special
- **Speed:** 24
- **Seal Speed:** 18
- **Tags:** Blunt, Area of Effect, Ground

**Effects:** Any damage from this technique is tripled against an inanimate, stationary object. Don't even ask if this tripling can be done against a player or NPC. This can selectively target anything on a line with a length of your Chakra Control skill ranks in yards, and a width of 5 yards. This also removes 6 gallons of water from the field, if any is present.

**–( Dorou Domu Kekkai — Earth Prison Dome of Magnificent Nothingness )–**

This creates a dome chamber around a small group of enemies, enlaced in your own chakra to make it extra hard to escape. The name is not ironic. The "magnificent nothingness" refers to the inside of the dome after the screaming stops. The Department of Internal Integrity has used this technique on seventeen documented occasions to contain "ideological contaminants." The dome's interior is soundproof.

Every 5 IC which pass, the following effects occur: Everyone inside the dome has their Chakra penalty increased by 1. You have your Chakra penalty reduced by 1 per person inside the dome. The dome regains (damage bonus)\*5 HP, up to its maximum. You must keep one hand on the dome at all times; if you break contact the above effects end (and cannot be reestablished), but the dome retains any HP it had at that point.

**–( Iwagakure no Jutsu — Hidden Among Rocks Technique )–**

This technique allows you to blend into rocks and remain hidden. Even the texture of your body takes on that of the stones. The technique predates the founding of the village that shares its name, which is either a coincidence or the most literal naming convention in shinobi history. When asked, Iwa historians change the subject. The technique is most effective when you don't move, don't breathe, and don't think about the weight of the mountain above you.

- **Upkeep:** 12
- **Speed:** 22
- **Seal Speed:** 28

**Effects:** This allows you to enter level 3 Stealth, and gives a +10 bonus to your stealth checks while it's maintained. However, it requires rocky terrain (such as the inside of a cave, a mountain path, the debris from a destroyed wall, or a large boulder), and you cannot move (blinking and breathing are okay) while it's maintained.

**–( Domu — Earth Spear )–**

You direct chakra to flow through the skin over all portions of your body, increasing your body's defensive power to its utmost limit. Your skin darkens to a dull gray-brown, almost black. This hardening also empowers your physical attacks. The transformation is not comfortable. Users report a persistent sensation of being buried alive, of pressure from all sides, of lungs that don't quite expand all the way. The alternative is getting stabbed. Pick your discomfort.

- **Chakra:** 27

- **Speed:** 16
- **Seal Speed:** 17
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** You gain CHA Damage Reduction and increase your physical damage bonus by half your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus. Raiton bypasses all of this technique's effects. Suiton has its damage lowered by twice as much — DR of CHA×2 rather than just CHA.

**–( Doryuuheki — Earth Style Wall )–**

The most powerful direct Doton-based defense. The user manipulates surrounding terrain or generates and spits out a prodigious amount of flowing earth which forms a wall and solidifies. Creates a barrier coated with chakra that stops most attacks totally. The wall's surface always shows the strata of where it was pulled from: a timeline of sediment, a geologist's diary of old lakes and older seas. The user adds their own layer to that record. Every wall is a fossil waiting to happen.

- **Chakra:** 34
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 24
- **Tags:** Ground, Wall

**Effects:** You create a wall of earth that blocks up to (50 + (ninjutsu damage bonus)_2 )_(Speed) damage. It takes 50% extra damage from Raiton, but only half damage from Suiton and Katon. You may use Action Points to reduce Doryuuheki's (base) Seal Speed, on a 1:1 basis, to as low as 12. Note that if you are able to use Doryuuheki as an interrupt, you may do so without the need for a roll. Earth Style Wall can only be used against attacks which originated more than 5 yards away from you. Used against area-of-effect attacks, this may protect multiple people, as long as they can all be placed behind the same 15 yard long line.

**–( Iwa Bunshin — Rock Clone )–**

You generate rocks with chakra, somehow protected from a cut esophagus or chipped teeth, which is a medical miracle no one has bothered to study, which take on a human shape and appearance. The clones are heavier than they look. They also tend to sink in soft ground, which is hilarious until one of them falls through the floor of a client's house and you have to explain why there's a man-shaped hole in the living room.

- **Chakra:** 10, +6 per clone
- **Speed:** 10, +2 per clone
- **Seal Speed:** 10, +3 per clone
- **Requires:** Bunshin no Jutsu
- **Tags:** Clone

**Effects:** You may create up to 3 clones that can look like yourself or anyone else present.
