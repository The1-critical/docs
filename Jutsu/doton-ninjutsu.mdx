Earth Release is the most defensive of the five basic elements. Its techniques favor protection and control over speed and aggression. A skilled Doton user can raise walls that stop armies, sink enemies into mud that swallows them whole, or harden their own body to become nearly impervious to harm. But Earth is not purely defensive. It can hit back when needed, and pulverize with the same relentless force that shapes mountains. 

Iwagakure has long been the home of Earth Release masters. The village's shinobi grow up surrounded by rock and soil, learning to read the land and bend it to their will. The Stone Village's techniques are among the most feared in the shinobi world, capable of reshaping entire battlefields in moments. But Doton is not confined to Iwa. Any shinobi who learns to feel the weight of the earth beneath them can call upon its power.

The techniques of Earth Release require patience, control, and a willingness to stand firm. A Doton user does not run from their enemies.

##  E-RANK

**–( Ganseki Tsuppari — Rock Thrust )–**

A brutal, unrefined eruption of the crust. The practitioner slams their heel into the earth to fracture the bedrock, then forces that shattered energy outward. It doesn't matter if it's a jagged spike or a blunt boulder; the result is the same. The target is met with the raw, uncompromising momentum of the mountain itself. It is the most basic Doton, yet it carries the terrifying honesty of a landslide.

- **Chakra:** 9
- **Damage:** 8d10
- **Range:** 6
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Blunt, Projectile

---

**–( Mimizu no Jutsu — Earthworm Technique )–**

A subterranean trap that turns the soil into a liquid-like slurry before snapping it back into solid, razor-edged needles. These spikes target the Achilles and shins, calcifying in a heartbeat. If the victim tries to run, the jagged earth shreds their legs like a woodchipper. The ground stops being a foundation and starts being a predator.

- **Chakra:** 8
- **Damage:** 6d4
- **Range:** 10
- **Area:** 5
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Piercing, Area of Effect, Environmental, Ground

**Effects:** Dodging this technique allows people to avoid its initial damage, but they are still inside the field of rock spikes. Anyone inside the affected area who moves by any means other than the Move action (Shunshin, Racing Thunder, Water Shark Blast, etc) takes Xd4+NDB\*X, with X being the number of yards travelled within the area. This technique can not inflict Wounds.

**–( Monosugai no Jutsu — Earth Shaping Technique )–**

Fingers sink into granite as if it were warm wax. The shinobi bypasses the rigid molecular structure of the stone, forcing the minerals to obey the heat of their chakra. It is the birth of a weapon directly from the environment, a crude hammer, a lockpick, or a set of handholds forged into a cliffside. The user rewrites the world's density to suit their whim. History notes this as the "Architect's War-Tool," used by the Stone Village to build fortifications in minutes that should have taken months.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 4

**Effects:** You can shape the surface of a rock, or mold it in your hands like clay. If you use this before climbing an earthen surface (be it a cliff face or a castle wall) you gain a +5 bonus to Athletics checks associated with climbing it, by making hand- and footholds (for yourself only). This doesn't work if you're, saying, climbing up the side of a cliff face using a rope. I really shouldn't have needed to say that, but, yanno.

**–( Yurokensei — Tremorsense )–**

The shinobi syncs their heartbeat to the planet's own tectonic rhythm. By pressing a hand to the dirt, the world becomes a map of vibrations. Every footfall, every shifting grain of sand, and every labored breath of a hidden enemy pulses in the user's mind. Silence is useless; as long as the target has weight, the earth will betray their location.

- **Chakra:** 10
- **Speed:** 10
- **Range:** 10 + Chakra Control Ranks
- **Seal Speed:** 6

**Effects:** Make a Chakra Control roll against anyone touching the ground or any rock surface (including worked stone, such as brick walls) to locate anyone and everyone within range (i.e., it opposes their Stealth TN). Anyone not hidden is located automatically; if you find someone you locate them just as if you’d used the Search action, though you do not retain ‘sight’ of them. This cannot be used to identify someone who is hidden amongst clones, and in fact means you cannot use the Observe action to differentiate someone from their clones if this technique is your only way of perceiving your opponent (from being temporarily blinded, etc).

You can use Yurokensei without breaking your Stealth, but repeat uses of Yurokensei grant no consecutive bonus.

**–( Shindou — Tremor )–**

A violent resonance cascade. The user overloads the local crust with earth-natured chakra, forcing the plates to grind together in a localized, artificial quake. It is designed to strip a target of their center of gravity. For those caught in the radius, the world becomes a shifting, unstable fluid that refuses to let them stand.

- **Chakra:** +5, +1 per rank above E
- **Speed:** +2
- **Seal Speed:** +3
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental, Ground

**Effects:** This technique is used in combination with a non-projectile damage-dealing Doton jutsu, increasing its Seal Speed and Chakra cost accordingly. This technique has a area of 3 yards, +3 yards per rank above E. Notably, anyone who is actually airborne ("jumping as they dodge" doesn't count) is unaffected. Anyone hit within this area makes a status roll to avoid taking a Stun 3, +1 per rank the jutsu Shindou was used with is above E; if the technique Shindou was used with already inflicts a Stun, that Stun is increased by the jutsu's rank (instead of Shindou's normal effect) with E being 1, D being 2, etc, and only requires a status roll if the jutsu it’s used with asks for one. Anyone not targeted by the original attack must dodge Shindou, in which case the original technique's Accuracy bonuses or penalties are ignored, and they do make a status roll.

This can also be used on its own as Chakra 11, Speed 8, and Seal Speed 4. In this case it’s Area 10, Stun 5, centered on (but not affecting) yourself, and in such case can be dodged or otherwise defended against as normal, in addition to the normal status roll for its Stun.

The "Rock Thrust" was a parlor trick for warriors; this is the weapon of a killer. The user forces a needle of sediment to erupt with such velocity that it achieves a state of hyper-compression, behaving more like tempered steel than common stone. As the spike pierces the air, it siphons every drop of moisture from the surrounding soil to maintain its structural hardness, leaving a parched, cracked crater at the point of origin.

- **Speed:** 14
- **Seal Speed:** 12

**–( Tama no Nendo — Clay Bullet )–**

A glob of chakra-saturated mud is launched with the force of a ballista. This isn't a simple projectile meant to bruise; it is a delivery system for "binding" chakra. Upon impact, the mud behaves like a non-Newtonian fluid, the harder the target struggles, the more the substance hardens and grips. Within seconds, the target's limbs are fused to their torso by a substance that has the weight of lead and the tenacity of industrial adhesive. It turns the victim's own momentum against them, leaving them a statue of filth on a shifting battlefield.

- **Chakra:** 14
- **Damage:** 8d10
- **Range:** 8

**–( Iwa Funsai Buin — Rock Smashing Staff )–**

The user reaches into the crust and drags forth a mass of stone, fusing it into a singular, unbreakable weapon. This isn't just a club; it's a localized anomaly of density. By maintaining a constant flow of Doton chakra through the grip, the user ensures the weapon doesn't shatter upon contact with steel. Every swing carries the crushing weight of the strata it was pulled from. Legendary vanguard units in the Stone Village favored the "Tetsubo" style, claiming that a single strike from a chakra-congealed staff could shatter the internal organs of a target even through the thickest samurai plating.

- **Chakra:** 15
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Nintaijutsu, Preparation

**Effects:** You create a bo staff or tetsubo out of rocks or dense, chakra-congealed earth. When using it, you add one-fourth of your Ninjutsu Damage Bonus to your Weapon Damage Bonus, and the weapon has Stun 4 (or increases its existing Stun by 3). This weapon persists until you let go of it.

**–( Shinjuu Zanshu no Jutsu — Double Suicide Decapitation Technique )–**

The absolute terror of the unseen floor. The user turns the earth around their body into a frictionless void, allowing them to "swim" through solid rock with the grace of a shark. They emerge directly beneath the target, snatching them by the ankles and dragging them down into the crushing pressure of the deep soil. The target is left buried to the chin, their lungs compressed by the earth, unable to draw enough air to even scream.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Range:** 8
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Melee, Ground

**Effects:** This technique can only be used if you are hidden from your target or underground, and uses your CQC Accuracy (rather than Ninjutsu). If used when hidden (but not underground), you briefly submerge underground and then move to melee range with your target to grab and pull them into the earth as you pop up from underneath them. If used while underground, you gain +3 to your Accuracy in exchange for ending your submerged state.

If you hit, you inflict them with a -1 Immobilization Penalty per point your Accuracy beats their defense, to a maximum of -6. They can dig themselves out from underground as a Speed 10 action which removes the penalty entirely. Until they get rid of the Immobilization penalty from this technique they can not move (as they are stuck to the spot), but can otherwise attack and defend themselves.

**–( Doro Bunshin no Jutsu — Mud Clone Technique )–**

A reactive trap disguised as a man. These clones are built from the viscous, oily sediment found in the deepest marshes of the Land of Fire and Earth borders. When struck, the chakra architecture holding the mud in human form intentionally collapses. The mud surges forward, coating the attacker's weapon and hands in a heavy, adhesive sludge that hardens instantly upon contact with foreign chakra.

- **Chakra:** 12, +4 per clone
- **Speed:** 6, +1 per clone
- **Seal Speed:** 4, +2 per clone
- **Requires:** Bunshin no Jutsu
- **Tags:** Clone

**Effects:** These follow all the rules for regular bunshin, with the following exceptions: If a mud clone is hit by an unarmed attack, their attacker suffers a -4 immobilization penalty. If they are struck by a melee weapon, the weapon becomes stuck in the clone (which is exposed as a clone and destroyed), and it must be wrested free from the clone’s mud body as a Speed 5 action which allows a grapple roll (with them as the defender) against your clone, using your NDB in place of your CQC DB; if they fail, they get +2 to future attempts to free the weapon until they manage to successfully do so.

Against ranged attacks, these clones can take 50 points of damage before being destroyed (and revealed as clones).

## C-RANK

**–( Retsudotensho — Split Earth Turn Around Palm )–**

A catastrophic siege technique that forces the ground to spiral inward on itself. The earth behaves like a giant stone millstone, grinding everything caught in the radius into fine dust. It is particularly devastating in mountainous terrain or caves, where the ceiling becomes the floor and the walls become the teeth of the world. During the fall of the Kusagakure border forts, a single master of this technique was said to have crushed an entire gatehouse into a sphere of rubble the size of a carriage, entombing everyone inside in a matter of heartbeats. The Confederacy's post-war noted the technique left no bodies to repatriate, which was considered either a mercy or a convenience, depending on which side of the negotiation table you sat.

- **Chakra:** 26
- **Damage:** 28d12
- **Range:** 25
- **Area:** 15
- **Speed:** 20
- **Seal Speed:** 18
- **Tags:** Blunt, Area of Effect, Ground

**Effects:** Anyone hit by this technique suffers a Stun and Immobilization Penalty equal to the amount their defensive roll failed by, to a maximum of Stun 8 and -8 Immobilization. The Immobility penalty lasts until they leave the affected area (and does not fade naturally over time), which has all other Ground effects removed.

If someone uses a defense which does not require a roll, and takes damage from this technique, they instead make a status roll (and take both the Stun and Immobility penalty if they fail, as described above). If used in a cave, the Stun and Immobilization penalty is always 8.

**–( Doryuu Taiga — Earth Flow River )–**

You cause a long stretch of nearby ground to become mud and slide your opponents down a torrenting river of rocks and dirt, spewing them out at the end. This often leaves people hurt, and disoriented. The technique doesn't discriminate – friend, foe, farmer's fence – everything goes in the same direction. The lucky ones only lose their footing. The unlucky ones lose their allies. The river doesn't care either way. It just flows.

- **Chakra:** 22
- **Damage:** 10d20
- **Area:** Line 6 wide, 12 long
- **Speed:** 16
- **Seal Speed:** 16
- **Tags:** Blunt, Area of Effect, Ground

**Effects:** This moves anyone hit by it to the end of the line in the direction the river ‘flows’: the line originates from you, but can flow out to knock opponents back, or toward, to push them closer. Everyone receives a Stun equal to how many yards they move -6; blocking results in no Stun. If this is blocked by an effect which halts its progress (such as Earth Shore Return), nobody behind that effect is affected (or pushed).

**–( Doroku Gaeshi — Earth Shore Return )–**

The user slams both palms into the dirt, triggering a localized tectonic hinge that flips a massive, rectangular slab of bedrock upward. This is a reinforced monument to survival. It serves as the ultimate "No" in the language of Doton, intercepting projectiles and ninjutsu with a sheer mass of chakra-saturated stone. Even as it crumbles into gravel, it ensures the user remains untouched by the storm.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Range:** 16
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 8
- **Tags:** Ground, Wall

**Effects:** This creates a wall 4 yards wide and 3 yards high that takes the hit from an attack. This wall absorbs 200 + 10\* (Ninjutsu damage bonus) damage. If destroyed, any remaining damage carries on to you (in which case you cannot defend against it further) or whoever else you used this to protect (in which case, they can). As implied up there, this can be used to protect anyone in range.

However, Earth Shore Return can't be used against melee attacks, attacks with the ‘Ground’ tag, or ranged attacks originating 5 yards or less away from who its meant to protect. After having all its HP depleted, or at the end of the IC it was used on, the wall crumbles into dirt and gravel. The wall takes +50% damage from Raiton, and half damage from Suiton.

**–( Kaigeki Chite — Crushing Earth Hand )–**

A gargantuan limb of reinforced rock manifests from the terrain, mirrored perfectly to the user's own hand movements. It is an extension of the shinobi's will through the medium of the planet. This massive construct can snatch a leaping opponent from the air or pulverize stone defenses with a single fist. Though it lacks a physical connection to the user, the chakra thread connecting them is so dense that the hand moves with a terrifying, human-like fluidness, making it look as though the earth itself is reaching out to claim a soul.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Range:** 10 +CHA/2
- **Speed:** 15
- **Seal Speed:** 15
- **Tags:** Utility, Ground

**Effects:** You can use the giant rock hand to perform any non-grapple E or D rank CQC Taijutsu technique, or simply a basic attack, as long as the ability is one that would use your hands. No kicks or clan jutsu are allowed. It may use C rank techniques, but doing so counts as 2 uses. The rock hand is good for only 2 uses, at which point it breaks apart into ordinary rocks unless you pay its Chakra cost again; doing so has no Speed or Seal Speed, but still has its Chakra cost and preserves it for 3 more uses. This hand uses your CHA +50 as its STR for the purpose of determining its damage bonus, and attacks with it use your Ninjutsu Accuracy.

**–( Doro Nami no Jutsu — Mud Wave Technique )–**

Your chakra applied to your legs turns the ground below your feet into mud, which you then stand upon and ride at increased speeds across the battlefield. Good for transportation and enhancing earth attacks. The technique was refined in the marshlands of the former Rain Country, where dry land was but a myth and shinobi who couldn't move on mud simply drowned. The Confederacy's logistics corps still issues it as standard for operations in the Western floodplains, though the budget for the required chakra pills was cut three years ago. You make do.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Upkeep:** 3
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** You increase your AGI stat for the purposes of movement by CHA/2. You may choose to end this technique at any time, or as part of another Doton jutsu. If you end it as part of a damage dealing Doton jutsu, that technique gains a Stun equal to 2 + (your ninjutsu damage bonus)/2, or increases its existing Stun by that much, from absorbing the mud used in this technique. This is incompatible with effects which increase your normal movement speed–such as the Runner ability, and Racing Thunder Technique. While Mud Wave is active, you do not receive their benefits. Likewise, if you are flying, or knocked airborne, you lose its effects until you are back on the ground.

**–( Moguragakure no Jutsu — Hiding Like a Mole Technique )–**

Channeling your chakra into the ground, you turn the dirt or earth or rock into fine sand. It affects your entire body so you slide down quickly, making only a tunnel big enough for one person. After digging your way down you can sense everything going on above and can even cover the hole to make it seem like you never left.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 12

**Effects:** You dive down a yard underground. If used as an Interrupt, this requires a dodge roll with a +5 bonus; success, even partial, will allow you to trigger the second half of this jutsu.

You immediately attempt to Hide, with a +5 bonus to your Stealth roll, with opponents getting an opposed Awareness roll. You are then placed in Stealth level 2 against anyone whose Awareness you beat, but can not move from where you are at, aside from resurfacing (a Speed 0 action). Repeat uses in the same combat of this technique reduce the bonus to Dodge (when used as an interrupt) by 1, to a minimum of 0, and adds to (and suffers from) the normal repeat attempts to Hide in combat.

While underground, attempts to track you take a -5 penalty (if fleeing and being chased, etc), but anyone can attack you while you are under the ground so long as they are aware of you (i.e., you are not hidden in Stealth from them). While underground you become immune to non-ground Area of Effect attacks (as if you were simply not in the area), but take a -6 penalty to defensive rolls made while underground. When attacked while underground, any attack which deals at least 100 damage will break through the earth above you, forcibly ending your submerged state.

You can attack (but again, can not move from where you are at) while underground, but only with taijutsu and doton ninjutsu with a range of 1 or more, which will lower (or break) your Stealth normally; any attack which ends your Stealth automatically ends your submerged state, but those which only lower it allow you the choice to end it or not as part of your attack.

**–( Dochuu Eigyo no Jutsu — Moving Underground as if Swimming )–**

This technique reduces the consistency of earth into less than fine sand by channeling chakra into it, allowing the user to dig through it with ease. The first recorded use was by a Stone Village tunneler who spent three weeks beneath a besieged fortress, surfacing inside the granary. The fortress fell not to an army but to starvation. The tunneler was never given a medal. His commanding officer said anyone who lives like a mole doesn't need recognition. He needs sunlight and to frolic in grass.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Upkeep:** 4
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Tags:** Preparation

**Effects:** This may be used while underground, such as from the effects of Moguragakure no Jutsu, or used to enable you to submerge underground up to a yard below the surface. It can also be used to burrow through solid rock, and can be used while Stealthed without revealing you. It only works on earth and unworked stone (you could burst out from under stone tiles, but not tunnel through a wall). You use your CHA/2 in place of AGI for determining your movement speed while underground.

While underground, attempts to Track you take a -5 penalty (if fleeing and being chased, etc), but anyone can attack you while you are under the ground so long as they are aware of you (i.e., you are not hidden in Stealth from them). While underground you become immune to non-ground Area of Effect attacks (as if you were simply not in the area), but take a -6 penalty to defensive rolls made while underground. When attacked while underground, any attack which deals at least 100 damage will break through the earth above you, forcibly ending your submerged state.

You can attack while underground, but only with taijutsu and doton ninjutsu with a range of 1 or more, which will lower (or break) your Stealth normally; any attack made while submerged automatically removes your submerged state unless you are in Stealth and attacking in such a way to not break your Stealth, in which case you can choose whether to end your submerged state as part of your attack or not.

**–( Doryuukatsu — Earth Flow Divide )–**

The user hammers chakra into the subterranean "Dragon Veins" – and no, Takuto, that doesn't mean a literal dragon's body – to trigger a tectonic rupture. The ground liquefies into a high-velocity torrent of mud and boulders, erupting from a newly birthed chasm. It is a landslide on command that sweeps platoons, walls, and camps into a killing zone. **–( Dorou Domu Kekkai — Earth Prison Dome of Magnificent Nothingness )–**

This creates a dome chamber around a small group of enemies, enlaced in your own chakra to make it extra hard to escape. The name is not ironic. The "magnificent nothingness" refers to the inside of the dome after the screaming stops. The Department of Internal Integrity has used this technique on seventeen documented occasions to contain "ideological contaminants." The dome's interior is soundproof.

- **Chakra:** 32
- **Upkeep:** 8
- **Area:** 20
- **Speed:** 18
- **Seal Speed:** 16
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Ground

**Effects:** This creates a dome of earth directly in front of you, with you at the edge of its area. Anyone who fails to avoid it is trapped inside the dome; those that succeed in their defense are allowed to move to any side of it, or to simply be on top of it.

The dome has 400 + (Ninjutsu damage bonus)\*10 HP, and is considered to be Blocking for when it is relevant (such as against a Large Thrown weapon), which does not reduce the damage it takes; anyone who can see or sense Chakra can target the dome’s weak spot to deal +50% damage to it. Every 10 IC which pass, the following effects occur:

- Everyone inside the dome has their Chakra Exhaustion penalty increased by 1.
- You have your Chakra penalty reduced by 1 per person inside the dome.
- The dome regains (Ninjutsu damage bonus)\*10 HP, up to its maximum.

You must keep one hand on the dome at all times, and accordingly can not move; if you break contact the above effects end (and cannot be reestablished), but the dome retains any HP it had at that point.

Attempts to go underground will be prevented and automatically fail while the Dome retains hit points, such as through Moguragakure no Jutsu. Earth Shaping, similarly, will not work to damage the dome, as it is constantly reinforced with Chakra. Similarly, area of effect upkeeps (such as Grudge Rain) will not apply to anyone within the prison, as it protects them from its effects while it exists; non-upkeep environmental effects (such as Dokugiri, or Mist Blaze Dance), on the other hand, if already present when the technique is used, continue to persist within its area both within and outside the dome. New effects can not be placed inside it, however.

If the Dome is broken by an area of effect attack, any overflow damage remaining from the attack which broke the dome carries through to affect everyone outside of the dome according to its normal area, though they can then defend against it as normal.

**–( Iwagakure no Jutsu — Hidden Among Rocks Technique )–**

This technique allows you to blend into rocks and remain hidden. Even the texture of your body takes on that of the stones. The technique predates the founding of the village that shares its name, which is either a coincidence or the most literal naming convention in shinobi history. When asked, Iwa historians change the subject. The technique is most effective when you don't move, don't breathe, and don't think about the weight of the mountain above you.

- **Chakra:** 32
- **Upkeep:** 12
- **Speed:** 22
- **Seal Speed:** 28
