Fire Release is the element of destruction. Once it catches, it doesn't care what stands in front of it. Flame spreads, heat builds, and the battlefield starts disappearing behind it. A Katon user doesn't always need to be precise. Sometimes it’s enough to put enough fire in the right place and let it take over. Unlike some other elemental releases, fire doesn't need much help from the battlefield itself. The shinobi creates the chakra, shapes the flame, and sends it where they want it. More fire usually means more chakra, so every Katon user has to learn where to spend their strength.

Konohagakure has always had a strong tradition of Fire Release. Its shinobi are taught to control flame from an early age, and the Uchiha clan became especially famous for their mastery of Katon. Their great fireballs and other techniques helped make Fire Release one of the clearest symbols of the Leaf's military strength. Still, Katon isn't a Leaf technique. Any shinobi with the right chakra nature and enough control can learn it.

## E-RANK

**–( Hinotama no Jutsu — Fireball Technique )–**

A fist of fire thrown at a problem. The chakra is compressed in the palm, spun once, and released, the spin is what keeps it coherent in flight rather than dispersing into heat haze. Practitioners who skip the spin produce a puff of warm air and an embarrassing sound. The technique's ceiling is low, but its floor is essentially zero: it asks almost nothing of the body, nothing of the bloodline, nothing of the lineage. Any child who can form a seal can learn it.

- **Chakra:** 8
- **Damage:** 4d6.extra(4)
- **Accuracy:** +1
- **Range:** 10
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 5
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

---

**–( Asayake no Jutsu — Morning Glow Technique )–**

Fire that refuses to burn. The orb produced by this technique runs hot enough to warm a room and bright enough to read by, but its chakra composition is stabilized against ignition, it cannot set off what it touches, only illuminate it. The light it casts is slightly warmer in color than a standard lamp, which practitioners insist is easier on long-strained eyes. Whether that's physiological or psychological is unresolved. Its real value is survival arithmetic: warmth costs chakra, chakra costs food, food costs money. This technique allows a shinobi to skip two of those steps.

- **Chakra:** 5
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Area:** 10 + CHA/10
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 4
- **Tags:** Point Blank Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** The orb sheds light equivalent to a small oil lamp. This reduces darkness-based visibility effects by 4, and any Environmental Visibility Penalties by 2; multiple instances of this technique do not stack either effect.

---

**–( Enmaku no Jutsu — Smokescreen Technique )–**

This is a byproduct of incomplete conversion. The user intentionally "chokes" the chakra flow into a Katon technique, preventing the energy from reaching its full exothermic potential. This forces the chakra to bind with atmospheric particulates and moisture instead of consuming them. The resulting cloud isn't just smoke; it's an oily, chakra-heavy vapor that resists dispersal. History records this as a "clumsy" technique perfected by the Kagerō Village before their collapse, born from a need to mask the seismic signatures of their underground traps.

- **Chakra:** +2, +1 per rank above E
- **Area:** 3, +3 per rank above E
- **Seal Speed:** +3
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** This technique is used in combination with a damage-dealing Katon jutsu, increasing its Seal Speed and Chakra cost accordingly. The resulting smokescreen has a duration of 10 IC, +5 IC per rank above E. Within this area (centered on the target of the jutsu or ignite it was used with), the smoke imposes a -2 visibility penalty, increasing by one per rank above E the jutsu Enmaku was used with is. This technique can also be used on its own, using a person suffering from the Ignite status as its source; in this case it’s Chakra 6, Speed 5, and Seal Speed 3. Every 2 points of Ignite is equivalent to one jutsu rank (so “rank D”, meaning -3 visibility, at Ignite 4).

**–( Enkounebai no Jutsu — Sticky Fire Technique )–**

Chakra-infused saliva. The substance is clear, odorless, adhesive enough to require deliberate effort to remove, and chemically inert until it contacts fire. At that point it becomes a problem. The delay between application and ignition is the technique's entire value, a target covered in it has no immediate reason to know they're covered in it, no pain, no visible indicator, nothing to prompt a behavioral change until they combust.

- **Chakra:** 8
- **Range:** 10
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 6
- **Tags:** Projectile

**Effects:** Your opponent is covered in the flammable saliva. If they are hit by a damage-dealing Katon technique, the saliva ignites, applying an Ignite 5 effect to the unfortunate victim. The severity of this effect is increased by 2 for each rank the jutsu is above E (7 for a D-rank jutsu, 9 for a C-rank, and so forth).

Sticky Fire does not interact with, and can not be set off by or used to set off, any environmental effects (though the Ignite which it creates when set off certainly can).

**–( Nenshou Yari no Jutsu — Burning Spear Technique )–**

A minor clan in the archipelagos surrounding the land of water have formed a culture of wielding flame weapons, of these arms was a polearm of pure heat. The mechanism is a chakra insulation layer maintained through the grip, the same principle that lets practitioners handle other energy techniques without self-harm. The weapon produces Ignite on contact, not from heat alone but from the way the chakra inside it responds to uninsulated flesh: it recognizes the gap and spreads into it.

- **Chakra:** 14
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 12
- **Status:** Ignite 4
- **Tags:** Nintaijutsu, Preparation

**Effects:** You acquire a naginata or yari made out of fire. It uses your NDB +33% instead of your WDB for its damage. Whenever someone is struck by it, they suffer Burns 2. You may perform handseals while holding this weapon, but doing so increases the Seal Speed by 6. The weapon persists until you let go of it.

What leaves the mouth is roughly the size of a person. By the time it reaches the target it is the size of a house.

**–( Kasumi Enbu no Jutsu — Mist Blaze Dance Technique )–**

The technique involves a specialized chakra-conversion in the lungs that halts just before ignition. The user exhales a dense, hydrocarbon-rich vapor that remains in a suspended "pre-combustion" state. It is translucent and oily, displacing breathable oxygen to cause hypoxic shock in those trapped within the cloud. The chakra architecture within the gas is specifically tuned to collapse upon contact with any foreign thermal energy, triggering a simultaneous detonation across the entire volume of the mist. This gas-displacement method was notoriously favored by Amegakure's infiltration units, as the persistent rain in their territory naturally compresses the cloud, making the eventual detonation twice as volatile in the high-pressure humidity.

- **Chakra:** 14
- **Damage:** 6d10.open(6)
- **Area:** 20
- **Range:** 20
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 10
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Energy, Environmental

**Effects:** This technique initially does no damage. However, it detonates and does the listed damage using half your NDB, across the listed area, if a non-environmental damage dealing Katon jutsu (which isn’t itself) contacts its area, or if a person with an Ignite status of 5 or higher is present within it. This explosion cannot be defended against (though the effect which triggered it, if an attack, can still be). The gas is also particularly thick, and not particularly breathable: Every 10 IC that someone spends in the cloud adds a Suffocation 1 status to them. A person is never considered to be affected by more than one Mist Blaze Dance, even if the areas of the techniques overlap. If hit by the technique which sets off this technique while within its area, you combine the two damage rolls together as a single damage for determining wounds.

If this technique is placed in an area where it immediately detonates on the same IC in which it is used, it can be dodged normally as an Area of Effect attack of its normal size and speed, even if it is detonated immediately by a person with an Ignite status setting it off.

**–( Yoen no Jutsu — Ember-Burning Technique )–**

Fire applied with surgical restraint. The chakra runs at a temperature calibrated precisely below the ignition threshold of organic material, hot enough to vaporize moisture, oxidize contaminants, and cauterize open tissue, not hot enough to take anything with it that wasn't already leaving. The technique requires more control than most practitioners at this rank possess, which is why its rank is what it is despite its lack of firepower.

- **Chakra:** 16
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 12

**Effects:** Removes all foreign particles and bodies (bugs, chemicals) from the user’s clothes and body, stops any ongoing bleeding (ending the status), evaporates any liquids covering them (exception: this will ignite the D-rank Sticky Fire Technique and set off Mist Blaze Dance) and ends most external immobilization penalties which apply to you, rather than an area (such as Clay Bullet), though its heat is not extreme enough to melt metal restraints (such as those from Attached Meteor). It can even remove recently spilled, not-yet-dried liquids (including blood!) from clothing. It will not, however, cure poison.

This provides a -10 penalty on any attempts to track the user by scent (i.e. Survival rolls), but only a -5 penalty to find them (i.e. Awareness vs. Stealth) via scent because while their own odor is eliminated, this jutsu leaves a faint scent of smoke. In fact, a TN 35 Research roll can identify the use of this technique at, say, a crime scene.

**–( Ikari o Moe no Jutsu — Blazing Fury Technique )–**

The chakra settles over the hands and knees and elbows in a reddish haze that looks decorative until the first strike lands. On contact it detonates inward, converting kinetic force into thermal force at the point of impact. Fighters who maintain it through extended engagements develop a specific habit: they stop pulling their strikes and go all in.

- **Chakra:** 18
- **Upkeep:** 5
- **Speed:** 8
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Nintaijutsu, Energy, Preparation

**Effects:** This technique alters the dice of any unarmed attacks you make. Its damage dice become .extra(3) for d4s, .extra(5) for d6s, .extra(6) for d8s, .extra(7) for d10s, .extra(9) for d12s, and .extra(15) for d20s. An attack which would normally deal 8d12 damage would be changed to deal 8d12.extra(9) damage under the effects of Blazing Fury. Additionally, if an attack augmented by Blazing Fury inflicts wounds of any sort, it also causes an Energy wound of one category lower, to a minimum of minor (a punch that inflicted a Minor Blunt wound would also inflict a Minor Energy wound).

**–( Gouryuuka no Jutsu — Great Dragon Fire Technique )–**

This relies on a heat-memory feedback loop. The user compresses a massive volume of fire chakra but releases only the forward-facing shell of the "dragon." A significant portion of the thermal energy is retained within the user's primary chakra coils, keeping the internal architecture pre-heated. This residual energy acts as a primer, lowering the friction for subsequent uses and allowing the practitioner to fire repeated bursts with drastically reduced handseal requirements as the chakra system becomes accustomed to the high-temperature throughput.

**–( Joushou Ootori — Rising Phoenix Blast )–**

An offensive vacuum-generator. The "wisps" seen during preparation are the result of the user vibrating the surrounding air to its limit, creating a localized low-pressure zone. When the dark stream of fire is unleashed, it is shielded by this vacuum, which incinerates and disintegrates any incoming physical projectiles or foreign chakra that tries to enter its flight path. Records from the Warring States period suggest this technique was the bane of the Fūma clan, as the sheer thermal overpressure of the "Rising Phoenix" would destroy their signature Fuuton Ninjutsu before they could even bridge the distance.

- **Chakra:** 26
- **Damage:** 10d20.open(16)
- **Accuracy:** +3
- **Range:** 20
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** When used as an attack, it receives double your ninjutsu damage bonus. When used as an Interrupt, Rising Phoenix Blast’s damage becomes 10d20.open(11). It functions differently if when used against a melee attack, or ranged ninjutsu attack. It may Interrupt attacks so long as its Speed is equal to or less than theirs (rather than needing to be equal to or less than half the original attack’s Speed).

Against a melee action: The user may choose to call off their attack, or press on regardless of the danger. If they do not cancel their attack, they take Rising Phoenix Blast’s full damage, without any defense. However, you are also hit by their attack automatically, and cannot defend against it. If they choose to defend themselves, their attack is cancelled; they do not pay any Chakra or Stamina costs, and it’s considered half its actual speed for determining when their next action comes; any AP or other effects spent on it, however, are not regained. They then defend against Rising Phoenix Blast, with a +3 bonus to any d20 rolls made as part of their defense.

Against a ranged ninjutsu attack: This can only be used against targeted, damage-dealing ninjutsu aimed at you, or any damage-dealing Fuuton ninjutsu which would hit you. Compare the damage of the two techniques; if the damage of theirs is higher, subtract Rising Phoenix Blast's damage from that technique's damage; that is how much you take. If your technique's damage is higher, it destroys the opponent's technique, protecting you from it, and your enemy must then defend against your Rising Phoenix Blast (which does its full damage normally). Rising Phoenix Blast's damage is raised by 50% if it interrupts (or is interrupted by) a Wind Release technique, and lowered by 50% when interrupting Water Release techniques. If an attacker is beyond the range of this technique, it can still be used to defend against it, but the return attack will automatically miss (due to its lack of range).

**–( Enjoukon no Jutsu — Blazing Soul Technique )–**

A suicidal manual override of the human blueprint. The practitioner forcibly converts their entire chakra circulatory system into a specialized engine of Fire Release. By stripping away the internal insulation that protects the organs from the spirit, the user achieves a state of hyper-efficiency where every breath is a spark and every thought is a blast. The skin glows with a terrifying, bone-deep heat as the user becomes a walking sun, burning through their own life force to ensure that every Katon technique they unleash hits with the absolute maximum output of their soul.

- **Chakra:** 20

- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 16
- **Requires:** 25 Chakra Control
- **Tags:** Preparation

However, the chakra costs for all your non-Katon elemental ninjutsu are increased by 5. Even after this technique ends, its effects persist for the next 20 IC as your chakra gradually returns to normal.

**–( Jikoku Kasou no Jutsu — Instant Cremation Technique )–**

This is the ultimate rejection of travel time, a technique that collapses the distance between intent and incineration. The practitioner establishes a visual lock, acting as a conductor for two invisible, high-frequency chakra currents that intersect at a single coordinate in space. There is no projectile to parry, no travel path to intercept. The rising hum is the sound of the atmosphere screaming as it reaches its thermal limit, shredded by the overlapping vectors just milliseconds before the air itself gives up and snaps into a pillar of white-hot fire. In the dark corners of the Bingo Book, this jutsu is whispered to be a refined assassination tool from the Land of Woods, a "silent" kill that leaves no evidence but a pile of ash and a faint smell of ozone.

- **Chakra:** 20
- **Damage:** 5d20.open(20 - X)
- **Accuracy:** +4
- **Range:** 25
- **Special:** Ignite X/2
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 16
- **Tags:** Energy

**Effects:** After using Instant Cremation, you can choose to gain a Special Upkeep X, which increases by 2 each time you use Instant Cremation. This Special Upkeep is unaffected by Uniques and Wounds which modify Upkeeps, but otherwise functions the same. So long as X is 2 or greater, Instant Cremation's does not require handseals. Thus, if you had used Instant Cremation three times, you would have an Upkeep 6, and your next use of it would deal 5d20.open(14) damage. Regardless of how high X goes, Instant Cremation may not deal more than 5d20.open(4) damage. If you end the Upkeep, and then use Instant Cremation again, the Upkeep will begin at 2, starting all over again.

Once ignited, the cloud doesn't expand outward; the chakra within the smoke is designed to consume its own volume first, causing the fire to collapse inward. - **Area:** 30
- **Range:** 20
- **Speed:** 12
- **Seal Speed:** 28
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

## A-RANK

**–( Karyuudan — Fire Dragon Bullet )–**

This is the pinnacle of persistent thermal ballistics. The user creates a stable, head-shaped chakra construct that act as a high-pressure nozzle. This turret manages the cooling and shaping of multiple heat-slugs, firing them in a rapid-fire volley. Each "bullet" is a self-contained unit of white-hot heat that recognizes the resistance of a physical target and dumps its entire thermal load into the breach upon contact. During the Third Shinobi World War, Iwagakure scouts noted that a single user of this technique held a mountain pass as their allies fled, as the projectiles could melt through reinforced earth defenses and stone barricades with industrial speed.

- **Accuracy:** +3
- **Range:** 60
- **Speed:** 26
- **Seal Speed:** 26
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** This technique inflicts Burns of (damage dealt)/100; on a failed status roll, it’s instead (damage dealt)/50. You may target up to three people with this technique, using the same Accuracy for each. You can not target the same person repeatedly to enhance the technique’s effect.

Invented by a notorious serial killer who obsessed over the severed hands of his victims and craved a "quiet life" amidst his atrocities, this technique is a perversion of another B rank Katon. The user injects a volatile, "sleeping" seed of chakra directly into the target's coils. **–( Maru o Moyashi no Jutsu — Burning Circle Technique )–**

The "Wall of No Return." This technique is the signature of the most ruthless bounty hunters in the Five Nations, designed to turn a battlefield into a cage. The user manifests a circular sheet of chakra-fueled fire that stands forty yards tall, a physical manifestation of a hunter's greed. The wall is tuned to recognize "foreign" movement; any attempt to breach the perimeter causes the chakra loop to collapse inward at that specific point, incinerating the escapee with the concentrated heat of the entire ring.

- **Chakra:** 34
- **Upkeep:** 8
- **Damage:** 40d20.extra(6)
- **Range:** 50
- **Area:** (NDB), Up to (ninjutsu damage bonus) \* 4
- **Speed:** 24
- **Seal Speed:** 26
- **Status:** (NDB) Burns, (NDB) Ignite
- **Tags:** Energy, Targeted Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** This creates a circular wall of flame centered on a target within its range (which can be you), with the listed area as its radius. The wall itself is 1 yard thick (wide), and (NDB \* 4) yards tall. Anyone who crosses the wall takes the listed damage and status effects on a failed status roll. As it does not constitute an attack at that point, defensive Interrupts can’t be used to mitigate it, though your NDB for its damage is halved.

If you attempt to place this wall directly on someone when first using it, anyone it is placed under is allowed to defend against it normally, suffering no penalties due to its Area, and can choose to end up on either side of it (on the inside or outside of the ring).
