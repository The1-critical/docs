Fire Release is the element of destruction. Once it catches, it doesn't care what stands in front of it. Flame spreads, heat builds, and the battlefield starts disappearing behind it. A Katon user doesn't always need to be precise. Sometimes it’s enough to put enough fire in the right place and let it take over. Unlike some other elemental releases, fire doesn't need much help from the battlefield itself. The shinobi creates the chakra, shapes the flame, and sends it where they want it. More fire usually means more chakra, so every Katon user has to learn where to spend their strength.

Konohagakure has always had a strong tradition of Fire Release. Its shinobi are taught to control flame from an early age, and the Uchiha clan became especially famous for their mastery of Katon. Their great fireballs and other techniques helped make Fire Release one of the clearest symbols of the Leaf's military strength. Still, Katon isn't a Leaf technique. Any shinobi with the right chakra nature and enough control can learn it.

## E-RANK

**–( Hinotama no Jutsu — Fireball Technique )–**

A fist of fire thrown at a problem. The chakra is compressed in the palm, spun once, and released, the spin is what keeps it coherent in flight rather than dispersing into heat haze. Practitioners who skip the spin produce a puff of warm air and an embarrassing sound. The technique's ceiling is low, but its floor is essentially zero: it asks almost nothing of the body, nothing of the bloodline, nothing of the lineage. Any child who can form a seal can learn it.

Chakra: 8 <br />Damage: 6d6.extra(4) <br />Accuracy: +1 <br />Range: 10 <br />Speed: 6 <br />Seal Speed: 4 <br />Tags: Energy, Projectile<br />

**–( Asayake no Jutsu — Morning Glow Technique )–**

Fire that refuses to burn. The orb produced by this technique runs hot enough to warm a room and bright enough to read by, but its chakra composition is stabilized against ignition, it cannot set off what it touches, only illuminate it. The light it casts is slightly warmer in color than a standard lamp, which practitioners insist is easier on long-strained eyes. Whether that's physiological or psychological is unresolved. Its real value is survival arithmetic: warmth costs chakra, chakra costs food, food costs money. This technique allows a shinobi to skip two of those steps.

- **Chakra:** 5
- **Upkeep:** 2
- **Area:** 10 + CHA/10
- **Speed:** 6
- **Seal Speed:** 3
- **Tags:** Point Blank Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** The orb sheds light equivalent to a small oil lamp. This reduces darkness-based visibility effects by 4, and any Environmental Visibility Penalties by 2; multiple instances of this technique do not stack either effect.

## D-RANK

**–( Enmaku no Jutsu — Smokescreen Technique )–**

This is a byproduct of incomplete conversion. The user intentionally "chokes" the chakra flow into a Katon technique, preventing the energy from reaching its full exothermic potential. This forces the chakra to bind with atmospheric particulates and moisture instead of consuming them. The resulting cloud isn't just smoke; it's an oily, chakra-heavy vapor that resists dispersal. History records this as a "clumsy" technique perfected by the Kagerō Village before their collapse, born from a need to mask the seismic signatures of their underground traps.

- **Chakra:** +2, +1 per rank above E
- **Area:** 3, +3 per rank above E
- **Seal Speed:** +2
- **Tags:** Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** Used in combination with a damage-dealing Katon jutsu, increasing its Seal Speed and Chakra cost accordingly. Duration 10 IC, +5 IC per rank above E. Imposes −2 Visibility Penalty within its area, increasing by one per rank above E the source jutsu is. May also be used standalone on a person with the Ignite status as a Chakra 6, Speed 5, Seal Speed 3 action. Every 2 points of Ignite equals one jutsu rank for the visibility calculation.

**–( Kasumi Enbu no Jutsu — Mist Blaze Dance Technique )–**

The technique involves a specialized chakra-conversion in the lungs that halts just before ignition. The user exhales a dense, hydrocarbon-rich vapor that remains in a suspended "pre-combustion" state. It is translucent and oily, displacing breathable oxygen to cause hypoxic shock in those trapped within the cloud. The chakra architecture within the gas is specifically tuned to collapse upon contact with any foreign thermal energy, triggering a simultaneous detonation across the entire volume of the mist. This gas-displacement method was notoriously favored by Amegakure's infiltration units, as the persistent rain in their territory naturally compresses the cloud, making the eventual detonation twice as volatile in the high-pressure humidity.

- Damage: 6d10.open(6)

This explosion cannot be dodged (though the effect which triggered it, if an attack, can still be dodged or otherwise defended against normally). The gas is also particularly thick, and not particularly breathable. Every 10 IC that someone spends in the cloud adds a Suffocation 1 status to them.

If used to treat an open external wound, Downtime requirement is reduced by 25%, but that wound can no longer benefit from Compound Hospitals.

Provides −10 to track by scent (Survival) and −5 to locate by scent (Awareness vs. Stealth) since a faint smoke scent remains. A TN 35 Research roll can identify this technique's use at a crime scene. If used to treat an open external wound, healing time is reduced by 25%, non-stacking with medical treatment.

**–( Joushou Ootori — Rising Phoenix Blast )–**

An offensive vacuum-generator. The "wisps" seen during preparation are the result of the user vibrating the surrounding air to its limit, creating a localized low-pressure zone. When the dark stream of fire is unleashed, it is shielded by this vacuum, which incinerates and disintegrates any incoming physical projectiles or foreign chakra that tries to enter its flight path. Records from the Warring States period suggest this technique was the bane of the Fūma clan, as the sheer thermal overpressure of the "Rising Phoenix" would destroy their signature Fuuton Ninjutsu before they could even bridge the distance.

- **Chakra:** 26
- **Damage:** 15d20.open(16) attack / 15d20.open(11) interrupt
- **Accuracy:** +3
- **Range:** 20
- **Special:** Interrupt
- **Speed:** 10
- **Seal Speed:** 14
- **Tags:** Energy, Projectile

**Effects:** When used as an Interrupt, Rising Phoenix Blast's damage becomes 10d20.open(11) and it receives double your ninjutsu damage bonus. It functions differently if when used

against a melee attack, or ranged ninjutsu attack. It may Interrupt attacks so long as its Speed is equal to or less than theirs (rather than needing to be equal to or less than half the original attack's Speed).

Against a melee action: The user may choose to call off their attack, or press on regardless of the danger. If they do not cancel their attack, they take Rising Phoenix Blast's full damage, without any defense. However, you are also hit by their attack automatically, and cannot defend against it. If they choose to defend themselves, their attack is cancelled; they do not pay any Chakra or Stamina costs, and it's considered half its actual speed for determining when their next action comes; any AP or other effects spent on it, however, are not regained. They then defend against Rising Phoenix Blast as a surprise attack, but have a +3 bonus to any d20 rolls made as part of their defense.

Against a ranged ninjutsu attack: This can only be used against targeted, damage-dealing ninjutsu aimed at you, or any damage-dealing Fuuton ninjutsu which would hit you. Compare the damage of the two  techniques; if the damage of theirs is higher, subtract Rising Phoenix Blast's damage from that technique's damage; that is how much you take. If your technique's damage is higher, it destroys the opponent's technique, protecting you from it, and your enemy must then defend against your Rising Phoenix Blast (which does its full damage normally). Rising Phoenix Blast's damage is raised by 50% if it interrupts (or is interrupted by) a Wind Release technique, and lowered by 50% when interrupting Water Release techniques

The rising hum is the sound of the atmosphere screaming as it reaches its thermal limit, shredded by the overlapping vectors just milliseconds before the air itself gives up and snaps into a pillar of white-hot fire. In the dark corners of the Bingo Book, this jutsu is whispered to be a refined assassination tool from the Land of Woods, a "silent" kill that leaves no evidence but a pile of ash and a faint smell of ozone.

- **Status:** (NDB) Burns, (NDB) Ignite
- **Tags:** Energy, Targeted Area of Effect, Environmental

**Effects:** Creates a circular wall of flame centered on a target within range (including yourself), with the listed area as radius. Wall is 1 yard thick and (NDB × 4) yards tall. Anyone crossing takes listed damage and status effects on a failed status roll — no defensive interrupts apply, though NDB is halved for wall crossing damage. If placed directly on someone during initial use, they may defend normally with no area penalties and choose which side to end up on.
