---
title: "Field Equipment"
---

The difference between Requisition and Savings, in practice, comes down to paperwork. Requisitioned equipment is supplied by the village, and has an implied understanding that unused equipment comes back when the mission is over. Savings equipment is yours. If you die with it, someone will find it eventually and wonder where you got it.

---

## Consumables

### Blank Scroll

**Cost:** 25 Ryo

A large scroll, staple of a bygone age. Comes with a scroll case, scroll satchel, or other scroll carrying case free of charge. They're useless, aside from the fact that a few Sealing jutsu make use of them.

---

### Hemogenic Compound

**Cost:** 250 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

Derived from an ancient medicinal compound clumsily known as the 'Blood Increasing Pill', the modern day Hemogenic Compound accomplishes essentially the same goal; it uses a ninja's chakra as a catalyst to generate additional blood and clotting factors. The modern compound is also replete with preservatives and artificial flavors, but the mechanism is essentially unchanged from the days of yore.

- **Speed:** 10

**Effect:** Upon taking a Hemogenic Compound, for the next 50 IC, you recover RES/10 HP per IC (not Vitality), take one‑third damage from poison, and take no damage from bleeding‑related effects.

Due to the nature of this agent, a ninja cannot take more than (RES/50) Hemogenic Compounds per day - this means that ninja with less than 50 RES don't have the fortitude necessary to consume any.

---

### Crowfoot

**Cost:** 20 Ryo per 5yd Area<br />**Tags:** Consumable, Environmental, Ground

Four-pronged metal spikes, designed so that one spike is facing up regardless of how they land. Typically scattered over an area as a form of area denial. They're primarily used for slowing down non-ninja targets, as skilled ninja often possess the means to evade them.

- **Area:** 5
- **Range:** STR/6 yards
- **Speed:** 10

**Effect:** Anyone moving through at more than half speed takes 1d4 damage per IC spent moving through the area. This does not add any damage from speed or damage bonus, and cannot wound.

---

### Gauze Roll

**Cost:** 3 Ryo per yard

Broad, sturdy cloth, most often medical-grade gauze. This comes in large rolls of up to 200 yards; carrying more than one roll inflicts a -1 immobilization penalty for each beyond the first, as they are both weighty and cumbersome. Confinement Scroll Seals can carry one roll each.

---

### Military Canteen

**Cost:** 50 Ryo

A catch-all for any form of fluid storage small enough to be worn on the hips; canteens, water skins, even small gourds. Holds up to 3 gallons of liquid, typically water. For every two small containers or fraction thereof beyond the second, you suffer a -1 penalty to dodge due to encumbrance. Can be discarded as per 'Discard Weapon'.

---

### Field Jerry Can

**Cost:** 150 Ryo

A catch-all for any fluid storage large enough to require wearing across the back; a massive gourd, a jerry can, even a barrel if worn properly. Whatever the chosen container, it's bulky enough to require your whole back, and any more than that would need to be carried in your arms; as such, any given ninja can only carry one. Holds up to 8 gallons of liquid.

---

### Stun Flash Grenade

**Cost:** 50 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A pyrotechnic charge in a convenient hand-held casing which, when ignited, produces a brilliant flash of light. Much like the flashbombs of old, it's great at creating a distraction.

- **Area:** 5
- **Range:** STR/5 yards
- **Speed:** 4 or +4
- **Tag:** Link

**Effect:** Anyone within Area 5 of the targeted location suffers a -3 Visibility penalty for the next 10 IC.

You may also drop a flash bomb when Dodging as a Speed +4 Link. Used in this way, the Flash Bomb's Area is centered on (but does not affect) you, and applies its Visibility Penalty to the attacker (modifying their attack's Accuracy accordingly), but applies only to that roll (rather than for 10 IC).

---

### Wire Spool

**Cost:** 15 Ryo per 10 yards<br />**Tags:** Consumable

High‑tension 16‑gauge wire on a flat military spool. Used in a surprising variety of ninja techniques, as well as other military and artistic applications that keep it in high demand.

---

### Rope

**Cost:** 10 Ryo per 5 yards<br />**Tags:** Consumable

Standard hemp rope, or any cordage that achieves the same goal. Available in Individual lengths up to 100 yards, though nothing stops you from tying multiple ropes together. Ten yards restrains a person, rendering them fully unable to move or use their arms; a target must be incapacitated, willing, or otherwise unable to resist in order to be restrained in this way.

When used as restraints, the escape TN is equal to 20. You may attempt to improve this TN with an Espionage roll, setting the new TN equal to the result. When restrained by rope, you may escape via one of two methods:

- **Slip the knots:** Roll Espionage + DEX/10, escaping upon success. Each attempt takes no less than 5 minutes, and any observers may roll Espionage or Awareness against your Espionage TN to notice you.
- **Snap the rope:** As a Stamina 25 action, roll Athletics/5 + STR/10 vs TN 30, escaping and breaking the ropes upon success. You cannot repeat this check on failure; either you're strong enough to break the ropes, or you aren't.

### Smoke Grenade

**Cost:** 50 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A pyrotechnic charge in a convenient hand-held casing which, when ignited, produces a thick blanket of smoke. The Confederacy issues them with gray and off‑white smoke.

- **Area:** 5
- **Range:** STR/5 yards
- **Speed:** 4

**Effects:** When thrown, a smoke grenade releases a cloud of thick smoke with an Area of 5 that applies a -6 Visibility Penalty within its area for 20 IC.

---

### Combat Stimulant Tablet

**Cost:** 250 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

The official Confederacy name is Standard Performance Supplement, Type‑4, though they're shorthanded in official documents as 'Combat Stims' or, if you have a stick up your ass, 'Combat Stimulant Tablets'.

These amphetamine tabs, commonly presented in wafer form and contained in waterproof plastic tubes, are a staple of modern warfare; great for short term performance enhancement, and for keeping awake during lengthy deployments. Shinobi are advised to contact their medical teams immediately if they begin experiencing withdrawal symptoms.

- **Speed:** 10

**Effect:** Upon taking a Combat Stimulant Tablet, you immediately reduce your Fatigue by one category, and gain a +5 to Chakra Exhaustion and Stamina rolls for the next hour.

Due to its rigorous effects on the body, a ninja cannot take more than (RES/50) Combat Stimulant Tablets per day - this means that ninja with less than 50 RES don't have the fortitude necessary to consume any.

---

### Field Antivenom

**Cost:** Varies<br />**Tags:** Consumable

Antidotes to several of the more popular and lethal poisons.

- **Speed:** 6

A single dose of an antidote costs half the amount for a single dose of a poison; multiple doses cost half as much as the first. So, buying 2 doses of antidote for Black Adder venom would be (40+20) 60 ryo, and buying 4 doses of antidote for Yew toxin would be (60 + 3 \* 30) 150 ryo.

Taking a dose of an antidote immediately cures you of the poison in question and all its lingering effects. Antidotes that act quickly enough to be useful in emergencies, such as combat, aren't gentle on the body: Each antidote taken applies a -2 penalty to your Accuracy and all d20 rolls for 100 IC. However, for that duration you are immune to the poison in question.

When purchasing an antidote, you must specify which Special Effect it inoculates you from. Unless you are immune to all Special Effects in the Poison, you still take the Poison condition. Poison without a non-damaging effect is stopped as long as you have any antidote in your system.

### Field Compass

**Cost:** 50 Ryo

A small, typically handheld metal case with a needle inside that points, approximately, to magnetic north when held level. These aren't exactly precise, but they're good enough most of the time. Notably, they can have trouble working underground, or in the presence of particularly potent Raiton.

When trying to navigate the wilderness, having a compass gives a +2 bonus to any Survival rolls. If you also have a map of the territory you're in, this is raised to +4.

---

### Trauma Kit

**Cost:** 25 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A collection of bandages, ointments, and antiseptics.

- **Speed:** Special

A trauma kit can cure the status effects Burns or Bleed, with a Speed of (Status Severity \* 2), ending one of those status effects. It can also be used to stymie the bleeding from select Wounds as a Speed 10 action per category of wound. A single first aid kit can do each of these once before being expended.

Alternatively, it can treat a wound (in the sense of, "this wound has penalty X until treated, and then only penalty Y"). This is a Speed 15 action per wound category, and completely consumes the first aid kit.

---

### Signal Rocket

**Cost:** 40 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A pyrotechnic device in a convenient hand-held casing which, when ignited, produces a brilliant flash of light. Unlike the Stun Flash Grenade, however, this device is typically used for signalling.

The vast majority of signal rockets are projectiles, with built-in propellant. They are typically intended to be fired directly into the air, but there's nothing stopping the enterprising shinobi from firing them at just about anything. They're not hot enough or powerful enough to cause mechanically relevant harm, but can be used in a pinch to ignite flammable substances at a distance.

- **Speed:** 3
- **Area:** 5

When lit, they shine brilliantly enough that it can be painful to look directly at them without squinting, and become blisteringly hot. They typically burn out within 30 seconds. During the day, this is difficult to notice unless one is explicitly looking for it, and knows exactly where it will be. In twilight and darker conditions, however, a flare can be seen for a considerable distance.

The flash is painful to look at directly. Anyone within the Area of the launch point who doesn't shield their eyes takes a −3 Visibility Penalty for 5 IC.

---

### Blackout Torch

**Cost:** 40 Ryo

A small, battery-powered torch with adjustable beam width and intensity, and an on/off switch. Durable, impact-resistant, with scratch and shatter-proof lenses.

Flashlights have a battery life of about half an hour. A spare set of batteries (for another half hour of use) costs 20 Ryo, and is considered to have the 'Consumable' tag.

---

### Chemlight

**Cost:** 20 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A small, transparent cylinder filled with chemicals that, when mixed, glow brightly. A swift blow or bend to the stick will shatter the internal glass dividers, mixing the chemicals and producing a pleasant illumination.

A glow stick will glow for about an hour, and cannot be turned off once activated - breaking it completely will just result in a glowing puddle. They're available in a variety of colors, though blue, green, and amber are the most popular.

---

### Tactical Survey Map

**Cost:** 50 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A map of a given territory. These have only basic information about terrain, landmarks, and settlements - anything more specific must be added by the user.

Maps come with a protective case for ease and safety of transportation. A map gives a +4 bonus to any Survival rolls made in the appropriate territory, within reason (they would help tracking someone, or finding shelter, but not exploring the inside of a cave).

### Field Respirator

**Cost:** 200 Ryo

The modern Field Respirator comes in a variety of designs, some more imposing than others, but all are intended to achieve similar results; protect the wearer from adverse atmospheric conditions, and allow oxygen tank attachments for underwater infiltration.

- **Speed:** 5

Putting this on or taking it off in combat is a Speed 5 action. When wearing a mask, the user has a -5 penalty to all stamina rolls, as it takes effort to breathe through it. Worn this way, the user gains a +5 bonus to Resistance rolls made against a variety of atmospheric hazards (poisonous clouds, sand and dust-choked air, areas of airborne disease, and similar hazards are the most common for ninja).

With an oxygen tank attached, this bonus increases to +8, the stamina penalty is decreased to -3, and the user becomes immune to external suffocation (such as a suffocating cloud, submersion in water, or the rare vacuum). This of course does not protect against being strangled, or otherwise having one's airways forcibly constricted.

---

### Compressed Air Cylinder

**Cost:** 100 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

A canister containing 30 minutes of fresh, clean, breathable air, designed specifically to be hooked up to the Field Respirator. Attaching one takes one hand and about five seconds outside of combat; in combat it is treated as a Speed 5 action.

---

### Field Reconnaissance Camera

**Cost:** 750 Ryo

A compact camera of some variety. Ruggedly built to survive travel and use in adverse conditions, but not so rugged as to survive a direct hit. Standard issue is a handheld model; anything more advanced is typically mission-limited, or purchasable only through disreputable black market sources. Most models include a flash that allows pictures to be taken in the dark.

Despite modern technological advancements, recon cameras like this still require film reels and specialized manual development to stymie counter-intelligence efforts. Each image uses a single piece of film, acquired separately. Facilities to develop the film are rare, but exist in all major shinobi villages, along with trained staff who will do so overnight for urgent matters, or within a week for personal needs.

---

### Intelligence Film Cassette

**Cost:** 50 Ryo<br />**Tags:** Consumable

Photosensitive film on a spool. Due to the lack of standardization among models, film spools generally cannot be exchanged from one camera to another. Unfortunately, the proprietary development methods for this highly specialized recon film have been thoroughly monopolized; because of this, spools are priced at 50 Ryo per picture.

---

### Assault Grapnel

**Cost:** 100 Ryo

A three- or four-pronged sturdy metal hook, typically attached to a rope\* for use in scaling, rappelling, and otherwise creating perilous means of traversing vertical terrain (such as scaling a wall, or creating an improvised zipline over a ravine).

These see limited use among experienced ninja, who typically have more efficient ways of easily doing the same thing, but beginners often find them useful, and they can be situationally helpful even for more advanced shinobi.

\*(rope not included)

---

### Lockpick Set

**Cost:** 100 Ryo per +1 bonus

A set of small tools, easily carried in a pocket, used for opening locks in absence of the key. These are necessary to pick most locks, and grant up to a +5 bonus to Espionage rolls made to pick locks. Lockpicks are reusable, though a roll of 5 or lower on the Espionage check means they break in the attempt (which may still be successful). A broken lockpick set purchased with Savings may be 'repaired' for half of its initial cost (costing 50 Ryo per +1 bonus).

---

### Squad Radio

**Cost:** 500 Ryo (½ Mile Range) · 1000 Ryo (2 Mile Range)

A multi-channel communication radio, commonly shortened to "Squad Radio" or "Comm Device". Typically worn as a headset with an ear insert and a microphone held suspended near the wearer's mouth, though more clandestine models are available upon request. Normal comm devices can be tuned to transmit and receive on any of a dozen frequencies by the user, allowing any set of devices to be synchronized with one another. Though each village also has a few sets of specially made comm devices built to operate on "secure" frequencies, these are not typically given to shinobi on missions.

Commonly used in missions that require precise coordination or infiltration, these always receive, and are touch-activated to broadcast. Standard models have a range of half a mile, advanced models range out to 2 miles.

Be aware that while these are generally a secure means of communication, it's possible for enemies to find your frequency if you're making continual use of the comms, they know you're using them, and they're actively trying to do so.

If you suffer a Major or greater Head wound while wearing a comm device, it breaks as a result of the damage. A comm device broken this way is too badly damaged to be repaired, and will need to be replaced.

---

### Observation Binoculars

**Cost:** 200 Ryo

A simple pair of rugged binoculars - impact-resistant lenses, a sturdy frame with rubberized and molded ergonomic grips, and even a convenient strap! Use of these binoculars allows clear vision to five hundred yards for general observation, and legible text at one hundred yards with good light. Mechanically, this means Awareness rolls at these ranges take no distance penalty.

## Infiltration

### Field Concealment Kit

**Cost:** 150 Ryo · Consumable

Terrain‑specific. Available in four patterns: woodland, desert, mountain/rubble, and _other_ (the fourth category has expanded considerably as the theaters of operation have diversified). Application takes fifteen minutes.

- Result: concealment at Stealth level 3, with the roll made after you're in position.
- One use per kit.
- The village stamps are different; the compound is the same.

---

### Disguised Sheath

**Cost:** 50 Ryo · Non‑consumable

Sword Canes, Hair Pins doubling as Shuriken, and other accoutrements. This item modifies a Small weapon, allowing it to be hidden in plain sight as an accessory or article of clothing. If used on small thrown weapons, a single purchase covers 10 such weapons. Identifying anything odd about them is an Awareness roll against your Espionage TN, which gains a +5 Bonus. After you use a modified weapon, it can no longer be disguised until you refresh your requisition.

---

### Entrenching Tool

**Cost:** 40 Ryo · Non‑consumable

A folding military shovel. The blade hinges and locks via a quarter‑turn collar; the handle is wrapped in canvas that starts olive and ends whatever color the ground has been. Issued at induction. Carried until the handle splits. Most veterans have an opinion about the correct way to sharpen one.

- Can be wielded as a weapon, Speed 8, Stamina 8, (1 + DEX/10)d6 Blunt, uses Weapon Accuracy. It is not a good weapon. It is a shovel being used as a weapon, and the distinction matters.
- Digging a fighting position takes 30 minutes of sustained work. A properly dug position accommodates one person and provides cover that reduces incoming ranged attack damage by 20% and grants +2 to defensive rolls against ranged attacks. A second person can share the position but only one benefits from cover at a time. Positions can be prepared before a mission and used throughout it.
- Clears caltrops from an area at twice the normal speed.
- Can also be used to dig under or through barriers that are soil rather than stone. The Natsugakure Mining Quarter has tunnels dug with these. So does the old Confederacy fortification on the eastern road, though the shovels used there are a different stamp and a different decade.

### Propaganda Leaflet Bundle

**Cost:** 10 Ryo per Bundle (roughly 100 sheets) · Consumable

Low‑grade paper. Ink that bleeds in heavy rain. The content varies—a statistical comparison of Confederacy pension structures and actual disbursements, a list of the names of missing operatives never officially acknowledged, a grainy reproduction photograph of something that may or may not show what the caption claims—but the format is consistent: dense, authoritative‑looking, formatted as though it came from somewhere official.

Sometimes it did. Sometimes the header is _VOICE OF THE PEOPLE_ in a font designed to suggest a printing press older than the one that produced it.

They come from every faction. They are designed to look like they came from the opposing one.

- **Scatter:** Speed 4 action, Area 15, up to STR/5 yards. Creates a Propaganda Zone for 30 IC.
- **Civilian effect:** Non‑combatant civilians who enter the zone are occupied reading, arguing about what they've read, or picking up sheets to show someone else. They do not proactively assist either party in the combat and do not notice routine shinobi activity (not combat, not direct confrontation) within the zone.
- **Morale effect:** Trained soldiers and conscripts (not shinobi) who fail a Resistance TN 12 roll suffer −2 to Stamina and Chakra Exhaustion rolls for the remainder of the battle. Trained shinobi are not affected—they know what a leaflet is and what it's for.
- **Cover dressing:** Carrying a selection of leaflets from multiple opposing factions is the fastest way to present as politically unaffiliated in any setting where allegiance is under scrutiny. +2 to Espionage rolls to pass as a civilian neutral in such contexts.
- **Concealed seals:** A character with the Fuuinjutsu ability may embed a single D‑rank seal within a printed bundle during preparation. Anyone who picks up and reads the seeded leaflet becomes subject to the seal's effect. Detecting the seal requires Espionage or Chakra Control against TN 15 + the seal‑user's Chakra Control ranks.
