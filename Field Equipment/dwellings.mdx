Players can own Compounds. These Compounds house three player teams. The size of these Compounds determines the game’s server wide XP cap. Each time a building is built in a compound, the XP cap is raised. Each building has an XP cap value listed with it, to keep the average rate of the XP cap’s growth consistent based on the size of the active player base, in order to find out how much your building increases the server XP cap, you take that value, and you divide it by the number of active Compounds. This information will be available in a room on the Discord for players to log when they’ve built a building in their compound.<br />

#### **-=[ Compound ]=-**

You have selected a secretive location within Natsugakure and you have bought, stolen, or otherwise made it your own. Having a Compound allows you to build Compound Buildings, and increase the XP cap of the server. Constructing Compound Buildings requires “development” (See the “Development” ability), and each building has a development time for it to be completed. Your compound starts with the building “Stores” at rank 1, and has a capacity for two Compound Buildings (Of which “Stores” counts as one). Three people can occupy a compound at once. For each person occupying your Compound, it generates 5 development per OOC day.

Some building have XP, skill, or talent requirements, in order to build the building only one person occupying the compound must meet those requirements, but if the compound ever doesn’t have anyone who meets the building requirements of a building that’s already been built, then everyone in the compound stops benefitting from the effects of that building (If someone in the compound meets the prerequisites of a lower rank version of a building built, then everyone in the compound simply benefits from the building as if it was that lower rank). The exception to this is “Expand Compound”, your buildings do not un-build themselves.

**Base Cost:** 10

**Limit:** 1

#### **-=[ Defining Institution ]=-**

Your compound has a reputation. It’s found a niche in the local ecosphere and it’s becoming increasingly apparent what your place in Natusgakure is. In short, you have found something that you are simply excellent at doing.

Pick one Compound Building you currently have in your Compound. This Building can be made one rank higher than you would have otherwise been able to build it. This stacks with the 2 UP unique Architect. A compound may only ever benefit from this ability once, even if multiple people within the compound have it.

**Base Cost:** 25

**Limit:** 1

The stores are where development resources for the Compound are stored. Here construction equipment, blueprints, valuable materials, and funds for hiring local labor are all kept. #### **-\_[Certification Office]\_-**

Getting actual government personnel of any authority out to this backwater village is a difficult task to achieve, and in times like these, limited authority is given to the people on location to act in place of the government when it comes to deciding who does and doesn’t deserve promotion. 

This is done by empowering certain trusted institutions to organize and administer tests for certifications.

Certification Office, unlike other Compound Buildings, does not consume a Compound Building slot when you build it, and you can build multiple Certification Offices, up to (Compound Buildings in Compound / 2). When you build a Certification Office, pick one Certification from the list of Certifications, your Compound Leader is now empowered to give out Certifications up to a level of the rank of the Certification Office to whomever they wish.<br /><br />Whilst corruption is plenty in this part of the world, and it’s not unheard of for the right greased palms and political maneuverings to lead to certifications simply being given for less than deserving reasons, the Compound Leader is still expected to construct and maintain equipment and criteria for administering proper certification tests.<br /><br />(When you build a Certification Office, put some thought into what kind of tests you’d like to run. Players are empowered to run ST like events when they’re administering their tests. If you’re familiar with how the Chunnin exams were ran in previous incarnations of this game, you’ll be familiar with what this might look like. For those that aren’t, this can be anything from a written test, a mechanical gauntlet, a combat against an NPC (Or another player!), but creativity is heavily encouraged here. If you have a neat idea for how your PC might distinguish the worthy from the unworthy, this is your time to shine.)
