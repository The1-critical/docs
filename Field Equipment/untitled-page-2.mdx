## Small Thrown

### Kunai

_(Small, Piercing, Thrown)_

One of the two mainstays of ninja combat, the kunai is a small metal knife shaped like an elongated diamond. The handle is wrapped in cloth for grip, with a metal ring at the end for attaching wire or signaling. They are cheap, plentiful, and easily replaced—the Confederacy's logistics corps produces them by the thousands in factory forges that never sleep.

- **Cost:** 10 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 5
- **Damage:** 2d6
- **Range:** 5 + STR/4
- **Speed:** 6
- **Special:** Kunai may be used as a melee weapon, in which case they gain the Dual tag and +1 Accuracy, with an additional +1 to Parry.

---

### Shuriken

_(Small, Slashing, Thrown)_

The other classic ninja weapon. Small, flat, four-pointed metal stars thrown to distract and wound. They are blackened to reduce reflection in low light—a detail that matters less in the age of electric torches, but old habits die hard. The Confederacy issues them in bundles of ten. Most genin lose them in the first hour of a mission.

- **Cost:** 5 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 5
- **Damage:** 1d6
- **Accuracy:** +2
- **Range:** 5 + STR/4
- **Speed:** 5

---

### Senbon

_(Small, Piercing, Thrown)_

Large metal needles, traditionally used in acupuncture. Rarely, particularly skilled shinobi use these with devastating efficiency. Frequently, particularly overconfident shinobi use them with depressing ineptitude. The Confederacy's medical corps has been trying to ban their use as weapons for decades, not because they are dangerous, but because battlefield medics keep mistaking them for their medical tools.

- **Cost:** 10 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 5
- **Damage:** 1d4
- **Accuracy:** -2
- **Range:** 5 + STR/5
- **Speed:** 7
- **Special:** Ignores armor and half of an opponent's DR.

---

### Gyro-Dart

_(Small, Piercing, Thrown)_

A weighted dart with a grooved metal shaft that spins in flight, stabilizing its trajectory through the air. The design was stolen from a failed Kirigakure engineering project; the original prototype was meant for underwater use, but the grooves proved more effective in open air. The dart is small enough to conceal in a sleeve, heavy enough to pierce light armor, and reliable enough to hit the same spot twice. In the frontier towns, gyro-darts are a favorite of assassins who value precision over volume.

- **Cost:** 25 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 4
- **Damage:** 2d4
- **Range:** DEX/2
- **Speed:** 5
- **Special:** The gyroscopic grooves grant the weapon a stable flight path, reducing the accuracy penalty for called shots by 2 (to a minimum of 0) for every 10 yards it travels.

## Large Thrown

### Fuuma Shuriken

_(Large, Slashing, Thrown)_

Four curved blades that fold together for carrying and lock open into a pinwheel shape for throwing. The weapon was once a specialty of the Fuma clan, who treated it as an art form. Now it's mass-produced in Confederacy factories, and the art has been replaced by efficiency. The blades still cut just as deep.

- **Cost:** 100 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 15
- **Damage:** (4+STR/10)d12
- **Range:** STR/3
- **Speed:** 14
- **Special:** For every point you hit by, you add 10% to the attack's total damage, to a maximum of +150% (so, if they dodged and you hit by 3 points you'd do 1.3 times damage, and that number would be reduced by the effects of partial success); if it is blocked, it simply gets +25% to its damage, instead. HP-based defenses which do not require a roll are considered to have a roll of 10. You may also use the Fuuma Shuriken as a melee weapon, in which case it loses all of its other special effects and deals only 6d12 damage.

---

### Large Star Shuriken

_(Large, Piercing, Thrown)_

A shuriken many times larger than normal—up to four feet from tip to tip. It sees limited use due to its cumbersome size, but the damage it delivers is undeniable. The Confederacy's ordinance office classifies it as "obsolete." Field operatives who have used it classify it as "overkill." Both are correct.

- **Cost:** 100 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 18
- **Damage:** (6+STR/10)d12
- **Range:** STR/3
- **Speed:** 14
- **Special:** For every point you hit by, you add 10% to the attack's total damage, to a maximum of +200%; if it is blocked, it simply gets +25% to its damage, instead. HP-based defenses which do not require a roll are considered to have a roll of 10. This weapon's damage is considered 25% higher for the purpose of inflicting wounds. Because these are large and encumbering, they are typically worn strapped to the back; for each large star shuriken beyond the first you are carrying you take a -1 Immobilization penalty which does not fade over time like normal, though reducing the number of Large Star Shuriken you carry will lower it.

---

### Hira-Shuriken

_(Large, Blunt, Thrown)_

A flat, disc-shaped throwing weapon with a weighted rim. Unlike the star-shaped shuriken, the hira-shuriken is designed for blunt force. The weapon originated in the Land of Earth, where miners used similar discs to break rock. Some shinobi realized that what breaks rock also breaks bone. The discs are heavy, slow, and devastating. They are also easy to make; any metalworker with a forge can produce them. The Confederacy has tried to restrict their production. They have failed.

- **Cost:** 80 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 16
- **Damage:** (4+STR/8)d12
- **Accuracy:** -2
- **Range:** STR/4
- **Speed:** 18
- **Special:** This weapon inflicts Blunt wounds and ignores half Damage Reduction. On a hit, the target must make a Stamina 15 roll or be knocked prone. This weapon cannot be used as a melee weapon. For every point you hit by, you add 5% to the attack's total damage, to a maximum of +100%.

## Exotic

### Bow, Daikyu

A large bow, generally taller than its wielder. The upper half is actually two-thirds of the total length. Most ninja find these near-useless in combat; they are too slow, too heavy, and too obvious. They remain popular for assassination, where one shot is all you need and no one cares if you're obvious after the target is dead.

- **Cost:** 250 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 20
- **Damage:** (STR/4) dice; die size determined by arrow
- **Accuracy:** -10
- **Range:** STR
- **Speed:** 20
- **Special:** The bow uses double your damage bonus. Using a bow always takes two hands (even with Monkey Grip). You may take a Speed 10 Aim action which gives the attack +4 Accuracy. You may Aim up to twice for a single attack. If you have to take any action which requires movement (including dodging), you lose the benefits of your aim.

---

### Bow, Yumi

Technically called a _hankyu_—kyudo practitioners would be offended to hear shinobi call all bows of this sort 'yumis'. Ninja hardly care. The yumi is a much smaller bow, typically not more than four feet long. The Confederacy's armories stock them in bulk. They are not special. They are not beautiful. They are reliable.

- **Cost:** 150 Ryo
- **Stamina:** 15
- **Damage:** (STR/6) dice; die size determined by arrow
- **Range:** STR/2
- **Speed:** 14
- **Accuracy:** -6
- **Special:** You gain +50% to your damage bonus. Using a bow always takes two hands (even with Monkey Grip). You may use a Speed 8 Aim action which gives the attack +4 Accuracy. You may Aim up to twice for a single attack. You may dodge while Aiming, though this increases the Speed of your Dodge action by 3.

---

### Arrow

_(Piercing)_

The ammunition for bows. Each attack consumes one arrow. Arrows can usually be retrieved after battle—assuming the battle ends with you alive. The expense comes not from construction but from quality. Shuriken and kunai are mass-produced. Arrows require craftsmanship, which means arrows cost more.

- **Cost:** 50 Ryo
- **Damage:** d12

---

### Arrow, Armor Piercing

_(Piercing)_

Arrows with narrow steel tips that excel at penetrating armor. They ignore the effects of enemy armor when dealing damage, but create smaller wounds. The trade-off is accepted by anyone who has ever faced an armored opponent with a standard arrow.

- **Cost:** 50 Ryo
- **Damage:** d8

---

### Arrow, Feather

_(Piercing)_

A simple arrow that is easily mass-produced and carries well through the air. It lacks the power of other types, but it is cheap, plentiful, and good enough for most purposes. The Confederacy issues them to genin. The genin who survive upgrade to better ammunition. The ones who don't don't need it.

- **Cost:** 10 Ryo
- **Damage:** d6

**Firearm Mechanics**

Firearms can only be fired once, and cannot be reloaded in combat. In order for them to be reloaded, you must have at least ten minutes out of combat per firearm and are required to have access to someplace your character could reasonably source gunpowder from.

Firearms do not benefit from the usual **(Speed × Weapon's Damage Bonus)** in their damage calculation. The weapon does the listed dice damage modified only by modifiers that affect final weapon damage amounts.

The type of gunpowder used in the firearms that Ninja have access to has a strange interaction with the chakra that constantly surrounds Ninja. When there is too much gunpowder being kept too close together whilst there is chakra nearby, even when kept in separate containers, it will simply explode. This is usually not an issue since gunpowder will not be densely stored in any place where Ninja frequent, but it does limit how many guns any one Ninja can have on their person. The limit is 2 loaded firearms at any given time. These are outfitted for holding multiple rounds, since gunpowder and chakra doesn't mix so well, when carried by Ninja it is traditionally carried with only a single round, pre-chambered and ready to fire. 

**Firearm Abilities**

-=[ **Custom Loads** ]=-

Whilst the sheer amount of gunpowder you can have on your person remains a hard limit, you become increasingly expert at packing that gunpowder into your firearms in such a way that you 'get more boom for your buck'.

Each rank of Custom Loads increases the damage of Pistols and Pepperboxes by 6d8, the damage of Battle Rifles by 2d10 (per round), and the damage of Sniper Rifles by 9d12.

## **Firearms**

### **Teiketsu **

The Southern Province has always understood that diplomacy only works on paperwork, but a state can only survive on the threat of force. The Teiketsu was designed by the Public Leaf's armory corps as the standard sidearm for internal security officers and diplomatic envoys. This is called the old reliable. It fits in a belt holster, clears a coat without snagging, and discharges a single copper-jacketed round with enough force to put a man down at fifteen yards. Every officer in the Department of Internal Integrity carries one. Most have never fired it outside a training range. The ones who have don't talk about it.

Cost: 120 Ryo<br />Range: 15<br />Damage: 14d8<br />Speed: 8<br />Tags: Ranged, Piercing, Firearm

### **Mitsubachi **

The West has never known peace, an endless toil of conflict since time in memorial.  Mitsubachi ** **was born in the desert, in the border towns between Wind and Earth where disputes are settled in the street and the winner is whoever is still standing when the dust clears. It is a compact, multi-barreled monstrosity designed to be fired at contact range into a crowd. The barrels are short, the spread is wide, and the sound is something between a thunderclap and a scream. The name comes from the way it stings, not once, but several times at once, and by the time you feel the first one, the rest have already landed.

 Cost: 200 Ryo<br />Range: 6<br />Area: 3<br />Damage: 14d8<br />Speed: 10<br />Tags: Ranged, Piercing, Firearm, Area of Effect

### **Raijū **

After the old town burnt down during the reformation period, the Raijū was developed in Kumogakure's military foundries as a response to the escalating arms race between the Silver and Golden Branches. It is a heavy, box-fed rifle with a ten-round magazine and a rate of fire that turns the battlefield into a corridor of lead. The name is a reference to the mythical beast that rides lightning and devours thunder, a fitting moniker for a weapon that can empty its entire clip in the time it takes a shinobi to form three handseals. 

Cost: 300 Ryo<br />Range: 40<br />Damage: 4d10 (per round)<br />Speed: 12<br />Tags: Ranged, Piercing, Firearm

Special: Unlike other guns, the Battlerifle can be fired more than once. It comes with a clip loaded with 10 rounds, though like other firearms, once you have shot these rounds the Battlerifle cannot be reloaded in combat.

When you fire the Battlerifle, you choose how many of the remaining rounds to shoot, doing more damage per round shot, but giving you an ACC penalty that increases depending on how many rounds you are firing beyond 3. The ACC penalty obtained from firing more than 3 rounds is not removed by performing the attack as a Sneak Attack, though it does benefit from the other benefits of Sneak Attacks.

- 1–3 Rounds: No penalty
- 4–7 Rounds: −1 ACC per round over 3
- 8–10 Rounds: −4 ACC, plus −2 per round over 7

The Battle Rifle counts as two loaded firearms for the purposes of determining how many loaded firearms you can carry on your person at once.

### **Shinkirō **

Hunters of the North popularized this weapon, specifically the little village of Shimogakure . The Shinkirō is not a weapon you carry into a firefight. It is a weapon you carry to a rooftop two hundred yards away, set on a bipod, and wait for three days. It is a long, heavy rifle chambered for a round that can punch through steel plating and keep going. Kiri's hunter-nin favours it for obvious reasons. So do the occasional Crowns operative who's managed to get their hands on one through the black market.

Cost: 250 Ryo<br />Range: 200<br />Damage: 21d12<br />Accuracy: −8<br />Speed: 20<br />Tags: Ranged, Piercing, Firearm

Special: You may take a Speed 10 Aim action which gives the attack +4 Accuracy. You may Aim up to twice for a single attack. If you have to take any action which requires movement (including dodging), you lose the benefits of your aim. Unlike the Battlerifle, attacking with the Sniper Rifle as a Sneak Attack does remove its Accuracy penalty.
