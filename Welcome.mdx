**Naruto World** is a labor-of-love homebrew roleplaying system that draws inspiration from games like Exalted and d20 systems to craft a unique experience set in the Naruto universe. 

This non-commercial project has been refined by a passionate community of contributors over many years, with a core philosophy of creating engaging stories where players can step into the roles of shinobi.

The system operates on a cyclical development model, with major restarts, featuring new campaigns and mechanical expansions, occurring approximately every 2-3 years. This keeps the game fresh and allows the ruleset to evolve while maintaining the heart of what makes the setting special.

You'll find regular updates to the Player's Handbook released on Friday evenings, along with a full changelog documenting each change as it's published. These updates represent the ongoing collaborative refinement of a project that exists purely for the enjoyment of those dedicated enough to master its nuances.

Shotai exists because of the countless designers and players who've contributed over the years. First and foremost, this game is a tribute to the late Green Rabite, whose vision made this project possible. A more complete [list of all contributors](Credits) throughout its history is available to recognize everyone who's helped shape this world and ruleset into what it is today.

We invite you to join the community and help create the next chapter of stories in this world.
